**Nine of the ten results are articles about courses. One is a course.**

That is the whole strategic read. The page that ranks with an actual timetable is a UK county council's
adult-learning service, which has no authority advantage over this domain, no national brand, and no
product beyond a two-hour beginner session.

**So do not write another listicle.** Ranking a vendor listicle against BestColleges and edX is an
authority fight this domain loses. Write the thing the SERP has almost none of: **a real, live,
scheduled, small-batch adult class with the timetable on the page.** That is a different content type
competing for the same query, and there is one weak occupant of that slot.

The same syndicated free-course roundup appearing on three separate university career-center blogs is
also a link-building signal, and it is picked up in `10-linkbuilding.md`.

## Content gaps

Covered by nobody in the top 10, and all of them things this business can honestly claim:

1. **A published schedule.** Every listicle sends you off-site to find out when anything runs. Put
   real evening and weekend slots on the page.
2. **Live small batches with the ratio stated.** The SERP is self-paced MOOCs and free videos.
   Completion rates for those run in the 5-15% band and every adult who has abandoned one knows it.
   Nobody is drawing that contrast.
3. **The maths blocker.** Adults returning to code stall on maths, not syntax. Not one result addresses
   it. This is the only school in the SERP that teaches both subjects.
4. **An honest account of what is harder after 35.** Every page says age is no barrier. None says which
   parts genuinely are harder, or what to do about them.
5. **Learning alongside your own child.** Structurally uncopyable by anyone in this SERP.
6. **The over-55 learner.** Entirely unserved.
7. **Price, shown.** Most competitors hide it. Showing it is an advantage, provided it stays
   region-switched and out of FAQ schema.

## E-E-A-T requirements

The page cannot ship honestly without these, and three of them need the owner:

- **A named instructor who teaches the adult batches**, with a credential and an external profile link.
- **At least one real adult student outcome**: age, starting point, what they built, where they are
  now. One real story beats five generic testimonials, and the site's existing testimonials are all
  parents of children.
- **The actual batch ratio and session length**, taken from `brand-facts.json` rather than typed by
  hand. Note the open contradiction in action item C: three different group sizes are currently live.
- **External citations:** US Bureau of Labor Statistics for the 17%-through-2033 software-developer
  growth figure that this SERP quotes second-hand, and Bloom (1984) on the tutoring effect.
