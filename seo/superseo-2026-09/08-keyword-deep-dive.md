# Keyword Deep Dive — the adult learner

Skill: `superseo:keyword-deep-dive` · Run 2026-09-03

Two keywords analysed, because the first one turned out to be the wrong thing to chase and the second
turned out to be already half-won.

---

## Keyword 1: "learn to code at 40"

| | |
|---|---|
| Intent | **Informational**, reassurance-seeking. The searcher is not buying, they are asking permission. |
| Difficulty | **Hard**, and hard in an unwinnable way |
| Zero-click risk | **High.** Informational queries end without a click roughly 60% of the time, and 77% on mobile ([Digital Bloom, 2026](https://thedigitalbloom.com/learn/organic-traffic-crisis-report-2026-update/)) |
| SERP features | AI Overview present. Organic CTR for the top result drops 58-61% when it is ([Ahrefs, Dec 2025](https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-overviews-reduce-clicks-update/)) |

**Who holds it:** SitePoint, learntocodewith.me, the **freeCodeCamp forum**, Medium, **Hacker News**,
computerscience.org, ECPI, learn.org, PurpleTutor.

**Verdict: do not fight for position 1-3.** The skill's own rule applies exactly here: forum and
aggregator results (freeCodeCamp forum, Hacker News) are a different content type and the fight is
unwinnable. Not one live-class provider ranks on this SERP, and that is not an oversight, it is the
SERP telling you it does not want a vendor page.

**What it is still worth:** citation, not clicks. The AI Overview for this query is assembled from
generic encouragement ("age is not a barrier", "companies value maturity"). A school that has actually
taught this cohort could supply the specific thing none of those sources have. Chase the citation,
budget zero clicks, and treat it as top-of-funnel brand exposure. That reframing matters because
brands cited in an AI Overview earn about 35% more clicks on their *other* queries.

## Keyword 2: "live online coding classes for adults" (and its long tail)

**This is the one.** And the surprise is that the site is already there.

Searching *"live online coding classes for adults beginners small batch"*, the AI answer names
**Modern Age Coders first**, ahead of General Assembly, Codeop, Code Platoon and MIT xPRO, and
describes it accurately: *"live and interactive classes with small batch sizes, real projects,
personalized feedback, weekend and evening batches."*

| | |
|---|---|
| Intent | **Commercial investigation**, with transactional intent close behind |
| Difficulty | **Moderate.** The competition is bootcamps and MOOCs, not kids-coding brands |
| Zero-click risk | **Low.** Commercial queries convert; people click through to compare |
| Our position | surfacing already, but through **`/courses`**, a generic catalogue page |

**The problem:** the page ranking for adult intent is the general course catalogue. It was not written
for a 41-year-old career changer, it does not speak to one, and it converts them worse than a page
built for them would.

**The competitive set is completely different from the kids SERPs**, and this matters: General
Assembly, Code Platoon, MIT xPRO, Codeop, Coursera. None of them are the kids-coding brands
(Codingal, WhiteHat Jr, Codeyoung, 98thPercentile) who dominate every other query this site competes
on. **Every one of those kids brands stops at 18.** In the adult SERP they are simply absent.

## The finding that makes this actionable

The site already has adult **spokes**. It has no adult **hub**.

Existing, live, already indexed:

- `/ai-classes-for-adults`
- `/java-classes-for-adults`
- `/ai-and-machine-learning-for-working-professionals`
- `/online-maths-classes-for-adults-in-usa`, `-in-uk`, `/maths-classes-for-adults-uae`
- `/summer-coding-camp-adults`, `/winter-coding-camp-adults`
- `/category/professionals`

Missing: **`/coding-classes-for-adults`**. There is no page that owns the head term, and nothing for
the spokes to link up into. This is the textbook topical-authority failure: seven or more spokes
pointing at nothing, so none of the equity concentrates and no single URL is a candidate for the head
term. Full architecture in `07-topic-cluster-planning.md`.

## Content gaps across the adult SERP

What the top results do not cover, and a school teaching 6-to-67 can:

1. **Adults learning alongside their own children.** This site teaches both. Nobody else in either
   SERP can write this, and it is a real and common motivation.
2. **What is genuinely harder after 35, said honestly.** Every page in the SERP says age is no
   barrier. None says which parts *are* harder and what to do about them. Honest beats cheerful, and
   it is more citable.
3. **Maths as the actual blocker.** Adults returning to code usually stall on maths, not syntax. This
   is the only school in either SERP that teaches both, so it is the only one that can address it.
4. **Small live batches versus self-paced MOOCs.** Coursera and Udemy dominate adult learning and
   have roughly 5-15% completion. A live batch with a mentor is a different product; nobody in the
   SERP is drawing that contrast for adults.
5. **The over-55 learner.** Completely unserved. The site's own upper bound is 67.

## Ranking strategy

**Build `/coding-classes-for-adults` as a hub.** Content type: landing page with pillar depth.
Target length 2,200 to 2,800 words, which is the top-5 average plus a margin.

Must cover: formats and batch sizes with real digits, evening and weekend scheduling across time
zones, the honest age discussion, maths-and-coding together, the beginner-to-first-project path, price
transparency (region-switched), and named adult student outcomes once the owner supplies them.

### Title tag options

1. `Live Online Coding Classes for Adults, Any Age | Modern Age Coders` (65) — slightly long, trim to
   `Live Online Coding Classes for Adults | Modern Age Coders` (56).
2. `Coding Classes for Adults: Live, Small Batches, Ages 18-67` (58) — **recommended.** Leads with the
   keyword, and "18-67" is the differentiator no competitor can copy.

### Meta description (152)

`Live online coding and maths classes for adults, from complete beginner to career changer. Small batches, real projects, evening and weekend slots worldwide.`
