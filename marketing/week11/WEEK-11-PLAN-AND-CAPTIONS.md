# Week 11 - Social Plan & Captions

**Theme:** MEME WEEK TWO. Fourteen meme formats we have never used, chosen because they are instantly recognisable on Instagram and Pinterest and they are all still selling the courses.
**Formats used (all new vs Week 10):** Trade Offer, Panik/Kalm/Panik, Choose Your Fighter, Starterpack, Green Flags, Bingo Card, Wrapped, Nutrition Facts label, Five Stages of Grief, Search Autocomplete, Iceberg, Wanted Poster, Hard No / Hard Yes, closing statement.
**Look:** THREE canvases so the grid never reads as templated.
  1. **Notebook frame** (warm brand) for most.
  2. **Full-bleed statement** (`body.bleed`) for 07 Wrapped (yellow) and 14 closer (pine).
  3. **Plain white** (`body.plain`, Georgia serif) for 04 Starterpack, because a starterpack meme is recognisable BY its plainness. Faking our usual polish there would kill the joke.
**Format:** 14 posts, 2 per day (07:00 and 17:00, continuing the running cadence).
**Rules kept:** age always "6 to 67" (never 65), no emojis, no em or en dashes, batch size "5 to 8", no fabricated students or testimonials, no price talk. The Wrapped post is labelled "a typical first 90 days" so no invented per-student data is claimed.

Post files live in `marketing/week11/NN-name.png` (1080x1080).
