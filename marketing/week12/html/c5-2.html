Two libraries

One has a system.
One does not.

LIBRARY A
Books stacked in no order
To find one title you walk every aisle. A million books, a million looks.
LIBRARY B
The title tells you the shelf
Work out the shelf from the title itself, walk straight there. One look.
Library B never searches. It calculates where the book must be, then goes there directly.
that calculation has a name: hashing.
