# Week 10 - Social Plan & Captions

**Theme:** MEME WEEK. Fully funny, fully relatable, still selling. Known meme formats (chat with mom, "nobody:", expanding brain, "we have X at home", report card, two buttons, expectation vs reality, POV, tier list, "it ain't much but it's honest work", the family-function question) rendered in our own brand so it reads as a trending meme AND as Modern Age Coders.
**Who it talks to:** parents and students equally. Half the set is a parent looking at their kid; half is the learner looking at themselves.
**Look:** TWO canvases on purpose, so the grid does not look templated.
  1. **Notebook frame** (our usual warm brand) for the "object" memes: chat, playlist card, report card, panels, terminal, tier list, code window.
  2. **Full-bleed statement** posts (posts 02, 11, 13, 14): flat brand colour, huge lowercase Bricolage, tiny logo, one line, a small mono asterisk footnote. Big-brand social energy, zero decoration.
**Format:** 14 posts, 2 per day (07:00 and 17:00, continuing the running cadence).
**Rules kept:** age always "6 to 67" (never 65), no emojis, no em or en dashes, no fabricated students or testimonials (every character is a generic "mom", "your kid", "a relative"), no price talk.
**Design rule learned:** on the full-bleed posters the yellow underline may only sit under text on the FINAL line, otherwise the tight leading makes it read as a strikethrough.

Post files live in `marketing/week10/NN-name.png` (1080x1080).
