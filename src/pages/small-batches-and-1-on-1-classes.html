How we run classes

Small batches. Private classes. Limited on purpose.

A mentor can only watch a few students think at once. So we keep classes small, and we accept that this makes our time scarce.

In short

Modern Age Coders teaches in small live batches and personal 1-on-1 classes, never in large webinars. Small groups let the mentor see each student's reasoning and correct it in the moment. The cost of that choice is that mentor time is limited, which is why free demos wait in a queue and guaranteed demo slots carry a small fee.

Why small

Teaching someone to think means watching them think. The mentor has to see the half-finished plan, the wrong turn and the moment of confusion, and respond right then. In a crowd that is impossible. The quiet students vanish and the quick ones coast.

In a small batch every student speaks, shares a screen and gets corrected individually, while still learning from classmates. That is the setting our whole method depends on, from teaching how to think to training like a software engineer.

When 1-on-1 is the better choice

  • The student is well ahead of or behind their age group.
  • There is a fixed goal and a deadline, such as an exam or a competition.
  • The family needs unusual timings or lives in a distant time zone.
  • The student is shy and needs to build confidence first.

Group and 1-on-1 fees are listed openly on the pricing page and explained further at coding classes fees.

What this has to do with demos

Everything. Because our mentors are in these classes most of the day, there is little spare time for free demos. We will not shorten a student's class to make room for one. So free demos wait for a gap, and a guaranteed slot is paid. The full explanation is in why we charge for a priority demo, and the two options are compared in free demo vs priority demo.

Questions parents ask

Are classes live or recorded?

Live, with a mentor, in small batches or 1-on-1. Recordings are there for revision, not as a replacement.

Can my child move between a batch and 1-on-1?

Yes. Talk to your mentor and we will arrange the format that suits the student best.

Do you run large webinar-style classes?

No. We do not believe thinking can be taught to a crowd.

Why are free demo slots so hard to get?

Mentors are teaching enrolled students most of the day, and we do not take time from those classes for demos.
