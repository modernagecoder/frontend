What CodaKid teaches

Its FAQ lists Python, JavaScript, Java, Lua, Scratch, C#, HTML, CSS and Unreal Blueprints, using tools such as Roblox Studio, Unity and Unreal Engine. Children build games, mods and websites rather than isolated exercises.

Its private tutors use what CodaKid calls the Mastery Method, a Socratic approach of asking guiding questions instead of handing over answers.

How long courses take

CodaKid says most of its courses can be finished in a couple of hours, while intermediate and advanced ones can take upwards of 20 hours. That short course length suits bite-sized progress.

When self-paced shines

A child who already loves building, works happily alone and hates waiting for classmates can fly through a library of short courses. CodaKid’s round-the-clock AI support is designed for exactly the moment when that child gets stuck at nine at night.

It is also a sensible fit for a family whose schedule changes every week.

When a live class helps

A child who stalls quietly, skips the hard parts or needs to talk ideas through usually learns more with a person present at a fixed time. The appointment itself does part of the work.

CodaKid recognises this with its live private lessons and camps; our school is built only on live classes.

A one-week test

Log one week of self-paced study and read it

Our suggestion for deciding with evidence: keep a simple log for a week, then let a few lines of Python summarise it. The numbers below are a made-up sample.

  1. Agree the rules with your child first. The log is a test of the format, not of them, and they should know that.
  2. Write two numbers each day: minutes spent coding, and minutes spent stuck on one problem without progress.
  3. Do not help during the week. The point is to see how the format works without a parent filling the teacher’s role.
  4. Type the numbers into the script on day seven, or simply add them on paper.
  5. Talk about the stuck moments. Ask what each one was about and how it ended.
Reading the log (our rule of thumb, not a test result)
What the week showsWhat it may suggest
Coding most days and rarely stuckSelf-paced is working; keep going
Coding most days but stuck a lotAdd live help: a teacher session or private lessons
Coding on few daysA fixed weekly class may build the habit
Stuck time spent on one topicA teacher can unblock that topic quickly

In the sample, almost a third of the coding time went on being stuck, mostly on two days. That is not failure; it is a sign the child is attempting hard things. The question is whether those stuck minutes end in a breakthrough or in closing the laptop, and only the child can tell you that.

CodaKid and alternatives: common questions

Is CodaKid self-paced or live?

Both. Its main program is self-paced video courses, more than 115 of them, and it also offers live 50-minute private lessons on Zoom and live week-long camps, according to its own site on 17 October 2026.

What ages does CodaKid teach?

Ages 6 to 18, with each course listing a starting age, from 6+ for Scratch to 12+ for Unity, Unreal and web development. Its FAQ says adults also use the self-study courses.

Does CodaKid have a free trial?

Its FAQ describes a 14-day free trial of the self-study program and a free first session for private tutoring, with billing starting afterwards.

Are CodaKid camps recorded?

Its camps page says every session is recorded, so a missed day can be caught up.

How is Modern Age Coders different from CodaKid?

We teach only live classes, in batches, mini batches of up to six or privately, for ages 6 and up, with maths as well as coding and teachers based in India. CodaKid is mainly self-paced, with live private lessons and camps.

Which is right for my child?

An independent child who enjoys working alone may do well self-paced; a child who needs prompts and conversation often does better live. Logging one week of self-paced study, as shown on this page, helps you see which.

Can my child use CodaKid and live classes together?

Yes. Self-paced projects between weekly classes give practice, and the teacher can help with whatever got stuck.

What languages does CodaKid teach?

Its FAQ lists Python, JavaScript, Java, Lua, Scratch, C#, HTML, CSS and the Unreal Blueprints scripting language, used in tools such as Roblox Studio, Unity and Unreal Engine.

When are your classes?

Weekdays or weekends, flexible: choose your batch, or for a very personalised plan choose 1-on-1 at your convenience, weekend mornings or weekdays.

Are you related to CodaKid?

No. Modern Age Coders is a separate school. We summarise CodaKid's public pages and link to them.
