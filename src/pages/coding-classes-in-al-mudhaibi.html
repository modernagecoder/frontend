Al Mudhaibi today

One wilayat, two niabas, settlements of every size

Al Mudhaibi is large enough and varied enough that any figure quoted for the whole of it has to say how it was put together.

Roughly three thousand square kilometres

Al Mudhaibi is the largest wilayat in Ash Sharqiyah North, estimated at about 3,000 square kilometres, sitting in the south west of the governorate and running into desert with golden sands. Area that size guarantees variety, and variety is what breaks a careless average.

Two niabas inside one wilayat

Sinaw and Samad Ash Shan are niabas of Al Mudhaibi. That is a real administrative level between the wilayat and the village, and a dataset that has no column for it will quietly aggregate two very different places into one row.

Villages that are not comparable in size

Al Sudairah, Barzman, Al Zahib, Al Alia, Al Habat, Mahlia, Al Ghariyin, Al Khadraa and Bani Difaa all sit inside the same name, along with the Al-Dhaqiq area between Lazq and Al Maysar. Treating that list as a list of equals is the mistake this page is about.

An average has an implied weighting whether you chose one or not

Averaging a column of rates weights every row equally. Sometimes that is what you meant. Usually it is not, and nothing in the output records which of the two you were doing. Saying out loud what each row is worth is the whole of the fix, and it takes one sentence.

Verified local facts

Al Mudhaibi facts with a trail behind them

Named, measured and dated, with the scope of each claim stated.

Bronze Age settlement at Samad

Samad al-Shan is noted for ancient sites of the Umm al-Nar period, dated to roughly 2500 to 2000 BCE. A place with four thousand years of continuous record is a place where a dataset needs an explicit period column rather than a single date.

One of Oman's best known markets

Suq Sinaw is among the most popular souqs in the country, trading dresses, khanjars and jewellery. A market with distinct product categories is a natural setting for learning why a category column beats a free-text description.

Agriculture as the first craft

Agriculture is described as the first craft the population practised, with palm, lemon, alfalfa and vegetable crops alongside grazing and camel rearing. Five crop types on one landholding is five different yield series, not one.

Desert on one side, cultivation on the other

The wilayat holds both cultivated land and a large stretch of desert. Averaging any per-area figure across ground that different produces a number describing nowhere in particular.

An administrative level people forget

Because Sinaw is a niaba rather than a separate wilayat, records sometimes list it independently and sometimes fold it into Al Mudhaibi. A reader comparing two such sources without noticing will conclude something changed when nothing did.

Named areas between named towns

The Al-Dhaqiq area is described as lying between the town of Lazq and Al Maysar. A place defined by what it sits between needs coordinates, not only a name, before anybody tries to measure anything about it.

No affiliation with any of the above exists or is claimed. Al Mudhaibi appears because a wilayat whose parts differ this much in size makes the averaging problem visible in a way a tidy example never could.

Signature project

Seventy seven per cent, or fifty seven, from the same five rows

Five settlements, two totals, one honest question. Both answers below are arithmetically correct and they are nineteen and a half points apart.

1. Record each settlement properly

Passes and entries, kept as two separate numbers. The learner does not store a percentage, because a percentage has already thrown away the thing that decides how much it should count.

2. Average the five rates

Four small settlements at 90, 80, 70 and 90 per cent, and one large one at 55. The mean of those five figures is 77.0 per cent, and it is the number a spreadsheet gives you without being asked anything.

3. Divide the totals instead

253 passes out of 440 entries is 57.5 per cent. That is the wilayat's actual rate. The large settlement holds 91 per cent of the learners and one fifth of the average, which is the entire distance between the two answers.

Both numbers are correct and only one answers the question

Reliable pipeline

Keep the numerator and the denominator all the way to the end

For younger learners

Two bags of counters, one with ten and one with four hundred, each with a share coloured. Children work out the share for each bag and then for both together, and see for themselves that the second answer is not the average of the first two.

For teenagers

Python computes both figures from the same rows and prints the gap. The learner then writes the weighted version and checks it reproduces the totals answer exactly, which is the proof that the weighting was the missing piece.

For adults

The query changes from AVG of a computed column to SUM over SUM, the denominator is carried into every report, and any average of rates is labelled as one wherever it appears.

The settlement names are real and published. The pass and entry counts are teaching values, because no public per-village result file exists to copy, and the page labels them as such. The arithmetic is a property of averaging rather than of these particular numbers, which is why it is worth an hour of anybody's time.

FAQ

Al Mudhaibi coding class questions

Local detail, project method and service terms, kept apart.

Are the pass numbers in the project real?

No, and the page says so. The settlement names are real and published, and the pass and entry counts are teaching values because no public per-village result file exists to copy. The arithmetic being taught is a property of averaging rates, so it reproduces on any figures you substitute.

Why does averaging percentages go wrong?

Because it gives every row one vote regardless of how many people that row represents. A village of ten and a town of four hundred contribute equally to the mean of the rates, which is almost never what anybody intended. The wilayat rate comes from dividing total passes by total entries, once, at the end.

Is there a Modern Age Coders centre in Al Mudhaibi?

No. No premises are claimed in Al Mudhaibi or anywhere in Ash Sharqiyah North. Classes are live online and need a laptop or desktop, working audio, a current browser and a stable connection. The page describes where the service reaches, not a building.

What is a niaba and why does it matter to a dataset?

A niaba is an administrative level between the wilayat and the village. Sinaw and Samad Ash Shan are niabas of Al Mudhaibi. It matters because some records list a niaba separately and some fold it into the wilayat, so two sources can disagree without either being wrong, and a reader who has not noticed will think something changed.

Can an adult here study rather than a school student?

Yes, up to 67. Adults take Python, databases, data analysis and automation. Anybody who reports a figure that somebody else acts on will get more from this project than a student will, and their own report makes the best exercise as long as the data is public or theirs to use.

What does a learner have at the end of the project?

Both aggregates computed from one dataset, a weighted version that reproduces the correct answer exactly, and the habit of printing the denominator beside every rate. That last one is one extra column and it makes a report arguable rather than merely believable.
