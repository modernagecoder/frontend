# Book your priority demo *in about two minutes.*

One short form, a slot you choose, one secure payment. A mentor confirms on WhatsApp within the hour.

[Book priority demo · ₹499](/book-demo)[What is the priority demo?](/priority-demo)

One-time fee. No enrolment required. Confirmed on WhatsApp within the hour.

**In short**

To book a Priority Live Demo, press the booking button on this page, enter a name, email and WhatsApp number, pick a day and an hour between 10 am and 8 pm IST, and pay the one-time fee (₹499) through Razorpay. Your slot is reserved immediately and a mentor confirms it on WhatsApp within the hour.

## The steps

1. **Open the booking form.** Use the button above, or submit any demo form on the site and choose the paid option when asked.
2. **Enter your details.** Student or parent name, email and a WhatsApp number. Pick your country code so we message the right number.
3. **Pick a day.** Today and tomorrow are offered first, then the next few days.
4. **Pick a time.** Hourly slots from 10 am to 8 pm IST. If you are outside India, your local time is shown under each slot.
5. **Pay once.** Payment runs through Razorpay. Cards, UPI, net banking and wallets are supported, depending on your country.
6. **Get confirmed.** You see your slot and order ID on screen, and a mentor confirms on WhatsApp within the hour.

## If you are not sure which time to pick

You can pay without choosing a slot, and we will call to fix a time that suits you. For younger children, late afternoon or early evening usually works best. Avoid the hour right after school.

## After you book

- Keep a laptop or desktop ready with Chrome and a steady connection.
- Tell the mentor on WhatsApp what the student is curious about: [Python](/courses/python-complete-masterclass-teens), [web development](/courses/full-stack-web-development-teens-masterclass), [AI](/courses/ai-ml-masterclass-teens), [C++](/courses/cpp-programming-masterclass-for-teens), [JEE foundation maths](/courses/jee-foundation-maths-course-class-8-10) or anything from the [course list](/courses).
- Read [what happens in a demo class](/what-happens-in-a-demo-class) so you know what to look for.
- Set aside about 45 to 60 minutes. It is a full class, not a quick call.
- Keep your Order ID. If you enrol, send it to us on WhatsApp and we adjust the demo fee against your first month.
- Expect the mentor's written skill report and learning roadmap on WhatsApp, usually within a day of the class.

## If something goes wrong with the payment

If the payment page does not open, or money is taken but the page does not confirm, do not pay a second time. Send the payment ID to us on WhatsApp at 9123366161 or through the [contact page](/contact), and we will confirm your slot by hand.
