Coding classes in County Roscommon

What are the best coding classes in County Roscommon?

Roscommon grew by 5,715 people between the last two censuses to reach 70,259, a little faster than the country. Its largest town, Roscommon itself, has 6,555 residents; Boyle, Ballaghaderreen and Castlerea each hold between two and three thousand. Seven in ten commuters here drive, the joint highest share in Ireland. A free trial lesson comes first. After it, a seat in a small ability group costs USD 100 a month and private tuition USD 150, all of it taught live at a set time each week to learners from six up to sixty-seven.

Open the county council's list of protected buildings in any ordinary data tool and ask it which towns hold the most. Boyle comes first with 109. Second, with 93 structures, is a town called na. There is no such town. Somebody typing into a form wrote na when a building had no town, and a colleague wrote n/a, and another left it blank, and others wrote NA and Na. Five spellings of nothing, and pandas, a very widely used analysis library, left on its defaults, recognises three of them and treats the other ninety-five rows as a real place. The file is honest, public and licensed for reuse. It just records absence in a way software was never told about, which happens in almost every spreadsheet anybody inherits.

Signature project

The second biggest town is called na

Roscommon County Council's Record of Protected Structures, 559 buildings, examined before any chart is drawn.

What the naive count says

Boyle 109, na 93, Roscommon 91, then Castlerea 33 and Strokestown 25. A chart made from that would put a non-existent town in second place, above the county town itself.

The line count that lies too

The downloaded file has 679 lines, one of them the header, but only 559 records. Long descriptions of the buildings contain line breaks inside quotation marks, so counting lines instead of reading the format overstates the rows by 119.

A town spelled two ways

Ballaghaderreen appears correctly once and as Ballaghdereen eighteen times. Group by that column and one town becomes two, with nearly all of its buildings under the misspelling.

Why missing values are a design decision, not an accident

Every system that collects information has to decide how to write down that something is not known. Most never decide, so each person entering data improvises, and the file ends up with a blank here, a dash there, na, n/a, unknown and zero all meaning the same thing. Software then has to guess which of those are absence and which are data, and its guesses are conservative by design. The fix is never cleverness afterwards. It is a list, written before any analysis, of every way this particular file says nothing, which is exactly what the table above is.

The deliverable is a short cleaning note and one honest chart. The note lists the five spellings of missing, the line-versus-record gap and the split town name. The chart shows protected structures by town with those fixed and with the unknown-town rows reported as their own labelled bar rather than quietly dropped, because 102 buildings without a recorded town is a fact about the register worth showing. The file is published under a Creative Commons Attribution licence and was last edited in December 2022, and the page says so.

Why an online class fits a driving county

Where 70% of commuters already drive and the average trip is among the longest in Ireland, every activity that needs a car competes with work for the same hours and the same fuel. A weekly lesson that arrives at the kitchen table is not a second-best version of a class in a hall. For a family outside the towns it is usually the only version that survives the winter.

Coding and AI

A summary of that file would mention the town of na

Not because anything is broken, but because nothing told the reader that na means nothing.

Hand the protected structures file to any automated summary and ask for the towns with the most listed buildings. The honest arithmetic produces na in second place, and a fluent paragraph will describe it with the same confidence as Boyle. The error is not in the arithmetic. It is upstream, in how absence was typed, and it is invisible to anything that only reads the values.

A learner who has once counted the five spellings of nothing looks for them in every file afterwards. That is a small habit with a large effect, because the people entering data never stop improvising and the tools will never be told what they meant.

Knowing what a blank really is remains a human judgement in 2026, and it is one of the most useful things a young programmer can be good at. The longer argument is in is learning to code still worth it in 2026.

Asked most often by Roscommon families.

How many people live in County Roscommon?

The 2022 census counted 70,259, which is 5,715 more than in 2016 and growth of 9%, slightly ahead of the State.

What is the Roscommon data project?

Learners examine the council's register of 559 protected buildings and find that "no town" is written five different ways, so a basic count makes na look like the second biggest town. They clean it and document every step.

How big are the main towns?

Roscommon town had 6,555 residents in 2022, Boyle 2,915, Ballaghaderreen 2,387, Castlerea 2,348 and Strokestown 850.

Why does the page say Roscommon commuters drive further?

Because the statistics office says so: an average of 24.9 kilometres to work, the second longest in the country, with 70% going by car, the joint highest rate.

Was a Roscommon school among the first to offer Leaving Certificate Computer Science?

No. Roscommon does not appear on the January 2018 list of forty schools that started first. That tells you about that list, not about what schools offer now.

Does Roscommon have a Gaeltacht?

No. The statistics office defines the Gaeltacht by electoral divisions in seven named counties, and Roscommon is not one of them.

What time of day are lessons?

Mostly after school, in the evening and on weekend mornings. Our teachers are based in India, four and a half to five and a half hours ahead of Ireland depending on the season, and the exact slot is fixed in the trial lesson.

Is there a Modern Age Coders classroom in County Roscommon?

No, and we never imply one. Teaching is live over video only, with no premises in Ireland. A learner needs a laptop or desktop, working sound and a steady connection, and the number shown here belongs to India.

What do County Roscommon coding classes cost?

The first lesson is free. After that a shared place costs USD 100 a month and brings two live lessons a week, about eight monthly, with five to ten learners; one to one on the same schedule is USD 150 a month. Everything is agreed before payment.

How many learners are in a group?

Five to ten, grouped by ability, pace and goal rather than age or address. Where nothing suitable meets at a workable hour, we teach one to one instead.
