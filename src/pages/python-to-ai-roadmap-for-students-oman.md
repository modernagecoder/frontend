# Python to AI Roadmap for Students in Oman

> This is the sequence Modern Age Coders teaches learners in Oman, from six to sixty-seven. Rung one is Python written by hand. Rung two is real, messy data. Rung three is machine learning with a measured error and a baseline to beat. Rung four is AI agents. Rung five, which runs alongside the rest, is publishing the work on GitHub with an honest account of what it does and where it fails. Group teaching is USD 100 a month, private USD 150, and the first class is free.

## Five rungs, with what you would see at home

| Rung | Typical age | What you would see at home | What it is not |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Python by hand | 9 to 12 | A blank file becoming a working program, and the child reading an error instead of asking for help | Dragging coloured blocks, or copying a tutorial line by line |
| 2. Data | 11 to 14 | Charts the child made, and complaints about how bad the file was | A tidy spreadsheet the teacher prepared |
| 3. Machine learning | 12 to 16 | The child saying how wrong their model is, in units, unprompted | A percentage accuracy quoted with nothing to compare it to |
| 4. Agents | 13 to 18 | A program that decides its next step, and a log the child can walk you through | A chatbot answering questions in a nice voice |
| 5. GitHub | From about 12 | A link you can open, with commits going back months | A folder on a laptop, or a certificate |

The order is a dependency chain. Ages are typical rather than prescriptive, and the rungs overlap: a student returns to data constantly, because that is where the errors live.

## The three places people skip

**Skipping typed Python.** Symptom: the child can describe what a program should do and freezes at a blank file. Blocks are a fine on-ramp and a poor destination. A student who has never sat with a traceback has not met the subject.

**Skipping messy data.** Symptom: everything works on the teacher's file and nothing works on a real one. Real data has missing rows, inconsistent units, duplicates and one value that is text pretending to be a number. A student who has never cleaned anything will trust their first result.

**Skipping the baseline.** Symptom: an impressive accuracy figure with nothing to compare it to. Before any model is believed, the student writes the dumbest defensible alternative: always guess the average, or always guess yesterday. Many first models fail to beat it, and discovering that is the most valuable hour in the ladder.

## How long each rung takes

At two ninety-minute sessions a week: rung one is a term to a year depending on starting age, rung two is a term, rung three is two terms, rung four is a term once three is solid, and rung five runs alongside from the middle of rung two onward. The strongest predictor of speed is not talent but whether the student has something they personally want to make.

**What we will not promise:** a timeline to a job, a university place or a competition result, and we will not claim this content appears on an Omani school certificate, because it does not.

## The same five rungs, for an adult

**What compresses:** rungs one and two, heavily. An adult who works with spreadsheets already has the mental model for data and simply lacks syntax.

**What does not:** rung three. Statistical intuition is slow for everybody, and confidence in an unchecked number is a habit adults have had longer.

**What changes shape:** rung five. An adult rarely wants a public portfolio; they want the thing running and a colleague able to maintain it. Same discipline, different audience.

Adults with a specific objective usually go one to one and finish within a few months.

## One entry point specific to Oman

Under a February 2021 agreement between the Ministry of Education and Omantel, an IT and coding curriculum ran in Grades 1 to 4 for three academic years from 2021/2022. A child who sat that in Grade 1 is now around Grade 5, and they typically arrive with real foundations and no idea what to do with them: they recognise sequence and condition, they have debugged something, and they have never typed a line of Python. That profile lands on rung one and moves through it faster than a complete beginner. The background is on the [academy hub](https://learn.modernagecoders.com/ai-and-python-academy-oman).
