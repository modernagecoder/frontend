# National 5 vs Higher Maths: what changes from S4 to S5

**The short answer.** National 5 Maths is at SCQF level 5 and Higher Maths at level 6. Both are graded A to D from two question papers, one without a calculator. National 5 has 90 marks over 2 hours 30 minutes; Higher has 120 marks over 2 hours 45 minutes and adds calculus, logarithms, vectors in three dimensions and the circle.

What each course covers

## Higher keeps the National 5 skills and adds a new layer.

### Examples from National 5

- Surds and the laws of indices, including negative and fractional indices
- Reverse percentages
- The equation of a straight line, gradient and intercept
- Simultaneous equations
- Quadratics: sketching, the quadratic formula and the discriminant
- Volumes, and the sine and cosine rules
- Adding and subtracting two-dimensional vectors

### Examples new at Higher

- Factorising and solving cubic and quartic polynomials
- Logarithms and exponentials, and their laws
- The addition and double angle formulae, and the wave function
- Differentiation, including stationary points and their nature
- Integration, including areas
- The equation of a circle
- Vectors in three dimensions, the scalar product and unit vectors i, j, k

Qualifications Scotland also offers Applications of Mathematics as a separate subject. This page is about the Mathematics courses.

The jump from S4 to S5

## Why Higher feels bigger than one level up.

### More marks, little more time

Higher has 120 marks against National 5's 90, in 2 hours 45 minutes against 2 hours 30. Each mark has less time behind it.

### Algebra everywhere

Calculus, logarithms and the wave function all run on algebra. A learner who scraped through the National 5 algebra will feel it in every Higher topic.

### The same 160 hours

Both specifications give a notional 160 hours of learning, yet Higher holds a heavier load of new ideas. Steady weekly work matters more than a late push.

National 5 style

**Find the equation of the line through (1, 3) and (4, 9).**

m = (9 − 3) ÷ (4 − 1) = 6 ÷ 3 = 2y − 3 = 2(x − 1)**y = 2x + 1**

Gradient first, then substitute a point.

Higher style

**Find the stationary points of y = x³ − 3x.**

dy/dx = 3x² − 3 = 0x² = 1, so x = 1 or x = −1**(1, −2) and (−1, 2)**

The same line-by-line algebra as National 5, now carrying calculus.

## National 5 and Higher Maths: what learners and parents ask.

What is the difference between National 5 and Higher Maths?

National 5 is at SCQF level 5 and Higher at SCQF level 6. Higher has more marks (120 against 90) and adds topics such as calculus, logarithms, the wave function, the circle and three-dimensional vectors.

How many papers are there in National 5 Maths?

Two: Paper 1 without a calculator (40 marks, 1 hour) and Paper 2 (50 marks, 1 hour 30 minutes).

How many papers are there in Higher Maths?

Two: Paper 1 without a calculator (55 marks, 1 hour 15 minutes) and Paper 2 (65 marks, 1 hour 30 minutes).

Is there coursework in National 5 or Higher Maths?

No. The grade is based on the total mark across the two question papers.

What grades can you get in National 5 and Higher Maths?

Both courses are graded A to D on the total mark for the course assessment.

Do you need National 5 Maths to do Higher?

The Higher specification recommends that candidates have achieved National 5 Mathematics or equivalent qualifications or experience. Entry is at the discretion of the school or college.

Is Higher Maths much harder than National 5?

It is a level higher, with more marks in only 15 more minutes and new topics that all depend on secure algebra. That is why a secure National 5 base matters so much.

Is it SQA or Qualifications Scotland now?

Qualifications Scotland replaced SQA on 1 February 2026. Its course pages say the SQA-era course documents remain current.

What comes after Higher Maths?

The Higher specification lists Advanced Higher Mathematics, Advanced Higher Mathematics of Mechanics and Advanced Higher Statistics among the progression routes.
