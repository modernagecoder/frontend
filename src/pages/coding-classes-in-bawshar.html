Bawshar data project

Can one histogram show one peak and three?

Bawshar's mix of national, Indian and private schools makes school-size data locally meaningful. It also makes a histogram dangerously easy to tune until it tells the story the analyst expected.

Source: Oman Ministry of Education open dataLevel: Grade 8 and upConcept: Histogram bin width

The Ministry publishes 2024/2025 student counts by government school, grade and sex, with a matching school file by governorate. Students identify the Bawshar schools in the current data, document the match and calculate one total per school.

The ordinary question is whether Bawshar has one typical school size or several groups. A histogram with wide bins can show one broad mound. Narrow bins can split the same values into several apparent peaks. Both charts use the same schools.

The trap

Bin width is a modelling choice, not decoration. Too wide hides structure. Too narrow turns sampling noise into a pattern. A responsible analysis shows several defensible widths, states the rule used and checks whether any claimed cluster survives the choice.

The issue generalises to ages, travel times, exam marks, prices and sensor readings. A chart can change the reader's conclusion without changing a single observation.

Bawshar questions, answered plainly

Do you teach students from Indian School Bousher?

Students can join from Indian School Bousher or any other Bawshar school because the classes are online. The Embassy of India documents the school in its Muscat community work. Placement still uses a live task, since school name and grade do not show whether a learner can code independently.

Does the programme suit ABQ Al Imtiaz students?

It can. ABQ states that Al Imtiaz opened in Bawshar in 2023 and is expanding through Grade 10 in 2026/27. The free class checks the student's current mathematics, logic and programming work before a course is chosen. Modern Age Coders does not claim to replace the school's curriculum.

Why choose online coding inside Bawshar?

Bawshar families already move between schools, homes and major roads. Muscat Municipality documented a two-kilometre duplication of Bawshar Road from the Muscat Expressway area towards the roundabout opposite Muscat Private Hospital. Online teaching removes one optional journey without pretending traffic disappears.

Is there a Modern Age Coders branch in Bawshar?

No. Modern Age Coders teaches Bawshar online from India. It has no office, street address or classroom in the wilayat. The Indian WhatsApp number and email on this page reach the teaching company directly, and the free class lets a family test the online format before paying.

Which spelling is correct, Bawshar or Bousher?

Both romanisations are used for the same wilayat. This coding cluster uses Bawshar in its URL and prose. Indian School Bousher keeps the spelling in its official name, so that proper name is not changed. The difference is spelling, not a second place.

Can classes fit Oman's Sunday to Thursday school week?

Yes, subject to available teaching slots. Oman is one and a half hours behind India, so an evening in Bawshar remains an evening for the teacher. Friday and Saturday weekend options can also be discussed. The exact recurring time is confirmed before payment, never promised generically.

What do Bawshar coding classes cost?

A small group of five to eight costs USD 100 a month. Private teaching costs USD 150 a month. Both plans include eight live classes, normally two each week. The first class is free, with no enrolment charge, materials fee or minimum term.

Can a Muscat College student or working adult join?

Yes. Muscat College is on Bawshar Street, and the wilayat has a substantial adult learner and working population. Modern Age Coders teaches ages 6 to 67. Adults are placed into Python, web, data, database or mathematics work without joining a children's group or receiving a job guarantee.

My child already knows Scratch. What comes next?

The teacher first checks whether the child can use loops, conditions, variables and debugging without copied instructions. If those foundations hold, Python or web development may be sensible. If they do not, a stronger Scratch project is more useful than moving to typed code for appearances.
