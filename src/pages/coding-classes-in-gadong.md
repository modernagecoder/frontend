## What does "Gadong" actually refer to?

Kampong Gadong is a village and neighbourhood inside Bandar Seri Begawan, in Mukim Gadong B, covering 254.4872 hectares. It recorded **3,356** residents at the 2021 census. Its landmarks are used by the whole capital: the Centrepoint Hotel, completed in 1994 as part of the Abdul Razak Complex, and The Mall with the Rizqun International Hotel, inaugurated on **9 July 2006**, a three-storey structure over two basement parking levels with a cinema, a supermarket, a food court and a range of shops.

The two mukims carrying the name are much larger. At the 2021 census Mukim Gadong A held **35,424** and Mukim Gadong B held **38,067**, giving **73,491** between them. Brunei-Muara district held **318,530**, and its eighteen mukims sum to exactly that figure.

## The Gadong student data project: the denominator is a choice

The learner is asked for something innocuous. How many shops are there in Gadong per thousand people? They count the shops, divide by the population, and write it down. The problem is the second half of that sentence.

**Four defensible populations, and the answer moves by a factor of ninety-five.** Divide by Kampong Gadong and it is 3,356. By Mukim Gadong B, which contains the village, 38,067. By both Gadong mukims, 73,491. By the district whose residents do the shopping, 318,530. The largest is ninety-five times the smallest, so the rate moves by that factor while the numerator never changes. Every one of the four is real, published and correctly cited.

Nothing in the data tells you which denominator is right, because rightness is not a property of the numbers. It is a property of the question. Asking how well served the residents are makes the residents the denominator. Asking how busy the shops are makes the people who arrive there the denominator, and most of them live somewhere else. Two sensible questions, two divisors, one arithmetic operation that looks identical in both cases.

So the learner writes the sentence before the code: **this is X per Y, where Y is the population of, as of, counted by.** If any of those three cannot be filled in, the rate does not get computed yet.

The general form turns up far outside shops. Crime per resident in a district full of visitors. Revenue per employee where half the staff are contractors. Cases per capita where the population moved during the period measured. **A rate is two numbers and an argument, and the argument is the part that gets left out.**

## Does coding still matter when AI can write it?

Ask any assistant how many people live in Gadong. You will get a number, delivered cleanly, and you will very probably not be told which Gadong it chose. That is not a failure of knowledge; ask it to distinguish the village from the mukims and it will. The gap is that the question contained an ambiguity, the answer resolved it silently, and nothing flags that a choice was made on your behalf.

**What time?** A permanent **two and a half hours** to the teacher, all year. Seven in the evening in Gadong is half past four in the afternoon there.

**When should a child start?** Years 7 and 8. They carry no national certificate, so there is room in them, and they sit before a stream narrows the options. By Year 10 the honest advice is usually fewer lessons rather than more.
