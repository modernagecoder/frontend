Coding classes in Saxony-Anhalt

Saxony-Anhalt has done something unusual. Since the 2026/27 school year every pupil at a Sekundarschule, Gemeinschaftsschule or Gesamtschule has Informatik as a compulsory subject from grade 5 to 10, while a pupil at a Gymnasium has it only as an elective in grades 9 and 10. This page explains that split, what the Oberstufe offers and how its exam is set, the holidays for 2026/27, how our live classes in English fill the gap, and a data project on the Land's long-distance cycle routes in which the first ranking measures the wrong thing.

Informatik in Saxony-Anhalt

Is Informatik compulsory in Saxony-Anhalt?

At some school types yes, at the Gymnasium no. The rules below are compiled by the Gesellschaft für Informatik in its Informatik-Monitor 2026/27.

Compiled from the Sachsen-Anhalt page of the GI Informatik-Monitor 2026/27 (consulted 2 November 2026). Since 2026/27 the compulsory subject at the Sekundar-, Gemeinschafts- and Gesamtschulen follows a trial curriculum (Erprobungslehrplan Informatik); the Gymnasium elective follows a curriculum updated in 2022 for grades 9 to 12. Because the Gymnasium has no compulsory Informatik, the GI's national summary still counts Saxony-Anhalt among six Länder without Informatik for all pupils.

A gap at the Gymnasium

The result is a curious gap. A ten year old at a Sekundarschule in Halle or Dessau-Roßlau now has a weekly Informatik lesson; a ten year old at a Gymnasium in the same street does not, and may not meet the subject as such until the elective in grade 9.

For Gymnasium families who want their child to start earlier, this is exactly where outside lessons help most: four years of programming before the first elective lesson.

Few teachers, small numbers

The monitor gives 186 Informatik teachers in Saxony-Anhalt for 2024/25, 2 % of all teachers, and 6 newly hired. In the same year 14 % of upper secondary pupils took Informatik, from KMK figures, and 4 % of Abitur candidates had it as an exam subject, from the Land's education ministry.

A new compulsory subject with so few specialist teachers is a big task for schools, which is one more reason to give a keen pupil practice beyond the weekly lesson.

Saxony-Anhalt is home to 2,135,597 people, 244,329 of them in Magdeburg, the Land capital (Destatis, 31 December 2024). After four years of Grundschule, pupils move to a Sekundarschule, a Gemeinschaftsschule, an integrated or cooperative Gesamtschule, or a Gymnasium. The KMK lists the Sekundarschule as a two-track school and the Gemeinschaftsschule as a school that combines all tracks.

Oberstufe and Abitur

What Informatik is there in the Oberstufe in Saxony-Anhalt?

A two-hour course at basic level, an exam set by the school and approved by the Landesschulamt, and a raised-level course at only two Gymnasien.

What is offered

According to the Informatik-Monitor, Informatik can be taken as a two-hour elective in the Einführungsphase and as a two-hour course at basic level in the Qualifikationsphase. Two Gymnasien with a mathematics, science and technology focus can also offer it at raised level.

Choosing Informatik never frees a pupil from a science course, and because it is mostly available only at basic level, the monitor notes it is not treated as equal to the sciences as an exam subject.

How the exam is set

If the course was taken continuously from the Einführungsphase, an oral Abitur exam is possible. Where a written Informatik exam is taken, the monitor says the tasks are set decentrally and approved by the Landesschulamt, and practical parts can be included; permitted aids depend on the task.

The ministry's 2027 Abitur dates were not available to us, so none appear here. For the Abitur itself only the school course matters; we add practice on the side and never write or grade school tasks.

Gymnasien and cooperative Gesamtschulen finish after twelve years (G8): the Einführungsphase is grade 10 and the two Qualifikationsphase years are 11 and 12. At the integrated Gesamtschule and the Gemeinschaftsschule the Abitur usually comes after grade 13. With only two hours a week in the Oberstufe, a pupil who wants to study computer science later usually needs far more practice than school can give.

A Saxony-Anhalt data project

Which is the longest cycle route in Saxony-Anhalt? Group first, then rank

The Land's Ministry for Infrastructure and Digital Affairs publishes its cycle route network as open data, including every long-distance tourist route cut into sections. Our pupils use it to learn that a table of rows is not yet a table of answers: you have to group the rows that belong together, and decide which rows count, before any ranking means something.

The data

We requested the layer of long-distance tourist cycle routes (überregionale touristische Radrouten) from the ministry's ALRIS web feature service on 3 November 2026. It is published under the Datenlizenz Deutschland Namensnennung 2.0, so it may be reused with credit. The service returned 678 sections, which our script saved as radrouten_ueberregional.csv with the route name, the section's category, its status and its length in metres.

One route, such as the Elberadweg, is spread over many rows. Some rows are the main route (Hauptroute), some are alternatives, side routes or detours, and many have no category at all.

The question

Which long-distance route is the longest in the Land? Version 1 answers the question it can answer straight away: which single row is longest. Version 2 adds up the rows of each route. Version 3 asks which rows should be added at all.

Each version gives a different answer, and each answer is honest about a different question.

One section of the Altmarkrundkurs, the circular route through the Altmark in the north of the Land, is stored as a single 346.7 km piece. That says something about how the data was drawn, not about which route is longest. Version 2 groups sections by route name and adds their lengths.

Now the 678 rows have become 23 routes, and the Altmarkrundkurs leads with 518.3 km. But adding everything counts alternatives and detours too, as if a cyclist rode both ways round a closed bridge. Version 3 looks at the category labels first.

Sections of the long-distance cycle routes by category label (our count)
Category labelSections
Hauptroute (main route)303
No label337
Alternativroute (alternative)16
Umleitung (detour)12
Nebenroute (side route)10

Data: Ministerium für Infrastruktur und Digitales Sachsen-Anhalt, WFS ST Radwegenetz ALRIS, layer überregionale touristische Radrouten, Datenlizenz Deutschland Namensnennung 2.0, retrieved 3 November 2026. Lengths are the section lengths stored in the data; the sums, rankings and the choice to treat unlabelled rows as main route are our own.

Half of all rows have no category, and Version 3 has to make a choice about them: here we treat them as main route, and say so. For the Elberadweg the answer moves from 237.7 km with every row to 131.1 km without alternatives, side routes and detours. Neither number is "the length of the Elberadweg"; each is the length of a clearly defined set of rows. That is the lesson: group first, then define what you count, and print the definition next to the number.

  1. Ages 9 to 11, no screen. Cut a long strip of paper into five pieces of different lengths and label two of them "detour". Measure the longest piece, then the whole route with and without detours.
  2. Ages 11 to 13, Scratch. Store pairs of route name and length in two lists, add up the lengths for each name, and show the longest route.
  3. Teenagers, Python. Run Version 1 and Version 2 and explain why their winners differ. Then change Version 3 to treat unlabelled rows as not counted and compare.
  4. Going further. Rank all 23 routes by main-route length only, and write one sentence for each of the top three that says exactly which rows were counted.

Contests

Which computing contests can pupils in Saxony-Anhalt enter?

The national ones, through school.

The Informatik-Biber, a free puzzle contest for grades 3 to 13 run by Bundesweite Informatikwettbewerbe, takes place from 9 to 20 November 2026, and schools register their pupils. For a pupil in grade 5 or 6 who has just started the new compulsory subject, it is a gentle first contest. Older pupils can try the 45th Bundeswettbewerb Informatik, whose first round runs until 23 November 2026. In our competitions course every puzzle is our own; we never copy the organisers' tasks and have no connection with BWINF.

Coding classes in Saxony-Anhalt: questions

Are the classes in German?

No. Our mentors, who work from India, teach every live lesson in English and set the pace and wording to suit your child. Where the school uses a German term, they mention it too.

Is Informatik compulsory in Saxony-Anhalt?

Since 2026/27, yes at the Sekundarschule, Gemeinschaftsschule and Gesamtschule, from grade 5 to 10, according to the Informatik-Monitor 2026/27. At the Gymnasium it is an elective in grades 9 and 10.

My child is at a Gymnasium. When will they learn programming at school?

Usually not before the grade 9 elective, if the school offers it and your child chooses it. Grades 5 to 8 have a cross-subject offer called Lernen in der digitalen Welt with less than one weekly hour of Informatik content, the monitor says.

Can my child take Informatik in the Oberstufe?

Yes, as a two-hour basic-level course, with raised level at two specialist Gymnasien. An oral Abitur exam is possible if the course has been taken since the Einführungsphase.

Who sets the written Informatik exam?

The Informatik-Monitor says written tasks are set decentrally and approved by the Landesschulamt, and they may include practical parts.

Can your lessons count for school?

No. Only the school course and its exams count. We teach alongside them and give no school grades or certificates.

When are classes in German time?

Weekdays or weekends, flexible: choose your batch, or for a very personalised plan choose 1-on-1 at your convenience, weekend mornings or weekdays. Early afternoon slots from about 13:30 are available on school days.

How big are the groups?

Fifteen to twenty learners at a similar level. One to one gives your child a mentor of their own.

How do we pay?

You see the monthly price in euro, worked out from the ECB reference rate; your card then pays the dollar amount and your bank handles the exchange. Nothing is charged for the trial lesson.

Are you connected to the ministry or a school?

No. We are an independent online school, not linked to the Ministerium für Bildung, the Landesschulamt, the KMK, BWINF or any school in Saxony-Anhalt.
