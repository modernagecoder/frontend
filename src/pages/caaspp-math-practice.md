# CAASPP math practice for California students

Every spring, California children in grades 3 to 8 and grade 11 sit the CAASPP math test, the Smarter Balanced Summative Assessment as California runs it. Two things about it surprise parents. First, it has no clock: the state calls it untimed. Second, the words on the report changed for 2025 to 2026. &ldquo;Standard Met&rdquo; is now &ldquo;Proficient&rdquo;, although California says not one cut score moved. This guide sets out what the California Department of Education publishes about the math test, then teaches the hardest part, the performance task, with a problem of our own.

## What is the CAASPP math test, and how is it structured?

It is the Smarter Balanced math assessment that California gives as part of CAASPP, its state testing system. Each student meets two parts: an adaptive set of questions and one performance task.

**Estimated CAASPP math testing times (CAASPP Online Test Administration Manual, Table 1; the state notes the tests are not timed and these figures are for scheduling only)**

| Grade band | Adaptive (CAT) items | Performance task (PT) | Math total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grades 3 to 5 | 45 minutes | 1 hour | 1 hour 45 minutes |
| Grades 6 to 8 | 1 hour | 1 hour | 2 hours |
| Grade 11 | 1 hour | 1 hour 30 minutes | 2 hours 30 minutes |

### The adaptive part

California&rsquo;s fact sheet describes the adaptive section plainly: &ldquo;As students answer correctly, they will receive more challenging questions.&rdquo; Wrong answers bring easier ones. Two classmates can therefore see quite different questions and still be measured on the same scale.

That is why a child sometimes comes home saying the test felt very hard. A hard-feeling test is often a sign the child was doing well, because the system kept raising the level.

### The performance task

The performance task is a short set of linked questions around one real-world situation. California&rsquo;s score guide says it lets students evaluate information and develop and communicate &ldquo;a solution to a real-world problem&rdquo;.

The manual tells schools to give the math performance task in one session, and passes on the consortium&rsquo;s advice that the adaptive items and the task be taken on separate days so children are not worn out.

Sources: [Smarter Balanced Assessments for ELA and Mathematics](https://www.caaspp-elpac.org/assessments/caaspp/sb-ela-math) and the [Assessment Fact Sheet](https://www.caaspp-elpac.org/s/docs/sbsumfactsheet.pdf) (May 2025), caaspp-elpac.org; [CAASPP Online Test Administration Manual](https://ca-toms-help.ets.org/caaspp-otam/prep-and-planning/general-test-admin-info/), all read 31 October 2026. We are not connected with the California Department of Education, ETS or any California district.

## Is the CAASPP timed, and can my child go back to earlier questions?

There is no time limit, but there are two rules about going back that every child should hear before test day.

### No clock

The state portal estimates five to six hours for ELA and math together, &ldquo;however, each is untimed.&rdquo; A careful child can keep working.

### The 20-minute pause

The manual warns that after a pause of more than 20 minutes, the student cannot return to items on earlier screens. Finish a page before a long break.

### Segments lock

From grade 6, the math test has a calculator segment and a no-calculator segment, and a submitted segment cannot be reopened.

In practice the second and third rules mean the same thing: check each answer before moving on, because the test is not built for a final sweep back through everything. We practise that habit from the first lesson, so that checking happens question by question rather than at the end.

### Who takes it

All students enrolled in grades three through eight and grade eleven take the Smarter Balanced math test, according to the manual, apart from the roughly 1 percent with the most significant cognitive disabilities who take the California Alternate Assessments.

English learners in their first 12 months in a US school get a one-time exemption from the ELA test only. The manual states they are still required to take the Smarter Balanced for Mathematics.

### Parent exemption

The manual also recognises a parent/guardian exemption (PGE) submitted to the test site. Whether to use it is a family decision; we simply note that California provides the route.

For grade 11, sitting the test has a second use, described in the next sections: the Early Assessment Program with the California State University.

## Which calculator is allowed on the CAASPP math test?

None in grades 3 to 5. From grade 6 an on-screen Desmos calculator appears for certain items, and its type changes with the grade.

**Embedded Desmos calculator by grade, CAASPP mathematics (caaspp-elpac.org Accessibility Resources page, Desmos Calculators section, read 31 October 2026)**

| Grade | Embedded calculator |
|---|---|
| 3, 4 and 5 | No embedded calculator used |
| 6 | Four-function calculator |
| 7 and 8 | Scientific calculator |
| 11 | Graphing calculator |

### Practise on the same tool

The portal notes that the same calculators are on the Desmos website so students can get used to them, and links California&rsquo;s own four-function, scientific and graphing versions.

We teach grade 6 and above with those exact links open. A child who has only used a handheld calculator loses time finding the fraction or square root keys on screen.

### Mental arithmetic still matters

Because the calculator appears only for calculator-allowed items, and never in grades 3 to 5, number fluency carries a lot of the test. Multiplication facts and fraction sense are not optional extras here.

In grades 6 to 8 the no-calculator segment comes with its own lock, so a child must be confident doing those questions by hand.

Desmos test calculators for California: [four-function](https://www.desmos.com/testing/california/fourfunction), [scientific](https://www.desmos.com/testing/california/scientific) and [graphing](https://www.desmos.com/testing/california/graphing), linked from the [CAASPP accessibility resources page](https://www.caaspp-elpac.org/resources/preparation/accessibility-resources).

## What do the new CAASPP levels Advanced, Proficient, Developing and Minimal mean?

They are the old four levels with new names. California&rsquo;s reporting guide for 2025 to 2026 says only the labels and descriptions changed and that no cut score or threshold moved.

**CAASPP Smarter Balanced overall levels, old and new labels (Scoring and Reporting Guide, What&rsquo;s New in 2025 to 2026, read 31 October 2026)**

| Level | Label before 2025 to 2026 | Label now | California&rsquo;s description, in short |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | Standard Exceeded | Advanced | Advanced grade-level knowledge and a sophisticated ability to apply it |
| 3 | Standard Met | Proficient | Proficient grade-level knowledge and a thorough ability to apply it |
| 2 | Standard Nearly Met | Developing | Developing grade-level knowledge and an initial understanding |
| 1 | Standard Not Met | Minimal | Minimal grade-level knowledge and a limited understanding |

### Two composite areas on the report

Besides the overall level, the math report gives two composite areas: &ldquo;Composite Area 1: Concepts and Procedures, and Composite Area 2: Mathematical Practices&rdquo;, in the words of California&rsquo;s March 2026 score guide.

Each area is marked Above Proficient, At or Near Proficient, or Below Proficient. These were Above, Near and Below Standard on older reports.

### Why the rename matters to families

A child who was &ldquo;Standard Nearly Met&rdquo; in 2025 and &ldquo;Developing&rdquo; in 2026 has not slipped. The labels changed; the thresholds did not, so the two years can be compared directly.

California also removed the dashed line between Level 2 and Level 3 from the gauge on the printed report, so read the scale score itself rather than the picture.

## What CAASPP math score is proficient for each grade?

Each grade has its own scale. California prints the current ranges in its Scoring and Reporting Guide, and they are the Smarter Balanced thresholds.

**Current CAASPP Smarter Balanced mathematics scale score ranges (Scoring and Reporting Guide, Appendix A, read 31 October 2026)**

| Grade | Minimal (1) | Developing (2) | Proficient (3) | Advanced (4) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 2190 to 2380 | 2381 to 2435 | 2436 to 2500 | 2501 to 2660 |
| 4 | 2205 to 2410 | 2411 to 2484 | 2485 to 2548 | 2549 to 2700 |
| 5 | 2220 to 2454 | 2455 to 2527 | 2528 to 2578 | 2579 to 2740 |
| 6 | 2235 to 2472 | 2473 to 2551 | 2552 to 2609 | 2610 to 2780 |
| 7 | 2250 to 2483 | 2484 to 2566 | 2567 to 2634 | 2635 to 2820 |
| 8 | 2265 to 2503 | 2504 to 2585 | 2586 to 2652 | 2653 to 2860 |
| 11 | 2280 to 2542 | 2543 to 2627 | 2628 to 2717 | 2718 to 2900 |

1. Our example: reading a grade 6 report. Suppose a sixth grader scores 2560. In the table, grade 6 Proficient runs from 2552 to 2609, so 2560 is Proficient, but only 8 points above the start of that band.
2. Look at the measurement error, too. Every score on the report comes with a plus-or-minus range. A score sitting 8 points above a threshold could, on another day, land just below it. We treat it as &ldquo;on the line&rdquo;, not &ldquo;safe&rdquo;.
3. Then read the two composite areas. If Concepts and Procedures is Above Proficient but Mathematical Practices is Below Proficient, the gap is in reasoning and problem solving, which is exactly what the performance task tests. That shapes the next term&rsquo;s lessons.

One warning from California&rsquo;s own guide: &ldquo;It is not possible to calculate a scale score by multiplying a student&rsquo;s percent correct&rdquo;. Because the test adapts, two children with the same number right can earn different scale scores. Ignore any website that converts a practice-test percentage into a CAASPP level.

Sources: [Appendix A: Scale Score Ranges](https://ca-toms-help.ets.org/scoring-and-reporting/appendices/appendix-a/), [What&rsquo;s New in 2025 to 2026](https://ca-toms-help.ets.org/scoring-and-reporting/whats-new/) and [What Do the Scores Mean? Mathematics](https://www.caaspp-elpac.org/s/docs/scoresmeanmath.pdf) (March 2026). The worked reading above is our example, not a real student.

## How do you answer a CAASPP math performance task?

One situation, several connected questions, and at least one answer that has to be explained. Here is a grade 5 problem we wrote in that spirit, worked the way we teach it.

1. Our problem (not a CAASPP item). A class is planting a garden with 6 raised beds. Each bed needs 3/4 of a bag of soil. Soil is sold only in packs of 4 bags. Part A: how many bags of soil does the class need? Part B: how many packs must they buy? Part C: Mia says they should buy 1 pack because 6 x 3/4 is less than 4. Is Mia right? Explain.
2. Part A. 6 x 3/4 = 18/4 = 4 2/4 = 4 1/2 bags. We write the multiplication and the simplified mixed number, not just the result.
3. Part B. One pack is 4 bags, which is less than 4 1/2, so one pack is not enough. Two packs give 8 bags. They must buy 2 packs. The context decides the rounding: you cannot buy half a pack, so we round up.
4. Part C. Mia is not right. 6 x 3/4 is 4 1/2, which is more than 4, not less. One pack would leave the class half a bag short for the last bed.
5. What makes this a performance-task answer. Each part uses the one before, the rounding comes from the situation rather than a rule, and Part C asks for a judgement with a reason. That is California&rsquo;s Mathematical Practices composite in miniature.

### Three habits we drill

Reread the situation before every part. Performance tasks hide the deciding fact (packs of 4, whole buses, a deadline) in the first paragraph.

Label every number with its unit, and answer the question that was asked: &ldquo;2 packs&rdquo;, not &ldquo;8&rdquo;.

### One habit we break

Writing &ldquo;Mia is wrong&rdquo; and stopping. The explanation is where the reasoning shows, and a reason that uses a number (4 1/2 is more than 4) is stronger than one that does not.

## When is the CAASPP math test in 2027?

California does not set one statewide test day. Each district chooses its window, and the state says most schools test between the middle of March and the middle of May.

**How the CAASPP math window is set (Assessment Fact Sheet, May 2025; CAASPP Online Test Administration Manual, read 31 October 2026)**

| Question | What California publishes |
|---|---|
| Who sets the dates | Each local educational agency&rsquo;s CAASPP coordinator sets test dates in TOMS, the state test management system |
| Typical spring window | Most schools test between the middle of March and the middle of May |
| Make-ups | All CAASPP testing, including any makeup testing, must take place within the school&rsquo;s window |
| Suggested pacing | Adaptive items and the performance task on separate days; the math task in one session |

For 2026 to 2027, ask your child&rsquo;s school for its window as soon as it is published, usually in the winter. Our ten-week plan below counts back from the first math day. California&rsquo;s August 2026 assessment timeline on the portal shows the year&rsquo;s tests in one chart for families who want the full calendar.

## Does the grade 11 CAASPP math score matter for college?

It can help with placement at the California State University through the Early Assessment Program, and the state says it does not affect admission.

### What the EAP is

California&rsquo;s manual calls the Early Assessment Program &ldquo;a joint collaboration of the CDE and the CSU.&rdquo; Every eleventh grader takes part simply by completing the Smarter Balanced ELA and math tests.

The program gives an early indicator of readiness for college-level work in mathematics and quantitative reasoning before senior year, and the CSU uses the results for course placement once a student is admitted.

### What it is not

The manual is explicit: &ldquo;The release of CAASPP results will not affect a student&rsquo;s application for admission.&rdquo; Students are asked to authorise release of their results to the CSU.

So the grade 11 test is worth taking seriously for placement, but it is not an admissions exam, and we never present it as one.

Grade 11 is also the only CAASPP math year with the graphing calculator. Teenagers who have only used a scientific calculator benefit from a few lessons on the Desmos graphing tool: entering a function, finding an intersection, and reading a table of values.

## Twelve CAASPP-style math practice questions with answers

Written by us, two per grade band and four for grade 11, labelled with the composite area each one leans on. They are not CAASPP items.

**Our original practice (not California or Smarter Balanced items), with answers**

| Grade | Question | Leans on | Answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | A shelf holds 7 rows of 8 books. How many books? Write the equation. | Concepts and Procedures | 7 x 8 = 56 |
| 4 | Is 3/5 closer to 0, 1/2 or 1? Explain using a number line. | Mathematical Practices | 1/2 (3/5 = 0.6, which is 0.1 from 1/2) |
| 5 | A recipe uses 2/3 cup of oats per batch. How many cups for 4 batches? | Concepts and Procedures | 8/3 = 2 2/3 cups |
| 5 | Leo says 0.4 x 0.2 = 0.8. Find and explain his error. | Mathematical Practices | 0.08: tenths times tenths gives hundredths |
| 6 | A phone plan costs 12 per month plus 3 per gigabyte. Write the cost for g gigabytes. | Concepts and Procedures | 12 + 3g |
| 6 | Which is the better buy: 5 pens for 4 or 8 pens for 6? Show unit rates. | Mathematical Practices | 8 for 6 (0.75 each against 0.80) |
| 7 | Solve 3(x - 2) = 15. | Concepts and Procedures | x = 7 |
| 8 | A line passes through (0, 2) and (4, 10). What is its slope and equation? | Concepts and Procedures | slope 2; y = 2x + 2 |
| 11 | Solve x&sup2; - 5x + 6 = 0. | Concepts and Procedures | x = 2 or x = 3 |
| 11 | A population of 500 doubles every 3 years. Write a model and find the size after 9 years. | Mathematical Practices | P = 500 x 2^(t/3); 4,000 |

## How to prepare for the CAASPP math test in ten weeks

Our plan, counting back from the first math day in your school&rsquo;s spring window.

1. Week 1: one guest practice test. On the official practice site, so we see both the adaptive section and a performance task, and note where the child hesitates.
2. Weeks 2 to 4: number fluency for the grade. Facts and fractions in grades 3 to 5, ratios and integers in grades 6 to 8, functions in grade 11, because so much of the test arrives without a calculator.
3. Weeks 5 to 7: one performance task a week. Our own tasks, written in parts, with every explanation said aloud and then written down.
4. Week 8: the Desmos tool for the grade. Four-function, scientific or graphing, on the same California links the test uses.
5. Week 9: test-day rules. Checking each screen before moving on, the 20-minute pause rule and the locked segments.
6. Week 10: rest and one short task. A light week. The test is untimed, so a calm child does better than a crammed one.

## CAASPP math: common questions

### What grades take the CAASPP math test?

Students in grades 3 through 8 and grade 11 take the Smarter Balanced math test as part of CAASPP. A small group with the most significant cognitive disabilities takes the California Alternate Assessments instead.

### How long is the CAASPP math test?

It is untimed. California's administration manual estimates about 1 hour 45 minutes of math in grades 3 to 5, 2 hours in grades 6 to 8 and 2 hours 30 minutes in grade 11, usually split over more than one day.

### Is there a calculator on the CAASPP math test?

Not in grades 3 to 5. An embedded Desmos calculator appears for calculator-allowed items from grade 6: four-function in grade 6, scientific in grades 7 and 8, and graphing in grade 11.

### What does Proficient mean on the CAASPP?

Proficient is Level 3, the label that replaced Standard Met in 2025 to 2026. California says only the labels changed, so a Proficient score means the same as Standard Met did before.

### What is a good CAASPP math score?

It depends on the grade, because each grade has its own scale. In grade 3 Proficient starts at 2436, in grade 6 at 2552 and in grade 11 at 2628, according to California's Scoring and Reporting Guide.

### When is the CAASPP test in 2027?

Each district sets its own window in the state system. California says most schools test between the middle of March and the middle of May, so ask your school for its 2027 dates.

### Where can I find a CAASPP math practice test?

California offers free practice and training tests through its student testing site, linked from caaspp-elpac.org, and students can sign in as a guest. The questions on this page are our own extra practice.

### Can I opt my child out of the CAASPP?

California's manual recognises a parent or guardian exemption submitted to the test site. Talk to your school about the process and what it means for your child's records.

### Does the grade 11 CAASPP affect college admission?

No. The manual says releasing CAASPP results will not affect a student's application for admission, but the Early Assessment Program lets the CSU use grade 11 results for placement after admission.

### When are your classes?

Weekdays or weekends, flexible: choose your batch, or for a very personalised plan choose 1-on-1 at your convenience, weekend mornings or weekdays. Times are set on Pacific Time.

No. Modern Age Coders is an independent online school with no connection to the California Department of Education, ETS, Smarter Balanced or any California district. We quote the state's public documents and link to them.
