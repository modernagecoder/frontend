# ESAT maths preparation: Mathematics 1 and Mathematics 2

The Engineering and Science Admissions Test is sat in modules, and mathematics runs through all of it. Mathematics 1 is compulsory for every candidate, every other module assumes its content, and most engineering and physics applicants sit Mathematics 2 as well. Each module is 27 multiple-choice questions in 40 minutes, on screen, with no calculator. This page shows which modules each Cambridge and Imperial course asks for, maps the two mathematics modules from UAT-UK's own specification, and works through the kind of exact, quick arithmetic they reward, using examples we wrote ourselves.

At a glance: Full name: Engineering and Science Admissions Test; Run by: UAT-UK, at Pearson test centres; Modules: Mathematics 1, Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Mathematics 2; Compulsory: Mathematics 1, for everyone; Each module: 27 multiple-choice questions, 40 minutes; Score: 1 to 9 per module, one decimal place; Sittings: 12 to 16 October 2026; 4 to 8 January 2027; Start: One free class. ## Three courses for an ESAT candidate

The A Level the mathematics modules are built on, a course that trains admissions-test thinking, and the Further Maths most engineering offers expect.

- [A Level Maths](/courses/a-level-maths-course-pure-mechanics-statistics): Mathematics 2 is AS-level pure in all but name; this is where its topics are taught properly.

- [A Level Further Maths](/courses/a-level-further-maths-course): Not examined in the ESAT, but asked for by many of the engineering courses that set it.

## What is the ESAT, and how many modules do you sit?

Five modules exist and most candidates sit three of them, back to back, for 120 minutes in total. Mathematics 1 is the only module every candidate sits; the other two depend on the course, and UAT-UK warns that they cannot be changed without cancelling and re-booking.

**ESAT structure for 2027 entry, from the UAT-UK ESAT page and content specification (read 31 October 2026)**

| Feature | What UAT-UK says |
|---|---|
| Modules on offer | Mathematics 1, Biology, Chemistry, Physics, Mathematics 2 |
| Who sits Mathematics 1 | All candidates |
| Who sits the others | Most candidates sit two of the remaining four, set by the course |
| Questions per module | 27 multiple-choice |
| Time per module | 40 minutes, each separately timed; unused minutes are not carried over |
| Total for most candidates | 120 minutes |
| Aids | No calculator or dictionary; an erasable booklet for working |
| Marking | One mark per correct answer; none deducted for a wrong one |
| Score report | One score per module, 1 (low) to 9 (high) to one decimal place |
| Pass mark | None: "There is no pass/fail score for the ESAT." |

### Mathematics 1 sits under everything

UAT-UK's specification says that "All modules will assume knowledge of the content of Mathematics 1". A Physics or Chemistry question can therefore expect a student to rearrange a formula, handle standard form or work with ratio as fluently as a Mathematics 1 question does.

That makes the compulsory module the most valuable hour of revision. A biologist who skips it to concentrate on Biology is weakening both scores at once.

### Scores are reported module by module

There is no single ESAT score. A Cambridge engineer receives three numbers on the 1 to 9 scale, one each for Mathematics 1, Mathematics 2 and Physics, and admissions tutors weigh them with the school record, reference and interview.

So a weak module is visible on its own; it is not averaged away. That is a reason to prepare every module the course requires, not only the one a student enjoys most.

### Applying to two ESAT courses

UAT-UK sets the rule for clashes: if one course has a free choice of modules and the other names a compulsory one, sit the compulsory module. A student applying to Cambridge Natural Sciences and Imperial Physics would therefore take Mathematics 2 and Physics, which satisfies both.

Where two courses demand modules that cannot both fit, UAT-UK asks candidates to contact it through their online account before booking, not after.

### Who else uses it

UAT-UK says Imperial College London, the University of Cambridge, the University of Oxford and UCL all use the ESAT. Imperial's own page lists seven universities, adding Durham, LSE and Warwick, for certain courses in engineering, science, natural sciences and veterinary medicine.

## What maths is in ESAT Mathematics 1 and Mathematics 2?

Mathematics 1 covers seven areas from units to probability, at roughly GCSE Higher depth. Mathematics 2 adds eight more, from algebra and functions to graphs of functions, which together look very like AS-level pure mathematics. The specification also names what is left out, which is worth knowing before revising.

**Topic headings in the ESAT Content Specification for October 2026 and January 2027**

| Mathematics 1 (labelled M) | Mathematics 2 (labelled MM) |
|---|---|
| M1 Units | MM1 Algebra and functions |
| M2 Number | MM2 Sequences and series |
| M3 Ratio and proportion | MM3 Coordinate geometry in the (x, y)-plane |
| M4 Algebra | MM4 Trigonometry |
| M5 Geometry | MM5 Exponentials and logarithms |
| M6 Statistics | MM6 Differentiation |
| M7 Probability | MM7 Integration |
|  | MM8 Graphs of functions |

### Inside Mathematics 2

The detail is precise. MM1.6 includes the Factor Theorem and the Remainder Theorem; MM2.3 includes the sum to infinity of a convergent geometric series; MM4.1 expects the sine rule's ambiguous case, in two or three dimensions; MM7.5 asks whether a trapezium-rule estimate is an overestimate or an underestimate.

Mathematics 2 assumes everything labelled M as well as everything labelled MM, so a student cannot sit it on calculus alone. Weak fractions or surds will show up inside a calculus question.

### What the specification leaves out

UAT-UK says plainly that "Questions requiring knowledge of the change of base formula will not be set", that differentiation from first principles is excluded, and that points of inflexion "will not be examined", though a qualitative sense of them is expected.

There is nothing from Further Maths, no mechanics as such, and no statistics beyond Mathematics 1. Revising those for the ESAT is time spent somewhere else.

**Units are mathematics here.** The specification expects the SI prefixes from nano to giga and negative indices in units such as m s&minus;1. Converting 72 km/h to 20 m/s, or 1 m3 to 106 cm3, is a Mathematics 1 skill that Physics and Chemistry questions quietly depend on.

Topic headings, inclusions and exclusions from the UAT-UK [ESAT Content Specification](https://uat-wp.s3.eu-west-2.amazonaws.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/03165424/ESAT_Content_Specification.pdf), read 31 October 2026. ## How do you answer ESAT maths questions without a calculator?

By keeping numbers exact for as long as possible and reaching for structure before arithmetic. Every example below is ours, written for this page in the spirit of the specification; none is an ESAT, ENGAA or NSAA question.

### Surds stay surds

Find &radic;48 + &radic;27. Pull out squares first: &radic;48 = 4&radic;3 and &radic;27 = 3&radic;3, so the sum is 7&radic;3. Squaring it gives 147. No decimal ever appears, which is how a no-calculator question wants to be answered.

### Disguised quadratics

Solve 4x &minus; 6 &times; 2x + 8 = 0. Write y = 2x, so 4x = y2, and the equation is y2 &minus; 6y + 8 = 0, giving y = 2 or y = 4. Then x = 1 or x = 2. Spotting the substitution is the whole question.

### Over or under?

Estimate the area under y = x2 from 0 to 2 with two trapezium strips: (1/2)(0 + 2 &times; 1 + 4) = 3. The exact value is 8/3. The curve bends upwards, so every straight top sits above it and the estimate is too large.

**Our ambiguous-case example.** In triangle ABC, a = 5, b = 7 and angle A = 30&deg;. How many triangles fit? The sine rule gives sin B = 7 &times; sin 30&deg; / 5 = 0.7. Two angles between 0&deg; and 180&deg; have a sine of 0.7, one acute and one obtuse, and they add to 180&deg;.

### Finishing the triangle

The acute angle is a little under 45&deg;, so the obtuse one is a little over 135&deg;. Add angle A: 30&deg; plus just over 135&deg; is still under 180&deg;, so the obtuse case leaves room for angle C. Both triangles exist, and the answer is two.

Nobody needs the angle to a decimal place. Knowing that sin 45&deg; is about 0.71 is enough to place the acute angle, and that is the kind of number sense the module rewards.

### The ninety-second habit

Each example above can be done in well under 90 seconds by a student who looks for structure first: squares inside surds, a hidden quadratic, the shape of a curve, the sum of angles. The same examples take three or four minutes by brute force.

Our lessons train that first look. Before writing anything, the student says out loud what kind of question it is; only then do they start working.

**Seven questions we wrote, answers alongside**

| # | Our question | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Simplify (&radic;12 + &radic;3)2. | 27, since &radic;12 + &radic;3 = 3&radic;3 |
| 2 | Find the remainder when x3 &minus; 2x + 5 is divided by (x &minus; 2). | 9, by the Remainder Theorem: 8 &minus; 4 + 5 |
| 3 | Find the sum to infinity of 6 + 3 + 1.5 + ... | 12, since 6 / (1 &minus; 1/2) = 12 |
| 4 | How many solutions does sin 2x = 1/2 have for 0&deg; &le; x < 360&deg;? | 4: x = 15&deg;, 75&deg;, 195&deg;, 255&deg; |
| 5 | Evaluate log2 8 + log2 (1/4). | 1, since 3 + (&minus;2) = 1 |
| 6 | Find the coefficient of x2 in (1 + 2x)5. | 40, since 10 &times; 22 = 40 |
| 7 | Find the gradient of the normal to y = x3 at x = 1. | &minus;1/3, since the tangent gradient is 3 |

## When is the ESAT in 2026 and 2027, and when do you book?

October is the main sitting, from 12 to 16 October 2026, and almost every Cambridge and Oxford applicant must use it. January, from 4 to 8 January 2027, is the second chance for other courses. October bookings shut on 28 September 2026; the January list has been open since 28 October 2026 and shuts at six in the evening, UK time, on 21 December 2026.

**ESAT sittings and deadlines for 2027 entry, from the Imperial, Cambridge and UAT-UK ESAT pages**

|  | Sitting 1 | Sitting 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Days on which to sit | Monday 12 October to Friday 16 October 2026 | Monday 4 January to Friday 8 January 2027 |
| Candidates in China, Hong Kong or Macau | Only 12 to 13 October 2026 | Only 6 January 2027 |
| Bookings accepted from | 3pm UK time on 20 July 2026 | 3pm UK time on 28 October 2026 |
| Last moment to book | 6pm UK time on 28 September 2026 | 6pm UK time on 21 December 2026 |
| Cambridge bursary voucher deadline | 21 September 2026 | See UAT-UK |
| Cambridge and Oxford applicants | Required, apart from the exceptions below | Only Cambridge mature-college January applicants and Oxford's Astrophoria Foundation Year |
| Results | About four weeks after the sitting, sent to the universities automatically | The same |

1. **Check every course page.** Write down the modules each course requires before opening the booking system, because UAT-UK will not change them at the test centre.
2. **Open a UAT-UK account.** Accounts could be created from 1 June, and a returning candidate keeps the same one.
3. **Ask for a bursary or access arrangements first.** Both must be approved before booking; UAT-UK says it cannot refund a test already paid for under a later bursary.
4. **Book the sitting, the modules and a Pearson centre.** UAT-UK advises that earlier bookings are more likely to find a slot at the preferred centre.
5. **Bring compliant photo ID on the day.** UAT-UK points candidates to its ID policy and a Pearson video on what to expect.
6. **Wait about four weeks.** Results arrive in the UAT-UK account and go automatically to every university that asked for the ESAT.

### One sitting per cycle

Imperial is explicit that the ESAT counts once in each application cycle: whichever sitting comes first is the score universities use. Booking January as a back-up for a disappointing October does not work.

Cambridge and Oxford applicants are tied to October in any case, which means their October modules travel to Imperial, UCL and anywhere else on the UCAS form.

### What registration mistakes cost

UAT-UK is blunt: "Not registering for the ESAT when it's required might make your application invalid." Missing a deadline is not an inconvenience; it can end the application to that course.

The same applies to the wrong modules. A Cambridge engineer who books Chemistry instead of Mathematics 2 has sat a test the course cannot use.

Dates, booking times and the one-score rule from Imperial's ESAT page; the Cambridge bursary, access arrangement and booking deadlines from the Cambridge ESAT page; the January exceptions, results timing and registration warnings from the UAT-UK ESAT page; all read 31 October 2026. Test fees are set by UAT-UK by test centre location and are not printed here.

## Do you need an ESAT tutor, and what does a good score look like?

UAT-UK and Cambridge both say good preparation means knowing the format and revising any specification topics you have not met. Neither publishes a target score. Cambridge says almost no one gets full marks and that some strong applicants do not finish in the time.

**Preparation and scores in the words of UAT-UK and Cambridge (read 31 October 2026)**

| Who | Their words |
|---|---|
| UAT-UK | Preparation means knowing the format and style of questions, reading the specification for unfamiliar topics, then doing the specimen test "under timed conditions" |
| UAT-UK | The test "is designed to be challenging in order to differentiate effectively between able applicants", including those with top school grades |
| Cambridge | "we recommend that you revise relevant topics and familiarise yourself with the format of the test" |
| Cambridge | "Almost no one gets full marks." and "Some strong applicants may not even complete the paper in the time given." |
| Cambridge | There is no pass mark, but "a strong performance is usually needed to get through to interview" |
| Cambridge | "Sharing admissions assessment or interview questions is strictly prohibited." |

### When a class earns its place

A Year 12 student who has not yet reached AS-level calculus, logarithms or the circle theorems in MM3.3 will meet Mathematics 2 topics for the first time while preparing. Some school courses teach them late in Year 12; some international curricula order them differently.

A second case is the student who knows the content but works slowly without a calculator. Ninety seconds a question is a skill, and it improves fastest with feedback on each attempt.

We do not teach the Biology or Chemistry modules, and our Physics help is limited to the mathematics inside physics questions. Students sitting those modules should use UAT-UK's free guides for them.

We do not circulate questions from past sittings, which Cambridge forbids sharing, and we do not promise a score on the 1 to 9 scale. The ESAT has no pass mark and we will not invent one.

## What ESAT applicants and parents ask

### What is the ESAT?

The Engineering and Science Admissions Test, a computer-based test run by UAT-UK at Pearson test centres. Cambridge and Imperial, among others, use it for engineering, physical and natural sciences and veterinary medicine courses.

### What replaced the ENGAA and NSAA?

The ESAT. UAT-UK still publishes ENGAA and NSAA papers from 2016 to 2023 as practice, because they contain questions of the type found in the ESAT, and marks the questions that fall outside the ESAT specification.

### How long is the ESAT?

Each module lasts 40 minutes and has 27 multiple-choice questions. Most candidates sit three modules, so the test is 120 minutes long; a course that asks for only Mathematics 1 and Mathematics 2, such as Imperial Design Engineering, means 80 minutes.

### Is Mathematics 1 compulsory in the ESAT?

Yes. UAT-UK says all candidates need to sit Mathematics 1, and its specification says every other module assumes Mathematics 1 content. Whether you also sit Mathematics 2 depends on the course.

### Can you use a calculator in the ESAT?

No. The specification states that calculators may not be used, and the only aid is an erasable booklet for rough work. That is why exact arithmetic with fractions and surds matters so much.

### Is there negative marking in the ESAT?

No. The specification says each correct answer scores one mark and nothing is deducted for an incorrect answer, so a candidate should answer every question before a module closes.

### When is the ESAT for 2027 entry?

UAT-UK gives a week in each window: 12 to 16 October 2026, which Cambridge and Oxford applicants use, and 4 to 8 January 2027. Imperial says January bookings close at 6pm UK time on 21 December 2026.

### What is a good ESAT score?

Each module gets its own score between 1 and 9, given to one decimal place, and UAT-UK sets no pass mark. Cambridge says almost no one gets full marks and that a strong performance is usually needed for interview, but publishes no target figure.

### Do I need an ESAT tutor?

UAT-UK recommends familiarity with the format, a check of the specification, and the specimen test under timed conditions. A class helps most when Mathematics 2 topics have not been taught yet or when working without a calculator is slow.

### What days are your classes on?

Weekdays or weekends, flexible: choose your batch, or for a very personalised plan choose 1-on-1 at your convenience, weekend mornings or weekdays.

### Are you connected with UAT-UK?

No. We are an independent online school with no link to UAT-UK, Pearson or any university on this page. Every candidate registers, picks modules and pays UAT-UK directly.
