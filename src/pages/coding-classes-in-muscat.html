What does the word Muscat cover?

Official boundaries matter because search pages often collapse the old city, the wider urban corridor and the governorate into one vague label.

The Muscat Governorate places the capital on the Sea of Oman, with the Eastern Hajar Mountains and North and South Sharqiyah to the east, South Al Batinah to the west and Ad Dakhiliyah to the south. Muscat Municipality describes the governorate as about 4,000 square kilometres. The official structure contains six wilayats. Muscat is also romanised as Masqat in some English references, but this page uses Muscat consistently.

School systems

How should Muscat school context change placement?

The Embassy of India records 22 CBSE Indian Schools and more than 48,000 students across Oman. Muscat contains a substantial part of that network, but the hub does not use school names as decorative keywords. Each district page is responsible for schools physically there. At the governorate level, the useful fact is that a CBSE learner and a learner in Oman's national system may reach coding through different vocabulary and assessment habits.

Oman's Ministry of Education publishes Information Technology assessment objectives for Grades 11 and 12. Those objectives allocate weight to knowledge, application, analysis and evaluation. A student who can reproduce a sample program but cannot trace a new input has not yet demonstrated the application or analysis that the document names. The lesson should expose that gap early.

International-school families need the same evidence-first approach. A British curriculum label does not tell the teacher which exam board or option the pupil takes. An American-school grade does not prove a particular computer science sequence. The free class asks for previous work, then gives a new problem that cannot be completed by recognising the worksheet.

Muscat data project

Where did the leftover students go?

The Ministry of Education publishes school-level student counts by school, grade and sex. That makes a Muscat cohort a clean way to expose a small programming operation that can silently erase real people.

Source: Oman Ministry of Education open dataLevel: Grade 6 and upConcept: Integer division and remainder

The Ministry's open-data catalogue lists a 2024/2025 dataset of student counts by government school, grade and sex, plus a matching school dataset by governorate. A learner filters the current files to Muscat and selects one grade cohort.

The ordinary task is to estimate how many coding groups are needed if each group holds eight students. A beginner writes groups = students / 8, stores the result as an integer and reports only the full groups. The output looks tidy.

The missing value is the remainder. If the cohort is not exactly divisible by eight, some students have vanished from the plan. Rounding down loses them. Rounding up produces the required group count but does not explain that the last group is smaller.

The trap

Integer division answers how many complete groups fit. It does not allocate every student. The quotient and remainder are both part of the result. A defensible report uses ceiling division for capacity, retains the remainder for the final group and verifies that allocated places are at least the cohort size.

The same bug appears in buses, hotel rooms, shipping cartons, database pagination and work rosters. It is especially dangerous when a dashboard reports only full units, because the people in the remainder disappear without an error message.

Questions

Muscat questions, answered plainly

Are these coding classes in person in Muscat?

No. Modern Age Coders teaches live online from India and does not claim a Muscat office or classroom. For a family crossing a metro area spread across six wilayats, the practical benefit is avoiding two extra journeys each week. The free class lets you test the online format before paying.

Which parts of Muscat do you serve?

Online classes are available across Muscat Governorate. The official governorate structure contains Muscat, Muttrah, Bawshar, Seeb, Al Amerat and Qurayyat. District guidance is being built separately so a local page can discuss its own schools and roads instead of repeating a generic Muscat paragraph.

Does this work for children at CBSE schools in Muscat?

Yes. The Embassy of India records a large CBSE network across Oman, and several of those schools serve Muscat families. An India-based teacher will recognise the academic vocabulary. Placement still follows the learner's actual work, because two pupils in the same CBSE class may have very different coding experience.

Will lessons fit around Muscat school transport and homework?

The exact recurring time is agreed on the free call before payment. Oman runs one and a half hours behind India, so evening teaching is feasible for both sides. A family should still protect homework, sleep and the Grade 12 examination calendar. No fixed weekday time is promised on this page.

Do you offer Friday or Saturday classes in Muscat?

Friday and Saturday are Oman's weekend, and a weekend arrangement can be discussed. Weekday evenings can also work because the time difference with India is only one and a half hours. Availability is confirmed on the demo call rather than advertised as a slot that may already be full.

What changes during Ramadan?

School hours and household routines change during Ramadan, so lesson timing can be reviewed with the family. Modern Age Coders does not publish one Ramadan timetable for every Muscat household. The useful arrangement is the one that protects rest, school responsibilities and concentration in that particular year.

What do Muscat coding classes cost?

A group of five to eight students costs USD 100 a month. Private teaching with one student and one teacher costs USD 150 a month. Each plan includes eight live classes, normally two each week. The first class is free and there is no enrolment charge or minimum term.

Can an adult in Muscat join?

Yes. Modern Age Coders teaches ages 6 to 67. Adults can study Python, web development, data analysis, databases or mathematics in an adult-appropriate path. The placement conversation starts with what you need to build or understand, not with school grade or a promise of job placement.

Is this the same as your Muscat machine learning page?

No. This page covers programming and mathematics from beginner blocks through Python and web development. The separate Muscat machine learning page serves a narrower model-building search intent. A learner usually needs reliable programming and data foundations before a specialist machine learning route makes sense.
