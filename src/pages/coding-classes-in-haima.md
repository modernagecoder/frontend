FAQ Haima coding class questions The place, the teaching and the terms, answered one at a time.
Are both Haima populations real? Yes. The 2020 census gives the town 7,814 and a population database gives the wilayat 10,093 for the same census. Both are published and both appear here, each labelled with what it counts. The 140 cases used to build the example rate are illustrative and the page says so.
Why is a sourced figure not enough? Because a citation covers where a number came from, not what it counts. Both halves of a broken rate can carry perfect references. A reviewer checking provenance finds two good answers and no reason to look at the join between them, which is where the error lives.
Is there a Modern Age Coders centre in Haima? No. No premises are claimed in Haima or anywhere in Al Wusta. Classes are live online and need a laptop or desktop, working audio, a current browser and a stable connection. The page describes where the service reaches, not a building.
Which population should somebody use? Whichever matches the numerator. If the count covers the wilayat, use the wilayat figure; if it covers the town, use the town. The rule is not which number is better, it is that both halves of a fraction have to describe the same area over the same period.
Is this open to working adults, or school students only? Both, with 67 as the upper limit. Adults work in Python, analytics and reporting. Anyone whose organisation reports a per-head figure where the head count comes from a different system than the numerator is looking at this exact fault. Bring the real numbers if they are yours to share.
What comes out of this project? Column names that carry their unit, an assertion that numerator and denominator share a boundary and a period, and the habit of publishing both official figures when two exist. All three apply to any indicator anybody builds.
