Playing or building

Can kids really learn to code with Roblox?

Yes, once they move from the Roblox app into Roblox Studio. The app is for playing; Studio is the free editor in which every Roblox game is made, and the code inside it is real code.

What changes when a child opens Studio

In the game, a lava floor simply hurts you. In Studio, your child places the floor, names it, and writes the few lines that decide what happens when a player touches it. The question stops being "how do I win?" and becomes "what should happen, and how do I tell the computer?"

That second question is programming. Roblox describes scripts as files that "add custom, dynamic behavior" to games: responding to player input, saving data, building leaderboards. A child who has played for years already knows what those things should feel like, which is a large head start.

What it does not do on its own

Studio does not teach. A child alone with it tends to copy scripts from videos, paste them in, and feel stuck the first time one breaks. Nothing in that loop builds the habit of reading an error or predicting what a line will do.

A live mentor supplies the missing piece. They ask the child to say what the script should do before running it, and when it fails, to read the Output window aloud and guess why. Roblox itself offers "free software and curriculum to teach students of all ages computer science"; our job is to turn that material into steady thinking, week after week.

The language

What language is Roblox coded in, and is it a real one?

Roblox games run on Luau. Roblox calls it "the scripting language creators use in Roblox Studio", derived from Lua 5.1, and everything a child learns in it carries over to other languages later.

Luau in plain terms

Lua is a small, fast language that has been built into games and programs for decades. Luau is Roblox's own version of it, extended with features such as an optional typing system. Roblox describes it as "gradually typed": a beginner can write local score = 0 without naming a type, and an older student can add types later to catch mistakes earlier.

Roblox's own advice is that "it's almost always better to declare variables and functions locally with the local keyword". We teach that from the first script, so children learn early that where a name lives in a program matters.

Luau beside Python

Children who later move to Python find most ideas familiar: variables, if, loops, functions, lists. One difference catches almost everyone. In Luau, Roblox writes, arrays "are one-based rather than zero-based, so the first item is [1]". In Python the first item is [0].

We point that out on purpose rather than letting it surprise a child later. Knowing that two languages count differently, and why, is exactly the kind of understanding that separates a coder from someone who memorised one tool.

Because Roblox Studio, its menus, its documentation and its error messages are all in English, a child who learns Roblox in English never has to translate between the lesson and the tool. For a family in Peru that is also daily listening practice with a purpose, since a script built on a misheard instruction simply does nothing.

At home in Peru

What does my child need at home to code in Roblox?

A Windows or Mac computer. Roblox Studio does not run on phones or tablets, and most Chromebooks cannot run it either, so a family planning Roblox classes should check the computer first.

Roblox's own minimum

Roblox lists Studio as "a free application available on Windows and Mac". The minimum it publishes is Windows 10 or macOS 10.14 with 3 GB of memory, and it recommends 8 GB. The screen should be at least 1600 by 900 pixels.

That screen size matters more than it sounds. Studio shows the game world, the Explorer, the Properties panel and the script all at once, and in class the video call needs room too. On a small laptop screen a child spends half the lesson moving windows.

The Peruvian picture

In the second quarter of 2026, INEI counted a computer of any kind in just 36.4% of homes across Peru. Many families with keen Roblox players therefore have a phone and a tablet but no machine that can run Studio, and it is better to know that before booking.

If your computer is shared, pick a class hour when it is free; flexible timing makes that easy. A headset with a microphone helps more than any other accessory, because the child can hear the mentor clearly over a busy house.

  1. Check the computer. Windows 10 or later, or macOS 10.14 or later, with 8 GB of memory if possible.
  2. Install Roblox Studio from Roblox's own site and sign in with the child's account.
  3. Check the account settings together: maturity rating, chat and spending limits.
  4. Book the free class at an hour when the computer and a quiet corner are both free.
  5. In the first lesson the mentor checks that Studio opens, the Output window is visible and the shared screen works.

Six months

What does a Roblox coding course for kids cover?

Our Roblox course runs for about six months, in three phases. Each phase ends with something the child has built and can show at home.

Months 1 and 2: Studio and first scripts

Parts and properties, building a small world, then Luau from zero: variables, decisions, loops and functions. The first script makes a part change colour; by the end of month two the child writes short functions alone.

Months 3 and 4: events and score

The Touched event, doors and buttons, then leaderboards, collectible coins, checkpoints and simple on-screen interfaces. This is where the coin lesson below lives, and where a scripted obstacle course becomes a real game.

Months 5 and 6: mechanics and publishing

A shop, timers and rounds, sound and light, then a capstone the child designs, builds in two sprints, tests, tunes and publishes. The last lesson is a short presentation in English to the mentor and family.

Every phase uses the same routine: the child says what the script should do, writes it, runs it, and explains the difference between the plan and the result. By month six most children can read an error message in the Output window and find the line it points to without help. That is the skill we care about most, because it outlasts Roblox.

A lesson from month four

How does a Roblox lesson teach real maths?

Through problems the game creates. Here is one from the collectibles week: a player should pick up any coin within 10 studs, Roblox's unit of distance. Most children's first solution works nearly all the time, and the interesting part is the coin it gets wrong.

The first idea

Children usually reason one direction at a time: the coin is close enough if it is within 10 studs left or right, within 10 up or down, and within 10 forwards or back. In code, that is three comparisons joined by and.

We rebuild the idea in Python first, outside Roblox, with four coins at positions we chose. The player stands at (0, 3, 0). It is quicker to test there, and the maths is identical.

The question the mentor asks

Before running anything, the mentor asks: "Which coins do you think are inside 10 studs?" Then the child runs the box check and sees all four collected, including coin B at (9, 3, 9).

Next question: "Is B really 10 studs away or less?" The child walks it out on paper. B is 9 studs along one way and 9 along the other, so it sits on the diagonal of a square. The diagonal is longer than either side.

The box check was wrong only near the corners, which is why nobody noticed it in testing. The mentor asks the child to name the rule in their own words: a pickup radius is a sphere, and only Pythagoras measures a sphere. Older students then meet a trick game programmers use: compare the squared distance with the squared radius and skip the square root. For coin B, 162 <= 100 is False, the same answer with less work.

  1. Ages 8 and 9. On squared paper, mark a player and three coins. Use a ruler to check which are within 10 squares, and see that the corner coin fails.
  2. Ages 10 and 11. Run Version 1 and Version 2. Move coin B to (7, 3, 7) and predict both results before running.
  3. Ages 12 to 14. Add a fifth coin that the box check refuses but the distance check accepts. Then explain why that is impossible.

The right age

What age is right for Roblox coding, and when should Python come in?

Eight is a comfortable start for Roblox Studio, with fourteen as our upper age for the kids' course. In Peruvian school terms that runs from about 3rd grade of primaria to 3rd of secundaria.

Ages and grades

MINEDU places children by their age on 31 March of each school year. On its normative-age table a child of 8 is in 3rd grade of primaria, a child of 11 in 6th grade and a child of 12 in 1st of secundaria. We place children by age and starting level, not by grade, but the table helps parents picture the step.

Below eight, reading speed is usually the limit: Studio's menus and Luau's error messages are text. Younger children do better starting with blocks and arriving at Roblox later with the logic already in place.

Roblox and Python together

Roblox is not a dead end. Children aged about 11 and up often run Roblox and Python side by side: the same loop or condition written twice, in two languages, makes the idea clearer than either alone. The coin lesson above is built that way.

Our page on Python for kids in Peru sets out a first year of typed Python by grade, and the Spanish-language guide for 12 year olds in 1st of secundaria shows how Python and Luau sit together at that age.

Thinking first

Will my child just copy Roblox scripts from videos or AI?

Not in our classes. Every line is typed by the child after they have said what it should do, and pasted code is the first thing a mentor notices on a shared screen.

Predict, then run

Before pressing Play the child says what the script will do. When the result differs, that difference is the lesson, as with coin B above.

Tools that fit the age

No child here signs in to an adult AI service. Anthropic sets 18 as the minimum age for Claude, and OpenAI wants a parent to agree before anyone younger uses ChatGPT.

One standard everywhere

A child in Lima, Trujillo or Iquitos gets the same mentors, the same course and the same patience. In Peru, where you live changes only the hour on the clock.

Roblox Studio is adding AI features of its own. Older students learn what such tools do and why their output must be read and tested, but only after they can write the script themselves. A child who can explain every line of their game owns it; a child who pasted it cannot fix it when it breaks.

Progress

How do I know my child is becoming a Roblox developer?

Look at what they build and listen to how they explain it. We wrote these six stages ourselves and use them to place each new child.

Our Roblox stages for children of 8 to 14
StageOn screenIn their own words
1. BuilderPlaces, sizes and colours parts; groups them into a small world"I anchored it so it does not fall."
2. First scriptsChanges a property from a script; uses a variable and an if"When the value is 0, the door opens."
3. EventsMakes the game react to a touch, a click or a player joining"This runs every time someone touches the pad."
4. Game stateKeeps score, saves checkpoints, shows a timer on screen"The leaderboard reads the score from here."
5. MechanicsBuilds a shop or timed rounds that work for every player"I tested it with two players and found the bug."
6. PublisherDesigns, tests and publishes a complete game"Here is my game, how it works and what I would add next."

For parents

Ask your child to open Studio and show you one script. If they can tell you what each line does, the class is working.

Stage 4 is where most children start inviting family members to test their game. That is a good sign: they want feedback.

For young developers

Read the red text in the Output window before asking for help. It usually tells you the line number.

When a script works, try to break it on purpose. That is how real game testers find problems before players do.

Parents in Peru ask

Roblox coding for kids: questions from Peru

Are the classes in Spanish?

No. We teach only in English, live and online. The mentor talks at an unhurried pace, tunes in to how your child speaks and how fast they work, and points to each Studio button on screen while naming it. Since Roblox Studio is in English too, lesson and tool share one vocabulary. Some of our Peru pages are written in Spanish for parents, yet no class is ever taught in Spanish.

What age can a child start Roblox coding?

Our Roblox course takes children from 8 to 14. At 8 a child usually reads well enough to follow Studio's menus and short error messages. Younger children start with blocks and come to Roblox later, already knowing what a loop and a condition are.

What programming language does Roblox use?

Luau. Roblox describes it as the scripting language creators use in Roblox Studio, derived from Lua 5.1 and gradually typed. It is a real language: variables, conditions, loops, functions and tables all work as they do elsewhere, which is why children move on to Python or JavaScript without starting again.

Does my child need Robux or a paid membership?

No. Roblox Studio is free, and nothing in our course needs Robux, a membership or any purchase. Roblox lets parents set monthly spending limits on the account, and we suggest setting them to zero if you prefer.

Can my child do Roblox coding on a tablet or phone?

No. Roblox Studio runs on Windows and Mac computers only; Roblox lists Windows 10 or macOS 10.14 as the minimum, with 8 GB of memory recommended and a screen of at least 1600 by 900. Most Chromebooks cannot run it either.

Is Roblox safe for an 8 year old?

Roblox gives children aged from 5 up to 8, in most regions, a Roblox Kids account with chat off and only the lowest-rated games, and children of 9 to 15 a Roblox Select account with more limits than an adult one. In our classes building happens in Studio on the child's own copy of a game, so no stranger joins the lesson. A parent should set up and keep the account.

When are the classes on Peru time?

Any day, any time, flexible: choose a batch, or for a very personalised plan choose 1-on-1 at the hour that suits your family. India is ten and a half hours ahead of Peru all year, so 16:00 in Lima is 02:30 the next morning for the mentor, who works those hours on purpose.

Group classes or one to one for Roblox?

A group of fifteen to twenty players of a similar age suits those who like showing off a level and testing friends' games. Private lessons fit the quiet child, the child still finding their English, the child far ahead, or the one who turns up with a huge game plan already sketched.

Will my child use AI to write Roblox scripts?

Not to write them. Children type every line after saying what it should do. Older students study what AI coding tools do and how to check them, but never through adult accounts: Claude requires users to be 18, and ChatGPT needs a parent's permission for anyone under 18.

Are you connected with Roblox, MINEDU or a school?

No. Modern Age Coders is an independent private online school, not linked with Roblox Corporation, MINEDU or any school in Peru. We give no Peruvian grades or qualifications; a child who finishes the course receives a completion certificate issued by us.
