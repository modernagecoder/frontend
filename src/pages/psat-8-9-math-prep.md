# PSAT 8/9 prep and PSAT 10 math

The PSAT 8/9 and PSAT 10 are the earliest tests in College Board's SAT Suite, given in school to eighth, ninth and tenth graders. Both have a 70-minute, 44-question Math section with Desmos built in, the same timing as the SAT itself. What differs is the mix: the PSAT 8/9 puts far more weight on algebra and data, and its geometry stops short of trigonometry. Neither counts for National Merit. This page compares the two from College Board's own guides and explains how families can use the results as a starting point, with problems we wrote at the right level.

## What is the difference between the PSAT 8/9 and the PSAT 10?

Grade, score scale, testing window and domain weights. The timing and the number of math questions are the same.

**PSAT 8/9 and PSAT 10 compared (College Board student guides, structure pages, score page and 2026-27 dates, read 31 October 2026)**

| Feature | PSAT 8/9 | PSAT 10 |
|---|---|---|
| Usual grades | 8 and 9 | 10 |
| Whole test | 2 hours 14 minutes | 2 hours 14 minutes |
| Math section | 44 questions, 70 minutes, two modules | 44 questions, 70 minutes, two modules |
| Calculator | Desmos or approved handheld, whole section | Desmos or approved handheld, whole section |
| Total score | 240 to 1440 | 320 to 1520 |
| Math score | 120 to 720 | 160 to 760 |
| 2026-27 windows | October 1 to 30, 2026; March 1 to April 30, 2027 | March 1 to April 30, 2027 |
| National Merit entry | No | No |

### What College Board says the PSAT 8/9 is for

The 2026-27 guide says the PSAT 8/9 "is designed to be taken as you start your high school journey" and that "Your results will help establish a baseline" and help teachers focus instruction.

It also notes that the PSAT 8/9 "follows the same timing as all other tests in the SAT Suite, so you'll be preparing yourself for the same format as the PSAT 10 and the SAT."

### Neither one is the National Merit test

National Merit entry comes only through the PSAT/NMSQT, normally in grade 11. The NMSC pages in College Board's fall guide state it in capitals: "The PSAT 10 and PSAT 8/9 will NOT be considered for entry to the National Merit Scholarship Program."

That takes the pressure off. A PSAT 8/9 or PSAT 10 score is information, not a verdict.

## What math is on the PSAT 8/9, and how is it different from the PSAT 10?

Both tests use four content domains, but the PSAT 8/9 gives Algebra and data more questions and Advanced Math fewer, and its geometry domain leaves out trigonometry.

**Math question distribution, PSAT 8/9 against PSAT 10 (2026-27 PSAT 8/9 Student Guide and spring 2026 PSAT 10 Student Guide; counts are of the 40 operational questions)**

| Domain | PSAT 8/9 | PSAT 10 |
|---|---|---|
| Algebra | 16 to 18 | 13 to 15 |
| Advanced Math | 7 to 9 | 12 to 14 |
| Problem-Solving and Data Analysis | 9 to 11 | 7 to 9 |
| Geometry (8/9) or Geometry and Trigonometry (10) | 4 to 6 | 4 to 6 |

### Reading the PSAT 8/9 column

On the PSAT 8/9, Algebra has 16 to 18 of the 40 scored questions and Problem-Solving and Data Analysis has 9 to 11, against 13 to 15 and 7 to 9 on the PSAT 10. That is the math of grade 7 to Algebra 1: linear equations and functions, systems, ratios, rates, percentages and data.

The guide lists the PSAT 8/9 geometry skills as "Area and volume; Lines, angles, and triangles, including right triangles", without trigonometry.

### Reading the PSAT 10 column

On the PSAT 10, Advanced Math rises to 12 to 14 questions: equivalent expressions, nonlinear equations and functions such as quadratics and exponentials. Right triangle trigonometry joins the geometry domain.

A tenth grader who has finished Algebra 1 but not yet started Algebra 2 will find the Advanced Math questions the least familiar part of the test.

Some things are identical across the two guides. A module holds 22 questions, 2 of them pretest items that are never scored; difficulty climbs through each module; and students can open a reference sheet, "a list of common formulas", during Math. Roughly 30% of math questions come with a real-world or science context, per College Board.

## When is the PSAT 8/9 and the PSAT 10 in 2026-27?

Your school chooses the day inside College Board's windows and registers you. Students do not sign up on their own.

**In-school testing windows for 2026-27 (College Board, SAT Suite Test Dates and Deadlines, and the 2026-27 PSAT 8/9 Student Guide)**

| Term | Tests in the window | Window |
|---|---|---|
| Fall 2026 | SAT School Day, PSAT/NMSQT and PSAT 8/9 | October 1 to 30, 2026 |
| Spring 2027 | SAT School Day, PSAT 10 and PSAT 8/9 | March 1 to April 30, 2027 |

1. Ask the counselor which test and which window. Some schools give the PSAT 8/9 in the fall, some in the spring.
2. Prepare the device. Most schools use school-managed devices; if yours allows a personal device, Bluebook must be installed beforehand.
3. Request accommodations early. The PSAT 8/9 guide says some requests can take 7 weeks to review, and that some One-Time Supports can be requested "for PSAT 8/9 only" when less time remains.
4. Homeschooled students, contact a participating school. College Board asks away students to reach out at least four weeks before the window begins.
5. Know the retest rule. The guide says each student "can only obtain one valid score during the testing window", with retests only for irregularities.

Some score features also differ by grade. The PSAT 8/9 guide says ninth graders may opt in to the Connections feature, "but it is not available to 8th graders taking the test." Your school will explain how and when scores are released.

## Do you need to study for the PSAT 8/9?

Not in the cramming sense. College Board describes the PSAT 8/9 as a baseline. The useful preparation is familiarity with the format plus steady school math.

### What a little preparation does

A student who has seen Bluebook, the Desmos calculator and the module structure once will spend test time on math rather than on the screen. College Board offers a test preview and full-length adaptive PSAT 8/9 practice tests in Bluebook.

Beyond that, the best preparation for the PSAT 8/9 is the ordinary math of grades 7 to 9 done well, especially linear relationships and percentages.

### What the score report is for

The score report shows performance in each content area. College Board describes the knowledge and skills section as a way "to identify content areas you may want to focus on to practice and improve."

A low Algebra area in grade 8 is a reason to strengthen Algebra 1, not to drill PSAT questions. A strong report is a reason to keep going, perhaps into competition math.

**Reading a PSAT 8/9 or PSAT 10 math result (ours; College Board publishes its own benchmarks in the score report)**

| What the report shows | What we would usually do next |
|---|---|
| Algebra is the weakest area | Linear equations, functions and systems, steadily, over a term |
| Problem-Solving and Data Analysis is weakest | Ratios, percentages and reading tables and graphs |
| Advanced Math is weakest (PSAT 10) | Quadratics and exponentials, often ahead of Algebra 2 |
| Geometry is weakest | Angles, triangles, area and volume, with the reference sheet |
| All areas strong | Keep up school math; consider competition math or early SAT practice |

## Three problems at PSAT 8/9 and PSAT 10 level

Our own problems in the domains that carry the most PSAT 8/9 weight. They are not College Board items.

**Algebra (ours): a phone plan's monthly bill depends on data used. Find the rule.**

| Data used (GB) | 0 | 2 | 5 | 8 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly bill (units) | 15 | 23 | 35 | 47 |

1. Check the rate is constant. From 0 to 2 GB the bill rises 8, so 4 per GB; from 2 GB to 5 GB it rises 12, again 4 per GB, and the last step of 3 GB adds another 12. A constant rate means a linear rule.
2. Read the starting value. At 0 GB the bill is 15, so the y-intercept is 15.
3. Write the rule. y = 4x + 15, where x is gigabytes and y is the bill.
4. Use it. At 12 GB, y = 4 x 12 + 15 = 63.

**Problem-Solving and Data Analysis (ours): a price rises 25%, then falls 20%. Where does it end up?**

| Step | Reasoning |
|---|---|
| 1. Rise as a multiplier | 25% up means x 1.25 |
| 2. Fall as a multiplier | 20% down means x 0.80 |
| 3. Combine | 1.25 x 0.80 = 1.00 |
| 4. Conclusion | Back to exactly the starting price |
| 5. Why the intuition fails | The 20% is taken from a larger number, so it removes as much as the 25% added |

**Geometry (ours): two angles on a straight line measure (3x + 15) degrees and (2x + 40) degrees. Find each angle.**

| Step | Reasoning |
|---|---|
| 1. Use the fact | Angles on a straight line add to 180 degrees |
| 2. Equation | 3x + 15 + 2x + 40 = 180, so 5x + 55 = 180 |
| 3. Solve | 5x = 125, so x = 25 |
| 4. The angles | 3(25) + 15 = 90 and 2(25) + 40 = 90 |
| 5. Notice | Both are 90 degrees: the lines are perpendicular |

## PSAT 8/9 and PSAT 10 math: common questions

### What math is on the PSAT 8/9?

College Board's 2026-27 guide lists Algebra (16 to 18 questions), Advanced Math (7 to 9), Problem-Solving and Data Analysis (9 to 11) and Geometry (4 to 6). It is mostly the math of grades 7 to 9.

### How many math questions are on the PSAT 8/9?

Forty-four, in two modules of 35 minutes each. Each module has 20 scored questions and 2 unscored pretest questions.

### What is a good PSAT 8/9 score?

The PSAT 8/9 is scored 240 to 1440, with 120 to 720 for Math. College Board describes it as a baseline, so the content-area breakdown on the report is more useful than comparing totals.

### Is the PSAT 10 harder than the PSAT 8/9?

It has the same length and timing, but more Advanced Math (12 to 14 questions against 7 to 9) and trigonometry in the geometry domain. Its scale is 320 to 1520.

### Does the PSAT 10 count for National Merit?

No. College Board's PSAT/NMSQT guide says the PSAT 10 and PSAT 8/9 are not considered for entry to the National Merit Scholarship Program.

### When is the PSAT 8/9 in 2026-27?

College Board lists two windows: October 1 to 30, 2026 and March 1 to April 30, 2027. Your school picks the day and registers you.

### Can you use a calculator on the PSAT 8/9?

Yes, on the whole Math section. The Desmos calculator is built into Bluebook, or students can bring an approved calculator.

### Should my eighth grader prepare for the PSAT 8/9?

A little: one look at the format in Bluebook and steady school math. Heavy drilling is not needed for a test College Board describes as a baseline.

### When are your classes?

Weekdays or weekends, flexible: choose your batch, or for a very personalised plan choose 1-on-1 at your convenience, weekend mornings or weekdays.

No. We are an independent online school. We describe the tests from College Board's published guides and use its free practice in Bluebook as it is.
