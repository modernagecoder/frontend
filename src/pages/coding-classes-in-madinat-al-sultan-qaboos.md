# Coding Classes in Madinat Al Sultan Qaboos

> British School Muscat moved to its Madinat Al Sultan Qaboos campus in 1978, while the local bilingual private school began in 1987. Modern Age Coders teaches coding and mathematics online between ages 6 and 67. A free live task places each learner by observed work instead of treating either school route as a level.

## A district shaped by two long school histories

Muscat Municipality names Madinat Al Sultan Qaboos among the areas through which Bawshar was extensively developed and modernised. British School Muscat's own history supplies a detailed education timeline. Its predecessor opened in Riyam in 1971, received a Royal Charter on 3 May 1973, and moved into the current Madinat Al Sultan Qaboos site on 1 April 1978 with 257 children in 14 classes.

The 2026 British Schools Overseas inspection records 1,073 pupils aged 3 to 18 and 61 nationalities. It divides the school into 71 early-years pupils, 418 primary pupils and 584 senior pupils, including 120 in sixth form. Senior routes include GCSE or IGCSE, A levels, BTEC National Diploma, EPQ and a skills programme.

Madinat Al Sultan Qaboos Private School says it was founded in 1987 and now serves more than 500 learners from KG1 to Grade 12. The British Council also lists an English language centre in the district, which makes the local learning audience broader than school pupils.

## The MSQ two-cohort diagnostic

The local data project begins with the two verified curriculum routes rather than with invented public scores. Consenting learners complete one common debugging task. The class records no name, school identifier or address. It keeps only a broad route category, prior coding exposure, completion time, repair count and rubric score.

The confident wrong report calculates one mean and calls it the typical Madinat Al Sultan Qaboos learner. A plot may instead reveal one cluster of first-time coders and another cluster with sustained prior programming, with few observations near the overall mean. In that case the mean sits in the gap and describes neither group.

The class compares the mean, median, modes and within-group spread. New observations test whether the gap persists. If the current data does not show two clusters, the student must discard the bimodal story. The assigned analytical trap does not authorise a fabricated result.

| Step | Student action | Safeguard |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Uses one task, time limit and scoring guide | Comparable measurement |
| 2 | Stores only broad route and prior-exposure categories | No identifiable school record |
| 3 | Plots all observations before summary statistics | Clusters remain visible |
| 4 | Compares centre and spread within each observed group | One average is not assumed |
| 5 | Adds new observations before claiming two modes | Small batches do not define the district |
| 6 | Reports the current shape and privacy limit | No invented result |

## Class rhythm and fees

The first lesson is free and checks placement. Groups contain five to eight learners at a compatible level. Private teaching pairs one learner with one teacher. Both paid plans normally meet twice a week, eight times a month.

| Plan | Monthly price | Live lessons |
|---|---:|---:|
| Group, five to eight learners | USD 100 | 8 |
| Private, one learner and one teacher | USD 150 | 8 |

There is no enrolment fee, materials charge or minimum term. Oman is one and a half hours behind India. Sunday to Thursday or Friday and Saturday options depend on availability and are agreed before payment. A learner near Grade 12, A level or BTEC examinations may be advised to delay or choose a narrow goal.

Use a laptop or desktop, microphone and stable video-capable internet. Practice between meetings remains necessary.
