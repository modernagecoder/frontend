## An average can fall exactly where no learner sits

The class builds a privacy-safe dataset from one common debugging task given to consenting learners from British-stage and bilingual-grade routes. It stores no name, school ID or address, only the route category, prior coding exposure and task measures.

Local frame**Two verified school routes**Measure**Common debugging task**Assigned trap**A mean hiding two modes**

The tempting report calculates one average completion time or score for the full group and calls it the typical MSQ learner. A histogram may instead show one cluster of first-time coders and another cluster with sustained prior programming, with few learners near the overall mean.

The mean is arithmetically correct but describes neither cluster. Students must plot the distribution, retain prior-exposure categories and report group medians or intervals when justified. If the current data does not show two modes, they must say so. The assignment does not permit manufacturing the split.

The trap

A mean is not a typical person when the distribution has two separated centres. Plot the values first. If the gap disappears, discard the bimodal story rather than protecting the lesson plan.

Yes. British School Muscat's 2026 inspection describes learners from age 3 to 18 following English curriculum routes through GCSE or IGCSE, A levels and BTEC options. It can complement a learner's existing work. The school describes bilingual teaching, with selected subjects in Arabic and others in English, plus STEM and robotics activity. Modern Age Coders is independent of the school. The free class checks current reasoning and projects before any coding or mathematics route is recommended.

Yes. Madinat Qaboos is a common shortened English form of Madinat Al Sultan Qaboos. The full official-style name is used in this page's URL, heading and schema. A school may shorten it in ordinary communication, but that does not identify a second district or another class location.

Yes, when a suitable teacher slot is available. British School Muscat publishes a Sunday to Thursday day, and Oman is one and a half hours behind India. Possibly, but examination commitments come first. A narrow Python, web, data or mathematics goal may fit if the learner has protected time. Adults up to age 67 can study programming, web, database, data or mathematics in an adult group or privately. The course does not confer British Council or Omani institutional credit.
