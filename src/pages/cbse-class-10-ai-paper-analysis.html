Ans. The short version

The CBSE Class 10 AI (417) paper of 27 February 2026 was moderate overall. Section A, 24 marks of objective items with no negative marking, was mostly direct but had a few traps in two-statement and assertion-reason items. Section B, 26 marks, rewarded exact terms: a confusion matrix case, Bag of Words on two short documents, and AI, ML and DL with a Venn diagram were the four-mark anchors, and teachers called the matrix and Bag of Words items scoring. The 2027 paper is on 24 February 2027, 10:30 AM. Modern Age Coders prepares students live, Monday and Wednesday at 9:00 PM IST, or one to one.

Q1. Was it easy?

Class 10 AI paper analysis 2026: was the AI paper easy?

It was moderate: easy to start, with marks lost in a handful of precise items rather than in hard topics. The paper kept the familiar frame, 21 questions with 15 to answer in two hours for 50 marks, and covered every theory unit. Our section-by-section reading of set 4, from CBSE's own marking scheme, is below.

What teachers said on the day

Teachers quoted by India TV and Careers360 on 27 February 2026 described the paper as balanced and within the syllabus, with an overall moderate level. They called the four-mark Bag of Words and confusion matrix questions scoring, said most objective items were application-based, and noted that a couple of objective items had closely related options. One school also reported that two written questions needed careful interpretation.

How we read it

  • Choice was generous. 72 marks were printed for a 50-mark paper, so a student could skip the item they disliked in every block.
  • Precision decided the top band. Close options and exact terms separated a high score from a full one, not knowledge of extra topics.
  • No unit was skipped. Every theory unit appeared in both sections, so dropping one unit was a gamble.
  • Only set 4 was read. Other sets carried different items of the same type; our ratings describe set 4.

Q4. What does it mean for 2027?

AI exam review: six lessons from the 2026 paper for the 2027 candidate

A paper analysis is only useful if it changes how the next student prepares. These are the habits the 2026 paper rewarded, each one now built into our mock reviews.

Learn the frameworks by type

Two items turned on whether a framework is sector-based or value-based. Students who knew bioethics only as "an ethics example" fell into the assertion trap.

Expect the unusual domain

A price comparison website belongs to Statistical Data, not NLP. Domain questions reward reasoning from the data the system uses, not from what it looks like.

Pick the calculation four-markers

The matrix and Bag of Words questions gave a mark per step, so a methodical student was close to certain of eight marks from two choices.

Draw what can be drawn

A labelled Venn diagram carried a full mark, and two network boxes another. A clean sketch is the quickest mark in Section B.

Give the example every time

Several two-markers split their marks between a point and an example. An answer with the right idea and no example stopped at half.

Read both statements on their own

Two-statement items ask about each statement separately. Judge statement one, then statement two, then choose; do not let one colour the other.

Questions about the AI paper

What students ask after the class 10 AI paper

Was the CBSE class 10 AI paper 2026 easy?

It was moderate. Teachers quoted on the day called it balanced and within the syllabus, and the objective section was mostly direct. Marks were lost on precision rather than hard topics: a two-statement item, two assertion-reason items, an unexpected domain question, and written answers that left out an example or a step.

Where can I find the CBSE class 10 AI answer key 2026?

This page gives the answer to every objective item of set 4 (question paper code 104/4), with what each item tested described in our own words. It is based on CBSE's own marking scheme for the 2026 main examination, which CBSE has posted on its marking scheme page. Other sets carried different items of the same types.

When is the class 10 AI paper analysis 2027 coming?

On the day of the paper. CBSE's 2027 date sheet puts the Artificial Intelligence (417) paper on Wednesday 24 February 2027, from 10:30 AM to 12:30 PM IST. This page will add the section-by-section shape and difficulty notes that afternoon, then an answer key in our own words in the following days.

How many questions were in the 2026 AI paper?

Twenty-one, of which students answered fifteen, in two hours for 50 marks. Section A had five objective questions of six parts each, answer any four or five parts. Section B had sixteen written questions, of which ten were answered: three employability two-markers, four AI two-markers and three AI four-markers.

Which questions were the most scoring in the AI paper?

Teachers named the four-mark Bag of Words and confusion matrix questions, and the marking scheme supports that: Bag of Words gave a mark per step, and the matrix question split its four marks between the matrix, one cell and precision. A methodical student could expect most of those eight marks.

Did the 2026 AI paper have assertion reason questions?

Yes. Set 4 had two assertion-reason items in Section A, one on bioethics and one on lowercasing text, plus a two-statement item on overfitting. CBSE has not said how many such items a 2027 paper will carry, so treat them as one of several Section A formats.

Was there any mistake or change in the AI answer key?

In set 4, the two-statement item on overfitting was keyed as first statement true and second false, but the marking scheme also accepted "both correct", noting that each statement could be read as true on its own. No other multiple-choice item in set 4 was keyed with a second accepted option.

Did the AI paper ask Python questions?

No. Statistical Data and Advance Python are assessed only in the practical, so the 2026 theory paper had no code. Statistical Data appeared only as an AI domain, in a question about which domain suits a price comparison website. Python is examined through the practical file, practical exam and viva.

Is this page a copy of the question paper?

No. We do not reproduce the paper, its options or its wording. Each item is described in our own words, the answers are stated as ideas, and worked methods use our own example sentences except where a figure, such as the confusion matrix counts, is needed to show the arithmetic.
