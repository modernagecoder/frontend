## Who should choose a class 10 AI private tutor over the batch?

Choose one to one when the problem is timing, a late start or a specific weak spot; choose the batch when the student needs the whole syllabus taught in order and can attend Monday and Wednesday at 9:00 PM IST. Find your child in the table.

- Your child's situation | Better fit | Why

- School tests, sport or other tuition on Monday or Wednesday evenings | One to one | Same course on free evenings; nothing missed for a clash

- Starting 417 support in November or later | One to one | The batch is moving into mocks; a private plan can teach the missing units first

- Scores well except in confusion matrix or TFIDF numericals | One to one | A few sessions on one unit cost less than a term of everything

- Practical file has fewer than half its programs | One to one | Sessions built around writing and explaining the student's own programs

- Quiet in groups, will not ask a question in front of others | One to one | No audience; the tutor asks, the student answers

- Living in a time zone where 9:00 PM IST is school hours or midnight | One to one | Scheduled at a local after-school hour

- Starting at the beginning of the session, roughly in step with school | Batch | The full syllabus in order, with classmates and a steady rhythm

- Motivated by working alongside others and racing the clock | Batch | Timed sets and mocks with a group are part of the point

## A sample one to one AI 417 lesson plan for eight weeks

Every 1 on 1 AI class 10 plan begins with a diagnostic session and is rewritten after it, so this is an example, not a fixed course. It is for a student who has covered most of the syllabus at school but is weak in Units 3 and 6 and has six programs in the practical file.

- 1 | Diagnostic: 12 mixed objective items, two written answers, one program explained aloud | Unit 3: building the confusion matrix from a word problem | Plan agreed with the family

- 2 | Unit 3: precision, recall, accuracy, F1; which metric for which risk | File: two programs on lists and NumPy statistics | Three numericals with new figures

- 3 | Unit 6: text normalisation, then bag of words worked by hand | Unit 6: TFIDF on three short documents | One full bag of words table

- 4 | Unit 6 in Orange: sentiment analysis; script bot against smart bot | File: two programs on charts with Matplotlib | One four-mark NLP answer, then a rewrite

- 5 | Check test on Units 3 and 6, marked on screen | Unit 2 recap: model types sorted from scenarios | Re-attempt every lost mark

- 6 | Unit 1 and Part A: case questions on the project cycle and ethics; two Part A units | File: reading a CSV and an image; programs reach the minimum fifteen | Read every file program aloud once

- 8 | Viva rehearsal on the file and the project | Practical mock: an unseen program on Units 4 to 7 | Next month's plan written

## Inside one personal AI tutor 417 session: evaluating models

Here is week 2 of the plan above, stage by stage. Unit 3, Evaluating Models, carries 10 theory marks and most of them depend on reading one table correctly, which is exactly the kind of thing one to one fixes quickly.

-
**Opening: last week's set work** The student shares the three numericals. Two are right; in the third, FP and FN are swapped. The tutor asks the student to explain their table before saying anything.

-
**Find the habit, not the slip** The student had put Predicted across the top. The fix agreed: always Reality across the top, Predicted down the side, written before any number.

-
**One new problem, worked together** The spam filter on the right, built cell by cell from the words.

-
**The student teaches it back** The student explains why a spam filter cares more about precision, and the tutor plays an examiner pushing back.

-
**Two solo problems, timed** New figures, no help. Both marked at once, with the working.

-
**Close: set work and the next step** Three more numericals; the next class moves to the NumPy statistics programs if both solo problems were right.

```
A filter checks 500 emails.
It marks 100 as spam; 80 really
are spam. Of the 400 it lets
through, 10 are spam.

              Reality: Spam  Reality: Not
Predicted Spam   TP = 80       FP = 20
Predicted Not    FN = 10       TN = 390

Precision = 80 / (80 + 20)  = 0.80
Recall    = 80 / (80 + 10)  = 0.89
Accuracy  = (80 + 390) / 500 = 0.94
F1 = 2 x 0.80 x 0.89 / (0.80 + 0.89)
   = 0.84
Wrong predictions = 20 + 10 = 30
```

Why precision here? A false positive sends a real email, perhaps a school notice, to the spam folder. People tolerate a little spam in the inbox far more than a lost important mail, so this filter is judged mainly on precision. For a disease screen the reasoning flips to recall.

## How a private tutor prepares the practical file and the viva

One to one is at its strongest on the 50 practical marks: the file of at least 15 programs, the practical exam, the project and two vivas. Every program is the student's own, and the viva is rehearsed on exactly those programs.

```
import numpy as np
import statistics

rain = [12, 0, 7, 12, 30, 5, 12]  # mm, one week

print("Total:", sum(rain))
print("Mean:", np.mean(rain))
print("Median:", np.median(rain))
print("Mode:", statistics.mode(rain))
```

```
Total: 78
Mean: 11.142857142857142
Median: 12.0
Mode: 12
```

### Viva questions the tutor asks on this program

- Why is the mean lower than the median here?Model answer The 0 mm day pulls the mean down more than the one 30 mm day pulls it up, while the median only looks at the middle value, 12.

- What would change if someone typed 300 instead of 30?Model answer The total becomes 348 and the mean jumps to about 49.7, but the median stays 12, because 300 still sorts to the end. That is why the median resists outliers.

- Why did you import statistics as well as NumPy?Model answer NumPy gives mean and median, but it has no mode function, so the mode comes from Python's statistics module.

- How would you show this week as a chart?Model answer With Matplotlib: plt.bar for rainfall by day, or plt.plot for a line, then plt.show().

- Which unit of the syllabus does this program belong to?Model answer Unit 4, Statistical Data, which is assessed in the practical rather than the theory paper.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is a private tutor better than a batch for class 10 AI?** Not for everyone. A private tutor is better when the student cannot attend fixed evenings, is starting late, or keeps losing marks in one unit. A batch is better value for a student who needs the full syllabus in order and enjoys working alongside others. The free demo is a fair way to decide.

**How many classes a week are there in 1 on 1 AI class 10 tutoring?** In India the personalised plan is one private class a week; outside India it is two a week. The days and the hour are set with the family and can move around school tests. Each class leaves short set work that opens the next one.

**Can a personal AI tutor 417 help with only one unit?** Yes. A student who is strong overall but weak in Evaluating Models or the NLP numericals can take one to one for that unit alone, then stop. Monthly billing makes a short plan possible.

**What is AI home tuition online?** It means one to one tuition delivered live over video instead of a tutor travelling to your home. For 417 it suits the subject well, because the practical is done on a computer and the tutor can see and fix the student's own Python or Orange work by screen share.

**Does a private tutor help with the practical file and project?** Yes, and this is where one to one is strongest. The tutor helps the student write and understand each program of the file of at least 15, guides the project in stages, and rehearses both vivas on the student's own work. The student writes everything; the tutor checks and questions it.

**How quickly can a private tutor cover the whole 417 syllabus?** It depends on where the student starts and how many classes a week. A student who has covered the theory at school mostly needs practice, marking and the practical, which can be organised in a couple of months. A student starting from nothing in winter needs a tighter plan; see the crash course page.

**Can we change the timing of one to one classes?** Yes. The days and hour are agreed with the family, and a class can move for a school test with notice. Outside India the time is set in your own time zone.

**What does the first one to one session involve?** It is a short diagnostic: a few objective items across the units, one written answer marked point by point, and one program from the file explained aloud. From that the tutor writes a plan for the month, listing the weak units first and the practical work needed, and shares it with the family.
