Coding and AI classes in New South Wales

Which coding and AI classes suit a New South Wales learner best?

Classes that speak the state's own language: Kindergarten and the stages, Year 11 and 12, the HSC and four school terms. New South Wales counted 8,072,163 people at the 2021 Census. We teach learners aged 6 to 67 live online, from a first Scratch game to Python, AI and HSC Software Engineering technique, either privately or with a handful of classmates at the same standard, and the trial lesson is free.

Every NSW school publishes its terms as dates. Every planner, spreadsheet and app turns those dates into week numbers. And here is the trap our project is built on: depending on how you count, the same term starts and ends in different weeks. Term 1 for Eastern division schools in 2027 runs from Thursday 28 January to Friday 9 April. By the international ISO standard that is week 4 to week 14. By the obvious home-made method, day of the year divided by seven, it ends in week 15. Worse, the first three days of 2027 are not in 2027 at all by ISO reckoning: Friday 1 January belongs to week 53 of 2026. NSW learners write the code, find every disagreement and learn why international software insists on one definition.

Signature project

Which week does each 2027 term start and end in?

The NSW Department of Education's published 2027 term dates, turned into week numbers three different ways. Our calculation, checked day by day.

NSW 2027 terms: ISO weeks against a home-made week count (our calculation)
Term (published dates)ISO weeksHome-made weeksWeekdays
Term 1, Eastern: Thursday 28 January to Friday 9 April4 to 144 to 1552
Term 1, Western: Thursday 4 February to Friday 9 April5 to 145 to 1547
Term 2: Tuesday 27 April to Friday 2 July17 to 2617 to 2749
Term 3: Monday 19 July to Friday 24 September29 to 3829 to 3950
Term 4: Monday 11 October to Monday 20 December41 to 5141 to 5151

1. Count it the obvious way

Take the day of the year, subtract one, divide by seven and add one. It is simple, and across 2027 it disagrees with the ISO week on 157 of the year's 365 days.

2. Ask the standard

ISO 8601 starts weeks on Monday and calls week 1 the week that contains the year's first Thursday. Python's isocalendar does exactly that, and it says Friday 1 January 2027 is in week 53 of 2026.

3. Try the shortcuts

Python's strftime offers two more week numbers. The Sunday-start %U disagrees with ISO on 54 days of 2027; the Monday-start %W on only 3, the first three days of January, which it calls week 0.

Weekdays are not school days

The weekday column counts Monday to Friday between the published first and last dates: 202 for the Eastern division across the four terms. It is not the number of days students attend. The department notes that its term dates include school development days, when students stay home, and public holidays fall inside some terms too. Learners have to say what their number counts before anyone uses it.

All week numbers and day counts on this page are our calculations from the department's published dates, made on 7 October 2026. The dates themselves are the department's; check its calendar page for any change before planning around them.

Dates in code

Five habits for any program that handles dates

Learned on NSW school terms, then used on rosters, bookings, payroll periods and anything that says "week" in its column heading.

How to stop week numbers from lying
HabitWhat it looks likeWhat it prevents
Name the definitionWrite "ISO week" or "school week", never just "week"Two teams meaning different things by week 15
Keep the year with the weekStore 2026-W53, not 53Filing 1 January 2027 under the wrong year
Use the library, then test itCall isocalendar, then check it on 1 to 4 JanuaryTrusting a function on the days it was not tried
Count what you meanSeparate weekdays, school days and calendar daysA plan built on days nobody attends
Re-read the sourceCheck the published dates again before relying on themPlanning against a calendar that has changed

The second habit catches the most bugs. A spreadsheet that stores only the number 53 cannot tell week 53 of 2026 from a week that does not exist in 2027, which has only 52 ISO weeks. Storing the year beside the week fixes it for good.

Coding and AI

An AI tool will number the weeks for you. Why should a NSW student learn to check it?

Because "week 15" and "week 14" can describe the same Friday, and a confident tool will not say which it means.

Ask an assistant which week of 2027 Term 1 ends in and you may get 14 or 15, delivered with equal confidence, because the question never said which definition of a week it meant. Ask it to write a week-number function and it may hand back day-of-year arithmetic that is right for most of the year and wrong on 157 days. The code runs. The numbers look reasonable. The bug only shows when someone compares two systems that counted differently.

A student who has lined up three week-numbering methods against the NSW terms knows the questions to ask: which definition, which year does the week belong to, and what happens on 1 January. Those questions apply to every generated answer that involves dates, money periods or time zones, and they are exactly the questions an HSC Software Engineering student is expected to ask of their own code.

So the reason for a New South Wales student to learn to code in 2026 is not to out-type an AI tool. It is to be the person in the room who asks which week, and why. The longer argument is in why teenagers should still learn to code in 2026.

New South Wales coding class questions

What NSW families ask us first.

How many people live in New South Wales?

The 2021 Census counted 8,072,163 people in New South Wales, with a median age of 39 against 38 for Australia.

What is an ISO week?

An ISO week is a week defined by the international standard ISO 8601: it starts on Monday, and week 1 of a year is the week containing that year's first Thursday. That is why Friday 1 January 2027 falls in ISO week 53 of 2026.

What is the NSW week-number project?

Learners turn the NSW Department of Education's 2027 term dates into week numbers three ways, ISO, Sunday-start and a home-made day-of-year count, and list every day they disagree. Across 2027 the home-made count disagrees with ISO on 157 days.

What is the HSC?

The Higher School Certificate is the credential NSW describes as the highest level of attainment its school students can achieve. NESA oversees it, and students complete at least 12 units of preliminary courses and 10 units of HSC courses, including English.

Do you help with HSC Software Engineering?

We teach the programming, design and testing technique the Software Engineering 11 to 12 syllabus draws on. The major software project is the student's own work under NESA rules, and we never write any assessed task.

Do you prepare students for the selective and OC tests?

We teach the maths and reasoning skills the Mathematical Reasoning and Thinking Skills sections draw on, as part of normal lessons. We do not give admissions advice and make no claims about selection.

What time are lessons for NSW learners?

Any weekday afternoon or evening, or on a weekend, agreed during the trial and always written in NSW time. India runs four and a half hours behind NSW in the cooler months and five and a half behind while daylight saving lasts.

Is there a Modern Age Coders centre in New South Wales?

No, and none is claimed. Every lesson is live and online, with no premises anywhere in Australia. A learner needs a laptop or desktop, working sound and a reliable connection, and the contact number shown is an Indian one.

What do coding classes cost in NSW?

The first lesson is free. After that a group place is USD 100 a month for two live lessons a week, around eight a month, with fifteen to twenty learners, or USD 150 a month for the same pattern one to one. Course, format and time are agreed before anything is paid.

How big are the groups?

Fifteen to twenty learners, matched on level, pace and what they want to build rather than on age or suburb. If no suitable group meets at a workable time, one-to-one lessons are offered instead.
