## Delhi has two CBSE regional offices, split by district

CBSE runs a Delhi East and a Delhi West regional office, and your school's district decides which one it reports to. We read this off the regional offices page on cbse.gov.in on 6 November 2026.

Very little, day to day. Your school deals with the office; you deal with the school. The school registers your child for the board exam with subject code 417, hands out the admit card, and the admit card names the exam centre, which may be a different school. If a CBSE notice ever says to contact the regional office, the table tells you which one.

## A worked Unit 1 case question, the way it is solved in the Monday class

Unit 1, the AI Project Cycle and Ethical Frameworks, carries 7 theory marks and is where long case answers often come from. This question is our own, set on a problem every Delhi school knows in winter, and it is solved the way we solve it live: plan, write, then mark against value points.

### Case question (4 marks)

A Delhi school wants an AI system that tells the PE teacher by 7:00 AM whether the morning's outdoor games period should move indoors because the air is too poor. (a) Write the problem statement using the 4Ws problem canvas. (b) Name two data features the team should collect and a source for each. (c) State one ethical concern and how the team should handle it.

### (a) The 4Ws, then one sentence
Who: students and the PE teacher. What: outdoor exercise on bad-air mornings can harm health, and today the call is a guess. Where: the school ground, early morning. Why: an early, consistent alert lets PE shift indoors in time. Statement: "Our students and PE teacher have a problem of deciding whether outdoor games are safe when the air is poor in the early morning. An ideal solution would warn the teacher by 7:00 AM so the period can move indoors."

### (b) Two features, two sources
Hourly PM2.5 or AQI readings, from the nearest public air-quality monitoring station or a sensor on the school roof. Wind speed and temperature for the morning, from a weather service forecast. Naming a source for each feature is what earns the mark; "data from the internet" does not.

### (c) Ethics, with a fix
Students with asthma have more at stake than anyone else, so a rights-based view says the system should lean towards caution: a false alarm only moves a games period, while a missed bad day can cause harm. The team should tune it to miss as few bad days as possible and tell parents how alerts are decided.

In class we mark this as 2 marks for a problem statement that covers all four Ws, 1 for two features with named sources
and 1 for an ethical concern paired with an action. That split is our own teaching rubric; the CBSE marking scheme sets the
value points for each year's paper. Notice that part (c) quietly sets up Unit 3: leaning towards caution means caring about
recall more than precision, which students meet again in
[the confusion matrix numericals](/confusion-matrix-class-10-ai-numericals). More questions of this
shape are on [the case based questions page](/cbse-class-10-ai-case-based-questions).

## A Delhi Class 10 week with AI tuition in it

The class is late on purpose. By 9:00 PM most Class 10 students in Delhi are home from school, sport and maths or science coaching, have eaten, and still have an hour of focus left. Here is how the week runs.

-
**Monday, 9:00 PM: one theory unit, then its questions** One part of a Part B unit is taught with examples, then attempted at once in the paper's shapes: one-mark objective items, a two-mark answer and a longer case. The teacher marks two or three answers on screen so everyone sees where marks fall away.

-
**Tuesday: a worksheet that takes about twenty minutes** A short worksheet on Monday's topic, built so it fits after school without eating the evening. It comes back marked in the next class.

-
**Wednesday, 9:00 PM: the practical half** A program for the file is written in Jupyter, run, broken on purpose and fixed, then explained aloud as if to an examiner. Some weeks it is an Orange workflow instead, or the next step of the project.

-
**Doubts in the class itself** Anyone can unmute and ask, or share their screen when code will not run. Doubts left over from the worksheet open the next class rather than waiting for a doubt day.

-
**From January: timed papers** Full mock papers are written at home under time, then gone through live, with every lost mark rewritten. The series is described on [the sample paper and mock tests page](/cbse-class-10-ai-sample-paper-and-mock-tests).

## The school runs the 50 practical marks; the preparation can happen at home

The practical half of 417 is conducted inside your child's school, on the school's dates, usually before the theory paper. Our job is to make sure the file, the project and the two vivas are ready before the school asks for them.

- Part C component | Marks | What the school does | How we prepare it

- Practical file | 15 | Checks at least 15 programs, often signed by the subject teacher | Built one program a week from the first Wednesday, each one run, explained and kept tidy

- Practical exam | 15 | Sets a task on the lab computers on the practical day | A full dress rehearsal in December, timed, on the same tools the school lab uses

- Viva on the practical | 5 | Asks about the programs and the practical units | Mock viva with follow-up questions, so a second "why?" does not end the answer

- Project | 10 | Looks at the project and its documentation | Idea fixed early, built in steps through the AI project cycle

- Project viva | 5 | Questions on what the student made and why | Each student explains their own project aloud at least twice before the real one

Before the practical, ask the school two things: which Python version and editor the lab computers use, and whether Orange is
installed there. Students practise on the same setup at home so nothing on the day is unfamiliar. The program list and the
project stages are set out on [the project and practical
file page](/cbse-class-10-ai-project-and-practical-file), and the oral questions examiners like are collected on [the
viva questions page](/cbse-class-10-ai-viva-questions).
