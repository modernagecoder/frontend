Does computer science count toward graduation in Wyoming?

Yes, as science. Wyoming Statute 21-2-304 requires three school years of science to graduate, one of which may be satisfied by one year of computer science, and W.S. 21-9-101 lists computer science in every district’s common core of knowledge. In 2024 to 2025, 59 of 92 Wyoming high schools offered computer science, according to Code.org (2025). We teach Wyoming students live online in Mountain Time. Modern Age Coders teaches this live online for US students, in a batch or one to one, and the first class is free.

Wyoming counted 92 high schools in the Code.org 2024 to 2025 count, and its law on computer science is short and specific. Computer science appears by name in the common core of knowledge every district must teach toward, computational thinking appears in the common core of skills, and a year of computer science can stand in for a year of science on the way to a diploma. Below are those statutes in the Legislature’s wording, the Wyoming access and enrollment series, and a project on the City of Green River’s own tree inventory that teaches one of the most useful ideas in statistics: why the average is often not the typical value.

The statute

Computer science in Wyoming law, in three places

We could not read the Wyoming Department of Education website, which returned an access error, so this section quotes Title 21 of the Wyoming Statutes as published by the Wyoming Legislature, read on 18 October 2026.

Where computer science appears in Title 21 of the Wyoming Statutes
SectionWhat it says
W.S. 21-2-304, graduation standardsFour school years of English, three of mathematics, three of science and three of social studies; one of the science years may be satisfied by one year of computer science
W.S. 21-2-304, evidenceCompletion shown by passing grades or by successful performance on competency-based equivalency examinations
W.S. 21-9-101(b)(i), common core of knowledgeDistricts provide programs to meet standards in a list of areas that includes, as item (O), Computer science
W.S. 21-9-101(b)(iii), common core of skillsThe skills list includes computational thinking and computer applications, beside problem solving and critical thinking
W.S. 21-9-101(b)(ii), grades one to eightReading, writing and mathematics are to be emphasized within the common core in grades one through eight

A substitution, not an addition

The graduation rule does not add a fourth subject. It allows one of the three science years to be a computer science year, so a student who takes it still meets the science count.

Every district, every level

Because computer science sits in the common core of knowledge, every Wyoming district has to provide programs that meet state standards in it, not only districts with large high schools.

Skills as well as content

Listing computational thinking among the core skills means the idea is meant to appear across subjects, from a math word problem to a science investigation.

We quote the statute text only. How the state board’s rules and each district’s course catalog apply it is a question for the school counselor, and we do not describe any specific Wyoming course list on this page.

Signature project

How big is a typical Green River tree? Mean versus median

The City of Green River publishes its tree inventory as a public map layer, with a trunk diameter for almost every tree. Students compute an average two ways and discover that the answers disagree for a good reason.

Why the mean is higher

A handful of very large trunks pull the mean up, while the median only asks which value sits in the middle. Across all kept trees the mean is 10.47 and the median 8, a sign of a long right tail.

The Populus lesson

Picea and Populus share a mean of 15.3, yet their medians are 16 and 10. The largest Populus trunk measures 52. Same average, very different typical tree: the median exposes it.

Read the labels too

The layer also carries a text band, DBH_Range, whose commonest value is 6-12in with 709 records, archived ones included. Comparing the band with the number is a quick check that the two fields agree.

In Python the comparison is two lines, statistics.mean(dbh) and statistics.median(dbh), after filtering to rows where Archived is No and DBH is not empty. Students then plot a histogram and mark both values on it, which makes the skew visible at a glance. Removing the largest 5% drops the mean from 10.47 to 9.28 while the median barely moves, the clearest demonstration that a median resists outliers.

The success curriculum

Computer science in the curriculum for state scholarship eligibility

Wyoming law sets a separate success curriculum that students follow to qualify for honor or performance scholarship eligibility, in W.S. 21-16-1307, for students graduating from the 2019 to 2020 school year onward.

Where computer science appears in the W.S. 21-16-1307 success curriculum
AreaWhat the statute allows
Math, four yearsAlgebra I, Algebra II, Geometry, and an additional math or computer science course taken in grades nine through twelve
Science, four yearsCourses from a list that includes Computer science I, alongside physics, chemistry, biology and geology
No double countingA computer science course cannot satisfy the math requirement if it is used to satisfy one year of science

For a student who enjoys computer science, these rules mean a computer science course can earn its place in either the math or the science column, but not both at once. We quote the curriculum rules only; scholarship amounts and eligibility decisions belong to the state and the school, and we make no promise about either.

Planning the four years

Our suggestion, and only that: decide early which column a computer science year will fill, then plan the rest of the math and science sequence around it with the counselor, so that no year is wasted on a course that cannot count twice.

Class times

Mountain Time across Wyoming

The IANA time zone database places Wyoming in its US Mountain list, kept under the America/Denver zone.

After school in Cheyenne

A 3:30 p.m. class in Cheyenne begins at 3:00 a.m. the next day in India during daylight saving, and at 4:00 a.m. after the clocks fall back.

Hours apart

India keeps UTC plus 5:30 all year. Wyoming moves between UTC minus 6 and minus 7, so India is 11 hours 30 minutes or 12 hours 30 minutes ahead.

This year’s dates

Wyoming clocks change on Sunday, November 1, 2026 and Sunday, March 14, 2027, under the US rule in the IANA data. We confirm times in Mountain Time before both.

An outlier in the calendar

The day the clocks fall back has 25 hours, an outlier in any table of daily totals. Students who compute hours per day with the America/Denver zone see it appear in their own output, and learn why time-aware code matters.

Coding and AI

Ask an AI for the average Green River tree. Which average will it give?

Usually the mean, and usually without a word about the skew.

An AI assistant asked for the typical trunk size in the Green River data will most likely compute the mean, 10.47, and present it as the answer. It will not necessarily mention that the median is 8, or that a few giant cottonwoods and elms pull the mean up. The number is correct; the description of it may not be.

Students who have computed both by hand know to ask which average was used and why. That is how we teach AI in every class: let the tool do the arithmetic quickly, and keep the judgement about what the arithmetic means firmly with the student.

Wyoming already counts computational thinking among its core skills; asking what an average hides is that skill in action. The longer argument is in why coding is still worth learning in 2026.

FAQ

Wyoming coding and AI questions

Answers from Wyoming statute, the published data and how our classes run.

Can computer science count toward graduation in Wyoming?

Yes. W.S. 21-2-304 requires three school years of science, and one of those years may be satisfied by one year of computer science.

Is computer science part of the Wyoming common core?

Yes. W.S. 21-9-101 lists computer science in the common core of knowledge and computational thinking and computer applications in the common core of skills.

How many years of each subject does Wyoming require to graduate?

The statute lists four school years of English, three of mathematics, three of science and three of social studies.

Does computer science count in the scholarship success curriculum?

W.S. 21-16-1307 allows an additional math or computer science course in the math years and lists Computer science I among science courses, but one course cannot count for both.

How many Wyoming high schools offer computer science?

Code.org (2025) reports 59 of 92 Wyoming high schools, or 64.1%, offering computer science in 2024 to 2025.

How many Wyoming students take computer science?

The same data reports 1,938 of 29,151 high school students, or 6.65%, enrolled in 2024 to 2025, against 6.1% nationally.

Do your classes count as the Wyoming computer science year?

We make no such claim. Schools and districts decide credit. What we add is practice and projects outside the school day.

What does the Green River project teach?

Mean versus median. Students find a mean trunk diameter of 10.47 and a median of 8 among 2,029 trees, then show how outliers pull the mean.

What time zone are Wyoming classes in?

Mountain Time. We confirm every time in Mountain Time around November 1, 2026 and March 14, 2027.

When do classes run?

Weekdays or weekends, flexible: choose your batch, or for a very personalised plan choose 1-on-1 at your convenience, weekend mornings or weekdays.

Do you have a Wyoming center?

No. Every class is live online from India, with no Wyoming premises, and none is claimed.
