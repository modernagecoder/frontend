Q2. Units 1 and 2 of Part A

Communication skills class 10 MCQ and self management skills MCQ, with answers

Questions 1 to 18, with the answer and reason under each.

Communication Skills-II

  1. In the communication cycle, the receiver turning a message back into meaning is called
    (a) encoding
    (b) decoding
    (c) feedback
    (d) the channel
    Answer (b) The sender encodes; the receiver decodes. Feedback is the reply that comes after decoding.
  2. A class teacher writes "Project due Friday" on the notice board. In the cycle, the notice board is the
    (a) sender
    (b) receiver
    (c) channel or medium
    (d) feedback
    Answer (c) It carries the message from teacher to students. The teacher is the sender, the students are the receivers.
  3. A listener gives a thumbs up to show she has understood. In the cycle this is
    (a) noise
    (b) encoding
    (c) feedback
    (d) a barrier
    Answer (c) It is a response sent back to the speaker, here without words. Being non-verbal does not stop it being feedback.
  4. A video call keeps freezing because the mobile signal is weak. Which barrier is this?
    (a) physical
    (b) linguistic
    (c) interpersonal
    (d) cultural
    Answer (a) The problem lies in the medium and surroundings, not in language, attitude or customs.
  5. Two students argue and then refuse to listen to each other's ideas in the group project. The barrier is
    (a) physical
    (b) linguistic
    (c) interpersonal
    (d) organisational
    Answer (c) It comes from the relationship and attitude between the people. Nothing is wrong with the language or the channel.
  6. Kabir writes a 500-word message just to say the meeting has moved to 4 PM. Which of the 7Cs has he broken?
    (a) courteous
    (b) concise
    (c) correct
    (d) concrete
    Answer (b) Concise means using only the words needed. The message may still be correct and polite.
  7. Which of these is NOT one of the 7Cs of effective communication?
    (a) clear
    (b) complete
    (c) complicated
    (d) coherent
    Answer (c) The seven are clear, concise, concrete, correct, coherent, complete and courteous. Complicated is the opposite of the aim.
  8. A road sign showing a person walking on stripes is an example of
    (a) verbal communication
    (b) visual communication
    (c) written communication
    (d) oral communication
    Answer (b) It sends the message through a picture. Gestures and posture would be non-verbal; a sign is visual.
  9. Which feedback is the most useful to a classmate who presented?
    (a) "It was fine."
    (b) "Good job."
    (c) "Your chart on slide 3 made the trend clear; speak a little slower at the start."
    (d) "I did not like it."
    Answer (c) Good feedback is specific and points to what to keep and what to change. The rest are too general to act on.

Self-Management Skills-II

  1. In the ABC model used for managing stress, B stands for
    (a) behaviour
    (b) beliefs
    (c) balance
    (d) breathing
    Answer (b) Adversity, Beliefs, Consequences: what we believe about a setback shapes how stressed we feel.
  2. Which is a healthy way to manage stress before the board exam?
    (a) cutting sleep to study more
    (b) regular exercise, yoga or a walk
    (c) avoiding all friends
    (d) more cups of tea at midnight
    Answer (b) Physical activity, rest and talking to people lower stress. The others raise it.
  3. Self-awareness mainly means
    (a) knowing your strengths and weaknesses
    (b) caring what others think
    (c) being shy
    (d) memorising facts about yourself
    Answer (a) It is an honest view of what you are good at and where you need help, the starting point for any plan.
  4. Riya practises sketching every evening because she enjoys it, with no prize in sight. Her motivation is
    (a) external
    (b) internal
    (c) forced
    (d) financial
    Answer (b) It comes from inside, from interest and enjoyment. A reward or praise would make it external.
  5. Aman studies only because his parents promised him a new bicycle for good marks. This is
    (a) internal motivation
    (b) external motivation
    (c) self-awareness
    (d) stress
    Answer (b) The push comes from an outside reward. Both kinds work; the paper asks you to name which one is shown.
  6. Which goal is written the SMART way?
    (a) "I will do better in AI."
    (b) "I will finish Unit 3 notes and 20 MCQs by Sunday 6 PM."
    (c) "I will become the topper."
    (d) "I will study a lot."
    Answer (b) It is specific, can be counted, is realistic, fits the exam and has a deadline. The others are wishes.
  7. In a SMART goal, the letter T stands for
    (a) tough
    (b) time-bound
    (c) targeted
    (d) tested
    Answer (b) The goal needs a deadline. "Targeted" sounds right but is not the word used.
  8. In time management, deciding which task matters most and doing it first is called
    (a) organising
    (b) prioritising
    (c) tracking
    (d) delegating
    Answer (b) Organising lists the tasks; prioritising puts them in order of importance; tracking checks progress afterwards.
  9. Setting your own goals and managing your own time, without being told, is part of
    (a) self-regulation
    (b) stress
    (c) external motivation
    (d) a communication barrier
    Answer (a) Self-regulation is controlling your own actions to reach a goal, and goal setting with time management is how it is done.

Q3. Units 3 and 4 of Part A

ICT skills class 10 MCQ and entrepreneurial skills MCQ

Questions 19 to 36. ICT items turn on naming the right threat; entrepreneurship items on spotting a myth.

ICT Skills-II

  1. The software that manages the hardware and lets other programs run is the
    (a) word processor
    (b) operating system
    (c) web browser
    (d) antivirus
    Answer (b) Every other program runs on top of the operating system, which handles memory, files and devices.
  2. Which of these is an operating system?
    (a) Ubuntu Linux
    (b) Microsoft Excel
    (c) Google Chrome
    (d) VLC media player
    Answer (a) The others are application programs that need an operating system underneath them.
  3. An interface where you click icons, windows and menus with a mouse is a
    (a) command line interface
    (b) graphical user interface
    (c) voice interface
    (d) network interface
    Answer (b) GUI means pictures and pointing. A command line interface needs typed instructions.
  4. A free game download secretly lets an attacker control the computer. This malware is a
    (a) worm
    (b) Trojan horse
    (c) cookie
    (d) firewall
    Answer (b) A Trojan hides inside something that looks useful. A worm spreads by itself across a network.
  5. The main job of a firewall is to
    (a) speed up the processor
    (b) watch network traffic and block what is not allowed
    (c) clean the keyboard
    (d) store backups
    Answer (b) It filters what comes in and goes out over the network. It does not remove files already infected.
  6. Which password is the strongest?
    (a) password123
    (b) Riya2009
    (c) T7#qv!m29Lz
    (d) 12345678
    Answer (c) Long, mixed letters, numbers and symbols, and no name or birth year that someone could guess.
  7. A website address starting with https and a padlock icon means
    (a) the site is free
    (b) data sent to the site is encrypted
    (c) the site has no ads
    (d) the site cannot be hacked
    Answer (b) The connection is secure. It does not guarantee the site itself is honest, so (d) overclaims.
  8. Keeping a regular backup of your files mainly protects against
    (a) slow internet
    (b) losing data if the disk fails or files are deleted
    (c) spam email
    (d) screen glare
    Answer (b) A copy kept elsewhere lets you restore lost work.
  9. An email that looks like it is from your bank asks you to reply with your OTP. This is
    (a) phishing
    (b) a backup
    (c) a software update
    (d) encryption
    Answer (a) It tricks you into giving away private details. A real bank never asks for an OTP by email.

Entrepreneurial Skills-II

  1. An entrepreneur is best described as a person who
    (a) works only for a fixed salary
    (b) turns an idea into a business and takes the risk
    (c) avoids all decisions
    (d) only buys shares
    Answer (b) Owning the risk of a new venture is what separates an entrepreneur from an employee.
  2. Which statement is a myth about entrepreneurship?
    (a) Entrepreneurs solve problems people have
    (b) Entrepreneurs are born, not made
    (c) A business can start small
    (d) Entrepreneurs take some risk
    Answer (b) The skills can be learned and practised. The other three are true.
  3. Which of these is a fact, not a myth?
    (a) You need a lot of money to start any business
    (b) Only big companies are enterprises
    (c) Many businesses begin with little money and grow slowly
    (d) Every idea must be completely new
    Answer (c) The rest are common myths: a small shop or service run by one person is an enterprise too.
  4. How do entrepreneurs help society?
    (a) they create jobs and meet people's needs
    (b) they raise unemployment
    (c) they reduce choice for buyers
    (d) they stop new products
    Answer (a) New businesses employ people, pay taxes and bring goods and services to places that lacked them.
  5. Deciding what to sell, at what price and to whom is the entrepreneur's function of
    (a) decision making
    (b) printing
    (c) auditing
    (d) voting
    Answer (a) An entrepreneur makes the key business decisions, alongside managing and taking risk.
  6. A tailor notices customers want masks that match their clothes and starts making them. The quality shown is
    (a) laziness
    (b) creativity and spotting a need
    (c) fear of risk
    (d) following the crowd
    Answer (b) She saw a new demand and found a fresh way to meet it, which is the heart of innovation.
  7. After his first food stall closed, Imran learned from it and opened a better one. This shows
    (a) perseverance
    (b) dependence
    (c) impatience
    (d) carelessness
    Answer (a) Keeping at it after failure and learning from it is a mark of a successful entrepreneur.
  8. Which usually comes first when starting an enterprise?
    (a) opening branches in other cities
    (b) identifying a need or a business idea
    (c) hiring fifty staff
    (d) paying yearly profits
    Answer (b) Every enterprise starts from an idea that meets a need. Growth comes much later.
  9. An electrician runs her own repair service and decides her own hours. She is
    (a) in wage employment
    (b) self-employed
    (c) unemployed
    (d) a government employee
    Answer (b) She works for herself and takes the profit or loss. Wage employment means working for someone else for pay.

Q4. Unit 5 of Part A

Green skills class 10 MCQ, including the SDG questions

Questions 37 to 45. Most marks here go to knowing the goals by number and title, and to telling a renewable resource from one that runs out.

  1. The best-known definition of sustainable development comes from the 1987 report "Our Common Future", usually called the
    (a) Kyoto report
    (b) Brundtland report
    (c) Paris report
    (d) Rio report
    Answer (b) It was named after Gro Harlem Brundtland, who chaired the commission. Kyoto and Paris are later climate agreements.
  2. How many Sustainable Development Goals did the United Nations set?
    (a) 8
    (b) 12
    (c) 17
    (d) 20
    Answer (c) Seventeen. Eight was the count of the older Millennium Development Goals, which is why it tempts.
  3. The Sustainable Development Goals are meant to be achieved by
    (a) 2025
    (b) 2030
    (c) 2040
    (d) 2050
    Answer (b) They are often called the 2030 Agenda for that reason.
  4. SDG 6 is
    (a) Quality Education
    (b) Clean Water and Sanitation
    (c) Climate Action
    (d) Zero Hunger
    Answer (b) Quality Education is 4, Zero Hunger is 2 and Climate Action is 13.
  5. A school canteen weighs its leftover food each week and cuts the waste in half. This supports
    (a) SDG 12, Responsible Consumption and Production
    (b) SDG 9, Industry and Infrastructure
    (c) SDG 16, Peace and Justice
    (d) SDG 17, Partnerships
    Answer (a) Less waste for the same use is exactly what Goal 12 asks for.
  1. Which of these is a renewable source of energy?
    (a) coal
    (b) diesel
    (c) sunlight
    (d) natural gas
    Answer (c) Solar energy is replaced naturally every day. The other three are fossil fuels that run out.
  2. Pumping far more groundwater than rain puts back leads mainly to
    (a) a falling water table
    (b) more forests
    (c) cleaner air
    (d) more fish in rivers
    Answer (a) Wells dry up when water is taken faster than it is replaced, a core problem sustainable development tries to fix.
  3. Carrying a cloth bag so that you take no plastic bag from the shop is an example of
    (a) reduce
    (b) recycle
    (c) dispose
    (d) burn
    Answer (a) You avoid creating the waste at all. Recycling would treat the plastic bag after use.
  4. Which of these is a green job?
    (a) solar panel technician
    (b) coal mine blaster
    (c) plastic bag maker
    (d) diesel generator seller
    Answer (a) Green jobs protect or restore the environment, such as clean energy, water saving and waste management work.

Questions about Part A MCQs

What students ask about employability skills class 10 MCQs

Where can I get employability skills class 10 MCQ with answers?

This page has 45 original MCQs for CBSE Class 10, nine from each Part A unit: Communication, Self-Management, ICT, Entrepreneurial and Green Skills. Each has four options, the answer and a short reason that explains why the tempting wrong option fails. They are written for the 2026-27 AI 417 paper.

How many marks is employability skills in class 10 AI?

Ten of the fifty theory marks, two for each of the five units. In the 2025-26 sample paper this came as six one-mark objective items, of which any four were answered, and five two-mark short answers, of which any three were answered. The 2026-27 sample paper for 417 has not been published yet.

Is there negative marking in employability skills MCQs?

No. The 2025-26 sample paper for 417 has no negative marking, so attempt every item you choose and use elimination when unsure. Read the instructions on the day about how many Part A items to answer, because answering extra ones does not add marks.

Are employability skills MCQs the same for AI 417 and IT 402?

The Part A units and the Class 10 employability skills book are shared by CBSE skill subjects, so the topics match and the same practice helps. Each subject has its own question paper, though, so the exact items differ. This page is set out for the AI 417 paper.

What are the most asked communication skills class 10 MCQ topics?

The steps of the communication cycle (sender, encoding, channel, receiver, decoding, feedback), the types of barrier (physical, linguistic, interpersonal, organisational, cultural), the 7Cs of effective communication, and telling verbal, non-verbal and visual communication apart. Questions 1 to 9 above cover each of these.

What is asked in self management skills MCQ for class 10?

Stress and how to manage it, including the ABC model (Adversity, Beliefs, Consequences), self-awareness of strengths and weaknesses, internal against external motivation, SMART goals, and the steps of time management such as organising and prioritising. Questions 10 to 18 practise each one.

What ICT skills class 10 MCQ topics should I revise?

What an operating system does and examples of one, graphical and command line interfaces, care of a computer, kinds of malware such as viruses, worms and Trojans, firewalls, strong passwords, https and the padlock, backups and phishing. Most items ask you to name the threat or the protection.

How many Sustainable Development Goals are there for green skills class 10?

Seventeen, set by the United Nations to be reached by 2030. Green skills MCQs often name a goal by number, so learn at least Goal 4 Quality Education, Goal 6 Clean Water and Sanitation, Goal 7 Affordable and Clean Energy, Goal 12 Responsible Consumption and Production and Goal 13 Climate Action.

What are common entrepreneurial skills MCQ questions?

Who an entrepreneur is, qualities such as risk-taking, creativity and perseverance, the functions of an entrepreneur, myths such as "entrepreneurs are born, not made", the role of entrepreneurs in society, and wage employment against self-employment. Many items tell a short story and ask which quality it shows.
