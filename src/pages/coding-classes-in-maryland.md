# Coding Classes in Maryland

> Under the Blueprint for Maryland's Future, students are expected to meet the College and Career Readiness standard by the end of tenth grade, with reevaluation in eleventh and twelfth. Algebra 1 appears in more than one route to meeting it. Modern Age Coders teaches live online coding and math to ages 6 to 67 in Maryland, in groups of five to eight or one to one, from USD 100 a month. The first class is free.

## Why does tenth grade matter so much in Maryland?

Most states settle the question in junior and senior year. Maryland moved it two years earlier.

The College and Career Readiness standard exists to establish that a graduate has the knowledge and skills to complete entry-level credit-bearing college courses and to work in high-wage, high-demand industries. Students are expected to meet it **by the end of tenth grade**, and can be reevaluated in eleventh and twelfth.

If the checkpoint lands at the end of tenth grade, then middle school and ninth grade are the years that matter, and eleventh grade is late rather than early. Almost nothing about how families plan has caught up with that.

## What are the routes to meeting the CCR standard?

There is no single test. There are three groups of routes, and a student needs only one.

| Route | What it requires |
|---|---|
| Option 1, academic benchmarks | Cumulative unweighted GPA of 3.0 at the end of tenth or eleventh grade, and a final grade of A, B or C in Algebra 1, or Performance Level 3 or 4 on the Algebra 1 MCAP |
| Option 2, standardized test benchmarks | Level 3 or 4 on the ELA 10 MCAP, together with a qualifying Algebra MCAP level or a minimum SAT math score of 520 |
| Option 3, career connected benchmarks, beginning 2025 to 2026 | Nine further measures, including a one-year GPA of 3.0 or above in eleventh or twelfth with a C or better in math, two dual enrollment courses, AP scores of 3 or above, IB scores of 4 or above, ACT WorkKeys at Silver or above, state-approved industry credentials, registered apprenticeships, or an ASVAB score of 50 or above |

A little more than 50 per cent of students met the standard at the conclusion of the 2024 to 2025 school year.

Sources: [Howard County Public School System on the CCR standard](https://www.hcpss.org/college-career-readiness/ccr-standard/), the [Maryland State Board CCR Standard policy document, March 2025](https://marylandpublicschools.org/stateboard/Documents/CCR-Standard-Policy-Adopted-03-2025-A.pdf), and [MSDE on the American Institutes for Research empirical study](https://news.maryland.gov/msde/empirical-ccr-standard/). Verified 17 August 2026.

## Why is Algebra 1 the most actionable detail here?

Read the routes for what they have in common and one subject keeps appearing.

Option 1 asks for A, B or C in Algebra 1, or Performance Level 3 or 4 on the Algebra 1 MCAP. Option 2 runs through ELA 10 MCAP together with a qualifying Algebra MCAP level, or SAT math of 520 or better. Option 3's first career connected measure asks for a C or better in math alongside the GPA.

**Whichever door a Maryland student goes through, algebra is usually standing in it.**

That is the reason we do not sell coding and mathematics as two separate products. A learner who is shaky on algebra and enthusiastic about coding is common, and the coding is often the best available route back into the algebra, because a variable stops being abstract the moment a program depends on it.

| Stage | What we would do |
|---|---|
| Elementary | Ordering, counting, predicting what a thing will do before running it. No syntax, and the best foundation for algebra later |
| Middle school | The strongest window in Maryland, because it sits before the checkpoint. Blocks into Python, algebra made concrete |
| Ninth and tenth grade | The checkpoint years. Narrow the goal, protect the algebra grade, treat anything optional as optional |
| Eleventh and twelfth | Reevaluation is possible, so it is not over. One lesson a week on something specific, often a portfolio |

**What we will not claim:** nothing here awards Maryland credit, appears on a transcript, or counts toward the CCR standard by itself.

## The Maryland student data project

Every market page here works through one analytical trap. Maryland's is provable in about ten lines of code, which is what makes it a good lesson: **a rate that rises when you add a door.**

The learner is given the three option groups and a headline: the share of Maryland students meeting the CCR standard went up. Did students get better?

Then they write the checker. A function that takes a student record and returns true if any route is satisfied. Option 1 or Option 2 or any of the nine in Option 3.

**Here is the part a learner can prove rather than be told.** Take any set of student records. Run the checker. Now add one more route to the OR and run it again. The set of students who pass either stays the same or gets bigger. It cannot shrink. Not for this data, not for any data, ever.

So a rising readiness rate has at least two causes the number cannot distinguish: students improved, or a door was added. Maryland's own analysis is explicit that adding a high school GPA route *increased* the percentage meeting the standard, and Option 3 adds nine more measures from 2025 to 2026.

None of that makes the policy wrong. The extra routes exist because a single test was a poor measure of a real thing. It makes the *headline* a poor instrument for the question people ask of it.

The fix is boring and it is the whole point: report the rate alongside the definition and the date the definition last changed, then re-run the old definition on the new data to compare like with like. That produces a much less quotable and much more honest number.

Every student record in the exercise is synthetic and generated by the learner. The option groups and thresholds are Maryland's, cited above.

## Will AI replace programmers before my child graduates?

Ask an AI whether Maryland students got better and it will tell you the readiness rate rose, correctly, with a source. It will very likely not tell you the definition acquired new routes in the same period, because that requires knowing to ask, and the question as posed did not ask.

The work that survives is exactly that: knowing which question to put to a number, noticing a definition changed, and being unwilling to publish a comparison that is not like for like. A learner who has watched their own pass rate jump because they added a clause to an `or`, and understood why, has learned something they will use on every dashboard they are ever shown.

- **Weekday slots are agreed case by case**, depending on the week and the group

- If the only slot that works for your family is one we cannot cover, you will hear that instead of a booking

Otherwise: groups of **five to eight** or **one to one**, **twice a week** for normally eight lessons a month, and a laptop or desktop with stable internet, working audio and a current browser.
