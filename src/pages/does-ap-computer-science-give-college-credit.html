Does AP Computer Science give college credit?

Often, yes, but never automatically, and not in the same way everywhere. Each college decides for itself whether a score on AP Computer Science A or AP Computer Science Principles earns credit, earns placement, earns both, or earns nothing at all. College Board says so, and the three college policies further down this page prove it: the same score of 4 is worth three credit hours at one university, four at another, and for one of the two exams nothing at a third. This page sets out the official recommendation, what those three colleges actually publish for 2026, and how to find the rule for the college your teen is aiming at.

In short

AP Computer Science can earn college credit, but each college sets its own rule. The American Council on Education and College Board recommend credit for a score of 3 or higher; their table suggests 4 credits for AP Computer Science A and 3 for AP Computer Science Principles. Real policies vary: for 2026, Case Western gives 3 credit hours for a 4 or 5 on CSA and none for CSP.

The short answer, sourced

Credit, placement, or both: what College Board actually says

College Board runs the exams, but it does not award college credit. Its own pages are careful about that distinction.

What College Board says about credit and placement (apstudents.collegeboard.org, read 17 October 2026)
PointIn College Board’s words
How common it is"Most U.S. colleges offer credit or advanced placement, or both, for qualifying AP scores."
What credit is for"You’ll usually need 120 credits to get a bachelor’s degree."
What placement meansSkipping courses whose material your AP score shows you already know, so you can go straight to more advanced ones
The search tool"Use this tool to find colleges that offer credit or placement for AP scores."
Changing colleges"There’s no way to transfer credits directly from one college to another." You send your scores again to the new college.

Credit and placement are different

Credit counts towards the total a degree needs. Placement lets you skip a course and start higher. A college might give one, the other, or both for the same score, and computer science departments often care more about placement than about the credit hours.

For a future computer science major, skipping an introductory programming course can matter more than three credits of general electives. For anyone else, the credits may matter more.

What each AP course is designed to match

The AP Computer Science A Course and Exam Description says the course is "equivalent to an introductory college course in computer science", taught in Java.

College Board’s AP CSP FAQ describes Principles as designed to be equivalent to "a first-semester introductory college computing course for nonmajors". That one word, nonmajors, explains a lot of the policies below.

Sources, read 17 October 2026: College Board’s Getting Credit and Placement, AP Credit Policy Search and the AP CSP college credit FAQ. We are not connected with College Board or any college named here.

The recommendation

What ACE and College Board recommend for each exam

College Board publishes credit-granting recommendations drawn from the American Council on Education’s review of AP. They are recommendations to colleges, not rules.

The general rule behind the table

AP Central puts it in one sentence: "The American Council on Education (ACE) and College Board recommend that colleges and universities award credit for AP scores of 3 or higher on any AP Exam."

So the recommended floor is a 3 for both computer science exams. In practice, as the next section shows, selective colleges often set the bar at a 4.

How widely CSP is recognised

For AP Computer Science Principles, AP Central says: "More than 1,300 colleges and universities offer credit, advanced placement, or both for qualifying scores on the AP Computer Science Principles Exam."

It also reports that academic leaders from over 100 colleges reviewed the course framework, and that 88% believed it is a college-level computing course, 86% would award credit, and 70% would offer a comparable course.

Sources: AP Credit-Granting Recommendations and AP CSP Higher Education Credit and Placement, AP Central, read 17 October 2026.

Three real policies

The same exam, three colleges, three different answers

We read each college’s own published AP table on 17 October 2026. They are examples, not a ranking, chosen because each page was readable in full and states its year.

Reading the three side by side (our reading)

A score of 3 on AP Computer Science A earns credit at Cincinnati and nothing at the other two. A 4 earns 3 credit hours at Case Western, 4 credits at Miami and 3 at Cincinnati. Only Cincinnati gives more for a 5 than for a 4, adding a second course.

AP Computer Science Principles splits further: no credit at Case Western, the same 4 credits as CSA at Miami for a 4 or 5, and credit from a 3 at Cincinnati. No single number answers "does AP CSP give college credit?"

Why Cincinnati starts at 3

Cincinnati’s page quotes State of Ohio policy that students who score 3 or above "will be awarded the aligned course(s) and credits" for the exam area, adding that more may be available for a 4 or 5.

That is a state rule for Ohio’s public colleges, not a national one. Other states have their own policies, and private colleges set theirs independently.

The small print

Conditions that come with AP credit

The tables are only half of each policy. The paragraphs around them carry conditions that can change what a score is worth.

Conditions stated on the three college pages (read 17 October 2026)
CollegeCondition
Case Western ReserveCredit and exemption only for AP exams taken before matriculation; if a student later takes the course they got AP credit for, the AP credit for it is forfeited
Case Western ReservePolicies are reviewed annually by departments and are subject to change

University of MiamiThe table applies only to students admitted for fall 2026 and spring 2027

Credit you might decline

Cincinnati’s advice about STEM sequences is worth taking seriously. A student who skips the first programming course and then struggles in the second has gained three credits and lost a foundation.

Some students accept the credit but take the introductory course anyway. At a college like Case Western, that choice forfeits the AP credit for that course, so ask before enrolling.

Scores have to be sent

Credit only appears once the college receives an official score report. College Board’s credit page lists June 20, 2027, at 11:59 PM ET as the deadline to choose a free score-send recipient for 2027 scores, and says "You can send one score report for free every year that you take AP Exams."

Scores sent later can still count, but usually for a fee. Miami’s end-of-first-semester deadline shows why it is worth doing on time.

Your own college

How to find the rule for the college you care about

This is the method we give families. It takes about ten minutes per college.

  1. Start with College Board’s search. The AP Credit Policy Search on apstudents.collegeboard.org lists colleges that offer credit or placement for AP scores. Use it to find the policy page.
  2. Open the college’s own page. College Board warns that colleges publish policies on "their own often varying schedules", so the college’s page is the authority, not a summary.
  3. Check the year. Look for the entering class or academic year the table applies to, as Miami and Case Western both state.
  4. Find both rows. Computer Science A and Computer Science Principles are listed separately, often with different minimum scores and credit.
  5. Read the conditions. Deadlines for scores, rules about retaking a course, and limits on total credit sit in the paragraphs around the table.
  6. Ask the department if you plan to major. For a computer science major, placement into the right first course matters more than credit hours. If the page is unclear, email the department.

Questions worth asking a college (ours)

Does a 3, 4 or 5 on AP Computer Science A place me out of your first programming course for majors, or only give elective credit?

Does AP Computer Science Principles count towards a general education requirement, the major, or neither?

If the policy says no

A college that gives no credit for an exam may still value the course on a transcript. Credit policy and admission are separate questions, and the credit table says nothing about how admission readers see a course.

The skills carry over regardless. A student who has really learned Java in AP Computer Science A starts college programming ahead, credit or not.

A common follow-up

Is AP Computer Science a math credit?

This question is usually about high school, not college: can AP Computer Science count towards a high school math graduation requirement?

Who decides

That is set by each state and often by each school district, not by College Board. The College Board credit pages we read are about college credit and placement and do not answer it.

So we will not give a blanket answer here. Ask your school counselor how your district counts AP Computer Science A and AP Computer Science Principles towards graduation.

AP Computer Science credit: common questions

Does AP Computer Science A give college credit?

At many colleges, yes, but each college sets its own rule. ACE and College Board recommend credit for a score of 3 or higher, with 4 credits for an Introduction to Java Programming course. Some colleges require a 4 or 5.

Does AP Computer Science Principles give college credit?

College Board says more than 1,300 colleges and universities offer credit, placement or both for qualifying CSP scores, and recommends 3 credits for a 3 or higher. Some colleges, such as Case Western Reserve for 2026-27, give no credit for it.

What score do I need on AP Computer Science for credit?

The ACE and College Board recommendation is a 3 or higher. Colleges set their own minimums: of the three policies on this page, one starts at 3 and two start at 4.

Is AP Computer Science A or Principles worth more credit?

It depends on the college. The ACE and College Board table recommends 4 credits for CSA and 3 for CSP, but one college on this page gives both the same 4 credits and another gives nothing for CSP.

What is the difference between credit and placement?

Credit counts towards the total your degree needs, usually 120 credits for a bachelor's degree according to College Board. Placement lets you skip a course and start at a higher level.

How do I find a college's AP Computer Science credit policy?

Use the AP Credit Policy Search on College Board's student site to find it, then read the policy on the college's own website, checking the year it applies to.

Is AP Computer Science a math credit in high school?

That is decided by each state and often each school district, not by College Board. Ask your school counselor how AP Computer Science counts towards graduation.

How do I send my AP scores to a college for free?

College Board lists June 20, 2027, at 11:59 PM ET as the deadline to choose your free score-send recipient for 2027 scores. You get one free score report each year you take AP Exams.
