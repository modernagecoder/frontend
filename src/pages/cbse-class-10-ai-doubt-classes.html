Q2. What kind of doubts come up?

AI 417 doubt solving: eight theory doubts, answered in full

These are written the way students ask them, and answered the way a teacher answers them live: the direct answer first, then the reason, then the habit that stops the doubt returning.

Units 2 and 3: modeling and evaluation

  1. "Why can't I just average precision and recall to get F1?"
    Answer Because F1 punishes a big gap between them and an average does not. Take precision 0.9 and recall 0.3. The average is 0.6, which looks fine. F1 = 2 x 0.9 x 0.3 / (0.9 + 0.3) = 0.54 / 1.2 = 0.45. The model misses most real cases, and F1 says so. Habit: write 2PR / (P + R) before touching the numbers.
  2. "My model is 99% accurate. Why did the answer get only half marks?"
    Answer In a data set of 1,000 bank transactions with 10 frauds, a model that calls everything genuine is right 990 times, so 99% accurate, yet it catches no fraud at all: recall is 0. The examiner wanted you to say accuracy misleads when one class is rare, and to name recall as the metric to check. Habit: when one class is rare, look at the minority class first.
  3. "What is the bias actually doing in a perceptron?"
    Answer It shifts how easily the neuron fires, whatever the inputs. With inputs 1, 1, 0, weights 0.5, -0.3, 0.8 and bias -0.1: 0.5 - 0.3 + 0 - 0.1 = 0.1. With a step rule that fires above 0, the output is 1. Make the bias -0.3 and the sum becomes -0.1, so it does not fire. Same inputs, different decision. Habit: add the bias last, as its own line of working.
  4. "Is a chatbot that answers from fixed menu options AI?"
    Answer It is a script bot: it follows rules a person wrote and cannot handle a question outside them. A smart bot, such as a voice assistant, uses NLP and learns from data, so it copes with new phrasings. The board likes this as a difference question, so give one example of each. Habit: ask "who wrote the rule, a person or the data?"

Units 1, 5, 6 and Part A

  1. "Is a 1920 x 1080 image 2 million or 6 million?"
    Answer Both, depending on the question. It has 1920 x 1080 = 2,073,600 pixels. If it is a colour (RGB) image, each pixel stores three values, so it holds 6,220,800 numbers. Read whether the question asks for pixels or values. Habit: underline the word "pixels" or "values" before multiplying.
  2. "Stemming and lemmatisation look the same to me."
    Answer Both reduce a word to a base form, but stemming just cuts off the ending and may leave a non-word, while lemmatisation returns a real dictionary word. A stemmer that simply strips "-ing" turns "caring" into "car", a real word with the wrong meaning, while lemmatisation gives "care". Lemmatisation is slower because it needs a dictionary. Habit: learn one example where the stem is not a real word.
  3. "In the 4Ws canvas, what goes under Where?"
    Answer The situation or context in which the problem happens: when and where the stakeholders meet it. For a canteen food-waste project, Where is the school canteen at lunch, where plates are scraped into bins. It is not the place the solution will be built. Habit: fill Who, What, Where and Why in order, one line each, before any data talk.
  4. "Is 'I will score well in AI' a SMART goal?"
    Answer No. It is not specific or measurable and has no deadline. "I will score 40 or more out of 50 in my AI theory pre-board by revising one unit every three days until 15 January" is specific, measurable, achievable, relevant and time-bound. Part A asks this shape for two marks. Habit: check each of the five letters in turn.

More doubts in the evaluation unit are answered with numbers on confusion matrix numericals; the NLP ones on bag of words and TFIDF; and the word pairs students confuse most on difference between questions.

Q4. How do I ask well?

How to ask a class 10 AI doubt so it is solved in one go

Bring three things: the exact question, what you tried, and where you got stuck. A doubt asked like that is usually solved in under two minutes and teaches the rest of the class something too.

  1. Name the source

    "Worksheet question 4", "my school pre-board, Section B", or "file program 7". The teacher can then see the exact wording.

  2. Show the attempt

    A photo of the written answer, or the notebook on screen. Even a wrong attempt shows where the thinking turned.

  3. Point to the stuck step

    "I got TP and FP, but not FN" is far more useful than "I don't get confusion matrix".

  4. Try the check question

    After the answer, the teacher gives a quick variant. Solving it is how both of you know the doubt is closed.

Students who are shy in a group can type the doubt in the class chat; the teacher reads it out without the name if asked. Those who prefer every minute spent on their own questions often choose a Class 10 AI private tutor.

Questions about Class 10 AI doubt classes

What students ask about class 10 AI doubt classes

Where can I ask class 10 AI doubts?

In our live batch, doubts are asked in every Monday and Wednesday 9:00 PM IST class: doubts from set work are taken first, and doubts during the lesson are answered on the shared screen at once. One to one students can spend whole sessions on their doubts. The first class is a free demo, so you can bring a doubt to it.

Are doubt classes separate from regular classes?

No. Doubt time is built into every class rather than held as a separate session, so no doubt waits for a special day. After each full mock, the review class works as a dedicated doubt session on lost marks.

Can I ask doubts from my school worksheet or pre-board?

Yes, if they are 417 questions. Bring the question and your attempt; the teacher answers it and shows how the CBSE marking scheme would award the marks.

How are Python doubts solved online?

The student shares their screen, the error message is read together, and the code is fixed in the student's own notebook so the corrected program can go into the practical file. Most Class 10 errors fall into a few types, such as TypeError and AttributeError, which students learn to read themselves.

Can I ask doubts on Orange Data Mining?

Yes. Orange doubts are taken in the Wednesday lab with the student's workflow on the shared screen. Common ones are a widget with no output because a link is missing, or a model that will not train because no target column is set.

What if I am too shy to ask a doubt in front of the batch?

Type it in the class chat; the teacher can read it out without your name. Students who prefer complete privacy usually do better one to one, where every question is theirs.

Is there doubt support for the practical file and viva?

Yes. Doubts about any program in the file are taken in class, and the viva is rehearsed, which tends to surface the doubts a student did not know they had.

Are AI doubt-solving apps good for CBSE 417?

For a quick definition, sometimes. But apps may answer from the older chapter list, or treat Statistical Data as a theory unit when in 2026-27 it is practical only. They also cannot see why you made a mistake. Check app answers against the current unit list.

Is live doubt solving included in the fee?

Yes. Doubt time is part of every class and is covered by the monthly fee shown on this page, for the batch or one to one. There is no separate doubt fee and no admission fee.
