Behind the number

What moves the price of primary maths tuition

SATs support or a selective test

Helping a child feel confident for the May SATs is everyday primary teaching. Preparing for a grammar school test is specialist work, and 11 plus tutors price it that way.

When the test falls

The 11 plus is usually sat early in Year 6, so demand for tutors peaks across Year 5. The SATs come in May of Year 6, and the multiplication tables check in June of Year 4.

Who is teaching

A cheerful graduate is often exactly right for a seven-year-old building confidence. An experienced 11 plus specialist who knows your area's test format costs more and is worth it later.

A centre or a tutor

Centres sell monthly memberships with set sessions; tutors sell hours. A membership looks dearer per month and can be cheaper per hour, so compare the same unit.

How long sessions run

Young children concentrate for shorter stretches. Some tutors sell 45-minute sessions at a lower price, which can suit a six- or seven-year-old better than a full hour.

Siblings

Some centres discount a second child. Explore Learning, for example, publishes about $13 off the monthly membership for a second child.Ask any centre whether it discounts a second child.

Before you pay anyone

What is free, and what families tend to miss

Past SATs papers are free. The Standards and Testing Agency publishes past KS2 test papers and mark schemes on gov.uk, so they should never be sold to you as a paid extra.

Registration fees. Some centres charge a one-off fee before the first session; the two Kumon centres we read charge about $59 and $65.Some centres charge a one-off fee before the first session.

Prepaid blocks. Some tutors sell ten lessons upfront. Ask what happens to unused lessons if your child's timetable changes.

Travel. Two trips a week to a centre through Year 5 add up to a lot of afternoons. Online lessons happen at home.

Practice books. Most families buy a few. They are cheap; a tutor who insists on an expensive bundle is selling, not teaching.

With us: no enrolment fee, no contract and no prepaid blocks. You pay month by month and can stop at any month end.

Common questions

Primary maths tuition costs: questions parents ask

How much does a KS2 maths tutor cost per hour?

It depends on the tutor's experience, whether lessons are online and where you live. The rates table lists the published figures we found, each with its source and the exchange rate used.

Why do 11 plus tutors charge more than other primary tutors?

11 plus preparation is specialist work for a selective test with a fixed date, and tutors who know an area's test format are in demand through Year 5. Everyday primary maths support is priced lower.

When should 11 plus maths tuition start?

Most families start in Year 5, because the test in many grammar school areas is sat early in Year 6. Our 11 plus pages cover each area's format.

Does my child need a tutor for the KS2 SATs?

Often not. Many children do well with school teaching and free past papers. A tutor helps when a child has a specific gap, such as fractions or times tables, or is anxious about tests.

What is the multiplication tables check?

A short on-screen check taken in June of Year 4 in England, covering the times tables up to 12 times 12. Regular short practice prepares children for it more than long lessons do.

Is a tuition centre or a private tutor better value?

Compare the same unit. Centres sell monthly memberships with set sessions; tutors sell hours. Our tutor vs tuition centre guide compares them.

Are there extra fees with you?

No. There is no enrolment fee and no contract, and you can stop at any month end.
