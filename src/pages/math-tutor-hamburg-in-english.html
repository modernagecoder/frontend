Centrally set papers

hamburg.de explains that for the MSA, Hamburg holds a final exam in German and maths with tasks set centrally, and that the ESA usually works the same way in those two subjects. Every pupil in the city writes the same maths paper on the same morning.

Central papers reward a different kind of preparation from class tests. A teacher's test follows what the class just did; a central paper can ask about anything in the standards, phrased by someone your child has never met.

The Hamburg spring gap

Hamburg's spring holiday comes early: the KMK table lists it from 1 to 12 March 2027. That leaves about four school weeks between the end of the holiday and the maths papers on 12 and 13 April.

For most families that fortnight in March is the natural revision block, and the four weeks after it are for timed papers and calm.

The content behind both papers is national. Since 2022 one KMK standards document covers maths for the ESA and the MSA together, in the version of 23 June 2022. It groups the subject into five Leitideen, running from Zahl und Operation to Daten und Zufall. We use those five headings to find where a Hamburg pupil is losing marks.

Taught in English

How does a German maths task read to a child who thinks in English?

Lessons are live and only in English, and the maths is identical. What needs practice is reading the task. Here is one of ours, in both languages.

The task, as a Hamburg paper might phrase it (ours)

Eine Stiel-Eiche an der Elbchaussee hat einen Stammumfang von 2,51 m. Berechne den Durchmesser des Stammes. Runde auf ganze Zentimeter.

Three things can stop a pupil here before any maths happens: Stammumfang (trunk circumference), Durchmesser (diameter) and Runde auf ganze Zentimeter (round to the nearest whole centimetre). And the decimal comma in 2,51 is a decimal point in English.

How we teach it in English

We read it together, underline the three words, and say the task back in English: the trunk is 2.51 m round; find how wide it is. Then the formula: circumference equals pi times diameter, so the diameter is the circumference divided by pi.

In centimetres, 251 divided by pi is 79.9, which rounds to 80 cm. Last, a check: a trunk about 80 cm across is roughly the width of a door, which fits a big old oak. The answer is written in the German format, 80 cm, because that is how the paper is marked.

English only

Every lesson is live and in English; we run no German-language lessons. Mentors match each pupil's pace and accent and explain a step again in other words when needed.

A Hamburg data project

How wide are the Elbchaussee trees? The circle formula on real Hamburg data

Hamburg keeps a register of its street trees, and the Transparenzportal publishes it, free to reuse. Each tree has a girth in centimetres, which is exactly the circumference a Klasse 8 circle task asks about.

The data

The Straßenbaumkataster Hamburg comes from the environment authority, BUKEA, under the Datenlizenz Deutschland Namensnennung 2.0. The whole register holds 229,876 trees. We asked its open API for one street, the Elbchaussee along the river in Altona, and on 3 November 2026 saved the answer as elbchaussee.json: 364 trees.

Each tree has a species, a planting year (pflanzjahr) and a trunk girth (stammumfang) in centimetres. Not every field is filled for every tree, which matters below.

The question

How wide is the widest trunk on the Elbchaussee, and how wide is a typical one? The register gives circumference, not width, so every answer needs the circle formula run backwards: d = U / pi.

Then a second question that every good maths pupil asks of any data: are the numbers possible? A tree cannot be planted in a year that has not happened yet.

The widest trunk on the street belongs to a pedunculate oak, a Stiel-Eiche, with a girth of 530 cm. Divided by pi that is 168.7 cm, so the trunk is nearly as wide as an adult is tall. The mean diameter, 49.0 cm, sits well above the median, 43.3 cm: a few old giants pull the mean up, while the median shows the typical tree. That difference is a Klasse 7 lesson about averages, here with real wood.

One tree is listed as planted in 2104. Work out its age the usual way, 2026 minus 2104, and you get minus 78 years, which no tree can have. The register does not say what happened; it may be a typing error, and the record has no girth either. A careful pupil reports it and leaves it out of any average, as Version 2 does. The oldest planting year on the street is 1750, for the same 530 cm oak, and half of the dated trees were planted in 1965 or earlier.

  1. Klasse 6. Wrap a string round a tree or a lamp post, measure it, then divide by 3. Is the result close to the width you see?
  2. Klasse 7. Why is the mean diameter above the median? Make up five trunk widths where the mean and the median are the same, then change one so they are not.
  3. Klasse 8. A lime on the list has a girth of 128 cm. Find its diameter and the area of its cross-section, to one decimal place.
  4. Klasse 9 and 10. Change Version 1 so it prints the widest tree of each genus. Then write one sentence on why you checked the planting years before computing any ages.

Abitur 2027

What does the Hamburg Abitur maths exam involve?

The written Abitur is part Hamburg, part shared with other Länder. Here is what the published sources say.

Dates and shared tasks

The education authority gives the regular written Abitur dates for 2027 as 13 April to 5 May 2027, with catch-up dates from 25 to 31 May 2027. Its exam page offers Hamburg's rules for the 2027 Abitur together with sample tasks for the parts the Länder set jointly.

Those joint parts come from the pool coordinated by the IQB. Each Land's own commission decides which pool tasks it uses and how far it adds its own.

The paper itself

The IQB describes its maths pool exams in two parts: Part A without aids and Part B with aids. At raised level the exam has 100 marks and 300 minutes; at basic level, 80 marks and 255 minutes, including time to choose tasks.

Part A rewards the fast, sure algebra that is built from Klasse 7 onward. Our Abitur maths in English page explains the paper in more detail.

Maths tutoring in Hamburg: common questions

Are the classes in German?

No. Lessons are live and taught in English only. Mentors adapt to your child's pace and accent, and because Hamburg papers are in German, every topic ends with the German words and a task read in German, so your child is ready for the real paper.

When is the MSA maths exam in Hamburg in 2027?

Tuesday 13 April 2027, according to the education authority's list of exam dates as of 9 September 2026. The catch-up date is Friday 21 May 2027, and re-examinations in maths are on Wednesday 11 August 2027.

When is the ESA maths exam in Hamburg in 2027?

Monday 12 April 2027, one day before the MSA paper. German comes first, on Thursday 8 April 2027. The ESA, the Erster allgemeinbildender Schulabschluss, can be taken at the end of grade 9.

Are the Hamburg MSA and ESA maths papers set centrally?

Yes. hamburg.de says the MSA has a final exam in German and maths with centrally set tasks, and that the ESA usually does too. Every pupil sitting the paper gets the same tasks on the same day.

When is the Abitur in Hamburg in 2027?

The authority lists the regular written Abitur dates as 13 April to 5 May 2027 and catch-up dates as 25 to 31 May 2027. It gives a range, not a day per subject, so your school confirms the maths day.

Do you teach Stadtteilschule and Gymnasium pupils?

Both. A Gymnasium pupil starts the Oberstufe in grade 10 and takes the Abitur after grade 12; a Stadtteilschule pupil starts it in grade 11 and finishes after grade 13. We plan around your child's route; the school course is the one that counts.

What time are lessons in Hamburg?

Weekdays or weekends, flexible: choose your batch, or for a very personalised plan choose 1-on-1 at your convenience, weekend mornings or weekdays. A lesson at 17:10 in Hamburg is 21:40 for the mentor in India during winter.

Where are your maths tutors?

In India. They teach over live video, and one mentor stays with your child for the whole year, so each lesson picks up where the last one stopped.

How much does it cost, and in which currency?

Fees are paid monthly for eight live lessons. On this page the fee appears in euro, using the rate the European Central Bank publishes; the card is debited in US dollars and the conversion is your bank's. Your first lesson is free.

Can you guarantee a grade in the MSA or the Abitur?

No, and nobody honestly can. We teach the maths, practise with our own tasks under exam conditions, and tell you plainly how your child is doing. The school and the Hamburg authority set and mark the exams.
