## The five pass-or-fail criteria

1. Live, with a named teacher who stays: a course page that cannot say who teaches has answered your question.
2. Inside the tools' own age terms: 13 with consent for some platforms, 18 flat for others; a provider ignoring them for enrolment convenience is showing you its character.
3. Outcomes you can inspect: a link to things students actually built, not "your child will master AI".
4. Verification on the syllabus: teaching AI without teaching how to catch AI being wrong trains half a skill, the dangerous half.
5. Pricing a family can read: a monthly figure, exit at any month, no Sunday-deadline discounts.

## Reading any course page in ninety seconds

Red flags: "become an AI expert in 30 days"; "certificate recognised worldwide" with no named recogniser; no teacher names anywhere; "lifetime access to recordings" as the product; no mention of tool age terms; "offer ends tonight". One is caution, two is a pattern, three is a decision.

Green flags: named teachers, the same one weekly; student projects you can open; tool ages stated with sources; reading and testing in the syllabus; monthly price with monthly exit; a free real class rather than a demo.
