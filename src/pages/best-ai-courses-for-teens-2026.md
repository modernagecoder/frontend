Before any list

## Five criteria that disqualify most of the market

Apply these to any teen AI course anywhere, ours included. They are pass or fail, and a course failing one is out regardless of its production values.

**One: live, with a named teacher who stays.** Recorded libraries teach watching; teenagers need someone who notices their specific stall. If the course page cannot tell you who teaches and whether it is the same person each week, that is the answer. **Two: inside the tools' own age terms.** The mainstream AI platforms publish minimum ages, 13 with consent for some, 18 flat for others, and a provider that ignores them for enrolment convenience is showing you its whole character. Ask which accounts your teen will need and at what published age; wrong or vague answers end the conversation.

**Three: outcomes you can inspect.** "Your child will master AI" is not an outcome; a link to things students actually built is. **Four: verification on the syllabus.** Any course teaching AI without teaching how to catch AI being wrong is training half a skill, and the dangerous half at that. Look for the words reading, testing, checking in the curriculum, not just building. **Five: pricing a family can read.** A monthly figure, an exit at any month, no expiring discounts. The Sunday-deadline pattern is not a pricing strategy, it is a personality test the provider is failing in public.

## How to read any course page in ninety seconds

You will compare us with others; you should. Here is the anatomy of a course page, both kinds, so the comparison takes minutes instead of evenings.

***RED FLAGS**close the tab*

```
"Become an AI expert in 30 days"
  > expertise has no express lane

"Certificate recognised worldwide"
  > recognised by whom? Named where?

No teacher names anywhere
  > who exactly meets your child?

"Lifetime access to recordings"
  > the product is a video folder

No mention of tool age terms
  > they have not read them. Next.

"Offer ends tonight"
  > urgency is doing the arguing
```

One of these alone is a caution; two is a pattern; three is a decision. The absence of teacher names is the most reliable single tell in this market.

```
Named teachers, same one weekly
  > a person is accountable to you

Student projects you can open
  > outcomes, inspectable, today

Tool ages stated, with sources
  > they did the reading

"Reading and testing" in syllabus
  > both halves of the skill

Monthly price, monthly exit
  > confidence, priced like it

A free real class, not a demo
  > they will be judged live
```

## What comparing families ask us

### How were these rankings decided?

By category need rather than a single score, after applying five pass-or-fail criteria: live teaching with a named consistent teacher, operation inside the AI tools' published age terms, specific project outcomes you can inspect, verification skills on the syllabus, and pricing a family can read without a sales call. Any course failing one of the five did not get ranked, whoever runs it, and the criteria matter more than our list, because they work on any provider.

### These are all your own courses. Is this really a ranking?

It is a ranked map of our catalogue, stated openly, and we would rather be upfront than dress it as neutral journalism the way most best-of pages in this market do. What we offer for comparison shopping instead is better than a disguised ad: the five criteria and the flag list on this page apply to every provider including us, and the free class exists precisely so you can test our claims against a real session before paying anything.

### Why pay at all when YouTube teaches AI free?

Videos are genuinely good at explanations and terrible at the two things that decide whether a teenager progresses: watching your specific work fail in its specific way, and making you ship on a schedule. If your teen is thriving on free material, continue, sincerely. The typical pattern we see is different: heavy watching, thin building, and a stall at the first project the video cannot debug, which is exactly the gap a live small batch closes.

### Which course should a total beginner teen start with?

A maker beginner starts with Vibe Coding for Teens, because shipping something in week one converts curiosity into momentum. A cautious or younger teen starts with AI Tools Mastery, which builds judgement and fluency before deeper commitments. A teen who already codes skips both and enters at AI and ML for Teens. The free class doubles as placement, so choosing wrong on this page costs nothing.

### Can a teenager take two courses at once?

Some do, usually pairing a building course with the maths-lighter tools course, and it works when the build-work hours genuinely exist in the week. Our default advice is one course done properly with its two to three hours of between-session building, because depth in one beats surface in two at every stage of learning. Teachers flag it early when a student's pairing is hurting both.

### How long does each course take?

Most teen tracks run in terms of roughly twelve weeks at one live session a week, and a category winner above typically means two terms for comfortable command of its material. Nothing is dripped or locked: a family can stop at any month end, and a student who wants to go faster can switch to one to one where the pace follows them.

### Do the courses give certificates that matter?

Each course carries a completion certificate, and the projects matter more, which we say on every page including this one. A reviewer, whether a school, a competition judge or eventually an employer, trusts a live link and an explainable repository over any PDF. Our courses are built so the student leaves with both, and the certificate is the smaller half.

### Which of these prepares a teen for school AI curricula?

The CBSE-aligned computational thinking course is the direct match for India's curriculum with its coming compulsory Class 9 examination, and the general tracks support any system's coursework indirectly by building the underlying skills. Families in the UAE and other systems adding mandatory AI subjects report the literacy-and-building combination covers the school material with room to spare.

The two agent-tool courses run one to one only at the USD 150 rate, for the supervision reasons explained on their own pages. The first class of any course is free without a card.
