What it adds

Deeper and broader, in the government's own words.

The Department for Education's subject content says Further Maths "is designed for students with an enthusiasm for mathematics, many of whom will go on to degrees in mathematics, engineering, the sciences and economics." It calls the qualification "both deeper and broader than A level mathematics".

In practice that means two things. The algebra and calculus of A level Maths are taken further, and new ideas arrive that A level Maths never touches, chiefly complex numbers and matrices, which the content describes as having "wide applications in mathematics, engineering, physical sciences and computing".

About half of the A level is a prescribed core that every board must teach. The other half is options set by each board, which can build on the applied content of A level Maths, introduce new applications or extend the pure core.

The core every board teaches

  • Proof, including proof by induction
  • Complex numbers
  • Matrices
  • Further algebra and functions, including Maclaurin series
  • Further calculus
  • Further vectors
  • Polar coordinates
  • Hyperbolic functions
  • Differential equations

Sections A to I of the DfE subject content for A level further mathematics.

What it asks of you

A whole extra A level, and a demanding one.

Time

Further Maths is a separate A level, so it takes a full share of the timetable and of homework. It may be a fourth A level alongside three others, or one of three when Maths and Further Maths are both taken.

Pace

It builds on A level Maths as it goes. A student who falls behind in A level Maths will feel it twice, because Further Maths topics assume the A level ideas underneath them.

Abstraction

Complex numbers, matrices and proof by induction ask for a more abstract way of thinking than most A level Maths. Students who enjoy that tend to enjoy the whole course.

What students usually gain

A second, deeper look at A level Maths topics, which the AS content says "broadens and reinforces" them; first contact with ideas used throughout mathematical degrees; and practice at sustained problem solving.

The AS route

AS Further Maths is a separate, smaller qualification. Every board must offer at least one AS route that can be taught alongside AS Maths, so a student can taste the subject without committing to the full A level.

How to decide

Four honest questions.

Do you enjoy maths for its own sake?

Further Maths is long and abstract. Enjoyment is what carries students through the hard weeks.

Is A level Maths going comfortably?

If A level Maths is already a struggle, Further Maths usually makes both harder. Secure one first.

Where are you heading?

The subject content links Further Maths to degrees in mathematics, engineering, the sciences and economics. If a particular course matters to you, read its published entry requirements yourself.

Full A level or AS?

If time is tight, the AS gives real Further Maths content in a smaller package.

A taste of the core

Three short examples we wrote.

Complex numbers

(2 + 3i)(1 − i)

= 2 − 2i + 3i − 3i²
= 2 + i + 3
= 5 + i

Multiply out as usual, then use i² = −1.

Matrices

What does the matrix with rows (3, 1) and (2, 4) do to area?

det = 3 × 4 − 1 × 2
= 12 − 2 = 10

Any shape it transforms has its area multiplied by 10.

Proof by induction

Show 1 + 2 + ... + n = n(n + 1)/2

n = 1: 1 = 1 × 2/2 ✓
assume true for k, add k + 1:
k(k + 1)/2 + (k + 1) = (k + 1)(k + 2)/2 ✓

True for 1, and each case gives the next, so it is true for all n.

Common questions

Further Maths: what students and parents ask.

What is A level Further Maths?

A separate A level that goes deeper and broader than A level Maths. It adds topics such as complex numbers, matrices, polar coordinates, hyperbolic functions and differential equations.

Is Further Maths harder than A level Maths?

It is generally more demanding. The Department for Education describes it as deeper and broader than A level Maths, it is more abstract, and it builds on the A level Maths content as it goes.

Do you need A level Maths to take Further Maths?

Further Maths builds on AS and A level Maths, so students take it alongside A level Maths or after it.

What is the difference between AS and A level Further Maths?

AS Further Maths is a separate, smaller qualification. Every board must offer an AS route that can be taught alongside AS Maths.

How much of Further Maths is the same on every board?

About half. The core is prescribed by the Department for Education; the other half is options chosen by each board.

Is Further Maths worth it if I am not doing a maths degree?

It can be, for anyone who enjoys maths and wants a deeper grounding. The subject content links it to engineering, the sciences and economics as well as mathematics.

Can I take Further Maths if my school does not offer it?

You can study it with a tutor, but the exams must be sat at an exam centre, so ask your school or local centres whether they accept external candidates.

How many hours a week does Further Maths need?

As a full A level it needs the same time as any other A level, plus independent practice. We plan lessons around the student's other subjects.

Will Further Maths help with A level Maths?

Often, because it revisits A level ideas in more depth. It only helps when A level Maths is already secure.
