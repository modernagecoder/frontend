Free demo or priority demo: an honest comparison.

Both are taught by our mentors. Both show you real teaching. The difference is when it happens and whether it is certain to happen at all.

Book priority demo · ₹499 What is the priority demo?

One-time fee. No enrolment required. Confirmed on WhatsApp within the hour.

In short

The free demo is a waiting list: mentors take it only when they are not teaching, so the wait can be several weeks and there is no guaranteed slot. The Priority Live Demo is a one-time paid class (₹499) with a slot you choose, usually today or tomorrow, and a mentor reserved for you. The teaching is the same in both.

When the free demo is the right choice

If you are exploring with no deadline, join the waiting list and use the time well. Watch the recorded lessons on how we teach, try the exercises in free resources and read what happens in a demo class so you know what to look for.

When the priority demo is the right choice

  • You want to decide this week, not next month.
  • You are comparing institutes and need a real class to compare.
  • You live outside India and need a time that fits your evening.
  • Your child is excited now, and you know that does not last forever.

The reasoning behind the fee is laid out in why we charge for a priority demo, and the steps are in how to book a priority demo.

What is identical in both

The teaching. In either demo the mentor will do what we do in every class: give the student a problem, let them wrestle with it, and coach the thinking rather than dictate the answer. That is the approach behind we teach you how to think and think like a software engineer.

You can sample any subject, from Scratch and Python to Vedic maths and high school mathematics.

  • We teach you how to thinkBefore any code is typed, the student learns to read a problem, break it apart and plan an answer.

Questions parents ask

Is the free demo lower quality?

No. The teaching approach is the same. The priority demo has a guaranteed time, runs as a full class of about 45 to 60 minutes, and ends with a written skill report.

How long is the free demo wait?

It depends on mentor availability and can be several weeks. We cannot promise a date.

Can I switch from the waiting list to a priority demo?

Yes, at any time. Book a priority demo and your slot is confirmed regardless of your place in the queue.

Is the priority fee adjusted against course fees?

Yes. If you enrol, send us your demo Order ID on WhatsApp and we adjust the demo fee against your first month. Our team applies it for you, so it does not show as a discount on the payment page. Current course fees are on the pricing page.
