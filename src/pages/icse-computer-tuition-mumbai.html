Q2. What kind of program is taught?

A menu-driven Section B program, solved and run four ways

Menu-driven programs ask the student to offer choices with a switch and do something different for each. They are a regular Section B shape because they test methods, loops, conditions and switch in one answer. This one is original, written for a Mumbai batch.

The question. Using a switch statement, write a menu-driven program. Choice 1 accepts a number and checks whether it is a Pronic number, the product of two consecutive integers (for example 132 = 11 × 12). Choice 2 accepts two limits and prints every Niven number between them, a number divisible by the sum of its digits, followed by how many there were. For any other choice, print an error message. Use a separate method for each check.

Q4. Where does the school lab file come in?

Internal assessment lab assignments: support that sits beside the school's own process

Your school owns the internal assessment. It sets at least twenty assignments over the year, fixes the dates, and its teacher and an external examiner each award 50 of the 100 marks. We prepare the student to do that work well and to explain it.

Each examiner marks four headings: execution or output carries 20, and class design, variable description, and coding with documentation carry 10 apiece. Of these, the one students most often throw away is documentation. A program that runs perfectly can still look careless on paper. So in our classes, every program is finished with the same short documentation habit before it counts as done.

What we do, and what we leave alone

  • We do teach the topic behind a school question, using a different example, until the student can attempt the real one alone.
  • We do ask the student to explain their class design and variable choices out loud, the way an external examiner might.
  • We do run their program with deliberately awkward input so execution marks are not lost on the day.
  • We do not write, type or correct-and-return an assignment for submission.
  • We do not change the school's questions, order or format.

The marking scheme in full, with ideas for a strong file, is set out on ICSE Computer Applications project and internal assessment.

Mumbai questions

What Mumbai parents ask about ICSE computer tuition

Do you have ICSE computer tuition classes in Mumbai I can visit?

No. Modern Age Coders has no centre in Mumbai, Thane or Navi Mumbai, nor tutors who visit homes. Classes happen only on screen: one teacher, six ICSE students at most, BlueJ shared for all to see. That is the point for many Mumbai families: there is no journey to make.

What are the timings for the online ICSE Java batch?

Tuesday at 5:30 PM and Wednesday at 8:00 PM, Indian time, which is Mumbai time. Tuesday is for concepts and Section A practice, Wednesday for a complete Section B program. If those evenings are taken, a one to one class can be fixed at another time.

Is online computer tuition effective for ICSE Class 10?

Computer Applications suits online teaching well, because the work happens in BlueJ on a computer. The teacher sees each student's screen, watches the program being typed and run, and corrects mistakes as they happen. Students who struggle to focus on a screen may still prefer a home tutor.

What is a menu-driven program in ICSE Computer Applications?

It is a program that shows numbered choices, reads the user's choice and uses a switch statement to run a different task for each one, with a default case for wrong input. It is a common Section B question because it tests methods, loops, conditions and switch together.

Do you teach the Maharashtra SSC computer syllabus?

No. The classes follow only the CISCE ICSE Class 10 Computer Applications syllabus for the 2027 examination, taught in Java with BlueJ. Students on the Maharashtra state board or CBSE need a different course. If you have one child on each board, ask on WhatsApp and we will point the other child to the right class.

Can you help with my child's school computer project?

We help the student, not the file. ICSE has no separate prescribed project: the internal assessment is at least twenty lab assignments set by the school. We teach the concepts, rehearse explanations and test programs, and the student writes and submits their own work. We never write assignments.

How much does online ICSE computer tuition cost?

Both fees, group and private, sit on this page as monthly figures shown in whichever currency applies to you. There is no admission charge, billing is month by month, and the first class is free so you can judge before paying.

How many hours a week should a Class 10 student give to Computer Applications?

In our plan, about three: two live classes plus two short sessions at home, one to rewrite the week's program alone and one for a worksheet. In the last weeks before the board exam, one full timed paper a week is added.

Can a Class 10 student join in the middle of the year?

Yes. A mid-year joiner does a short diagnostic in the demo, then follows a catch-up plan ordered by marks: whole programs before short answers, and full papers once both are steady. Joining after the first term is perfectly workable, and parents see the plan so nobody wonders what a given week is for.

What will my child do in the free demo?

Your child takes part in a real class: types a small Java program, runs it and talks through what it does as the teacher listens. You then get a frank view of where they stand and whether the evening batch or a private slot fits better.
