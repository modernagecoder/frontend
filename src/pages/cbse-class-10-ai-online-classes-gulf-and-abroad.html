Q3. Is the board exam the same outside India?

The same curriculum and the same scheme, with a few things only your school can confirm

What is the same for a student in Dubai and in Delhi

  • The 2026-27 curriculum for code 417, unchanged from 2025-26: Part A employability skills and seven Part B units.
  • The paper design: 50 theory marks in two hours, 15 of 21 questions answered, objective and written sections.
  • The 50 practical marks: a 15-program file, a practical exam, a project and two vivas, conducted by your school.
  • The date sheet: CBSE publishes one schedule for the main examinations; the 2027 date sheet was not out when we checked on 13 October 2026.
  • The rule that 417 cannot be taken with Computer Applications (165).

What to check with your school

Your school registers your child with CBSE through the LOC, the list of candidates. The regular window runs from 24 September to 21 October 2026, with a correction window from 2 to 4 November. Ask the class teacher to confirm that subject code 417 is on your child's record.

Your school also sets the practical exam dates and arranges the examiner. Schools abroad often announce these at short notice around their own winter break, so our Wednesday classes aim to have the practical file and project ready well before January. The full paper pattern and calendar are on CBSE Class 10 AI exam date and paper pattern.

Q4. What does a week in the batch look like from the Gulf?

Two evenings, one teacher, and doubts answered in every class

Students in the Gulf join from home on a laptop with a headset. They see the teacher, the shared screen and their classmates, and can speak or type at any point.

  1. Monday, 7:30 PM in Dubai: theory and Part A

    One Part B unit is taught and then practised in the paper's question shapes: objective questions, a two-mark answer within thirty words, a four-mark case study within eighty. From November, a short Part A drill keeps the ten employability marks warm.

  2. Wednesday, 6:30 PM in Riyadh: the practical half

    A practical file program is written in Jupyter Notebook, run and explained, and the project moves forward a step. Students install Python and Jupyter in the first week with the teacher's help, so the setup matches what the school lab uses.

  3. Live doubts, raised and settled in class

    Any student can stop the class with a question, share their screen and get it answered while the class is still on that topic. Doubts from homework open the next class, three days later at most.

  4. Board-season papers on a Gulf weekend

    From January, full mock papers are written at home on a weekend morning, close to the time the real paper runs, and reviewed live in the next class with lost marks rewritten. The full series is on the sample paper and mock tests page.

  5. Progress reports for parents

    After the diagnostic, when the practical file closes and after each mock, parents get a short progress report: marks by unit and what to practise at home. For parents in a different city from the teacher, that report is the clearest picture of how the year is going.

What families in the Gulf and abroad ask about CBSE Class 10 AI classes

Do you offer CBSE Class 10 AI classes in Dubai and the UAE?

Yes, live online. Our Monday and Wednesday Class 10 AI batch runs at 9:00 PM IST, which is 7:30 PM in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah and the rest of the UAE. It covers the full 417 syllabus, the practical file and project, the sample paper and mocks, with live doubt solving. Personalised one to one is available at any other local time.

What time are the classes in Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait and Bahrain?

The batch is at 6:30 PM local time on Monday and Wednesday in Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam, Doha, Kuwait City and Manama, because these countries are two and a half hours behind India. They do not change their clocks, so the time holds all year.

Is the CBSE AI syllabus the same for schools outside India?

Yes. CBSE schools abroad follow the same 2026-27 curriculum for Artificial Intelligence (417): Part A employability skills, seven Part B units, a 50-mark theory paper and 50 practical marks conducted by the school. Our classes teach that curriculum unchanged.

Can students in Nepal join the batch?

Yes. Nepal is fifteen minutes ahead of India, so the 9 PM IST batch is at 9:15 PM in Kathmandu, which works for many Class 10 students. Families who prefer an earlier class choose personalised one to one at a time that suits them.

We live in Singapore or Malaysia. Can we join?

Yes, one to one. The batch falls at 11:30 PM there, which is too late on a school night, so students in Singapore and Malaysia study personalised one to one at a local time the family chooses, with the same teachers and syllabus.

How are fees charged outside India?

Outside India, fees are shown and charged in US dollars, at one price for every country, and billed monthly with no admission fee. You can stop at the end of any month. The fee for the batch and for personalised one to one appears on this page when you visit from outside India.

Do the classes cover the practical exam that our school conducts?

Yes. The practical is half the subject: a 15-program file, a practical exam, a project and two vivas, all conducted by your school. Wednesday classes build the file and the project step by step and rehearse the viva, so your child is ready whenever the school sets the dates.

What if we travel or a class clashes with school events?

Classes are live. A missed class is covered with homework and a short catch-up in the next class, and for several missed classes, such as during a long trip, a one-off catch-up session is arranged. Families moving between countries keep the same teacher and adjust the class time to the new city.

Do you teach the UK or US curriculum?

Not on this page. These classes are for students at CBSE schools sitting the CBSE Class 10 AI (417) exam. CBSE has no affiliated schools in the UK or the US, so the table lists only countries where CBSE schools operate.
