## How much does a maths tutor cost in Peru?

There is no official rate. A tutor's fee in Peru moves with five things, and two families quoting very different prices may simply be buying different services.

1. **At home or online.** A tutor who travels to the family's home charges for travel time too; online removes it, and so removes the commute for the learner.
2. **The level.** Primaria arithmetic, secundaria algebra, admission-exam preparation, IB or IGCSE and university calculus are different jobs. Exam-specific work usually costs more.
3. **One to one or a group.** A private tutor's whole hour goes to one learner. Our maths groups have fifteen to twenty learners at the same level; one-to-one classes are the other option.
4. **Lessons per week.** Some fees buy one lesson a week, others two or more. Our monthly maths fee covers eight live classes, normally two a week.
5. **How many months.** The one families forget. A month of tutoring three weeks before an exam costs the same as a month in March, and does far less.

We never print other tutors' or academies' prices, and our own appears only in the fee cards below. Our Spanish [guide to tutor prices](/precio-profesor-particular-matematicas-peru) converts school hours and travel into clock hours for a fairer comparison.

### Reading the table as a budget

Peruvian law requires a knowledge exam as the main route into university: PUCP quotes Ley Universitaria 30220 saying the admission contest consists of "un examen de conocimientos" as the principal compulsory step. Maths sits at the centre of most of these exams.

For a pupil in 4th of secundaria in late 2026, U de Lima and PUCP's first-option dates are about a year away. That is a long, cheap runway: one or two maths lessons a week, building understanding rather than cramming.

### Why the date changes the format

With a year to go, a group is usually enough. With twelve weeks, one-to-one classes spent only on the learner's gaps often cost less in total, because nothing is wasted on topics they already know.

U de Lima deducts points for wrong answers, so preparation must include judging when not to answer. We teach that with original timed sets. Admission exams are in Spanish; we teach the maths reasoning in English and the learner practises the Spanish wording on the university's own sample material.

## How many classes fit before an exam? A short Python program

Subtract two dates and Python tells you the days between them. From there it is one line to weeks and classes. This is a lesson we give secundaria pupils, and it settles budget arguments at home quickly.

### The question

A pupil plans to start maths classes on Monday 16 November 2026, an invented date for the lesson, at two classes a week. How many classes fit before each of three exams in the table?

One of the exams falls the day before the first class. A careless program would report a negative number of classes; this one checks first.

### The ideas

date(2027, 2, 21) - date(2026, 11, 16) gives a timedelta, and .days turns it into a plain number. Floor division by 7 gives whole weeks.

timedelta(weeks=13) works the other way: add it to a date to find when a thirteen-week plan would end. Python handles month lengths and the change of year for you.

**exam_countdown.py: days, weeks and classes left** (Python)
```python
from datetime import date, timedelta

start = date(2026, 11, 16)                       # an invented first class, a Monday
exams = {
    "U de Lima school exam": date(2026, 11, 15),
    "PUCP Primera Opcion": date(2026, 11, 29),
    "PUCP Evaluacion del Talento": date(2027, 2, 21),
}
for name, day in exams.items():
    gap = (day - start).days
    if gap <= 0:
        print(f"{name}: too late, it falls {-gap} day(s) before the first class")
        continue
    print(f"{name}: {gap} days, {gap // 7} full week(s), about {gap // 7 * 2} classes at two a week")

print("Thirteen weeks after the start:", (start + timedelta(weeks=13)).strftime("%A %d %B %Y"))
```
It prints U de Lima school exam: too late, it falls 1 day(s) before the first class, then PUCP Primera Opcion: 13 days, 1 full week(s), about 2 classes at two a week, then PUCP Evaluacion del Talento: 97 days, 13 full week(s), about 26 classes at two a week, and finally Thirteen weeks after the start: Monday 15 February 2027.

The output makes the budget point by itself. Starting in mid-November leaves about two classes before PUCP's first-option exam, which is barely worth paying for as exam preparation, and about twenty-six before February, which is enough for real work. The same fee buys very different value depending on the date it starts. Accents are left out of the exam names in the code to keep the example simple to type.

1. **Change the start.** Set start to 1 March 2026 and run it again. How many classes would have fitted before U de Lima's exam?
2. **Add an exam.** Add the UNI ordinary exam of 10 August 2026 and see the guard catch it.
3. **Count only school weeks.** Harder: skip any week that falls in a school break from the RM 501-2025 calendar.
4. **Explain it.** In two English sentences, tell a parent why the start date matters more than the monthly fee.

## How much maths does a secundaria pupil already get at school?

Enough hours that a tutor should not repeat the lessons. The point of paying is the part a class of thirty cannot do: finding one learner's gaps and fixing them.

The national curriculum gives Matemática four periods a week in every secundaria grade in schools on the regular day, rising to six where a school runs the longer Jornada Escolar Completa; the CNEB defines a period as 45 minutes. Under RM 501-2025 the 2026 year ran thirty-six teaching weeks, with the last block from 19 October to 18 December 2026. Our arithmetic: four periods is three clock hours of maths a week, about a hundred and eight hours across a teaching year.

Against that, one or two tutoring hours a week is small, so they must be targeted. A diagnostic in the first class, problems chosen for the gap rather than the chapter, and a learner who explains their own working are what make the money count. If a tutor spends the hour doing the homework, the family is paying twice for the same lesson. Our page on choosing a [profesor particular de matemáticas online](/profesor-particular-de-matematicas-online-peru) covers this in Spanish.

## Is a pre-university academy cheaper than a maths tutor?

They are different products, and we do not compare prices. An academia or a university's own pre-university centre teaches every admission subject to large groups on a fixed schedule; a tutor works on one learner's maths.

### What the universities run

Several universities run their own centres. UNI offers direct entry through CEPRE-UNI, whose 2026-2 cycle ended with a final exam on 2 August 2026. PUCP lists CeprePUCP as one of its regular admission routes, and U de Lima's 2027-1 process includes a route through its pre-university centre.

Families should read each university's own guide; terms, dates and fees are set there, not by us.

### Where tutoring fits

Many pupils combine the two: a centre or academia for the full syllabus in Spanish, and a tutor for the maths that is not clicking. Others need only the maths.

We do not run an academia and do not prepare the Spanish-language parts of admission exams. We teach the maths reasoning, in English, that the exams depend on, and we never promise admission.
