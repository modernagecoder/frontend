# Coding and AI classes in Maine

**Do Maine students have to take computer science?** No. Maine&rsquo;s diploma statute, Title 20-A section 4722, sets minimums in English, social studies and history, mathematics, science and fine arts, and names no computer science course. The Maine Department of Education instead encourages computer science woven into other subjects through project work. Modern Age Coders adds focused, live online coding and AI classes for Maine students, from block games to AP Computer Science.

Maine has taken a quieter route to computer science than states that legislated a course. Its diploma law has stayed with five subject areas, and the Department of Education has said plainly that it does not want computer science to be a separate course only some students take or an add-on. It wants it inside everything else: teacher-designed MOOSE modules that bring programming and data science into ordinary lessons, a pilot grant for teacher training, and an annual showcase of student work. That approach has a strength and a weakness. Every subject can carry some computing, but a student who wants sustained, sequenced programming may find only a few hours of it in a year. This page sets out the statute, the department&rsquo;s own words, the published figures for Maine, and a small project on Maine&rsquo;s lighthouses that teaches filtering and counting in Python.

## What Maine law requires for a high school diploma

Title 20-A, section 4722, as published by the Maine Legislature and read on 17 October 2026.

**Minimum diploma requirements in Title 20-A section 4722, subsection 2**

| Subject area | Minimum in the statute |
|---|---|
| English | 4 years or the equivalent in standards achievement |
| Social studies and history, including American history, government, civics and personal finance | 2 years or the equivalent |
| Mathematics | 2 years or the equivalent |
| Science, including at least one year of laboratory study | 2 years or the equivalent |
| Fine arts, which may include art, music, forensics or drama | One year or the equivalent |
| Computer science | Not listed in the statute |

### At least four years

The instructional program leading to a diploma must last at least four years and meet any further requirements set by the commissioner and the local school board.

### Multiple pathways

The statute lets students show they meet the standards through multiple pathways, with evidence from examinations, portfolios, performances, exhibitions, projects and community service.

### Local boards add more

Because local boards can set further requirements, a particular Maine district may ask for more than the statute. A school counselor can confirm whether computing counts anywhere locally.

The maine.gov pages for the Department of Education&rsquo;s computer science and diploma guidance refused our requests, so this page does not state the adoption year or content of any Maine computer science standards. What we can say from the statute is that computer science is not one of the five named diploma areas, and from the department&rsquo;s newsroom that it is meant to live inside them.

## Computer science inside other subjects: what the Maine DOE says it is doing

Three posts from the Maine Department of Education newsroom, from 2022, quoted as published.

**Maine Department of Education computer science initiatives named in its newsroom**

| Date posted | Initiative | What the department said |
|---|---|---|
| July 14, 2022 | Computer Science Education Showcase | Held at the Roux Institute; Maine encourages all schools to provide interdisciplinary, project-based computer science at all grade levels and across subjects |
| August 30, 2022 | Pilot professional development grant | Created by the 130th Legislature for teacher-developed or teacher-led PK-12 computer science training; applications opened September 1, 2022 |
| November 16, 2022 | Computer Science MOOSE modules | Interdisciplinary, project-based modules designed by Maine teachers that introduce computational thinking, programming and data science |

### In the commissioner&rsquo;s words

At the 2022 showcase, Education Commissioner Pender Makin said: &ldquo;Computer science is about approaching a problem with optimism, logic, critical thinking, design thinking, creativity and vision.&rdquo; The department added that it wants computer science to be meaningful for every educator and every student, not only an elite group.

### The strength

A science class that models data and an art class that codes a pattern both teach computing in context, which is how many students first see why it matters.

### The gap

Woven-in lessons rarely build a long chain of skills, from variables to functions to data structures, in order. That sequence is what an AP exam or a first internship expects.

### Where we fit

Our classes supply the sequence: weekly, cumulative programming with one teacher, so the interdisciplinary projects at school have a solid technical base under them.

## How many Maine lighthouses are still active? Filtering and counting in Python

The Maine GeoLibrary publishes a lighthouse layer with a name, a county and a status for each light. Students load it and answer questions with filters.

**Maine GeoLibrary lighthouse records by county, with the active ones counted (our counts, downloaded 18 October 2026)**

| County | All records | Status active |
|---|---|---|
| Knox | 15 | 13 |
| Hancock | 13 | 10 |
| Washington | 11 | 7 |
| Lincoln | 6 | 6 |
| Cumberland | 6 | 6 |
| York | 5 | 5 |
| Sagadahoc | 4 | 4 |
| Waldo | 2 | 2 |
| County left blank | 1 | 1 |

### 1. One condition

Our download contained 63 records. Filtering on Status gives 54 active, 7 inactive, 1 demolished and 1 marked unknown. Students check that the four counts add back to 63.

### 2. Two conditions

Active and in Knox County gives 13, the largest group. Combining conditions with and, or and not is the core skill, and a wrong operator is the classic bug.

### 3. Fields that disagree

Six names contain the word historical in brackets, yet two of those six have Status active. One field says the light is history, another says it is working. Which do you trust?

**Text filters are a second exercise** Searching the names finds 29 containing Light Station and 10 containing Lighthouse. Students learn that a text search depends on exact wording and capital letters, and that a name is a poor substitute for a proper category field.

### Our counts, their layer

Every count above is ours, from one download of the Maine GeoLibrary layer, which credits the Maine Office of GIS. The publisher&rsquo;s only stated condition is that the data is not to be used for navigational purposes, and nothing here is navigation advice. The publisher has not checked our results.

## If AI writes the filter, who notices the historical light marked active?

A person who understands the data. That is why Maine students still benefit from learning to code.

Ask an AI tool how many Maine lighthouses are active and it may answer 54 from this same layer, and stop there. It probably will not mention that two lights it just counted are named as historical, or that one record has no county. Those details surface only when a student looks at the rows behind the number.

The Maine commissioner described computer science as approaching a problem with logic, critical thinking and design thinking. That is exactly the skill an AI tool does not replace: deciding what a count should include, checking the edge cases, and saying clearly what the answer does and does not mean.

A Maine student who can filter a dataset and then question it is ready to use AI tools well, rather than simply believe them. The longer argument is in [is coding still worth learning in 2026](/blog/is-coding-worth-learning-2026).

## Maine coding and AI questions

### Is computer science a graduation requirement in Maine?

No. Title 20-A section 4722 lists English, social studies and history, mathematics, science and fine arts as the diploma minimums, with no computer science course. Local boards may add requirements.

### How many years of maths does Maine require?

The statute sets 2 years of mathematics or the equivalent in standards achievement, alongside 4 years of English and 2 of science including one year of laboratory study.

### How does Maine teach computer science?

The Maine Department of Education says it encourages all schools to provide interdisciplinary, project-based computer science at all grade levels and across subjects, rather than as a separate course only some students take.

### What are the Maine MOOSE computer science modules?

The department describes them as interdisciplinary, project-based modules designed by Maine teachers that introduce students to computational thinking, programming and data science.

### How many Maine high schools offer computer science?

The Code.org 2025 data counts 72 of 123 Maine high schools, or 58.5%, in 2024 to 2025, based on state education agency data and school catalogs.

### How many Maine students take computer science?

The Code.org 2025 sheet publishes no participation figure for Maine, so that number is not available from this source.

### What time are classes for Maine students?

Weekdays or weekends, flexible: choose your batch, or for a very personalised plan choose 1-on-1 at your convenience. Times are confirmed in Eastern Time, America/New_York.

### Do your classes count for Maine school credit?

No. Credit and diploma requirements are decided by the school and district. Our classes add a full programming sequence alongside school, and graded school work stays the student's own.

### What does the lighthouse project teach?

Filtering and counting. In our download of the Maine GeoLibrary layer, 54 of 63 lighthouse records have Status active, and two lights named as historical are still marked active.
