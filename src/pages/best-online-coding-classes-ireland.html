Best online coding classes in Ireland

An Irish parent searching for coding classes meets a crowded field: free clubs, summer camps, exam grinds, recorded courses and live online classes, each promising the best. They are not the same thing, and the best one for a child in Second Class is not the best one for a Fifth Year choosing between Leaving Cert subjects or an adult changing career. This page does three things. It sets out where coding actually sits in Irish schooling, stage by stage, from the official descriptions. It gives six checks any online coding class in Ireland should pass, with the question to ask a provider for each. And it explains what each kind of option is genuinely good for, so you can choose the right one even if it is not ours. Our own answer to each check is at the end, limits included, and the first class is free so that you can test the answers yourself.

Where coding sits in Irish schools

From Junior Infants to the Leaving Cert: where coding appears, and where it does not

The descriptions below come from the Department of Education and from the NCCA's specifications on curriculumonline.ie. They are the frame any class for Irish learners should fit around.

Coding through the Irish school path
StageLengthWhere coding appears
PrimaryAn eight year cycle: junior infants, senior infants, and first to sixth classesThe NCCA's Coding in Primary Schools initiative, which the Digital Strategy for Schools names; no stand-alone coding subject
Junior CycleThree yearsThe Coding short course, an optional course a school may choose, designed for approximately 100 hours of student engagement and aligned to NFQ Level 3
Transition YearOne optional yearWherever a school builds it into its own Transition Year programme
Senior CycleTwo years, for the 15 to 18 age groupLeaving Certificate Computer Science, an optional subject designed for 180 hours of class contact time

The Coding short course aims, in the NCCA's words, to help students "formulate problems logically; to design, write and test code through the development of programs, apps, games, animations or websites". Its revised 2025 specification, introduced for first years from September 2026, has three strands: Introducing computer science, Let's get connected, and Coding at the next level, and it now includes learning to identify different AI models.

Leaving Certificate Computer Science began in 40 pilot schools in September 2018, with a first exam in 2020, and has been available nationally as an optional Senior Cycle subject since September 2020.

Availability is the catch. The Department's Inspectorate reports that the number of post-primary schools offering Computer Science "increased from 36 in 2018 to 194 schools in 2024". The Department counted 722 post-primary schools in 2024, so by our arithmetic about one in four offered the subject that year, and three in four did not.

Primary schools are more numerous still: 3,082 mainstream primary schools in 2024/25. A child's first contact with coding therefore depends heavily on which school, which teacher and which year.

Sources: Department of Education, A guide to the Irish education system; NCCA, Junior Cycle Coding short course (2025 specification); NCCA, Leaving Certificate Computer Science; Digital Strategy for Schools to 2027; Inspectorate, Post-primary insights (November 2025); Department of Education Statistical Bulletin, December 2024. Read 16 September 2026. The one-in-four share is our division of the two published figures.

The six checks

Six things the best online coding class in Ireland should do

Each check comes with the question to put to any provider, ours included. The research behind the first three is set out on our page about the best coding class in the world.

Six checks for an online coding class in Ireland, and the question to ask
CheckWhy it matters in IrelandAsk the provider
1. A live teacher, not only videosFree recorded courses are good material, but most people who start one alone do not finish.Who teaches my child, and will it be the same person each week?
2. Reading before writingThe Coding short course asks students to formulate problems logically before they design, write and test code.How is a new idea introduced: by writing code from a template, or by predicting what code does first?
3. Code read by a personA school subject marks work at the end; learning happens when someone reads it along the way.How often will a teacher read my child's code, and what feedback comes back?
4. Irish hours and the Irish yearAfter-school hours, mid-term breaks, the Junior Cycle, Transition Year and a June Leaving Cert shape every family's calendar.What time is the class in Irish time, and what happens during exam weeks?
5. AI used openly and acknowledgedIrish schools now expect AI use to be acknowledged, and exam coursework must be the student's own work.When are AI tools allowed, and how will my child learn to say what the tool did?
6. Honest evidence and a free trialA rating means little without its source and its size.Where can I read your reviews, and can we try a real class before paying?

None of these checks is exotic, and a good provider answers each one in a sentence. What they filter out is common: a course of videos presented as teaching, a class that starts every topic by copying a template, a fixed slot at an hour no Irish child is free, or a promise about Leaving Cert points that nobody can keep.

The fifth check has become more important since October 2025, when the Department of Education published guidance on AI in schools. Our page on understanding the code instead of copy-pasting sets out what it says and what it means for a learner.

For a child in primary school

A club or a camp for the spark, then a weekly class once the child wants to build more than one thing. Blocks first, typed code from about Fourth Class.

For a Junior Cycle or TY student

A weekly class that goes deeper than a short course can, whether or not the school offers one, and a real project in Transition Year.

For a Senior Cycle student

If the school offers Computer Science, a class that builds programming depth beside it; if not, the same ground at an Irish hour. Grinds, if needed, for the exam itself.

The ladder

Five rungs through the Irish school years

The school years are typical, not rules. A learner moves up when a rung is secure, which the teacher judges from the work.

Coding rungs set against Irish school stages
UsuallyRungThe learner can
First to Third Class1. BlocksBuild and explain a small game in blocks, and predict what it will do
Fourth to Sixth Class2. Typed codeWrite short Python programs and fix their own errors
Junior Cycle3. ProblemsBreak a problem down, design a solution, then write and test it
Transition Year4. A projectFinish a real app, game or analysis and show it in public
Senior Cycle and beyond5. DepthWork with algorithms, data and AI models, and judge code written by others or by AI

Why the rungs ignore the school year

Two children in Fifth Class can be two rungs apart, and a Fifth Year who starts late climbs the first rungs quickly. Placing each learner by what they can do keeps both learning.

The whole order of subjects, beyond school, is on the coding roadmap.

Around the Irish calendar

Most learners take two lessons a week and pause for mid-term breaks. The 2026 Leaving Certificate written exams ran from 3 June to 23 June, and many Sixth Years lighten their lessons in the weeks before.

Adults follow the same rungs at the pace their working week allows.

Questions from Irish families

What Irish parents and learners ask about online coding classes

What is the best online coding class in Ireland?

The one that fits the learner's stage and passes six checks: a live teacher, reading before writing, code read by a person, Irish hours, AI used openly and acknowledged, and honest evidence with a free trial.

Does my child's school teach coding?

It depends on the school. The Junior Cycle Coding short course is optional for schools, and in 2024 the Inspectorate counted 194 post-primary schools offering Leaving Certificate Computer Science out of 722.

What is the Junior Cycle Coding short course?

An NCCA short course designed for approximately 100 hours, with three strands in its 2025 version: Introducing computer science, Let's get connected, and Coding at the next level.

How many hours is Leaving Certificate Computer Science?

The specification is designed for 180 hours of class contact time. It has been available nationally as an optional Senior Cycle subject since September 2020.

Is a free coding club enough?

For a first taste, often yes. For steady progress from one level to the next, most learners also need a weekly class with the same teacher.

Are your classes grinds?

Not in the usual sense. We build programming skill and understanding. For Leaving Cert Computer Science specifically, see our page on Computer Science grinds, which explains how the two fit together.

Can my child use ChatGPT in class?

Yes, after they can predict what the code should do, and always with a record of what the tool suggested and what they changed. That is also what Irish schools now expect.

What does it cost?

The first class costs nothing. After that it is USD 100 a month for a group place or USD 150 a month for one-to-one lessons, charged in US dollars, with no enrolment fee and no yearly contract.

When are classes, in Irish time?

At a fixed weekly hour agreed in the free first class: usually after school, in the evening or on a Saturday morning. Irish time is five and a half hours behind India time in winter and four and a half in summer.

What happens after I send the form?

A member of the team rings back at a suitable Irish hour and books the free first class. Nothing is charged unless the learner continues.
