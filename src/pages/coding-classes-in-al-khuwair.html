Al Khuwair evidence file

A district where one label covers several education routes

These details come from the institutions and Muscat Municipality. They describe context, not endorsements of Modern Age Coders.

Beaconhouse: British stages with two senior routes

Beaconhouse International Private School's Al Khuwair site says it teaches the British curriculum through Key Stages 1, 2 and 3, then IGCSE at Key Stage 4. Its senior choice is A-Level or the General Education Diploma for Omani students, and the campus also offers KG1 and KG2.

The same official page publishes a current local-impact snapshot of 950 students and 160 professional teachers. Those figures show scale, but they do not tell a placement teacher whether one learner can debug a loop, explain a percentage or organise a spreadsheet. That requires direct evidence.

Philippine School Oman: a community-school history

Philippine School Oman gives an Al Khuwair address and says the school was conceived in 1989 by Filipino overseas workers, with support from the Philippine Honorary Consul General and cooperation from the Overseas Workers Welfare Administration.

A community-school grade and a British year are not interchangeable labels. The free Modern Age Coders class therefore asks for a recent task, a personal project or a short live attempt. It respects the learner's school route while measuring the separate skill the external course will teach.

College of Sharia Sciences: a documented institutional sequence

Oman's official higher-education directory traces the institution to the Institute of Sharia Judiciary, Preaching and Guidance established under Royal Decree 86/24. It records the 1999 name change to the Institute of Sharia Sciences, the first admission of women in 2000, and the move to an Al Khuwair campus in 2006.

The directory then cites Royal Decree 35/2014 establishing the College of Sharia Sciences and its regulations. That chronology is useful beyond local history: data work should preserve changing names and dates as separate fields, not flatten several institutional states into one undated label.

Public space with measurable dimensions

Muscat Municipality's Al Khuwair Square announcement specifies an 18,000-square-metre project and a 126-metre flagpole, alongside green areas, palms, walking and cycling paths, an outdoor arts and crafts area, a skate park and spaces for sport.

A separate municipality account of walkways reports a Wadi Al Khuwair route 220 metres long and 3 metres wide, running toward the beach. Dimensions can be verified. Visitor totals cannot be inferred from them.

Another local learning venue

A Ministry of Education report locates the Muscat education directorate's theatre opening at Dohat Al Adab School in Al Khuwair. It names a Scholars Hall, Science Hall, Conference Hall and Scholars Council, plus a student exhibition of 53 paintings. Named rooms and counted works are facts. Attendance still needs its own records.

Al Khuwair data project

Fifteen visits are not fifteen visitors

The project uses invented identifiers. It does not count people at the square, walkway, theatre, schools, college or ministries.

The deliberate trap

A practice event log contains 15 rows. The token AK-04 occurs 11 times; AK-12, AK-19, AK-23 and AK-31 occur once each. Counting rows reports 15 visits. Counting distinct tokens reports 5 unique visitors. Reporting 15 people would turn one repeat visitor into eleven people.

events = ["AK-04"] * 11 + [
  "AK-12", "AK-19", "AK-23", "AK-31"
]

visits = len(events)              # 15
unique_visitors = len(set(events)) # 5
repeat_visits = visits - unique_visitors # 10

The arithmetic is simple. The reasoning is not. A report must name the unit, time window, identity rule, bot exclusions and treatment of missing tokens before either number is useful.

What the learner must prove

  1. State whether the question is about traffic, people, repeat use or reach.
  2. Keep an event table separate from the derived visitor table.
  3. Use a distinct-count operation and test it against a hand-counted example.
  4. Show how resetting identifiers changes the result without changing activity.
  5. Label the sample synthetic and refuse to generalise it to Al Khuwair.

Older learners can compare cookie, login and device definitions, then explain why none is a perfect synonym for a person. Younger learners can sort coloured cards by token before translating the same logic into a set.

Stop condition

If the learner cannot explain what creates, expires or merges a token, the result must be described as distinct tokens, not distinct people. Precision is more valuable than a confident headline.

Direct answers

Al Khuwair coding class questions

Can a Beaconhouse Al Khuwair student join these coding classes?

Yes. Beaconhouse says its Al Khuwair campus follows the British curriculum through Key Stages 1 to 3, IGCSE at Key Stage 4, and A-Level or the General Education Diploma for Omani students. Modern Age Coders is independent of the school. Placement uses a live coding or mathematics task rather than assuming ability from a year-group label.

Can Philippine School Oman learners enrol?

Yes. Philippine School Oman identifies its campus in Al Khuwair and traces the school's conception to 1989. A learner can bring the current grade, a recent exercise and any personal project to the free class. The teacher checks transferable logic directly and does not claim that one external course is part of the school's own programme.

What is the Al Khuwair unique-visitors project?

It is a synthetic event-log exercise, not a claim about local footfall. Fifteen practice records contain only five distinct visitor tokens because one token appears eleven times. Learners compare the number of rows with the number of unique identifiers, explain why visits and visitors answer different questions, and document the time window and deduplication rule.

Does Modern Age Coders have an office in Al Khuwair?

No. Modern Age Coders teaches live online from India. It has no classroom, street address, branch or Omani telephone number in Al Khuwair. The displayed +91 WhatsApp number reaches the actual provider, and the first lesson is free. Families can therefore test the teacher, platform and connection from home before deciding whether to pay.

Are Al Khuwair and Al Khwair the same place?

Yes. Al Khwair is a frequent English romanisation of the same Muscat district. This page uses Al Khuwair consistently in its URL, title and service-area schema. Spelling variation should not be treated as a separate class location, campus or service. The lessons remain online regardless of which English form a family uses.

Can classes fit an Oman school or working week?

Yes, subject to teacher availability. Oman is one and a half hours behind India, so both sides confirm the recurring time explicitly. Two lessons per week normally produce eight lessons per month. Friday and Saturday options may be discussed, and a family can request a timing review when Ramadan or examination schedules materially change availability.

Can an Al Khuwair college student or working adult join?

Yes. Modern Age Coders teaches ages 6 to 67. Al Khuwair is also home to the College of Sharia Sciences, while the district contains ministry and professional activity. Adult placement can focus on Python, databases, web development, automation, data analysis or mathematics. It does not award College of Sharia Sciences or Omani institutional credit.

How much do coding classes in Al Khuwair cost?

The group plan costs USD 100 per month for eight live lessons with five to eight learners. The private plan costs USD 150 for eight lessons with one student and one teacher. Both usually meet twice weekly. The first class is free, with no enrolment charge, required materials purchase or minimum term added by Modern Age Coders.

Does the visitor-count project collect personal data?

No. The teaching version uses invented tokens and a deliberately small event list. Learners do not track visitors to Al Khuwair Square, a school, a mosque, a ministry or any other real place. The exercise also teaches that even pseudonymous identifiers require a stated purpose, limited retention and care before they are used with real people.

What is needed for the free Al Khuwair class?

Use a laptop or desktop, a microphone and stable video-capable internet. Bring a recent project, school task or mathematics solution if one exists, plus the learner's age or grade. The teacher may change an input and ask the learner to predict, test and explain the result. A tablet is limiting for file handling and code editors.
