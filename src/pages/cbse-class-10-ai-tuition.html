Where a local tutor wins

Sitting beside the student, a good tutor sees handwriting, posture and attention in a way a screen never fully does. If you find one who has taught 417 since the curriculum change and can run Orange on a laptop, that is real value.

Where recorded videos win

They are free or cheap and can be paused. For a quick recap of bag of words the night before a test, a good video is fine. They cannot mark an answer, notice a habit or ask a viva question back.

Where live online wins

A teacher who does only this subject, a shared screen that shows the student's own notebook, and no travel in a Class 10 year already full of school tests and other tuition.

Q4. Can I see the teaching?

A sample AI 417 tuition lesson: classification, regression and clustering

This is a cut-down version of a real Unit 2 Monday. Unit 2, Advanced Concepts of Modeling in AI, carries 11 theory marks, the most of any unit, and most of its lost marks come from mixing up three words.

Step 1: one sentence for each

  • Classification predicts a label from a fixed set, using labelled examples: pass or fail, spam or not spam.
  • Regression predicts a number on a continuous scale, using labelled examples: tomorrow's rainfall in millimetres.
  • Clustering groups similar items when no labels exist at all: shoppers with similar baskets.

Step 2: sort these before reading on

  1. A bank predicts whether a loan will be repaid.
    Answer Classification. Two labels, repaid or not.
  2. An app estimates a flat's rent from its size and area.
    Answer Regression. Rent is a number.
  3. A music service groups listeners by what they play, with no categories given.
    Answer Clustering. No labels, so unsupervised.
  4. A model predicts a student's board percentage from mock scores.
    Answer Regression. The output is a percentage, a number.
  5. A camera app decides whether a leaf is healthy, rusted or blighted.
    Answer Classification. Three labels is still classification.
  6. A school groups 600 students by reading habits to plan library clubs.
    Answer Clustering. The groups are discovered, not given.

Question 5 is the one most students miss: they think classification means only two classes.

Step 3: a four-mark answer, marked

Question. Differentiate between supervised and unsupervised learning with one example of each. (4)

More answers in this shape, for every unit, are on CBSE Class 10 AI important questions, and the side-by-side comparisons examiners like to ask are collected on difference between questions.

Q5. How is progress checked?

How online AI tuition for CBSE marks work, runs mocks and handles doubts

Every written answer is marked against the way CBSE's marking schemes award points, every mock is reviewed in class, and doubts are taken live rather than sent to a queue. The steps below are the same in the batch and in one to one; only the pace differs.

  1. Answers are written, then marked on screen

    Students write their answer first. The teacher then shows the point-by-point marking, as in the Unit 2 example above, and the student rewrites any answer that lost a mark.

  2. Unit tests feed a weak-unit list

    Marks are logged by unit. The unit with the most lost marks is the one that comes back in the next mixed practice, with new numbers and new scenarios.

  3. Full mocks in the paper's shape

    From December, mocks follow the 2025-26 sample paper layout: 24 marks of objective items and 26 of written answers, two hours, with the choices inside each section. The 2026-27 sample paper for 417 is not out yet.

  4. The practical is rehearsed, not assumed

    Practical mocks give an unseen program on Units 4 to 7, then a short viva on the student's own file and project.

  5. Doubts first, then new work

    A doubt raised in class is worked on the shared screen at once. Doubts from set work open the next class before anything new is taught.

How a doubt is actually answered, with worked examples, is shown on Class 10 AI doubt classes. The full mock calendar is on the sample paper and mock tests page, and families weighing a private teacher should read the Class 10 AI private tutor page.

Questions about Class 10 AI tuition

What parents ask before choosing class 10 AI tuition

Is tuition needed for class 10 AI?

Not always. A student whose school teaches 417 well, including Python in Jupyter and the Orange tool, may only need practice papers. Tuition helps when the school covers theory but rushes the practical, when written answers keep losing marks, or when the student has no one to rehearse the viva with. Half of the 100 marks are practical, so that gap matters.

How do I find a good AI 417 tutor?

Ask three things: which units are practical only in 2026-27 (Units 4 and 7), whether they teach Orange Data Mining and Python in Jupyter, and whether they can show a marked four-mark answer. A tutor who knows the current unit list and marks against the scheme is worth trying. Book a trial class before paying for a term.

Is there AI tuition near me for CBSE Class 10?

In most cities, AI 417 tutors are fewer than maths or science tutors, and many teach general computers rather than this subject. Online tuition removes the distance problem: the same live class reaches a student in any city or country. If you do find a local tutor, check they know the 2026-27 unit list.

Is online AI tuition as good as offline for CBSE?

For 417 it often works better, because the practical is done on a computer anyway: the teacher sees the student's own notebook or Orange workflow on the shared screen and fixes it live. Offline tuition is stronger for students who need someone beside them to stay focused. The free demo is the honest test.

How much does class 10 AI tuition cost?

The batch and one to one fees are shown on this page in your own currency, billed monthly with no admission fee. To compare with a local tutor, divide the monthly fee by the classes in that month: eight to ten for the Monday and Wednesday batch, four for one to one in India, eight outside India.

How many classes a week is the AI tuition?

The batch meets twice a week, Monday and Wednesday at 9:00 PM IST. One to one is one private class a week in India and two outside India, on days you choose. Each class leaves a short piece of set work, and every unit ends with a test.

Does the tuition cover the practical file and project?

Yes. The file of at least 15 programs is built from the Wednesday lab work, the project is guided in stages, and both the practical viva and the project viva are rehearsed. These are 50 of the 100 marks, so they are taught, not left to the last month.

When should a Class 10 student start AI tuition?

At the start of the session gives the most even pace, with time for every unit and the file. Starting by October still leaves room for the full syllabus before December mocks. After that, a compressed plan or one to one is the realistic route.

Can my child join AI tuition in the middle of the year?

Yes. A student joining mid-year enters the batch at the unit it is on, and missed units are covered through set work and doubt time, or through a few one to one sessions if the gap is large. The mentor suggests which after the free demo.

Is the first tuition class free?

Yes. The first class is a free live demo with a teacher. Afterwards the mentor tells you which units need the most work and whether the batch or one to one suits your child. There is also a paid priority demo with a written skill report.
