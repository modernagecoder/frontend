Why a shop program at 12

Many Chiclayo children know how a bodega or a market stall works: prices, a total, change. Turning that into a program teaches lists, loops, sums and rounding, and the child already knows what the right answer should be.

It also teaches a habit we care about: checking the program against a calculation done by hand before trusting it.

Classes are in English. Young children follow by watching and copying, and they pick up the words as they use them. Mentors keep sentences short and repeat key words every week.

Secundaria

Do Chiclayo schools teach programming to teenagers?

No. Secundaria has an area called Educación para el Trabajo, focused on entrepreneurship, but no programming area. Code is where a business idea becomes something a customer can use.

What the curriculum gives

Under the regular school day, each secundaria grade has 35 teaching hours a week, two of them for Educación para el Trabajo. That area has one competency: "Gestiona proyectos de emprendimiento económico y social", managing economic and social enterprise projects.

Digital skills appear only as a transversal competency, shared by every area. Nothing in the plan asks a student to write a program, so a Chiclayo teenager with a good business idea usually has no way to build it.

What coding adds

A teenager who can build a simple website, an order form or a small app can test an idea for real: does anyone click, does anyone order? That is the step school projects rarely reach.

MINEDU has started a Python pilot, Fábrica de Programadores, for 1,000 public-school students of 4° and 5° de secundaria in Lima Norte. Students in Chiclayo who want the same path take it outside school.

Adults and business

How can coding help an adult who runs a business in Chiclayo?

Mostly by saving time and catching mistakes. You do not need to become a software engineer; a few months of Python and data work changes how you handle stock, sales and orders.

Tidy records

Turn a notebook or a messy spreadsheet into a clean table with one row per sale, then let a program add up the week, find the top sellers and flag unusual days.

Check before trusting

Learn to test a total against a hand count, to spot a date typed the wrong way, and to see when an AI tool has made something up. These habits protect money.

Use AI tools safely

Our adult courses show how to use AI assistants for code and analysis while keeping the decisions, and the checking, with you.

Adults learn in adult groups, at hours that fit around opening times. Many start with Python or data analysis and later move to databases or AI coding agents. A real Chiclayo project

When is 2042024? A puzzle in Chiclayo's business register

Before a shop, a cevichería or an office opens in Chiclayo, the provincial council checks whether that activity is allowed in that zone. The council publishes its register of those checks as open data, and its dates hide a puzzle about how computers read text.

The data

The dataset is "Certificado de compatibilidad de uso", published by the Municipalidad Provincial de Chiclayo on Peru's open-data portal under the Open Data Commons Attribution licence (ODC-By), first released on 16 March 2026 and updated on 8 June 2026. The council describes the certificate as the document in which it assesses whether an economic activity is compatible with the zoning of the property.

The file we hold, downloaded 9 November 2026, contains 1,322 rows from 2024 to 2026, with business names and tax numbers masked. Activities range from "RESTAURANTE" with a "CHIFA" alongside, to a "LOCAL DE COMIDA AL PASO - CEVICHERIA".

The puzzle

The data dictionary says the entry date, FEC_INGRESO, is written "ddmmaaaa": two digits for the day, two for the month, four for the year. But 383 of the entry dates have only seven digits, because a leading zero was dropped.

Take "2042024". Is it 2 April 2024 (2, 04, 2024) or 20 April 2024 (20, 4, 2024)? Both are real dates. Version 1 asks Python's strptime to read every entry date and see what it does with this one.

Python read 1,268 dates without a single complaint, and it read "2042024" as 20 April. It never said that 2 April was just as possible. A program that quietly chooses one meaning is more dangerous than one that stops with an error, because nobody knows to check.

Version 2 does the honest thing. Its function readings() tries every way of cutting the digits before the year into a day and a month, and keeps every cut that makes a real date. Then it uses a second column: an application must be entered on or before the day the council issues its answer, FEC_EMISION. If only one reading of the entry date comes before the issue date, that reading wins.

What happened to the 1,322 entry dates (our calculation)
ResultRowsWhat it means
One reading1,141Only one real date fits the digits
Issue date decides101Two readings, but only one comes before the issue date
Still two37Both readings are possible; the data cannot settle it
No reading43Blank, not digits, or not a real date

Data: Municipalidad Provincial de Chiclayo, Certificado de compatibilidad de uso, datosabiertos.gob.pe, ODC-By. Counts are ours. A further caution: in 664 rows, text has spilled into four unnamed columns at the end of the file, so later columns of those rows are shifted; the two date columns we use come before the spill.

Of 138 entry dates with two possible readings, the issue date settles 101. For the last 37, an honest report says "unknown between these two days" rather than picking one. That is the whole lesson: when text can mean two things, write code that finds both, use what else you know to choose, and admit what you cannot choose.

  1. Ages 8 to 10, on paper. Write today's date as eight digits, then drop every zero. Can a friend still read it? Try 1 November and 11 January.
  2. Ages 10 to 12, in Python. Write a program that takes "1112024" and prints both possible dates.
  3. Ages 13 to 15. Run Version 1 on the issue date column, FEC_EMISION. Does strptime make the same silent choice?
  4. Ages 15 and over. Count, for the 37 dates still in doubt, how many days apart the two readings are. Would the difference matter to a shop owner?
  5. Adults. Look at your own sales records. Are any dates written in a way a program could read wrongly?

What time could a Chiclayo learner have classes?

At the time you pick. Chiclayo keeps Peru time throughout the year, and the mentor works around school, work and opening hours.

Chiclayo, like all of Peru, sits at UTC minus five and has not moved its clocks for summer since 1994, according to the America/Lima entry of the time-zone database; INACAL issues the legal hour. India sits at UTC plus five and a half, so the two clocks are always ten and a half hours apart.

A shop owner might choose an hour before opening; a teenager might choose late afternoon; a child often does best on a weekend morning. You book in Chiclayo time, and because neither country changes its clocks, the slot holds all year.

Coding classes in Chiclayo: questions and answers

Are the classes in Spanish?

No. Every lesson is taught in English only, live and online. Mentors use clear, simple English, put new words on screen and adapt to each learner's accent and pace; a learner who answers in Spanish is understood and given the English words.

Do you have a centre in Chiclayo?

No. Our classes are only live online, so a learner in José Leonardo Ortiz, La Victoria, Pimentel or the centre joins in the same way from home.

What age can a child start?

Six. Young children use Scratch blocks with no typing, and most move to typed Python around nine or ten. Teenagers and adults start from zero in their own groups.

Does school in Chiclayo teach programming?

Not as a subject. Secundaria has Educación para el Trabajo, focused on entrepreneurship projects, and digital skills spread across all areas, but no programming area in the national plan of study.

I run a small business. Is there a course for me?

Yes. Most business owners start with data analysis or Python, learning to organise sales and stock records and check totals, then move on to databases or AI tools if they wish. Adults learn with adults, at hours around work.

What time are classes?

Any day, any time, flexible: choose a batch, or for a very personalised plan choose 1-on-1 at the hour that suits your family. Chiclayo keeps Peru time all year, so 09:30 in Chiclayo is 20:00 for the mentor in India.

Can students at Chiclayo universities join?

Yes. University students take Python, Java, databases and data analysis with us alongside their degree. We are not connected with the UNPRG, the USAT, the USS or any other university, and we never do assessed work for students.

How much is it, and in which currency?

The monthly fee shown on this page is in US dollars and is charged to your card in dollars; your bank converts it into soles. Visitors from India are shown rupees. The trial class is free and no card is asked for.

Is there a certificate?

Where a course includes one, the learner receives a Modern Age Coders certificate of completion. It is our own, not issued by MINEDU, a school or a university, and not a Peruvian qualification.
