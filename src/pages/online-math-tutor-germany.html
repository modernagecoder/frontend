Which maths tests does a pupil in Germany sit, and when in 2027?

German schools have few external maths tests, and they sit at fixed points. Knowing where your child stands in that sequence tells you what kind of help makes sense now.

Two things stand out in that table. The first is that Grundschule has no leaving exam at all: the KMK says plainly that no final examination is held at the end of primary school. What decides a child's next school is the school's own picture of their work over the years, so steady understanding matters more than a crash course. The second is that the first maths exam that counts toward a certificate comes in Klasse 10, and only for pupils who take the MSA or a comparable exam in their Land.

Between those points sit the Klassenarbeiten, the written tests every class teacher sets several times a year. They are where most families first notice a problem, and where a tutor helps most often: a pupil who understood fractions in English last year now meets Brüche, Kürzen and Hauptnenner in a test written in German, with ten minutes less than they need.

Counting, place value, times tables, lengths and weights, telling the time. The base everything else stands on.

Klasse 5 to 10

Fractions to quadratics, percentages, geometry and probability. The longest stretch, and where gaps grow quietly.

Oberstufe

VERA

What do VERA 3 and VERA 8 test in maths, and do the results count?

VERA (Vergleichsarbeiten) are short comparison tests written by the IQB, the Länder's institute for quality in education. Every Land uses them; some give them another name.

VERA 3, in Klasse 3

The IQB describes VERA 3 as written tests that the Länder run every year, and they are compulsory. In maths they are built on five Leitideen, the content strands of the KMK standards: Zahl und Operation; Raum und Form; Muster, Strukturen und funktionaler Zusammenhang; Größen und Messen; and Daten und Zufall.

In some Länder the same tests are called KERMIT (Kompetenzen ermitteln), Kompetenztests or Lernstandserhebungen, so the letter from school may not say VERA at all. The 2027 test window runs from 12 April to 26 May 2027; each school picks its own day inside it.

VERA 8, in Klasse 8

VERA 8 checks what pupils in Klasse 8 have reached in German, maths, English and French. The maths tests follow five Leitideen again, in their secondary form: Zahl, Raum und Form, Funktionaler Zusammenhang, Messen, and Daten und Zufall.

The 2027 window is earlier in the year, 8 February to 25 March 2027. A pupil in Klasse 8 this school year therefore has about three months before the window opens, counting from November 2026.

The most useful fact for a worried parent is this one: VERA results are not graded, and the IQB produces no rankings of schools or Länder. VERA is meant as an early check, a year before the KMK standards for Klasse 4 and Klasse 9 are due, so that a teacher can see where a class stands. It is not a test to cram for. We never sell VERA as an exam. What we do is use the five Leitideen as a checklist: if one strand is weak in Klasse 3 or 8, it will be weak in the Klassenarbeiten too.

MSA and ZP10

What is the MSA or ZP10 maths exam, and how is it different in Berlin and NRW?

At the end of Klasse 10 most Länder hold central written exams for the Mittlerer Schulabschluss, the intermediate school certificate. Maths is one of the subjects every time. Names and dates differ by Land; Berlin and Nordrhein-Westfalen show how much.

Berlin: the MSA

In Berlin a pupil at a Gymnasium reaches the MSA through results at the end of Klasse 10. The Berlin Senate names German, maths and the first foreign language as the core subjects (Kernfächer) of the MSA, which carry special weight.

The Berlin exam plan for 2026/27, dated 15 September 2026, puts the written maths exam for the MSA and the erweiterte Berufsbildungsreife on Thursday 15 April 2027, with a catch-up date on Monday 24 May 2027. Berlin's written MSA exams begin at 10:00.

Nordrhein-Westfalen: ZP10

In NRW the central exams at the end of Klasse 10 are the Zentrale Prüfungen 10, in German, maths and English, at most secondary school types and at a Gymnasium with a Klasse 10 in the lower secondary stage.

The NRW ministry has set the main ZP10 maths date for Thursday 20 May 2027 and the resit date for Thursday 3 June 2027. NRW's written ZP10 papers start at 9:00, and the ministry publishes the previous year's tasks for download.

Behind both exams stands one document. Since 2022 the KMK has published a single set of maths standards for the Erster Schulabschluss and the Mittlerer Schulabschluss (version of 23 June 2022), and those standards are binding in every Land. Besides content, they list six process competences: communicating mathematically, solving problems, modelling, representing, working with mathematical objects, and working with media. Three of those six are about words and reasoning, not only arithmetic. That is exactly where a pupil who thinks in English loses marks, and exactly what can be practised in English and then carried into German.

For the step after Klasse 10, the Abitur, our page Abitur maths in English explains the exam structure, the marks and the points table in detail. In both NRW and Berlin, the 2027 written maths Abitur falls on Wednesday 5 May 2027.

Länder

Is school maths the same in every German Land?

The goals are shared; the route is not. A family moving between Länder, or arriving from abroad, meets three kinds of difference.

  1. How long primary school lasts. Grundschule runs from Klasse 1 to Klasse 4 in most of Germany and to Klasse 6 in Berlin and Brandenburg, according to the KMK. A ten-year-old moving from Hamburg to Berlin may well start the next year in a primary school again.
  2. What the secondary schools are called. The KMK describes three secondary tracks that each Land organises in its own school types. Alongside the Gymnasium, which exists in every Land, there are names such as Integrierte Sekundarschule, Stadtteilschule, Oberschule or Gemeinschaftsschule. Switching between tracks is possible in Klasse 5 and 6.
  3. Which exams come at the end of Klasse 10. The KMK says most Länder hold central exams at Land level for the Klasse 10 certificates, but each sets its own papers, dates and rules, as Berlin and NRW show above.

What does not change is the content target. The KMK standards for primary maths, adopted in 2004 and current in the version of 23 June 2022, are binding in all Länder, and so are the standards for Klasse 10. A fraction is a fraction in Bremen and in Bavaria. That is why we can teach a pupil anywhere in Germany from one plan, and adjust only the pace, the order and the exam practice to the Land.

Practice

How does an online maths tutor build one habit? A practice set on estimating

Our own five tasks, one for each of the five strands and spread from Klasse 3 to Klasse 10. The habit is the German Überschlag: before calculating, say roughly what the answer must be. They are written by us, not taken from VERA, the MSA or ZP10.

Tasks 1 to 3

1. Größen und Messen, Klasse 3 or 4. A ribbon 3 m 40 cm long is cut into 4 equal pieces. How long is each piece? Estimate: 3 m 40 cm is less than 4 m, so each piece is less than 1 m. Calculate: 340 cm ÷ 4 = 85 cm. The estimate of "just under 100 cm" is rough, but it rules out the two classic slips, 8.5 cm and 850 cm.

2. Funktionaler Zusammenhang, Klasse 8. A tank already holds 30 litres and fills at 12 litres a minute, so V(t) = 12t + 30. When does it hold 160 litres? Estimate: 130 more litres at about 12 a minute is about 11 minutes. Calculate: 12t = 130, t = 10.83 minutes, a little under 10 minutes 50 seconds.

3. Raum und Form, Klasse 9 or 10. A 5 m ladder leans on a wall with its foot 1.4 m from the wall. How high does it reach? Estimate: the foot is close in, so the height is just under 5 m, say 4.9 m. Calculate: by Pythagoras, h = √(25 − 1.96) = √23.04 = 4.8 m.

Tasks 4 and 5

4. Zahl, Klasse 10. A town of 50,000 people grows by 3 % a year. How many live there after 5 years? Estimate: five times 3 % is 15 %, so about 57,500. Calculate: 50,000 · 1.035 ≈ 57,964. The estimate is a little low, and the reason is the lesson: each year's 3 % is taken of a larger number, so growth compounds.

5. Daten und Zufall, any Klasse from 5. Seven pupils measure the same pencil: 12.1, 11.8, 12.4, 12.0, 11.9, 12.3 and 12.2 cm. What is the mean? Estimate: every value sits close to 12, so the mean is close to 12. Calculate: 84.7 ÷ 7 = 12.1 cm.

A note on commas. A German school writes 4,8 m and 12,1 cm, with a decimal comma, where an English book writes 4.8 and 12.1. We teach in English but always show the German form too, because the test paper will use it.

Four of the five estimates land within about 2 % of the exact answer, and the first is 17.6 % high on purpose: "less than a metre" is all a Klasse 3 pupil needs to see that 850 cm cannot be right. An estimate is not meant to be the answer. It is a fence. If the calculated answer falls outside the fence, the pupil looks again before handing in the test, and that single check saves more marks in a Klassenarbeit than any trick.

  1. Say the size first. Before writing anything, say aloud roughly how big the answer must be, and in which unit.
  2. Then calculate carefully. Write each step, so a teacher can follow it and award method marks.
  3. Compare the two. If they disagree by a factor of ten, find the slip; if they disagree a little, ask why, as in task 4.

For parents

After each month the mentor tells you which strand is secure and which is not, using the same five strands as VERA, so the report means the same thing as the school's.

If a Klassenarbeit comes back with a low mark, send a photo: the next class starts from the actual mistakes, not from a fresh chapter.

For pupils

Write an estimate in the margin before every calculation, even in homework. It takes five seconds.

Keep a short word list, German on one side and English on the other: Bruch, Nenner, Gleichung, Steigung, Wahrscheinlichkeit.

Online maths tutoring in Germany: questions from families

Can my child get a maths tutor in English while attending a German school?

Yes. Modern Age Coders teaches maths live online in English to pupils at schools in Germany, from Grundschule to the Abitur. The mentor follows what the class is doing, explains it in English, and gives the German term for each idea so your child recognises it in the next test.

Are the classes in German?

No. All our classes are live and taught in English only. Our mentors speak clearly, slow down where a step is hard, and adapt to your child's pace and accent, and they always show the German word and the German way of writing numbers, such as the decimal comma.

Does VERA count toward my child's grades?

No. The IQB says VERA results are not graded and no rankings of schools or Länder are made. VERA 3 and VERA 8 show a teacher where a class stands a year before the KMK standards are due, so there is no need to cram for them.

When is VERA 8 in 2027?

The IQB gives the VERA 8 test window as 8 February to 25 March 2027, and the VERA 3 window as 12 April to 26 May 2027. Each school chooses its own day inside the window.

When is the MSA maths exam in Berlin in 2027?

The Berlin exam plan for 2026/27 sets the written maths exam for the MSA on Thursday 15 April 2027, starting at 10:00, with a catch-up date on Monday 24 May 2027.

When is the ZP10 maths exam in NRW in 2027?

The NRW school ministry has set the ZP10 maths exam for Thursday 20 May 2027, starting at 9:00, with the resit on Thursday 3 June 2027.

Do you teach for the Abitur too?

Yes, through our Abitur Mathematik in English course for Leistungskurs and Grundkurs. We teach alongside school and do not award German grades or certificates; the school course is the one that counts.

What times are possible from Germany?

Weekdays or weekends, flexible: choose your batch, or for a very personalised plan choose 1-on-1 at your convenience, weekend mornings or weekdays. India is 4 hours 30 minutes ahead of Germany in winter and 3 hours 30 minutes ahead in summer, so German afternoons are evenings for our mentors.

Who are the tutors?

Our mentors are based in India and teach live online in English. Your child keeps the same mentor from week to week, so the mentor knows the weak strand and the last Klassenarbeit.
