### New South Wales

HSC subjects taught in Sydney, Wollongong, Newcastle and beyond. Python, algorithms, OOP, project work. UAC-bound students often pair this with UNSW / USyd / Macquarie applications.

VIC

### Victoria

VCE Algorithmics (HESS) · VCE Applied Computing

VCE in Melbourne, Geelong and across regional Victoria. Algorithmics is one of the most rigorous senior secondary CS subjects in Australia. We coach it specifically.

QLD

### Queensland

QCE Digital Solutions · Information & Communication Technology

QCE in Brisbane, Gold Coast, Sunshine Coast, Cairns, Townsville. Strong Python + project focus. UQ / QUT / Griffith / Bond are the natural pathways from here.

WA

### Western Australia

WACE Applied Information Technology · Computer Science

WACE in Perth, Fremantle, Mandurah. We coach the WACE CS course content and project work. UWA / Curtin / Murdoch are the local university pathways.

SA

### South Australia

SACE Stage 1 & 2 IT · Digital Technologies

SACE in Adelaide. Defence-tech and AI-research industries nearby (AIML, DST Group). Strong U of Adelaide / Flinders / UniSA pathway, including the AI capital that Adelaide quietly is.

TAS

### Tasmania

TCE Computer Science · Information Systems & Digital Technologies

TCE in Hobart and Launceston. UTAS pathway. Smaller cohort but tightly served, flexible time slots available.

ACT

### Australian Capital Territory

ACT Senior Secondary · Information Technology

Canberra Tertiary Bound. ANU is a 15-minute drive, a serious pull. We tune the advanced track to ANU CECC / Engineering admissions specifically.

NT

### Northern Territory

NTCET in Darwin, Alice Springs, Katherine. CDU pathway. Smaller cohort but excellent slot flexibility (ACDT zone).

## We line up with what schools should already be teaching: and go deeper.

The Australian Curriculum: Digital Technologies covers F–10 with two strands, *knowledge & understanding* and *processes & production skills*. Most schools deliver the basics; we take the same content and ship real projects from it.

Foundation: Year 2

Years 3: 6

## A live 45-minute lesson, minute by minute.

The honest version. This is what one 1:1 session looks like for a Year-9 Aussie student in their third month with us.

00:00: 02:00

#### G'day + warm-up

Two minutes of "how was the week", the human bit. Mentor scrolls back the chat history; cameras on for both. Settles the student.

02:00: 08:00

#### Recap and tiny quiz

A 6-minute recall test of last lesson. Not for marks, for the brain. The mentor watches for the lightbulb moments and the cobwebs.

08:00: 18:00

#### New concept + live coding

Ten minutes of new material. Live-shared VS Code via Zoom. Mentor types two lines, student types the next two. Wrong moves welcome. Errors read aloud.

18:00: 35:00

#### Project work · the heart of the class

The student leads. The mentor coaches. This is where the real learning happens, push a feature, fix a bug, refactor a function, run a test. The mentor types nothing; only asks.

35:00: 42:00

#### Code review & reflection

Five minutes to look back. What's good? What could be cleaner? The student names one thing they're proud of. This bit matters more than it sounds.

42:00: 45:00

#### Next steps + see ya later

Mentor writes a 4-line note to the parent: what was covered, how the student felt, optional homework, plan for next class. Cameras off. Done.
