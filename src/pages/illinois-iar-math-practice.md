# Illinois IAR math practice for spring 2027

The Illinois Assessment of Readiness tests math in three units, and every one of them stops at 60 minutes. The state&rsquo;s coordinator manual calls the test strictly timed. That single fact shapes how an Illinois child should prepare: knowing the math is necessary, but so is finishing, and so is writing an explanation that a scorer can credit, because the state&rsquo;s own scoring guidance says a right answer with no work shown will not get the top score point.

## How long is the IAR math test, and how is it split up?

Three units of 60 minutes each in every grade from 3 to 8. The time is fixed: the proctor script says each section is a 60 minute timed test and the proctor stops students when time is reached.

**IAR mathematics units by grade (IAR Unit Testing Times and 2026 Test Coordinator Manual, Illinois testing portal, read 31 October 2026)**

| Grade | Unit 1 | Unit 2 | Unit 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3, 4 and 5 | Non-calculator, 60 minutes | Non-calculator, 60 minutes | Non-calculator, 60 minutes |
| 6 and 7 | Non-calculator part, then calculator part, 60 minutes in total | Calculator, 60 minutes | Calculator, 60 minutes |
| 8 | Non-calculator, 60 minutes | Calculator, 60 minutes | Calculator, 60 minutes |

### Strictly timed means strictly

The 2026 Test Coordinator Manual states that the IAR ELA and math tests are &ldquo;strictly timed, and no additional time may be permitted&rdquo;, with the exception of a documented extended time accommodation.

So pacing is a skill we teach directly. A child who spends twelve minutes on one hard question in a 60-minute unit has borrowed that time from every question after it.

### The grade 6 and 7 trap

In grades 6 and 7, unit 1 has a Part A without a calculator and a Part B with one, sharing the same 60 minutes. The proctor script warns students: &ldquo;Once you start the calculator section, you cannot return to Part A.&rdquo;

We practise the switch: finish and check Part A, decide, then move on. Leaving Part A half done to grab the calculator is a mistake a child only needs to make once in practice.

Sources: [IAR Unit Testing Times](https://il.mypearsonsupport.com/resources/training-resources/IARUnitTestingTimes.pdf) and [IAR Summative Resources](https://il.mypearsonsupport.com/iar-summative-resources/), Illinois Support Page, read 31 October 2026. The ISBE website did not respond when we tried it. We have no connection with ISBE or its testing contractor.

## Can you use a calculator on the IAR math test?

Not in grades 3 to 5. Grades 6 and 7 get a four-function calculator with square root and percentage keys in the calculator parts; grade 8 gets a scientific calculator.

**IAR calculators, reference sheets and tools by grade (Math and ELA Tools guide, Test Coordinator Manual and ISBE Math Test Scoring Expectations, Illinois testing portal)**

| Grade | Calculator | Reference sheet | Other tools |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | None, except as an accommodation | None provided | Ruler |
| 4 | None, except as an accommodation | None provided | Ruler and protractor |
| 5 | None, except as an accommodation | Illinois grade 5 sheet | Ruler and protractor |
| 6 and 7 | Four-function with square root and percentage, calculator parts only | Illinois grade sheet | Ruler and protractor |
| 8 | Scientific, calculator units only | Illinois grade 8 sheet | Ruler; protractor, tracing paper, reflection tools, straight edges and compasses optional |

### The practice-item reference sheet is different

ISBE&rsquo;s scoring expectations document points out that the reference sheets inside the practice item sets are not the Illinois sheets used on the IAR. A grade 4 practice set shows a reference sheet, but the grade 4 IAR has none.

For a fourth grader that matters: perimeter and area formulas have to be known, not looked up. We drill them as facts in grades 3 and 4.

### Built into the platform

The calculator sits in the TestNav toolbar during calculator units. Schools may also allow hand-held calculators that meet the policy, with memory cleared before and after testing.

Text-to-speech is embedded on all math tests, according to the proctor script, so a child who wants questions read aloud needs working headphones.

## How are IAR math constructed-response answers scored?

For the reasoning as well as the number. ISBE&rsquo;s scoring expectations say the correct answer with no work shown or explanation provided will not receive the top score point.

1. Our grade 5 problem. A garden bed is 3/4 of a yard wide and 2 2/3 yards long. What is its area? Explain how you found it.
2. Answer A, which we would expect to fall short. &ldquo;2 square yards. I used the calculator.&rdquo; The number is right, but there is no work, and ISBE&rsquo;s guidance says that simply stating that one used a calculator is not sufficient. In grade 5 there is no calculator anyway.
3. Answer B, written to the guidance. &ldquo;Area = width x length. 2 2/3 = 8/3. 3/4 x 8/3 = 24/12 = 2. The area is 2 square yards.&rdquo; Each step is a separate, true equation, the mixed number is converted openly, and the answer carries its unit.
4. Why we avoid run-on equations. ISBE gives the example &ldquo;3+4=7, and 7+5= 12&rdquo; as the right way and &ldquo;3+4=7+5=12&rdquo; as the wrong way, because the second line claims that 7 equals 12. Children write run-ons all the time in class; on the IAR it can cost a point.
5. Every bullet answered. The guidance tells students that the entire question, including every bullet, must be addressed for full credit. We teach children to tick off each bullet in the question as they answer it.

The garden-bed problem and both answers are ours, written to illustrate ISBE&rsquo;s public scoring expectations. They are not IAR items and we make no claim about how any real response would be scored.

Source: [ISBE Math Test Scoring Expectations for Constructed Response Questions and Reference Sheets](https://il.mypearsonsupport.com/resources/training-resources/ISBE%20Math%20Test%20Scoring%20Expectations_2.28.25_Final_1.2.pdf), read 31 October 2026.

## What does the IAR math test measure?

The Illinois Learning Standards for the grade, reported in four Areas of Readiness. The parent guide says the math tests focus on applying skills and concepts, multi-step problems that need abstract reasoning, and modeling real-world problems.

**IAR mathematics Areas of Readiness, with examples from the 2026 Math ISR expected skills (Illinois testing portal, read 31 October 2026)**

| Area of Readiness | What it means | Grade 3 example | Grade 6 example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Major Content | The most important grade-level concepts | Multiplication and division within 100; area and multiplication; fractions | Ratios and unit rates; dividing fractions by fractions; expressions and equations |
| Additional and Supporting Content | Supporting skills that strengthen the major work | Place value, perimeter, scaled graphs | Statistical variability, distributions, area and surface area |
| Expressing Mathematical Reasoning | Explaining thinking and why a solution makes sense | Equal groups, data tables, properties of operations | Variable relationships, positive and negative numbers |
| Modeling and Application | Solving real-world problems with numbers, symbols or models | One-step measurement and estimation problems | Percentage, ratio and signed-number problems |

### Higher, Middle, Lower

Each Area of Readiness gets a gauge on the report pointing to Higher, Middle or Lower. The guide explains these compare the student with students who were proficient or approaching proficient.

A Lower on Expressing Mathematical Reasoning is the most fixable rating on the report. It usually means the math is there and the writing is not.

### Item types

The parent guide says students also answered selected-response and fill-in-the-blank items, alongside the constructed responses. The Student Readiness Tool on the portal walks children through the TestNav tools and item types.

We use it once, early, so the platform is familiar; after that, lesson time goes to math.

## What are the IAR performance levels and cut scores for math?

Four levels on a scale from 650 to 850: Below Proficient, Approaching Proficient, Proficient and Above Proficient. Levels 3 and 4 mean the student has demonstrated readiness for the next grade.

**IAR mathematics cut scores by grade (IAR Parent Score Report Interpretation Guide, Appendix A, updated May 2026)**

| Grade | Level 2 Approaching Proficient from | Level 3 Proficient from | Level 4 Above Proficient from |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 705 | 732 | 781 |
| 4 | 708 | 740 | 784 |
| 5 | 709 | 740 | 782 |
| 6 | 705 | 742 | 773 |
| 7 | 712 | 745 | 781 |
| 8 | 705 | 745 | 791 |

### What the levels say

The parent guide&rsquo;s wording is direct: students at levels 3 and 4 &ldquo;were proficient or above proficient and have demonstrated readiness for the next grade level/course.&rdquo; Level 1 starts at 650, the bottom of the scale.

The guide adds that a scale score of 800 on one form of the grade 8 test should mean the same as 800 on any other form, which is why the cut points are fixed by grade.

### Reading a near miss

A grade 6 student on 738 is four points under Proficient. In our experience the quickest gains at that distance come from finishing every unit and writing complete explanations, not from new topics.

Reports reach families through the Family Portal, which, when we read it, said the PDF student reports would be available on August 10.

Source: [IAR Parent Score Report Interpretation Guide](https://il.mypearsonsupport.com/resources/reporting/parent/IAR%20Scoring%20Interpretation%20Guide%20for%20Parents%20Final.pdf), Illinois testing portal, read 31 October 2026 from the portal.

## When is the IAR test in 2027?

The online testing window for spring 2027 runs from March 1 to April 16, 2027. Paper testing, used only by students who need it, closes earlier, on April 2, 2027.

**IAR key dates, spring 2027 (IAR Summative Resources, Illinois testing portal, read 31 October 2026)**

| Event | Dates |
|---|---|
| IAR spring 2027 testing window, online | March 1, 2027 to April 16, 2027 |
| IAR spring 2027 testing window, paper | March 1, 2027 to April 2, 2027 |
| Training administration live | December 22, 2026 |
| Manuals due in districts | January 11 to January 14, 2027 |

The manual says sections may be scheduled at any time on regular school days during the testing window, and all sections, including make-ups, must finish inside it. Because the window opens on March 1, a plan that starts after spring break is too late. Ask the school which week your child&rsquo;s math units fall in.

## How to prepare for the IAR math test in eight weeks

Our plan, counted back from the start of the March 1 to April 16, 2027 window, so it begins in early January.

1. Week 1: one timed unit. Sixty minutes of our own grade-level questions, to see both accuracy and how far the child gets.
2. Weeks 2 and 3: Major Content. The grade&rsquo;s most important concepts from the Areas of Readiness table.
3. Week 4: explanations. Two constructed-response questions every lesson, written to ISBE&rsquo;s scoring expectations: separate equations, labels and units, every bullet answered.
4. Week 5: the calculator rule for the grade. No calculator at all for grades 3 to 5; the Part A to Part B switch for grades 6 and 7; scientific calculator fluency for grade 8.
5. Week 6: pacing. Timed half-units, with a rule for when to move on from a hard question.
6. Week 7: the official practice items. The grade&rsquo;s set on the portal, plus the Student Readiness Tool if not done yet.
7. Week 8: three units on three days. A full rehearsal at 60 minutes each, then a light week.

### Two habits that earn points

One equation per line, and a final sentence that answers the question with a unit. Both come straight from ISBE&rsquo;s guidance.

### One habit that loses them

Running out of time with easy questions unanswered at the end of a unit. Pacing practice fixes this faster than anything else.

## Illinois IAR math: questions from parents

### When is the IAR test in 2027?

The Illinois testing portal lists the spring 2027 online window as March 1 to April 16, 2027, and paper testing from March 1 to April 2, 2027. Schools schedule their own units inside the window.

### Is the IAR still called the IAR?

Yes. The official Illinois testing portal, which hosts the 2027 key dates, calls it the Illinois Assessment of Readiness. It covers ELA and math in grades 3 to 8.

### How long is the IAR math test?

Three units of 60 minutes each in every grade from 3 to 8. The coordinator manual says the test is strictly timed and no additional time may be given beyond a documented extended time accommodation.

### Is a calculator allowed on the IAR math?

Third, fourth and fifth graders have no calculator unless it is an accommodation. Grades 6 and 7 use a four-function calculator with square root and percentage in calculator parts, and grade 8 a scientific calculator.

### What is a good score on the IAR math?

Level 3, Proficient, is the target the state treats as ready for the next grade. In grade 5 math, Proficient starts at 740 and Above Proficient at 782, on a scale from 650 to 850.

### What are the four IAR levels?

Level 1 Below Proficient, Level 2 Approaching Proficient, Level 3 Proficient and Level 4 Above Proficient. The parent guide says levels 3 and 4 show readiness for the next grade level or course.

### Does my child need to show work on the IAR math?

On constructed-response questions, yes. ISBE's scoring expectations say a correct answer with no work or explanation will not receive the top score point, and saying you used a calculator is not enough.

### Is there a reference sheet on the IAR math test?

In grades 5 to 8 there is an Illinois reference sheet. Grades 3 and 4 have none, even though some practice sets show one, so those formulas need to be known.

### Where can I find IAR math practice tests?

The Illinois portal's Practice Items page has items for each grade from 3 to 8 with answer keys, plus the Student Readiness Tool for the TestNav platform.
