# Are IntelliJ IDEA and PyCharm free for students?

Short answer: yes, in two different ways. JetBrains has merged the old free Community editions into single products, so the core of IntelliJ IDEA and of PyCharm is now free for anyone. On top of that, students at an accredited high school, college or university can apply for a free educational license that unlocks the paid tier as well. The details matter, though: there is an age floor, a definition of who counts as a student, rules on commercial use, and a yearly renewal. This page sets out what JetBrains&rsquo; own documents say, then covers the setup problems students hit next, such as a missing interpreter or JDK.

## The free core, the trial and the paid tier

Older guides talk about Community and Ultimate, or Community and Professional. That split has gone. Here is how JetBrains describes the products now.

**IntelliJ IDEA and PyCharm editions, from the JetBrains installation guides (read 17 October 2026)**

|  | IntelliJ IDEA | PyCharm |
|---|---|---|
| Merged into one product | Starting with IntelliJ IDEA 2025.3 | Starting with PyCharm 2025.1 |
| Free for everyone | &ldquo;The core functionality of IntelliJ IDEA remains free&rdquo; | &ldquo;PyCharm&rsquo;s core features remain free and open-source.&rdquo; |
| Included in the free core | More features than the former Community Edition | Jupyter support is now part of the core |
| Trial on install | A free 30-day trial of the Ultimate subscription | A 30-day free Pro trial |
| After the trial | Subscribe, or continue using IntelliJ IDEA for free | Get a Pro subscription, or keep using the core features for free |
| Free paid tier for students | Yes, through the educational license | Yes, through the educational license |

### Do you need the student license at all?

For most high school work, probably not. An AP Computer Science A course, a first Python class or a school project runs comfortably on the free core of either IDE, and nothing has to be renewed.

The license is worth having when a course or project needs the advanced tools in the paid tier, which JetBrains lists in its comparison matrix. College students in a computer science major are the usual case.

### What happens when the trial ends

Both installation guides say the same thing in different words: when the 30-day trial ends, you can subscribe to keep the advanced features or simply carry on with the free core. Nothing you wrote is lost either way.

If you hold a student license, the paid tier stays switched on for as long as the license is valid, which is the next section.

## Who qualifies for the free JetBrains student license

The rules are in two places: the license agreement itself, which is the legal document, and JetBrains&rsquo; sales help center, which explains it in plain words. They agree.

**Educational license rules, from the Toolbox Subscription Agreement for Students and Teachers (Version 4.2, effective as of May 01, 2024) and the JetBrains sales help center (read 17 October 2026)**

| Question | What JetBrains says |
|---|---|
| Minimum age | A student or instructor &ldquo;who is at least 13 years old&rdquo; |
| Does high school count? | Yes: the agreement names university, college and high school; the help center says accredited high schools, colleges and universities |
| How long the program must be | Full-time study, or a vocational program lasting no less than 1 year; the help center says one or more years of full-time study |
| Proof | You must be able to show your active academic status for the current academic year if JetBrains asks |
| What you may use it for | Non-commercial, educational purposes only |
| Part-time jobs and internships | Not covered: the help center says strictly for non-commercial educational purposes |
| How many computers | It can be used on multiple machines, for example a classroom computer and a laptop at home |
| When you stop being a student | The agreement says to discontinue use of the licensed products |

### Students under 13

Middle schoolers younger than 13 do not meet the agreement&rsquo;s age floor, so the student license is not for them. The free core of PyCharm is the usual route for a younger student who wants a full Python IDE, and a parent should read JetBrains&rsquo; general terms with them before installing.

For most children under 13, though, a lighter editor or a browser tool is a better fit than a professional IDE. That is a teaching view of ours, not a JetBrains rule.

### What the student pack says it includes

JetBrains describes its free student pack as access to all JetBrains IDEs, plugins and courses for your studies. Its page loads through scripts, so we quote only its own summary here.

Some JetBrains products include AI features with terms of their own. We have not reviewed those terms for students under 18, so we do not recommend them on this page; check the age rules for any AI feature before switching it on.

Sources: [Toolbox Subscription Agreement for Students and Teachers](https://www.jetbrains.com/legal/docs/toolbox/license_educational/); JetBrains sales help center, [who is eligible](https://sales.jetbrains.com/hc/en-gb/articles/11557849416594-Who-is-eligible-for-free-individual-educational-licenses), [part-time work](https://sales.jetbrains.com/hc/en-gb/articles/11564854623378-I-m-a-student-and-I-work-part-time-Can-I-use-my-free-educational-license-for-my-work-apprenticeship-or-internship-projects) and [several computers](https://sales.jetbrains.com/hc/en-gb/articles/11564856632338-Can-I-use-my-educational-license-on-several-computers); all read 17 October 2026.

## Applying for the license, and keeping it each year

JetBrains lists three ways to prove you are a student. Which one works depends on your school.

### When a school email does not work

JetBrains has two help articles for this. If the domain is not recognized, it can be submitted to the swot repository. If the domain is blocked by JetBrains&rsquo; automated system, the article says to use the GitHub or ISIC/ITIC tabs at the top of the application form instead.

Some school accounts also cannot receive email from outside the district. That is a setting at your school, not at JetBrains, so a teacher or the IT office is the right person to ask.

### Keep it honest

The agreement asks for information that is complete and accurate, including real names, and says JetBrains may ask for proof of student status. Do not borrow a sibling&rsquo;s college email; the license belongs to the person studying.

Use it for coursework and your own learning, and not for paid work, which the help center rules out.

Sources: JetBrains sales help center, [how to apply](https://sales.jetbrains.com/hc/en-gb/articles/11558649766674-How-do-I-apply-for-a-free-educational-license), [institutional email not accepted](https://sales.jetbrains.com/hc/en-gb/articles/11559296363282-My-institutional-email-address-is-not-accepted-Can-I-still-apply), [no institutional email](https://sales.jetbrains.com/hc/en-gb/articles/19480024581650-I-don-t-have-an-official-institutional-email-address-How-should-I-apply-for-a-license) and [renewing for another year](https://sales.jetbrains.com/hc/en-gb/articles/11564534574994-How-can-I-renew-my-free-educational-license-for-another-year), read 17 October 2026.

### Java students: the IDE is not the JDK

The IntelliJ IDEA guide is precise about this. You do not need to install Java to run the IDE, because JetBrains Runtime is bundled with it, but to develop Java applications a standalone JDK is required.

You do not have to hunt for one. When you create a project or open its SDK settings, IntelliJ IDEA offers Download JDK, where you choose a vendor and a version and let the IDE install it.

### Python students: the IDE is not Python

The PyCharm documentation says you need to configure at least one Python interpreter to work with your code. That can be the Python already on your computer or a virtual environment made with tools such as Virtualenv, uv or conda.

JetBrains recommends its Toolbox App for installing and updating its IDEs, which is handy if a course asks for a specific version.

## Package not found, wrong JDK, and an IDE acting strangely

When PyCharm or IntelliJ IDEA seems not to be working in a new project, ask one question before any other: which Python, or which Java, is actually running the code? Here is how to find out.

### About Invalidate Caches

IntelliJ IDEA&rsquo;s help explains that it caches files for every project you have opened, that the caches are not deleted until you restart, and that the files are rebuilt the next time you open a project.

It also notes that Local History, the IDE&rsquo;s own record of your edits, has a retention period of 5 working days by default and is not deleted unless you choose that option. Leave that box unticked.

## IntelliJ IDEA and PyCharm for students: common questions

### Is IntelliJ IDEA free for students?

Yes. Since IntelliJ IDEA 2025.3, its core functionality is free for everyone, with a 30-day trial of the paid Ultimate tier on install. Students aged 13 or older at an accredited school can also apply for a free educational license that covers the paid tier.

### Is PyCharm free for students?

Yes. Since PyCharm 2025.1, Community and Professional are one product whose core features, including Jupyter support, are free and open-source. A 30-day Pro trial comes with the install, and eligible students can get the Pro tier through the free educational license.

### Can high school students get the JetBrains student license?

Yes, if they are at least 13. The license agreement names high schools alongside colleges and universities, and the JetBrains help center says students at accredited high schools are welcome to apply.

### How do I apply for the JetBrains student license?

JetBrains lists three ways: an email address from your school, an ISIC or ITIC card, or a GitHub Student Developer Pack through Authorize with GitHub. Apply through the student form on the JetBrains site.

### What if my school email is not accepted?

JetBrains says your school's email domain can be submitted to the swot repository it uses to recognize schools. Once the domain is accepted you can apply. Meanwhile, an ISIC or ITIC card or the GitHub Student Developer Pack are alternatives.

### How long does the student license last?

It runs for a subscription period and can be renewed for another year while you are still studying full-time. JetBrains sends a reminder email 2 weeks before it expires, with a renewal link.

### Can I use the student license for a part-time job or freelance work?

No. The JetBrains help center says free educational licenses are strictly for non-commercial educational purposes, and the license agreement says the same.

### Why does PyCharm say No module named when I installed the package?

The package was most likely installed for a different Python interpreter. Packages belong to an interpreter, so check the interpreter selector in the status bar and install the package in the Python Packages tool window for that interpreter.

### Do I need to install Java for IntelliJ IDEA?

Not to run the IDE, which bundles JetBrains Runtime. To write Java programs you need a JDK, and IntelliJ IDEA can download one for you from the SDK settings with Download JDK.
