An IB maths tutor for Diploma students in Peru

Who it is for

Who needs an IB maths tutor in Peru?

More students than most people expect. MINEDU reports that 2,502 students in Peru earned the IB Diploma in 2025, and that students of the public COAR network made up 47% of them.

COAR students

Pupils who won a place at one of the 25 public Colegios de Alto Rendimiento can take the Diploma Programme there. Most come from Spanish-medium schools. For a COAR student who wants IB maths in English, perhaps with a university abroad in mind, a tutor who teaches only in English adds the language of international mathematics to the subject.

Private IB school students

Students at private IB schools in Lima and other cities often study in English from an early age. For them the question is depth: an HL student aiming at engineering, or an AI student who finds the statistics slippery, needs time with one teacher that a full class cannot give.

Families arriving or leaving

Families who move to Peru mid-Diploma, or who plan to send a student to university abroad, need the maths to stay continuous whatever the school calendar does. Online tutoring travels with the student.

Everything on this page assumes the school leads. The IB school teaches the course, sets the internal deadlines and submits the work; our tutors work alongside, in English, on the mathematics and the habits of writing it. We are not connected with the IB, COAR or MINEDU, and nothing here is advice from them.

What the table means

A Lima student taking the Diploma and applying through a school-leaver route may face a university exam, the IB papers and the end of the school year inside about five weeks. That is the November squeeze, and it is the reason we plan IB maths backwards from it.

Not every student meets every date: routes and universities differ, and some students wait for their Diploma results and apply through a Diploma route instead. Each university decides its own admissions; we only point out where the dates sit.

How a tutor plans around it

The heavy lifting moves earlier. We aim to finish new syllabus content by the July break, spend August to October on full papers and corrections, and keep November for short, calm sessions that rehearse the student's weakest question types.

Students who also sit a university exam in Spanish get a different kind of help from us: not that exam's content, which we do not teach, but the same habits of reading a question twice, estimating first and leaving a guess blank where wrong answers cost marks.

COAR and the IB

How do public-school students in Peru reach the IB Diploma?

Mainly through COAR, MINEDU's network of 25 public high-achievement schools. Students win a place after 2nd of secundaria and can take the Diploma Programme in their final two years.

Getting in

MINEDU's single admission process for 2026 offered 2,650 places to students from public schools who had finished 2nd of secundaria. Registration was online and free, from 12 to 30 January 2026.

MINEDU describes COAR as a specialised service with personal tutoring, a student residence, meals and materials. It also says that the Diploma Programme widens students' access to more than 2,000 universities in Peru and abroad; that figure is MINEDU's statement, not ours.

How the 2025 class did

The COAR students assessed in the November 2025 session had followed the Diploma Programme through 2024 and 2025. Of 2,296 who took part, 1,740 entered for the full Diploma and 1,179 earned it; another 556 entered for individual course certificates and 390 earned them.

By our own division, that is about 68% for the Diploma and 70% for course certificates. MINEDU adds that ten COARs, in Arequipa, Cajamarca, Ica, La Libertad, Lambayeque, Lima, Lima Provincias, Piura, Puno and San Martín, saw more than 70% of their Diploma candidates succeed.

Ages help with planning. MINEDU's normative ages put a student in 4th of secundaria at 15 and in 5th at 16, measured on 31 March, so a typical Peruvian Diploma student sits the final exams at 16 or 17. Inside a school week, the national curriculum gives maths four periods of 45 minutes on the regular day and six on the full day; IB schools organise their own Diploma timetables on top of that.

AA or AI

Which IB maths course should a student in Peru take, AA or AI?

That choice belongs to the student, the school and, often, the university course the student hopes to enter. What we can give is our own description of how the two feel to study, as our course teaches them.

Analysis and Approaches

In our course, AA is the algebra-and-calculus route: proof, functions, sequences and calculus done by hand. As our course page describes it, AA includes a paper sat without a calculator, so fluent manipulation is a skill we train on purpose. It tends to suit students heading for engineering, physics, maths or economics.

Applications and Interpretation

In our course, AI leans on modelling and statistics, with the graphic display calculator used throughout. The hard part is not the algebra; it is deciding which model fits and saying in clear English what a number means. It tends to suit students heading for social sciences, biology, business or design.

SL or HL

Higher Level adds depth and an extra paper. Our honest advice is sometimes "stay at SL", when a student's other subjects need the time. The university's own entry requirements, not our view, should settle the question; check them before choosing.

A note on sources. Every description above is ours, based on our course. We could not read the IB's own subject guides for this page because ibo.org refused our request, and we did not try to get round that. For the exact syllabus, the weight of each paper and the exploration rules, the school's IB coordinator is the right person to ask.

A real lesson

What does IB maths tutoring look like? The derivative as a limit, in Python

This is a lesson we use with AA students before they meet differentiation from first principles, and with AI students who want to know what their calculator is doing. It shows a limit being approached, predicts the error with algebra, and ends with a surprise about computers.

The set-up

Take f(x) = x³ at x = 2. The derivative is 3x², so the exact gradient there is 12. A difference quotient estimates it from two nearby points: the forward quotient uses x and x + h; the central quotient uses x - h and x + h.

Version 1 computes both for h = 0.1, 0.01 and 0.001. Before running it, we ask the student to expand (2 + h)³ by hand and predict the forward quotient exactly.

The algebra that predicts it

Expanding gives a forward quotient of 12 + 6h + h², so its error shrinks in step with h. The central quotient simplifies to 12 + h², so its error shrinks with h squared: ten times smaller h, a hundred times smaller error.

When the output matches the algebra to every printed digit, the student has seen the definition of the derivative behave exactly as the theory says. That is a far stronger memory than a formula in a textbook.

Now the question every good student asks: if smaller h is better, why not make h tiny? Version 2 tries h from 10-4 down to 10-15 with the forward quotient.

The error falls until h is about 10-8, then rises again, and at 10-15 the estimate is off by more than one whole unit. The computer stores numbers to about sixteen significant digits, so subtracting two almost equal values of f throws most of those digits away. A limit is a mathematical idea, not a button you can press forever.

For an AA student this is first principles made visible, with the algebraic error terms checked by experiment. For an AI student it explains why a calculator's numerical derivative is an approximation and how to judge it. For both, it is the kind of question, "where does this method stop working?", that turns an ordinary exploration into a thoughtful one, provided the student chooses and writes it themselves.

  1. Before the Diploma. Compute (f(2 + h) - f(2))/h by hand for f(x) = x² and h = 0.1. What number is it heading towards?
  2. SL. Expand (2 + h)³ and show that the forward quotient equals 12 + 6h + h². Explain in English why the error is roughly proportional to h.
  3. HL. Show that the central quotient for x³ at any x is 3x² + h². Then predict which h gives the smallest error on a computer, and test it.
  4. AI students. Repeat Version 1 with f(x) = eˣ at x = 0, where the exact answer is 1, and report the errors in a table with one honest sentence of interpretation.

What a tutor does

What does an IB maths tutor do across the two Diploma years?

Less lecturing than parents imagine and more careful practice. A tutor's value is in the gaps a school class has no time for, and in the written English that the papers reward.

  1. Repair the base early. Algebra, functions and trigonometry from the MYP or from secundaria are checked in the first weeks. A weak base costs marks in every later topic.
  2. Follow the school's order. We teach topics when the school teaches them, so tutoring deepens class work instead of competing with it.
  3. Train each paper differently. As our course teaches it, AA work includes non-calculator fluency and AI work includes efficient calculator routines. Both include writing working that an examiner can follow.
  4. Teach the command words. "Show that", "hence", "justify" and "interpret" each ask for something specific. We practise answering exactly what was asked, in full English sentences where needed.
  5. Support the exploration honestly. We teach the mathematics a student's idea needs, the assessment criteria and how to critique a draft of their own. We never write, edit or ghost-write the real exploration; the school's rules decide what help is allowed.
  6. Rehearse under time. From August of the final year, full timed papers, then careful correction of every lost mark, sorted by cause: knowledge, method, reading or rushing.
  7. Keep November calm. In the final weeks, short sessions on the student's three weakest question types, and nothing new.

One tutor stays with the student across both years. That matters in IB maths, where the same student may be confident in calculus and lost in probability, and a teacher who knows their pattern of mistakes saves months.

Integrity

Will an IB maths tutor do the work for my child?

No, and a tutor who would is putting the Diploma at risk. We teach students to think through problems and to own every line they submit.

The student writes

In every session the student holds the pen on the shared board. Explanations come after an attempt, not instead of one.

AI with care

Most Diploma students are under 18. Claude's consumer terms require users to be at least 18, and OpenAI requires under-18s to have a parent's permission for ChatGPT. Students who do use AI with permission should use it to test their reasoning, never to produce assessed work.

One standard, every city

A COAR student in Puno and a private-school student in Miraflores get the same tutors and the same expectations. The internet removes the distance, not the standard.

Questions from Peru

IB maths tutor in Peru: questions and answers

Are the classes in Spanish?

No. All our classes are live, online and taught only in English, so a student who sits IB maths in English practises in the language of the exam. Tutors speak clearly, adapt to each student's pace and accent, and write key terms on the shared board.

Do you tutor both AA and AI, at SL and HL?

Yes. Our IB course covers Analysis and Approaches and Applications and Interpretation at both levels, and a tutor works on the course and level the student is actually taking.

Are you approved or endorsed by the IB?

No. We are an independent online school and are not connected with or endorsed by the International Baccalaureate, COAR, MINEDU or any school. We teach alongside the student's IB school, which teaches, sets deadlines and submits assessed work.

Will you help with the IB maths exploration?

We teach the mathematics a student's idea needs, the assessment criteria and how to critique their own draft. We never write, edit or ghost-write the exploration itself, and the school's IB coordinator decides what help is allowed.

When are the IB exams for students in Peru?

It depends on the school. MINEDU's report on COAR results refers to the November 2025 session, which fits Peru's March to December school year, but each school chooses its session. Exact paper dates for any year come from the school's IB coordinator; we do not print them here.

What time would tutoring be?

Any day, any time, flexible: choose a batch, or for a very personalised plan choose 1-on-1 at the hour that suits your family. Peru is 10 hours 30 minutes behind India all year, so a 19:30 session in Lima is 06:00 the next morning for the tutor.

Does the IB Diploma help with university entry in Peru?

Some universities publish Diploma routes. The Universidad de Lima admits Diploma holders who apply within three years of earning it, and PUCP has a Diploma de Bachillerato route that can recognise up to 23 credits, or 36 after an extra Bachillerato year. Each university decides; we promise no admission.

Can you prepare me for a Peruvian university admission exam?

Not as an exam course: those exams are in Spanish and set by each university. Our tutoring strengthens the mathematics and the habits of careful reading and estimation underneath any exam.

When should tutoring start?

Ideally at the start of the first Diploma year, or in 3rd of secundaria for a student whose algebra needs work. Students who start in the final year can still gain a lot, but there is less time to repair the base.

How are fees paid from Peru?

By card, in US dollars; your bank converts each payment into soles. The first class is free and booking it needs no card.
