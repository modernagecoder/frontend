More than fifteen of them Over 15 castles stand within the boundary, each described as a testament to the past. Crafts that are still worked Skilled craftsmen here continue making daggers, jewellery, pottery and objects from palm fronds. FAQ Wadi Al Maawil coding class questions Local facts, teaching method and terms of service, each answered separately.
Are the castle figures real? The wilayat is published as having over 15 castles, with Al Safala Castle carrying its emblem, and those are used as published. The fifteen floor areas in the project are teaching values, because no survey of them was found, and the page labels them wherever they appear.
Why is the famous example such a bad one to measure? Because fame is a selection process and it does not select for typical. A site becomes the emblem by being unusual, so the very property that made it easy to find is the property that makes it unrepresentative. The easier a case was to obtain, the more carefully its selection needs stating.
Is there a Modern Age Coders centre in Wadi Al Maawil? No. No premises are claimed in Wadi Al Maawil or anywhere in Al Batinah South. Classes are live online and need a laptop or desktop, working audio, a current browser and a stable connection. The page describes where the service reaches, not a building.
Why does the page not give one population figure? Because two references disagree. One gives 20,206 for the 2020 census and a population database gives 20,174 for the same census, a gap of 32 people. Both are stated and neither is adopted, because a page about choosing evidence badly should not quietly pick a side.
Do you teach adults here as well as school students? Yes, to an upper age of 67. The adult track runs through Python, analytics and reporting. Anyone who has ever sized a whole market from the one customer they could get a meeting with has already run this experiment. Where the figures are yours to bring, they work better than ours.
What is a learner left holding at the end? A sample with its selection method written beside it, a summary that prints mean and median together, and a refusal to scale from a single case without a stated range. Those three travel with them into any tool.

Private tuition is one learner with one teacher. If no suitable group is ready, a learner can discuss private tuition or wait for an appropriate cohort.

Friday and Saturday options may be discussed subject to availability. School, work and travel schedules, examinations and Ramadan routines should be mentioned before confirmation.
