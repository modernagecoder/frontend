## Fifteen castles and one that stands for all of them

A wilayat with one famous landmark and many quiet ones is a wilayat where the available evidence and the representative evidence are different things.

### One building on the emblem

Al Safala Castle carries the emblem of this ancient wilayat and takes centre stage in every account of the place. Being chosen as the symbol is precisely what disqualifies it from standing in for the average.

### More than fifteen of them

Over 15 castles stand within the boundary, each described as a testament to the past. Fifteen is small enough to measure properly and large enough that nobody bothers, which is the size at which sampling errors live.

### Crafts that are still worked

Skilled craftsmen here continue making daggers, jewellery, pottery and objects from palm fronds. Four named crafts described in one sentence is four categories somebody will eventually try to count.

Availability is not representativeness

The castle with a road to it, a photograph and a paragraph written about it is the castle you will measure. Nothing about that process selects for typical. It selects for notable, and notable is the opposite of typical by definition.

## Twenty seven thousand square metres of castle that does not exist

One measurement, taken carefully, from the one site anybody would pick, scaled up the obvious way, and wrong by nearly a factor of five.

### 1. Measure the castle you can actually get to

Al Safala, the one on the emblem, with a road and a description. Say it is 1,800 square metres. The floor areas throughout this project are teaching values, since no survey of them is published, and the page says so.

### 2. Multiply by the number of castles

Fifteen castles at 1,800 square metres each gives 27,000 square metres of historic floor area in the wilayat. Every step of that is arithmetic nobody would query.

### 3. Measure the other fourteen and add them up

The real total is 5,740. The estimate is 370 per cent too high. Al Safala on its own is 31 per cent of all the floor area in the wilayat, and only four of the fifteen castles are even above the mean.

| Approach | Figure | Error | Why somebody would do it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Measure all fifteen | 5,740 sq m | none, this is the truth | Slow and nobody funds it |
| Scale up the emblem | 27,000 sq m | 370 per cent too high | It is the one with a road |
| Scale up the median | 3,900 sq m | 32 per cent too low | Sounds cautious, still wrong |
| Al Safala as a share | 31 per cent of the whole | n/a | One site, a third of the total |
| Castles above the mean | 4 of 15 | n/a | The mean describes almost none of them |
| Mean against median | 383 against 260 | 47 per cent apart | The gap is the warning |

### The mean and the median disagreeing is the alarm you already had

Nothing in this data hides. A mean of 383 beside a median of 260 says plainly that something large is pulling the average, and only four of fifteen sitting above the mean says it again. Both numbers were available before anybody extrapolated. The failure is not statistical subtlety, it is not looking. Every figure above was computed before this was written.

## Say how the sample was chosen, before you say what it shows

Five habits, each quick, and any one of them stops the extrapolation.

| Stage | Rule | What it prevents | What it gives you |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Declare | Write down how this item was selected | Convenience passing as randomness | A sentence a reader can attack |
| Compare | Print the mean beside the median | A skew nobody looked for | A one-glance warning |
| Count | Say how many items beat the mean | An average describing almost nothing | The shape of the distribution in one number |
| Bound | Give a range rather than a point when extrapolating | One tidy figure quoted onward | An honest span |
| Resist | Refuse to scale from a single item at all | Fifteen times the wrong number | A measurement instead of a guess |

## Wadi Al Maawil coding class questions

Local facts, teaching method and terms of service, each answered separately.

### Are the castle figures real?

The wilayat is published as having over 15 castles, with Al Safala Castle carrying its emblem, and those are used as published. The fifteen floor areas in the project are teaching values, because no survey of them was found, and the page labels them wherever they appear.

### Why is the famous example such a bad one to measure?

Because fame is a selection process and it does not select for typical. A site becomes the emblem by being unusual, so the very property that made it easy to find is the property that makes it unrepresentative. The easier a case was to obtain, the more carefully its selection needs stating.

### Is there a Modern Age Coders centre in Wadi Al Maawil?

No. No premises are claimed in Wadi Al Maawil or anywhere in Al Batinah South. Classes are live online and need a laptop or desktop, working audio, a current browser and a stable connection. The page describes where the service reaches, not a building.

### Why does the page not give one population figure?

Because two references disagree. One gives 20,206 for the 2020 census and a population database gives 20,174 for the same census, a gap of 32 people. Both are stated and neither is adopted, because a page about choosing evidence badly should not quietly pick a side.

### Do you teach adults here as well as school students?

Yes, to an upper age of 67. The adult track runs through Python, analytics and reporting. Anyone who has ever sized a whole market from the one customer they could get a meeting with has already run this experiment. Where the figures are yours to bring, they work better than ours.

### What is a learner left holding at the end?

A sample with its selection method written beside it, a summary that prints mean and median together, and a refusal to scale from a single case without a stated range. Those three travel with them into any tool.
