Nearly everyone online

In 2025, 89.1% of people aged six and over in the department used the internet, slightly above Lima Metropolitana's 89.0% in the same table and well above Peru's 81.1%. Seven in ten Ica homes had an internet service.

For a teenager in Ica, Parcona or La Tinguiña, the connection is rarely the obstacle to a live class.

Fewer computers than phones

Only 36.9% of Ica homes had a computer in 2025, a little lower than in 2015. Much of that online life happens on phones.

Coding needs a keyboard. A teenager without a computer at home can often use a relative's laptop for two hours a week; we will check what works at the free class rather than assume.

Digital, but not coding

The curriculum treats digital skill as transversal: competency 28 belongs to every area and to none in particular. A teacher can meet it with research, slides and video, and many do so well.

What it does not require is that any teenager ever writes a program, tests it, and explains why it works. That is the part our classes add.

English, hour by hour

An Ica teenager has three hours of Inglés a week on the regular day, five in a full-day school. EF's 2025 index scores Ica city 467 and the Ica region 468, both in its low band, against Lima city's 555.

So for many Ica teens a class in English is a stretch. Mentors adapt, and coding helps: the vocabulary is small, the screen shows everything, and errors are read aloud and solved together.

AI, carefully

Should a teenager in Ica learn AI, and which tools can they use?

Yes, they should learn how AI works, and that is different from letting an AI do their homework. Age rules apply to the tools themselves.

Learn how it works first

In our AI courses for teens, models are built in Python from small pieces: a rule that guesses, a score that measures how wrong it is, a change that makes it less wrong. A teenager who has built one knows why a chatbot can sound sure and still be mistaken.

That understanding is the useful skill. Tools change every year; the habit of checking an answer against the data does not.

Age rules we follow

Popular AI tools set their own minimum ages. ChatGPT needs a parent's permission for anyone under 18, and some assistants, Claude among them, are for adults only. We never ask an under-18 learner to use a tool above their age.

Our AI tools age guide lists what each tool allows. In class, AI output is something to test, never something to paste.

  1. Write it yourself first. Before asking any AI, try the problem. Even a wrong attempt shows what you do not yet understand.
  2. Ask for an explanation, not an answer. "Why does this loop stop early?" teaches more than "fix my code".
  3. Test what comes back. Run AI-suggested code on a case where you already know the answer. If it fails there, it will fail elsewhere.
  4. Say what you used. In school work and in contests, follow the rules. The OPI, for one, bans generative AI for solving its problems.

Parents who want the full argument will find it on our page about building with AI without copying, written for Peruvian families.

A real Ica project

Are Ica's exporters mostly big firms?

Ica is famous for wine and pisco, and PRODUCE, the ministry of production, publishes a register of the department's agro-industry firms. Our teenagers use it to learn how a percentage can be read in two directions, and why AI models get this wrong as often as people do.

The data

PRODUCE published "Empresas Agroindustriales Ica" on Peru's open data platform under the Open Data Commons Attribution Licence (ODC-By). The file, DataSet1_Agroindustria_ICA_2023_rev.csv, lists 494 manufacturing firms in the department for 2023, with each firm's size band and whether it exports.

Wine makers are the biggest group, 117 firms, followed by 98 that distil or blend spirits, the category that covers pisco. The file also has estimated sales ranges, which this lesson leaves alone.

The question

A teenager hears that "big firms export". Two questions hide in that sentence. Of all the large firms, how many export? And of all the exporters, how many are large?

Most people assume the two answers are close. The first program counts the pieces needed to answer both.

Eight firms are both large and exporters. Divide by the 10 large firms and you get 80%: most large firms export. Divide by the 22 exporters and you get 36.4%: most exporters are not large. Same eight firms, two denominators, two true and very different statements.

Ica agro-industry firms in 2023, read in both directions (our calculation)
Size bandFirmsShare of the band that exportsShare of all 22 exporters
Micro4571.3%27.3%
Small (pequeña)2528.0%31.8%
Medium (mediana)250.0%4.5%
Large (gran empresa)1080.0%36.4%

Data: Ministerio de la Producción (PRODUCE), Empresas Agroindustriales Ica, 2023, on datosabiertos.gob.pe, licence ODC-By. Shares are our own calculation. The file spells small as PEQUENA, without the ñ. Firms by province: Ica 284, Chincha 140, Pisco 39, Nazca 27 and Palpa 4.

The micro row is the surprise. Only 1.3% of micro firms export, yet they are more than a quarter of all exporters, because there are 457 of them. A small rate applied to a big group can outweigh a large rate applied to a small one. Statisticians call the size of each group the base rate, and ignoring it is one of the commonest reasoning errors there is.

AI models make the same slip. A model trained to spot exporters that leans on "large" will be right about large firms and still miss most exporters, because most of them are not large. Our teenagers learn to ask "a percentage of what?" before they trust any number, theirs or a machine's.

  1. Without code, ages 13 to 14. In a class of 30, 4 students play the cajón and 3 of those 4 are in 4° de secundaria; the class has 12 students in 4°. Work out both percentages, and say which one a music teacher would want.
  2. First Python. Run Version 1, then add the line that prints both percentages for large firms with the right labels.
  3. Ages 15 and over. Change Version 2 so it groups by province instead of size. Which province has the highest share of exporters, and is it also where most exporters are?
  4. Going further. Write a function both_ways(a, b) that takes any two yes/no columns and prints the share in each direction, then test it on wine makers and exporters.

Contests

Which maths and computing contests can an Ica teenager enter?

The national maths olympiad and UTEC's computing and AI olympiads are open to secundaria students across Peru. We are not connected with any organiser and teach only with our own problems.

ONEM for maths

The XXII Olimpiada Nacional Escolar de Matemática, in 2026, drew more than 1.7 million secundaria students. Students enter through their school, which held the first stage nationwide on Wednesday 8 July 2026, and winners move through UGEL and regional stages to the national final.

There are three levels: Nivel 1 for 1° and 2° de secundaria, Nivel 2 for 3° and 4°, and Nivel 3 for 5°. Our ONEM preparation page explains how we train the reasoning with problems of our own.

OPI and OPEIA for computing and AI

UTEC organises the Olimpiadas Peruanas de Computación free of charge for every secundaria student in Peru. The OPI's first round accepts C++ or Python; its in-person final uses C++ only, and solving problems with generative AI is not allowed.

UTEC also runs the OPEIA, an olympiad in artificial intelligence, whose 2025 team represented Peru at the International Olympiad in Artificial Intelligence (IOAI).

A practice problem of ours. A register lists 30 firms: 6 export, and 4 of the exporters are large; there are 5 large firms in all. What share of large firms export, and what share of exporters are large? Then: if you picked a firm at random and were told only that it exports, would you bet it is large? (Ours, written for this page. Answers: 80% and about 66.7%; yes, slightly, since two in three exporters are large here, unlike in Ica's real register.)

Peru keeps UTC minus 5 hours every day and has not used daylight saving since 1994 (tz database, America/Lima; INACAL publishes legal time). India keeps UTC plus 5 hours 30 minutes, so India stays 10 hours 30 minutes ahead of Ica.

Coding and AI for teens in Ica: questions and answers

Are the classes in Spanish?

No. All of our classes are live, online and in English, with no Spanish teaching. Mentors speak clearly, slow down, adapt to the teenager's pace and accent, and check understanding often. Code and error messages are in English too, so the practice adds up.

Are there coding classes for teenagers in Ica?

Yes, online and live. Teenagers in Ica, Parcona, La Tinguiña, Subtanjalla or anywhere in the department join from home at the hour the family books.

Does secundaria in Ica teach programming?

No. Neither the regular 35-hour week nor the 45-hour full-day plan has a programming area. Digital skills are transversal, so a teenager may never write a program at school.

Can my teenager use ChatGPT or Claude in your classes?

Only within each tool's age rules. ChatGPT requires a parent's permission under 18 and Claude is for adults, so we never ask an under-18 learner to use it. In class, AI suggestions are tested, not pasted.

What should an Ica teenager learn first?

Python. It is readable, used in data science and AI, and accepted in the first round of the OPI. After a few months a teenager can choose AI, data, apps, web or Java.

What time are classes for Ica?

Any day, any time, flexible: choose a batch, or for a very personalised plan choose 1-on-1 at the hour that suits your family. India is 10 hours 30 minutes ahead of Ica all year, so 07:00 in Ica is 17:30 for the mentor.

Can you prepare my teenager for the ONEM or the OPI?

We train the reasoning both contests reward, with original problems of our own. Students enter the ONEM through their school and the OPI through UTEC. We are not connected with MINEDU, the ONEM or UTEC and cannot promise any result.

My teenager's English is basic. Will it work?

Often, yes: coding vocabulary is small and everything is shown on screen. EF rates Ica's English low on average, so our mentors expect beginners. The free class tells you honestly whether it is too soon.

How are fees paid?

Monthly in US dollars by card; your bank converts the charge to soles. From India you would see rupees. The first class is free and asks for no card.

Does my teenager get a certificate?

Courses that offer one end with our own certificate of completion. It does not count toward secundaria grades or university admission in Peru and is not issued by MINEDU or any school; it shows the work actually done.
