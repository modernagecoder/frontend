## Rank Seeb school cohorts without sorting the wrong field

The Oman Ministry of Education publishes open-data files with school, grade and student-count fields. A learner can filter documented Seeb records and create a reproducible cohort ranking without publishing any personal data.

Dataset**Ministry school and student tables**Question**Which filtered cohort is largest?**Main risk**A stable sort applied in the wrong order**

Suppose the report ranks schools by a numeric total, highest first, and uses the school name as an alphabetical tie-break. Many programming languages use stable sorting, so equal values preserve their previous order. That helps only when the sorts are applied correctly.

The deceptive bug sorts by the primary numeric total first, then sorts the finished rows by school name. Because the second operation becomes the dominant order, the output is alphabetical. It may still look neat and locally plausible, especially when totals are close. The correct approach is a single tuple key such as descending total then ascending name, or a stable secondary sort first and primary sort last.

| Audit stage | Student action | Evidence produced |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Define | State the school, grade and year filters before running code | A written unit of analysis |
| Join | Match school identifiers and report missing or duplicate keys | A join-quality table |
| Calculate | Convert count fields to numbers and aggregate the chosen cohort | One checked total per school |
| Sort | Use descending total as primary and school name as tie-break | A ranking with explicit keys |
| Challenge | Shuffle the input and rerun the result | Proof that row order does not control the answer |
| Report | Publish the method, exclusions and retrieval date | A result another learner can reproduce |

Primary source: [Oman Ministry of Education open-data catalogue](https://home.moe.gov.om/region/opendata/days-day20). Students download the current files and document every filter. The exercise fits [Python for Teens](/courses/python-complete-masterclass-teens), [Statistics and Probability](/courses/statistics-probability-maths-course) and [Data Analysis](/courses/data-analysis-mastery-course-college).

Why a polished table can still be wrong

Presentation does not validate a sort key. The student must inspect the first rows, the last rows and every tie, then rerun after shuffling the input. A ranking that changes with file order is not ready to support a claim.

### Can Pakistan School Seeb students join these coding classes?

Yes. Pakistan School Seeb says its Al Chradi branch teaches through Level 12 and has more than 1,300 students. They can. A'Soud Global School describes a British and Cambridge pathway on Al Bahia Street and says it opened in September 2016. ### Why does a Seeb page cover several different neighbourhoods?

Seeb is a large wilayat, not one compact neighbourhood. Official sources place schools and services across areas including Al Chradi, Al Bahia, Al Hail and Al Khoud. This page explains the wilayat-wide choice. The dedicated Al Hail and Al Khoud guides provide narrower local detail without treating them as separate cities.

### Is there a Modern Age Coders branch in Seeb?

No. Modern Age Coders teaches Seeb students online from India and has no office, street address or physical classroom in the wilayat. The Indian WhatsApp number and email shown here reach the teaching company directly. The free first class lets a family test the live format before making a payment.

### Are Seeb and As Seeb the same place?

Yes. Seeb, As Seeb and A'Seeb are English romanisations used for the same Omani wilayat. This page uses Seeb in its URL and general prose. It keeps the spelling used by an official school or source inside that institution's proper name, so readers can verify the reference.

### Can lessons fit Seeb's Sunday to Thursday school week?

Yes, when a suitable teaching slot is available. Oman is one and a half hours behind India, so a Seeb evening can align with an Indian evening. Friday and Saturday options can also be discussed. The recurring time is agreed with the family before payment rather than advertised as universally available.

### What do online coding classes in Seeb cost?

A group with five to eight learners costs USD 100 a month. Private teaching with one student and one teacher costs USD 150 a month. Each plan includes eight live classes, normally two a week. The first class is free, with no enrolment fee, materials fee or minimum term.

### Can an adult or university learner in Seeb enrol?

Yes. Modern Age Coders teaches ages 6 to 67. Adult placement begins with the intended output, such as a Python automation, checked data report, database or web application. Adults are not placed in a children's group, and the programme does not promise employment, accreditation or credit from an Omani institution.

### How do you place a child who has already learnt Scratch?

The teacher asks the child to change and debug a small project. Independent use of sequence, variables, conditions and loops matters more than the number of projects completed. A learner who can explain each decision may be ready for Python. A learner who copied steps may benefit from a deeper Scratch project first.

### What equipment is required for a lesson at home in Seeb?

Use a laptop or desktop with a microphone and a stable video-capable internet connection. A tablet is restrictive once file management, typing and browser developer tools become part of the work. If the learner has a previous project, bring it to the free class so the teacher can inspect real work.
