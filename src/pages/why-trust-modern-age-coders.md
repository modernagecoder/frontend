# Do not take our word for it. *Check us.*

We set out to give the best coding and maths education in the world. That is a big claim, so here is how we work toward it and how you can test it yourself.

[Book priority demo · ₹499](/book-demo)[What is the priority demo?](/priority-demo)

One-time fee. No enrolment required. Confirmed on WhatsApp within the hour.

**In short**

Modern Age Coders aims to give the best coding and maths education in the world by teaching students how to think, solve problems and build real software, in small live batches and 1-on-1 classes taught by working mentors. You can verify this before enrolling: watch full recorded classes for free, read open pricing, see student projects, and sit in on a live demo.

## What we promise

- **Real understanding.** Students learn why things work, as set out in [we teach you how to think](/we-teach-you-how-to-think).
- **Real skills.** Students plan, build, debug and finish projects, as described in [think like a software engineer](/think-like-a-software-engineer).
- **Real attention.**[Small batches and 1-on-1 classes](/small-batches-and-1-on-1-classes), never webinars.
- **Both subjects, properly.** Coding and maths taught as one way of reasoning. See [why coding and maths together](/why-coding-and-maths-together).

## What we refuse to do

- We do not run demos with a sales team in place of teachers.
- We do not promise a free demo date we cannot keep. We tell you it is a waiting list.
- We do not hide fees. They are on the [pricing page](/pricing).
- We do not tell every parent their child is gifted. You get honest feedback.

That honesty is also why the [Priority Live Demo](/priority-demo) exists. Rather than pretend free slots are plentiful, we explain the situation in [why we charge for a priority demo](/why-we-charge-for-a-priority-demo).

## How to check us before you pay for a course

1. **Watch a whole class.** Full recorded lessons are free at [how we teach](/how-we-teach).
2. **Read the syllabus.** Every course under [courses](/courses) lists what is taught, week by week.
3. **Look at student work.** Projects are in [student labs](/student-labs) and results in [success stories](/success-stories).
4. **Try the free material.** Work through [free resources](/free-resources).
5. **Sit in a live class.** Read [what happens in a demo class](/what-happens-in-a-demo-class), then [book a priority demo](/how-to-book-a-priority-demo).
6. **Ask us anything.** The [FAQ](/faq), the [about page](/about) and [contact](/contact) are open.

## What Modern Age Coders teaches

- **We teach you how to think**Before any code is typed, the student learns to read a problem, break it apart and plan an answer.
- **We teach you to solve problems**Every class is built around a problem worth solving, not a list of commands to memorise.
- **We teach you to build software**Students plan, write, test and fix real programs, the way working engineers do.
- **We teach maths as a way of reasoning**Number sense, proof and patterns, so maths stops being a set of tricks.
