Mawaleh evidence file

Bilingual schools and a market whose role changed

These details come from the schools, Muscat Municipality and official public-sector reporting. They are local evidence, not endorsements.

Al Mawaleh Private School has taught locally since 1996

Al Mawaleh Private School says it is a private bilingual school supervised by the Ministry of Education and has educated Omani and expatriate children in South Mawaleh since 1996.

The school describes bilingual education from KG through Grade 12, with a monolingual programme in Grades 9 to 12. Its Primary section follows Cambridge Primary for English, mathematics and science. A programme label supplies context, while the free Modern Age Coders class checks what one learner can independently do.

The school publishes a detailed campus inventory

The same official page lists 27 classrooms, two IT labs, two science laboratories and four SmartBoard classrooms. It also records four separate school yards, two playgrounds, a library, art room, cafeteria, swimming pool and site-wide wired and wireless internet.

An inventory is not an ability score. Access to two IT labs does not reveal whether a learner has designed a database, and Cambridge mathematics does not reveal which concepts transfer into code. Direct work remains the safer placement evidence.

Sarh Al Elm adds another bilingual stage pattern

Sarh Al Elm School says it was founded in 2004 in South Mawaleh. It describes a bilingual programme divided into Kindergarten, Level 1 for Grades 1 to 4 and Level 2 for Grades 5 to 6.

Those grouped levels are useful when asking for current school work, but a Grades 5 to 6 label is still broader than a coding placement. The teacher changes an input or representation and observes whether the learner can transfer the underlying rule.

Mawaleh's market is now retail-focused

An official OPAZ publication says Silal began operations at the end of June 2024, with wholesale moving to the new market in Barka while Mawaleh focuses on retail. It says the change followed nearly 30 years of relying on Mawaleh for both roles.

A 2026 Consumer Protection Authority report confirms ongoing inspection of vegetable and fruit prices and availability at Silal. Current wholesale facts therefore belong to Barka, not to a timeless Mawaleh record.

Historic figures need a date and status

Muscat Municipality's market account records a historic total area of 185,244 square metres, a 5,600-square-metre main canopy, about 500 companies and 438 numbered tables for local producers. These explain Mawaleh's former central-market scale. They are not a current Silal inventory.

Mawaleh statistics project

The middle stall's middle is not the market's middle

All values are synthetic USD unit prices. They are not observed prices from Mawaleh, Silal or any named trader.

The shortcut that returns 4

Stall A contributes USD 1, USD 2 and USD 100, so its median is USD 2. Stall B contributes USD 3, USD 4 and USD 5, so its median is USD 4. Stall C contributes the nine values from USD 6 through USD 14, so its median is USD 10.

The three stall medians are 2, 4 and 10. Taking their median returns USD 4. That calculation gives each stall one vote even though Stall C contributes three times as many observations as each smaller stall.

stalls = {
  "A": [1, 2, 100],
  "B": [3, 4, 5],
  "C": [6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14]
}

stall_medians = [2, 4, 10]
median_of_medians = 4

The pooled median is 8

Pooling all 15 prices produces the ordered sequence 1 through 14 followed by 100. The eighth observation is USD 8, so USD 8 is the overall median. The group summaries cannot reconstruct that order because they discarded twelve of the fifteen observations.

from statistics import median

all_prices = [x for prices in stalls.values() for x in prices]
overall_median = median(all_prices)  # 8

A weighted average of the stall medians would not repair this. Medians are order statistics, not totals that combine through group weights. The correct approach is to retain the raw observations or use a valid distributed quantile method with sufficient rank information.

Required conclusion

Ask for the statistic over the combined observations, not the middle of independently computed middles. If raw rows are unavailable, say the overall median cannot be recovered from the group medians alone.

Mawaleh coding class answers

Can Al Mawaleh Private School students join these classes?

Yes. The school describes bilingual education from KG to Grade 12, with a monolingual option in Grades 9 to 12 and Cambridge Primary for English, mathematics and science. Modern Age Coders is independent of the school. A free live task places each learner from actual reasoning and code rather than a grade or programme label.

Can Sarh Al Elm learners enrol?

Yes. Sarh Al Elm says it was founded in 2004 in South Mawaleh and offers a bilingual programme from Kindergarten through Grade 6. Modern Age Coders begins at age 6. The teacher uses a recent task and a changed example to find the learner's working level without claiming that the external course belongs to the school.

What is the Mawaleh median-of-medians project?

Three synthetic stalls contain 3, 3 and 9 values. Their individual medians are 2, 4 and 10, so the median of those medians is 4. Pooling all 15 observations gives a true overall median of 8. Learners explain why equal weight for unequal groups loses information, then implement and test the correct aggregation.

Are the project prices from Mawaleh market?

No. The values are invented for teaching and are not observed fruit, vegetable, wholesale or retail prices. Official sources establish the market's history and changing role, but they do not supply this dataset. The exercise deliberately separates local facts from synthetic values so no result can be mistaken for a market price claim.

Does Modern Age Coders operate a Mawaleh centre?

No. Modern Age Coders is an Indian provider delivering live online lessons. It has no classroom, branch, street address or Omani telephone number in Mawaleh. The displayed +91 number is the real Indian WhatsApp and telephone contact. The first class is free, allowing a family to check the teacher and connection before paying.

Did wholesale activity remain at Mawaleh Central Market?

No. Official reporting says Silal began operations in Barka in late June 2024 and wholesale activity moved there, while Mawaleh's market retained a retail focus. Historic Mawaleh figures remain useful when dated, but they should not be presented as the current wholesale structure. The distinction is an example of why data needs status and effective dates.
