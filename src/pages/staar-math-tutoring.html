STAAR math tutoring and practice for grades 3 to 8

Every Texas public school student in grades 3 to 8 sits the STAAR mathematics test each spring, online, against the TEKS for their grade. In 2027 that test runs from April 19 to April 30, and it is the last spring of STAAR as families know it: from the 2027 to 2028 school year the state switches to a new program called the Student Success Tool. This page sets out what the Texas Education Agency publishes about the math test at each grade, what changes next year, and how a child can prepare by understanding the math rather than memorizing old questions.

In short

STAAR (State of Texas Assessments of Academic Readiness) is the Texas Education Agency’s online math test for every public school student in grades 3 to 8, written to the TEKS. Grade 3 has 30 questions worth 37 points and grade 8 has 40 worth 48. The spring 2027 window is April 19 to 30. Modern Age Coders teaches STAAR math live online, in a batch or 1-on-1, through our Elementary Maths course and middle school math classes.

How many questions are on the STAAR math test in each grade?

Between 30 and 40, depending on the grade. TEA’s blueprints, effective since the 2022 to 2023 school year, give the totals and split them into one-point and two-point questions.

What a two-point question is

The blueprints label every two-point question “non-multiple choice”. On screen that means the child builds the answer: types a fraction into an equation editor, plots points on a grid or drags values into place. There are no answer choices to eliminate.

In grade 5, eight two-point questions carry 16 of the 42 points. That is more than a third of the score coming from questions where guessing does not help.

Who sits it

TEA’s STAAR resources page states that all students enrolled in Texas public schools and open-enrollment charter schools in grades 3 to 8 are required by both federal and state law to take part. A child taking an above-grade course may sit the matching higher test instead, as TEA’s testing requirements flowchart shows.

The test is online. Spanish versions of the math test exist for grades 3 to 5.

Sources: STAAR Mathematics Resources (blueprints for each grade) and STAAR Resources, Texas Education Agency, read 31 October 2026. We are not connected with TEA, any Texas district or the test contractor.

Reporting categories

What do the four STAAR math reporting categories cover?

Every grade reports math in four categories. The names stay almost the same from grade 3 to grade 8; the weight moves steadily toward algebra and geometry.

Points available in each reporting category, three grades compared (TEA math blueprints, read 31 October 2026)
Reporting categoryGrade 3 pointsGrade 5 pointsGrade 8 points
1. Numerical representations and relationships10 to 125 to 92 to 5
2. Computations and algebraic relationships13 to 1717 to 2117 to 22
3. Geometry and measurement6 to 108 to 1215 to 20
4. Data analysis and personal financial literacy3 to 63 to 75 to 9

Reading the shift

In grade 3, number sense (category 1) is worth up to 12 points. By grade 8 it is worth at most 5, while geometry and measurement climbs to as many as 20. A child who was strong at place value in grade 3 can still struggle in grade 8 if volume, the Pythagorean theorem and transformations were never secure.

Grade 7 renames category 1 “Probability and Numerical Representations”, the one year where probability sits inside it.

Readiness and supporting standards

The blueprints mark each TEKS as readiness or supporting. Readiness standards “make up approximately 55-70% of the total points on the base test”; supporting standards make up the rest.

TEA adds that all TEKS, readiness or supporting, “are required to be taught in their entirety”. So a tutor who teaches only the readiness list is gambling with up to 45 percent of the points.

2027 and after

When is the STAAR math test in 2027, and what replaces it?

Grades 3 to 8 math runs April 19 to 30, 2027, according to TEA’s 2026 to 2027 testing calendar. It is the final spring before House Bill 8 replaces STAAR with the Student Success Tool.

What House Bill 8 changes

TEA’s HB 8 FAQ, updated 20 August 2026, says the bill was passed in September 2025 and replaces STAAR, beginning in the 2027 to 2028 school year, with the Student Success Tool (SST): a beginning-of-year, a middle-of-year and an end-of-year test.

The BOY and MOY tests will be adaptive, so each child sees different questions and those questions will not be released. The EOY test will be “static and linear”, the same for every student.

Shorter sittings

The FAQ sets expected time frames. Grades 3 and 4: 60 minutes for BOY and MOY, 90 minutes for EOY. Grades 5 to 8: 75 minutes and 105 minutes.

The four performance levels stay, and TEA must set SST cut points “at an equivalent level of rigor” to STAAR. Results are due no later than two business days after each window closes.

For a family, the practical point is continuity. The TEKS do not change because the test changes, and HB 8 FAQ question 13 confirms that TEA’s practice test site will be updated and kept. A child who learns grade-level math well in 2026 to 2027 is ready for either format.

Sources: 2026 to 2027 Student Assessment Testing Calendar, 2027 to 2028 calendar and Updated HB 8 FAQ, Texas Education Agency, read 31 October 2026. Your district sets the exact day inside each window.

Since spring 2023, online STAAR math has mixed multiple choice with question types where the student builds the answer. Calculators are allowed only in grade 8.

The calculator rule

TEA’s STAAR Calculator Policy says calculators “are not permitted” for students taking the grades 3 to 7 STAAR mathematics tests unless a student qualifies for one as an accommodation. In grade 8 a calculator is required, and each student must have a graphing calculator for the entire test.

Calculator apps on smartphones and smartwatches are not allowed, and the policy asks that students use the same kind of device they use in class.

What this means for practice

Five years of STAAR math, grades 3 to 7, are pencil and number sense. Practising with a calculator at home in those grades trains the wrong habit.

The equation editor rewards exact answers. A child who writes 0.6 when the question wants a fraction, or 1 7/8 when the box needs 15/8, loses a point that understanding had already earned. We practise entering answers in the form asked.

Sources: STAAR Calculator Policy and New Question Types, TEA, read 31 October 2026. Try the real interface on TEA’s Practice Test Site.

Scores

What score passes STAAR math, and what do the levels mean?

TEA reports four categories. Approaches Grade Level or above counts as passing; the scale score needed for each level is published for every grade.

How TEA defines the levels

TEA’s performance standards page puts it plainly: “Students who achieve Approaches Grade Level or higher on STAAR have passed the test.” Meets Grade Level means a high likelihood of success next year with perhaps some short-term support; Masters Grade Level means success expected with little or no intervention.

The scale scores are not percentages. They convert from raw points through tables TEA publishes after each administration.

How much rides on it

TEA’s one-page guide for families says “STAAR is not a high-stakes test for students” and that recent changes in Texas law removed student-level promotion consequences. A student who does not test earns a score of zero, and the school must then provide extra support.

Schools are rated partly on STAAR, which is why the pressure in March can feel bigger than the stakes for the child. Our aim is a child who is calm because they understand the math.

Sources: STAAR Performance Standards and What Families Should Know about STAAR, TEA, read 31 October 2026.

Worked examples

Two STAAR-level problems, worked the way we teach them

Both problems are ours, written at the level of the grade 5 and grade 7 TEKS. Neither is a released STAAR item; those are on TEA’s own site.

  1. Grade 5, our problem. A recipe needs 2 1/2 pounds of flour. Maya makes 3/4 of the recipe and shares the flour she uses equally among 3 mixing bowls. How much flour goes in each bowl? Type the answer as a fraction.
  2. Step 1: name the two operations before calculating. “3/4 of” is multiplication; “shares equally among 3” is division. Children who skip this step often add.
  3. Step 2: multiply with improper fractions. 2 1/2 = 5/2, and 3/4 x 5/2 = 15/8, which is 1 7/8 pounds.
  4. Step 3: divide by 3. 15/8 / 3 = 15/24 = 5/8. Each bowl gets 5/8 of a pound.
  5. Step 4: check the size. 1 7/8 pounds split three ways should be a bit more than half a pound each. 5/8 is a little over 1/2, so the answer is reasonable. The box wants a fraction, so we enter 5/8, not 0.625.
  1. Grade 7, our problem, no calculator. A store sells 6 notebooks for 4.50. At the same price, how much do 15 notebooks cost?
  2. Find the unit rate in your head. 4.50 / 6 = 0.75, so each notebook costs 0.75.
  3. Scale up. 15 x 0.75 = 11.25, which we get as 10 x 0.75 + 5 x 0.75 = 7.50 + 3.75.
  4. Check with a second route. 15 is 2.5 times 6, and 2.5 x 4.50 = 11.25. Two routes agree, so we trust it.

The pattern matters more than the numbers: say what each phrase means, calculate exactly, then test the answer against common sense and a second method. That habit is what turns a two-point question from a coin toss into two points.

Six weeks out

A six-week plan to the April 2027 STAAR math window

Our plan, counted back from April 19, 2027. It assumes two lessons a week plus short practice at home.

  1. Week of March 8: diagnose by category. One released test from TEA’s site, scored by reporting category, not just in total. The weakest category gets the next three weeks.
  2. Weeks of March 15 and 22: rebuild the weak category. Teach the ideas again from scratch with drawings and real objects, then short mixed sets. No timed tests yet.
  3. Week of March 29: two-point questions only. Equation editor, graphing and fraction model practice on the Practice Test Site, so the format holds no surprises.
  4. Week of April 5: mixed review and one full timed test. Grades 3 to 7 without a calculator, grade 8 with the same graphing calculator the child will use on the day.
  5. Week of April 12: light and confident. Ten-minute sets of mental math, a good night’s sleep before the district’s test day, and no new topics.

If the test is weeks away, not months

Focus on the category with the most points in your child’s grade. From the blueprints, that is computations and algebraic relationships in every grade except grade 8, where geometry and measurement nearly matches it.

If it is a year away

Learn the grade properly. With STAAR giving way to the SST beginning-of-year test in late summer 2027, steady understanding through the year will show up three times, not once.

For students

Read the box: fraction, decimal or whole number. Give it what it asks for.

Questions from Texas families

STAAR math: common questions

When is the STAAR math test in 2027?

TEA's 2026 to 2027 testing calendar sets grades 3 to 8 mathematics for April 19 to April 30, 2027, with April 30 the last day for make-ups. Your district chooses the exact day inside that window, so ask the school.

How many questions are on the STAAR math test?

It depends on the grade. TEA's blueprints give 30 questions in grade 3, 32 in grade 4, 34 in grade 5, 36 in grade 6, 38 in grade 7 and 40 in grade 8, each with some two-point questions.

Can my child use a calculator on STAAR math?

Only in grade 8. TEA's calculator policy does not permit calculators on the grades 3 to 7 math tests unless a student qualifies for one as an accommodation, and it requires a graphing calculator throughout the grade 8 test.

What score do you need to pass STAAR math?

Approaches Grade Level or higher counts as passing. TEA publishes the minimum scale score for each grade: for example 1360 in grade 3 and 1754 in grade 8.

Is STAAR being replaced?

Yes. Under House Bill 8, passed in September 2025, TEA replaces STAAR with the Student Success Tool from the 2027 to 2028 school year, with beginning, middle and end-of-year tests. Spring 2027 is the last STAAR administration for grades 3 to 8.

Does failing STAAR math mean my child repeats the grade?

TEA's family guide says STAAR is not a high-stakes test for students and that recent changes in Texas law removed student-level promotion consequences. Schools do use results to plan extra support.

Where can we find STAAR math practice tests?

TEA releases its tests. Use the Practice Test Site to try them in the real online interface, and the STAAR Released Test Questions page for answer keys and rationales.

What is a two-point question on STAAR math?

A non-multiple-choice question, such as typing an answer in an equation editor or drawing on a graph, worth two points. In grade 5, eight of them carry 16 of the 42 points.

When are your STAAR math classes?

Weekdays or weekends, flexible: choose your batch, or for a very personalised plan choose 1-on-1 at your convenience, weekend mornings or weekdays. Lessons are scheduled on Central Time for Texas families.

Are you connected with TEA or STAAR?

No. Modern Age Coders is an independent online school. We teach the TEKS, quote TEA's published documents and link to TEA's own practice material; we never copy released questions.
