11 plus maths tuition in Wiltshire

Wiltshire's grammar schools are in Salisbury, and each sets its own entrance test. The boys' grammar school has already published its arrangements for September 2028 entry, so a child now in Year 5 can see exactly what is coming: two papers from Quest on Saturday 25 September 2027, with maths sharing the second paper with non-verbal problem solving in about 45 minutes. Every question is multiple choice, usually from five options, and a recording paces the room because no watch or timer is allowed. Modern Age Coders teaches the upper primary maths behind that paper live online, to a class or to one child. This page sets out the format, the dates, how the qualifying standard works, and one topic that repays early work: per cent.

Is there maths in the Salisbury 11 plus?

Yes. The boys' grammar school in Salisbury sets its own 11 plus, written and marked by Quest Assessments. Its leaflet for September 2028 entry lists two papers: "English and Verbal Problem Solving" and "Maths and Non-Verbal Problem Solving", each of about 45 minutes, on the morning of Saturday 25 September 2027. Quest says every question is multiple choice, usually from five options, marked on an answer sheet. The 2027 entry test was on Saturday 26 September 2026. The girls' grammar school sets its own test, which we could not read. Modern Age Coders teaches the maths behind the Salisbury paper live online, in a group or 1-on-1, through our 11 plus maths preparation course.

The two papers

What is in the Salisbury boys' grammar school 11 plus?

Two papers sat in one morning session with a short supervised break. The school's 2028 entry leaflet gives about 45 minutes each and about an hour and a half in total.

Maths shares its paper

There is no maths-only paper. Maths and non-verbal problem solving come together in about 45 minutes, so the time a child spends on one is time not spent on the other. The leaflet does not say how the 45 minutes divide between them, and we will not guess.

What a family can work on is speed of recall. A child who knows that a fifth is 20 per cent, or that 7 x 8 is 56, without thinking, leaves more of the paper for the questions that need thought.

How answers are marked

Quest explains that "Rather than writing out answers, your child draws a horizontal line through a box next to their chosen answer." One mark per question unless told otherwise; mistakes are rubbed out fully with a clean eraser.

That has a maths consequence. There is no credit for a method, only for the right box. Checking an answer against the options before marking it is part of the skill.

No watch, no timer

The leaflet bans "mobile phones, smart watches, timers or any other electronic devices". Quest says a recording plays before, during and after each paper with the instructions.

One attempt only

"Each candidate may only sit the 11+ examination" for the school once, the leaflet says. There is no second sitting the following year.

No past papers

The leaflet says: "For security and copyright reasons, papers from previous years will not be made available to parents." Quest's free familiarisation booklets show the format.

Two forms, two systems

The leaflet asks for the school's own online 11 plus registration form first, and says plainly that this "does not replace applying for admission through the LA application form". Offers for Year 7 are coordinated by Wiltshire Council.

Families in Hampshire and Dorset apply through their own councils by the same 31 October deadline, naming the school; their council then passes the preference to Wiltshire.

If a child is unwell

The school must be told by email before the test or by 9.00am on the day. An alternative date needs valid evidence, such as a GP letter, and the leaflet notes that ordinary tiredness after a school trip is not enough.

A parent who believes performance was affected on the day must write to the school by Friday 1 October 2027, with evidence. Circumstances raised after that cannot be considered.

Source: the school's Year 7 admissions page and 2028 entry leaflet, read 31 October 2026. We print a weekday only where the school's weekday matches the calendar; some of its listed weekdays do not, so for those dates we give the date alone.

Scores and places

What score do you need for the Salisbury 11 plus?

There is no published pass mark. The leaflet says the qualifying standard "is determined by the headteacher in consultation with the School Governing Body", and is set at 10 per cent lower for disadvantage-funded candidates.

The 10 per cent rule in plain numbers

Our illustration, not the school's figures: if a qualifying standard were 100 points on some scale, 10 per cent lower would be 90. The school does not publish its standard, so no real number can be put in the example; the point is only what "10 per cent lower" means arithmetically.

Eligibility follows the leaflet: looked after or previously looked after children, and children recorded for free school meals in the last six years (FSM Ever 6), with evidence.

What the school says about tutors

The leaflet calls private tutoring "entirely a matter for parental choice", says the school and Quest "are not responsible for what is taught", and adds that "a tutor's opinion will have no bearing on any subsequent appeal".

It also asks parents to "monitor and limit any activities that might put undue additional pressure" on a child before the test. We take that seriously: our lessons are school maths, paced for a ten-year-old.

Our lesson family

How should a Year 5 child think about percentages?

As hundredths. The Year 5 national curriculum asks pupils to "recognise the per cent symbol (%) and understand that per cent relates to 'number of parts per hundred'", and to know the percentage and decimal equivalents of a half, a quarter, a fifth, two fifths and four fifths. Everything below is ours, written for this page; none of it is a Quest question.

What is 25% of 64?25% is a quarter; half of 64 is 32, half again is 1616

Which is larger, 3/5 or 0.65?3/5 is 6/10, which is 0.6, and 0.65 is more than 0.60.65

Our practice set. Try each in your head first, then check on paper. (1) 50% of 86. (2) 20% of 45. (3) Which is closest to 1/5: 0.15, 0.21 or 0.3? (4) 75% of 120. (5) 3 out of 25 children walk to school; what percentage is that? (6) Order 0.45, 2/5 and 42%. Answers, ours: (1) 43; (2) 9, because 10% is 4.5; (3) 0.21, since 1/5 is 0.2; (4) 90, because 25% is 30; (5) 12%, since 3/25 = 12/100; (6) 2/5, 42%, 0.45.

Why this suits a five-option paper

Fractions, decimals and percentages written three ways are a natural source of near-miss options. A child who converts everything to hundredths before choosing, as in the ordering example, is rarely caught by an option that looks right.

It is also fast. Finding 10 per cent by dividing by 10 and building from there takes seconds, which matters when maths shares 45 minutes with another kind of reasoning.

The mistakes we see most

Reading 0.3 as smaller than 0.25 because 3 is less than 25. Writing both as hundredths, 30 and 25, fixes it permanently.

Taking 20 per cent as "divide by 20". We teach the fraction first, a fifth, and only then the percentage, so the operation follows from the meaning.

Wiltshire 11 plus questions

What Wiltshire parents ask

Is there a maths paper in the Salisbury 11 plus?

At the boys' grammar school, maths is half of Paper 2, called Maths and Non-Verbal Problem Solving, which lasts about 45 minutes. There is no maths-only paper.

When is the Salisbury eleven plus for 2028 entry?

The school's leaflet gives the morning of Saturday 25 September 2027. Its 11 plus applications open on Monday 22 March 2027 and close on Friday 2 July 2027.

Who sets the Salisbury boys' grammar school test?

Quest Assessments, which the provider says is part of Atom Learning. Quest prepares, marks and standardises the papers, and the school sets the qualifying standard.

Are the questions multiple choice?

Every one, according to the provider, normally with five lettered choices. A child answers by drawing a horizontal line through the box beside the chosen letter.

Can my son wear a watch in the 11 plus?

No. The leaflet bans mobile phones, smart watches, timers and other electronic devices. Quest says a recording gives instructions before, during and after each paper.

What is the pass mark for the Salisbury 11 plus?

None is published. The headteacher sets the qualifying standard with the governing body, and it is 10 per cent lower for disadvantage-funded candidates. Raw scores are not given to the school or to parents.

Are there Salisbury 11 plus past papers?

No. The school says papers from previous years will not be made available. Quest publishes free familiarisation booklets in English, maths, verbal and non-verbal reasoning, with a practice answer sheet.

Does qualifying guarantee a place?

No. If more boys qualify than there are places, the oversubscription criteria decide, and the school advises naming it as first preference on the council form.

Do I need an 11 plus tutor in Salisbury?

The school calls tutoring entirely a matter for parental choice and says a tutor's opinion has no bearing on an appeal. We teach curriculum maths and make no promise about any outcome.

When are your maths lessons?

Weekdays or weekends, flexible: choose your batch, or for a very personalised plan choose 1-on-1 at your convenience, weekend mornings or weekdays. Booking times are in UK time.
