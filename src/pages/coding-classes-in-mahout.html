Mahout today

A wilayat that changes size twice a day

Barr Al Hikman is flat, vast and tidal, which means its most basic property is not a constant but a schedule.

A coastline measured in hundreds of kilometres

The peninsula carries a coastline of over 160 km bordered by an inland sabkha. On ground that flat, a small rise or fall in water covers or uncovers an enormous area, which is what makes the variation so large here and so small elsewhere.

Birds that arrive on their own timetable

Barr Al Hikman is a haven to nearly half a million waterbirds in winter. A count taken in July and a count taken in January are both honest and describe different things, and neither is the number of birds.

Bounded by four different waters

The peninsula borders the sands to the north, the Masirah Channel to the east, the Gulf of Masirah to the south and Hashish Bay to the southwest. Four boundaries described by four names is four chances for two datasets to draw the edge differently.

Some quantities are a function, not a value

The area of an intertidal flat is not a fact you look up. It is a curve over time. Recording it as a single number does not simplify the truth, it discards the only part of the truth that made the number meaningful.

Mahout facts with a trail behind them

Each claim traced, with the moment it describes stated where it matters.

One of the region's great coastal wetlands

Barr Al Hikman is described as one of the most prominent coastal wetland regions in the Middle East. Prominence is a judgement; the coastline figure beside it is a measurement, and the page keeps them apart.

Over a hundred and sixty kilometres of coast

The published coastline is over 160 km. The word over makes it a lower bound and it is kept as one here rather than treated as a total.

An inland sabkha behind it

The coast is bordered by an inland sabkha, a supratidal flat where evaporite minerals accumulate under an arid climate. Sabkha is exactly the ground that floods and dries, which is why the area question has no fixed answer.

Nearly half a million waterbirds

The site holds nearly half a million waterbirds in winter. Both the hedge and the season belong to the source and both are kept, because a bird count without a date is not a bird count.

A peninsula with named edges

Barr al Hikman is a peninsula in the northeast of Al Wusta Governorate, bounded by the sands, the Masirah Channel, the Gulf of Masirah and Hashish Bay. Named edges make a boundary arguable in a way coordinates would not.

A beach people actually go to

Hufnat Beach in Mahout is written about as a destination in its own right. A wilayat that is famous for a wetland also has an ordinary life, and the two produce different kinds of record.

None of the above involves Modern Age Coders and no association is claimed. Mahout is used here because an intertidal flat is the least ambiguous example there is of a quantity that genuinely has no single value.

Signature project

One hundred and forty square kilometres, or five hundred and twenty

The same place, the same week, the same method. Only the hour is different, and the answer changes by a factor of nearly four.

1. Measure the exposed flat every hour

Thirteen readings across one tidal cycle. The areas here are teaching values, since no hourly survey is published, but the behaviour is the ordinary behaviour of a tidal flat and the page says so.

2. Look at what the readings do

The exposed area runs from 140 square kilometres at the top of the tide to 520 at the bottom. That is a ratio of 3.7. Every one of the thirteen readings is correct at the moment it was taken.

3. Try to summarise it with one number

The mean across the cycle is 315 square kilometres. The flat is at that state in none of the thirteen hours. The average is not a compromise between the readings, it is a condition the place is never actually in.

The average is the answer that is wrong at every single moment

A minimum is true at high water and a maximum is true at low water, so both are at least honest about something. The mean is true at no point in the cycle at all. It is nonetheless the figure most likely to be published, because it sounds balanced and it hides the one fact a reader needed, which is that the question was incomplete. For younger learners

A tray of water tipped slowly, with the wet area traced on paper at intervals. The child is then asked how big the wet part is. The argument that follows is the whole lesson, and there is no screen in it.

For teenagers

The thirteen hourly areas in Python, printing minimum, maximum, mean and the count of readings equal to the mean. The learner writes the check that refuses to report an average when no observation lies near it.

For adults

Timestamps are mandatory on measurements, comparisons are restricted to matching conditions, and any quantity that varies on a cycle is reported as a range with its phase named rather than as a single figure.

Are the Barr Al Hikman figures real?

The coastline of over 160 km, the inland sabkha, the named boundaries and the nearly half a million waterbirds in winter are all published. The thirteen hourly areas in the project are teaching values, because no hourly survey was found, and the page says so wherever they appear.

Why is the average the worst single answer here?

Because the minimum is genuinely true at high water and the maximum is genuinely true at low water, so each describes a real moment. The mean describes no moment in the cycle at all. It is still the figure most likely to be published, because it sounds balanced and it conceals that the question was incomplete.

Is there a Modern Age Coders centre in Mahout?

No. No premises are claimed in Mahout or anywhere in Al Wusta. Classes are live online and need a laptop or desktop, working audio, a current browser and a stable connection. The page describes where the service reaches, not a building.

What is the right way to report a quantity like this?

Give the range and name the phase. Four hundred and twenty square kilometres at mid ebb, or 140 at high water, are both usable statements. The area of Barr Al Hikman is not a statement at all until somebody adds a time to it.

Do you teach adults in Mahout as well as school students?

Yes, up to 67. Adults work through Python, analytics and reporting. If a figure you report moves depending on what time the job runs, and somebody has already asked you why it changed, this is that problem. Bring the real numbers where they are yours to bring.

What does a learner take away from this?

Measurements that always carry a timestamp, comparisons restricted to matching conditions, and a habit of publishing a range with its phase named instead of a single tidy figure. That survives any change of tooling.
