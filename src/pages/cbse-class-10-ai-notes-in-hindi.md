## Artificial intelligence kaksha 10: Part A, project cycle aur modeling ke notes

Part A ke 10 marks sabse aasaan marks hain, aur Unit 1 plus Unit 2 milkar 18 marks dete hain. Ye teen hisse theory paper ka sabse bada chunk hain.

### Part A: Employability Skills, ek line mein har unit

- Communication Skills-II: baat pahunchane ke tareeke, jaise verbal, non-verbal aur visual, aur kaun si cheez message ko rokti hai (barriers).

- Self-Management Skills-II: stress ko sambhalna, khud ko motivate rakhna, aur SMART goals banana: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Realistic, Time-bound.

- ICT Skills-II: computer ka sahi istemaal aur dekhbhaal, files sambhalna, aur virus se bachav.

- Entrepreneurial Skills-II: entrepreneur kaun hota hai, uske gun, aur business ka idea kaise shuru hota hai.

- Green Skills-II: sustainable development, yaani aaj ki zaroorat aise poori karna ki aane wali peedhi ka hissa na chhine.

Har Part A unit ke short answers [Part A employability skills](/cbse-class-10-ai-employability-skills-part-a) page par hain.

### Unit 1: AI Project Cycle aur Ethical Frameworks

- AI Project Cycle ke stages, isi order mein: Problem Scoping (problem ko saaf samajhna), Data Acquisition (data ikattha karna), Data Exploration (graphs se data ko samajhna), Modelling (model chunna aur banana), Evaluation (naye data par test karna), aur CBSE handbook ke hisaab se aakhri stage Deployment (model ko asli duniya mein lagana).

- 4Ws Problem Canvas: Who (kise problem hai, yaani stakeholders), What (problem kya hai), Where (kahan aur kab hoti hai), Why (solve karne se kya fayda hoga).

- AI ke teen domains: Statistical Data, Computer Vision aur Natural Language Processing.

- Ethical Framework ek set of rules hai jo batata hai ki AI banate waqt sahi aur galat kya hai. Do type: sector-based (ek field ke liye, jaise healthcare ke liye bioethics) aur value-based (rights-based, utility-based, virtue-based).

- Bioethics ke chaar principles: Respect for Autonomy (patient khud faisla le sake), Do not harm, Ensure maximum benefit for all, aur Give justice (sabke saath barabar vyavhaar).

### Unit 2: Advanced Concepts of Modeling in AI

- English term | Hinglish mein matlab | Ek example

- AI, ML, DL | AI sabse bada circle hai; ML uske andar, jahan machine data se seekhti hai; DL, ML ke andar, jo neural networks use karta hai | Chess khelne wala program (AI), spam filter (ML), face unlock (DL)

- Rule Based Approach | Insaan rules likhta hai, machine bas follow karti hai; naya case aaye toh rule dobara likhna padta hai | "Agar temperature 40 se upar, toh alert bhejo" wala program

- Learning Based Approach | Machine khud data se pattern seekhti hai | Photos dekh kar billi aur kutte mein farq seekhna

- Supervised Learning | Data par label laga hota hai, yaani sahi jawab pehle se pata hota hai | Classification (category batana) aur Regression (number batana)

- Unsupervised Learning | Label nahi hota, machine khud groups ya rishte dhoondhti hai | Clustering (milte-julte groups) aur Association (saath mein khareedi jaane wali cheezein)

- Reinforcement Learning | Sahi kaam par reward, galat par penalty; machine trial and error se seekhti hai | Game khelna seekhta hua agent

- ANN aur CNN | ANN: insaani dimaag jaise neurons ka network. CNN: images ke liye khaas network jo chhote filters chalata hai | Handwriting pehchanna (CNN)

Neural Network ki teen layers hoti hain: Input Layer (data andar aata hai), Hidden Layers (calculation hoti hai) aur Output Layer (result bahar aata hai). Ek neuron har input ko uske weight se guna karta hai, sab jodta hai, aur bias add karta hai. Jaise inputs 1 aur 1, weights 0.5 aur 0.3, bias -0.6: weighted sum = 0.5 + 0.3 - 0.6 = 0.2. Is unit ke aur sawaal [advanced modeling questions](/cbse-class-10-ai-advanced-modeling-questions) page par hain.

## AI class 10 Hindi medium students ke liye: Evaluating Models ke formulas aur ek solved numerical

Evaluation ka matlab hai model ko aise data par test karna jo usne pehle kabhi nahi dekha, taaki pata chale woh kitna sahi hai. Is unit ke 10 marks mein numericals zaroor aate hain, isliye formulas pakke karo.

### Zaroori terms

- Train-test split: data ke do hisse. Ek se model seekhta hai (training data), doosre se test hota hai (testing data). Same data par test karoge toh score jhootha achha aayega, kyunki model ne ratt liya hoga (overfitting).

- Confusion Matrix: 2 x 2 table jo prediction ko reality se milati hai.

- True Positive (TP): model ne Yes kaha, sach mein Yes. True Negative (TN): model ne No kaha, sach mein No.

- False Positive (FP): model ne Yes kaha, par sach mein No. False Negative (FN): model ne No kaha, par sach mein Yes.

- Ethical concerns: Bias (data ek group ki taraf jhuka ho), Transparency (model ka faisla samjhaya ja sake), Accuracy (galat score par bharosa na karein).

### Formulas, ek jagah

- Accuracy | (TP + TN) / total

- Error | 1 - Accuracy

- Precision | TP / (TP + FP)

- Recall | TP / (TP + FN)

- F1 Score | 2 x P x R / (P + R)

```
Ek model 100 emails check karta hai.
Spam = Positive.

              Reality: Yes  Reality: No
Predicted Yes   TP = 36      FP = 4
Predicted No    FN = 12      TN = 48

Accuracy  = (36 + 48) / 100 = 0.84
Error     = 1 - 0.84        = 0.16
Precision = 36 / (36 + 4)   = 0.90
Recall    = 36 / (36 + 12)  = 0.75
F1 = 2 x 0.90 x 0.75 / (0.90 + 0.75)
   = 1.35 / 1.65 = 0.82 (2 d.p.)
Galat predictions = FP + FN = 16
```

### Kaun sa metric kab?

- Recall tab zaroori jab FN mehnga ho: bimaari ka test, jahan beemar ko "healthy" bolna khatarnak hai.

- Precision tab zaroori jab FP mehnga ho: spam filter, jahan zaroori email spam mein chali jaaye toh nuksaan.

- Dono zaroori hon toh F1 Score dekho.

Aise aur numericals, ek mark se chaar mark tak, [confusion matrix numericals](/confusion-matrix-class-10-ai-numericals) page par hain, aur saare formulas ek page par [Class 10 AI formula sheet](/cbse-class-10-ai-formula-sheet) mein.

## AI class 10 Hinglish notes: Computer Vision aur Natural Language Processing

Computer Vision machine ko images aur videos "dekhna" sikhata hai; Natural Language Processing use insaani bhasha samajhna sikhata hai. Dono milkar 12 theory marks hain.

### Unit 5: Computer Vision (4 marks)

- CV Tasks: Image Classification (poori image ki ek category), Classification + Localisation (category aur uski jagah par box), Object Detection (kai objects, sabke box), Instance Segmentation (har object ki exact outline, pixel tak).

- Pixel: image ka sabse chhota hissa, ek chhota rang ka dabba.

- Resolution: width x height pixels. 100 x 50 image mein 5,000 pixels.

- Pixel value: 0 se 255 tak. 0 matlab kaala, 255 matlab safed. 256 values kyunki ek byte mein 8 bits hain aur 2 ki power 8 = 256.

- Grayscale vs RGB: grayscale mein har pixel ki ek value; RGB mein teen (Red, Green, Blue). Isliye 100 x 50 ki RGB image 15,000 values rakhti hai.

- Applications: face unlock, self-driving cars, medical imaging, retail mein shelf check.

Convolution, kernel aur CNN ki layers curriculum mein practical wale table mein rakhe gaye hain, Teachable Machine aur Orange ke saath. Unit 2 mein CNN ka naam theory mein bhi aata hai.

### Unit 6: Natural Language Processing (8 marks)

- Applications: voice assistants, auto generated captions, language translation, sentiment analysis (review positive hai ya negative), text classification, keyword extraction.

- Stages of NLP: Lexical Analysis (words pehchanna), Syntactic Analysis ya Parsing (grammar check), Semantic Analysis (matlab banta hai ya nahi), Discourse Integration (pichhle sentence se jodna), Pragmatic Analysis (asli duniya mein kya matlab hai).

- Script-bot vs Smart-bot: script-bot fixed script par chalta hai, jaise customer care ka menu; smart-bot data se seekhta hai aur khule sawaal samajh leta hai.

- Text Normalisation: sentence segmentation, tokenisation (sentence ko words aur punctuation mein todna), stop words hatana (the, is, and), sab ko lowercase karna, phir stemming (ending kaatna, non-word bhi ban sakta hai) ya lemmatisation (sahi dictionary word milta hai).

- Bag of Words: har document mein har word kitni baar aaya, uski ginti. TFIDF batata hai ki koi word ek document ke liye kitna khaas hai.

```
Doc 1: AI helps farmers
Doc 2: AI helps doctors and farmers

Vocabulary: [ai, helps, farmers, doctors, and]
Doc 1 vector: [1, 1, 1, 0, 0]
Doc 2 vector: [1, 1, 1, 1, 1]
```

```
IDF = log(N / documents jismein word hai)

"doctors": log10(2 / 1) = 0.301
"ai":      log10(2 / 2) = log 1 = 0
```

## Frequently asked questions

**Where can I find class 10 AI notes in Hindi?** This page gives unit-wise notes for CBSE Class 10 AI (417), 2026-27, in Hinglish: Hindi written in Roman script, with every technical term kept in English. It covers Part A, the five theory units with their marks, the two practical-only units and solved numericals for confusion matrix, Bag of Words and IDF.

**Is there a Hindi medium book for AI class 10?** When we checked the CBSE skill education site on 6 November 2026, we found the Class 10 AI facilitator handbook in English only, and the 2025-26 sample paper for 417 is also English only. Some publishers and teachers offer Hindi explanations, but always learn the English terms, because those are what the paper uses.

**Can I write the AI 417 board exam in Hindi?** We have not been able to confirm this from a CBSE document, so ask your school. Even where Hindi answers are allowed, technical terms such as precision, recall, tokenisation and pixel should be written exactly as they are in English, since they have no standard Hindi equivalents in the curriculum.

**Which units of artificial intelligence kaksha 10 come in the theory paper?** Part A Employability Skills (10 marks), Unit 1 AI Project Cycle and Ethical Frameworks (7), Unit 2 Advanced Concepts of Modeling (11), Unit 3 Evaluating Models (10), Unit 5 Computer Vision (4) and Unit 6 Natural Language Processing (8). Unit 4 Statistical Data and Unit 7 Advance Python are assessed only in the practical.

**What are the stages of the AI project cycle in Class 10?** The CBSE Class 10 handbook lists six stages in order: Problem Scoping, Data Acquisition, Data Exploration, Modelling, Evaluation and Deployment. Problem Scoping uses the 4Ws canvas: Who, What, Where and Why. Learn the stage names in English, in order, because one-mark questions often ask which stage comes next.

**How do I remember precision and recall?** Both have TP on top. Precision divides by everything the model called Yes (TP + FP), so it asks how many Yes answers were right. Recall divides by everything that really was Yes (TP + FN), so it asks how many real cases were caught. For F1, use 2PR / (P + R), never the plain average.

**Are AI class 10 Hinglish notes enough for the board exam?** They are enough to understand the units, but not on their own to score. You also need practice writing answers in English with the right keywords, solving numericals under time, and preparing the practical file, project and viva, which together carry 50 of the 100 marks.
