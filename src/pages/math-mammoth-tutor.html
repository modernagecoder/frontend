Math Mammoth tutor online, for when self-teaching needs a voice

Math Mammoth is built to be read by the child: the explanations, the pictures and the exercises are all in the same worktext, and many homeschoolers work through it with little help. Then a chapter arrives where the written explanation is not enough, often in multiplication, long division or fractions, and the child needs someone to talk it through. Below, using Math Mammoth's published pages, we set out how the Light Blue Series is put together, then show the point at which a live teacher is worth adding and how to do it without taking over.

What comes after grade 8

Math Mammoth is clear: "Math Mammoth does not offer algebra 1 or above." It adds: "After MM7 or MM8, students can proceed directly to high school math (such as algebra 1)."

That handover is a natural moment for a live teacher, because the next curriculum will be written in a different voice.

Why the operations are mixed

The site explains that "The operations used in the problems vary so that children learn to think, and not simply apply the operation that is in the title of the lesson."

That is excellent design, and it is also why some children stall: they can do the lesson's skill, but not yet decide when to use it.

Worktexts

How is a Math Mammoth lesson structured?

There is no separate teacher script. "Each grade level consists of two student worktexts (A and B), which contain both the teaching (explanations) and all of the student work (exercises)."

Read

Each lesson opens with an explanation and a picture, written to the child. Reading it slowly, aloud if needed, is the first step.

Try

Exercises follow straight after, beginning close to the example and moving further from it.

Mix

Word problems near the end use a mix of operations, which is where real understanding is tested.

Because everything is in one book, a parent often sees only the finished page. The reasoning behind it may never be said aloud. Asking your child to explain one exercise each day, in their own words, is the simplest check that the self-teaching is working.

Placement

How do I place my child in Math Mammoth?

Math Mammoth publishes free placement tests and gives unusually direct advice: "Many popular math curricula are easier than Math Mammoth and thus there is a danger the child is placed too high" if you go by grade alone.

  1. Know what the tests are. Math Mammoth says "They are end-of-year (EOY) tests", meant to be taken after studying that grade.
  2. Start high. Choose the highest level you think your child will pass, and give that test first.
  3. Read results by section. The questions are grouped by topic, so a low geometry or fractions section shows exactly where a gap is.
  4. Test lower if needed. Math Mammoth suggests giving sections of lower-level tests to find how large a gap is.
  5. Keep it low-pressure. It advises against strict time limits, and suggests postponing tests for a child who is already anxious about math.

Using the results with a tutor

Bring the section scores to a free first class. A child who scored well overall but low in one topic does not need to drop a whole grade.

Our teachers can work on that topic live while your child continues the main worktext, or you can use a Blue Series topical book for the same gap, as Math Mammoth suggests.

Switching from another curriculum

Children moving from a gentler program sometimes find Math Mammoth's word problems a shock. That is not failure; it is the placement warning in action.

A few weeks of explained mixed problems usually settle it.

The self-teaching trap

A capable reader can finish pages by copying the pattern of the example. Scores look fine until the chapter test or the cumulative review.

If the review pages go badly after good lesson pages, the explanation was read but not understood.

When to bring in a teacher

Not for one bad page. A teacher helps when the same kind of mistake repeats over a chapter, or when your child stops wanting to open the book.

Often a few sessions on one chapter are enough before the worktext can lead again.

Worked examples

What mental math strategies help with Math Mammoth?

Math Mammoth teaches mental math explicitly, and these are the kinds of strategies our teachers practise aloud with students. All examples and wording are ours, not taken from the worktexts.

Multiplication facts from facts you already know (our examples)
FactStrategySaid aloud
7 x 8Double, double, double: 7 x 2 = 14, x 2 = 28, x 2 = 56"Times 8 is doubling three times."
9 x 6Ten times, minus one group: 60 - 6 = 54"Nine sixes are ten sixes take away one six."
6 x 7Five sevens plus one more: 35 + 7 = 42"Six groups is five groups and one more."
25 x 12Four 25s make 100: 12 is three fours, so 300"Group the 25s into hundreds."
4 x 19Round and fix: 4 x 20 = 80, minus 4 = 76"I used one too many in each group."

Dividing 96 by 4 in your head

Split 96 into parts that divide easily: 80 and 16. Then 80 / 4 = 20 and 16 / 4 = 4, so 96 / 4 = 24.

Check by multiplying: 24 x 4 = 96. The idea to say aloud: you may split the number you are dividing, as long as every part is shared by the same amount.

Making change by counting up

Our problem: something costs 13.65 dollars and you pay with a 20-dollar bill. Count up from the price: 35 cents makes 14.00, then 1 dollar makes 15.00, then 5 dollars makes 20.00.

Change: 5 + 1 + 0.35 = 6.35 dollars. The idea to say aloud: it is easier to add up to the bill than to subtract across zeros.

With a tutor

How does a live tutor work alongside Math Mammoth?

Math Mammoth is designed for independence, so a tutor should protect that. Our lessons follow the worktext and hand the work back to your child as soon as possible.

  1. Tell us the grade, worktext (A or B), chapter and lesson. Placement test section scores help too.
  2. Read the explanation together. The teacher asks your child to put it in their own words, which shows what was missed.
  3. Practise the strategy aloud. Mental math in particular is learned by saying it, so classes are full of talk.
  4. Mix the problems. Fresh mixed word problems written by our teachers, so your child practises choosing the operation.
  5. Hand back to the book. When your child can explain the chapter, they carry on alone with the worktext and its reviews.

For a child who reads the explanations, explains answers when asked and does well on chapter tests.

Worktext plus a batch

Strategies

From counting to choosing a strategy

Our description of how mental math grows in a Math Mammoth child.

Stages of mental math (ours)
StageWhat it looks like
1. CountingCounts on or uses fingers
2. RecallingKnows some facts by heart
3. DerivingBuilds new facts from known ones
4. ChoosingPicks the quickest strategy and says why

For parents

Stage 3 matters more than speed. A child who can build 7 x 8 from 7 x 4 will never truly forget it.

Ask "how did you know?" more often than "what is the answer?"

For students

If you forget a fact, build it from one you know.

Before a word problem, say what is happening in the story.

Homeschool questions

Math Mammoth tutoring: common questions

Do you offer a Math Mammoth tutor online?

Yes. Homeschool students who use Math Mammoth join our teachers live online, working on the chapter where they are stuck. Classes run in a batch of 15 to 20 or one to one.

What grades does Math Mammoth cover?

The Light Blue Series is a full curriculum for kindergarten to grade 8. Math Mammoth says it does not offer Algebra 1 or above, and that after grade 7 or 8 students can go straight to high school math such as Algebra 1.

Is Math Mammoth self-teaching?

Math Mammoth describes it as nearly self-teaching, with all instruction written directly to the student and free matched videos for grades 1 to 7. Many children manage alone for long stretches; a teacher helps when a chapter does not click.

How does Math Mammoth teach multiplication?

Math Mammoth says mental math strategies are taught and practised explicitly. In our classes we build each fact from known ones, such as 7 x 8 by doubling three times, so children can recover a fact they forget.

Where can I find Math Mammoth extra practice?

Each grade includes a worksheet maker, and levels 1 to 8 include cumulative review worksheets; the site also offers free online practice. Our teachers add fresh mixed problems of their own in class.

Which Math Mammoth placement test should my child take?

Math Mammoth suggests starting with the highest level test you think your child will pass. The tests are end-of-year tests grouped by topic, so look at section scores, and test lower sections if a gap appears.

Why is my child struggling with Math Mammoth word problems?

In our experience it is usually because the operations are mixed on purpose, and the child is still using whatever the lesson title suggests. Asking what is happening in the story before using any numbers helps a lot.

Will a tutor replace Math Mammoth?

No. The worktext stays your curriculum. We teach the stuck chapter live and hand the work back to the book as soon as your child can explain it.

When are classes?

Weekdays or weekends, flexible: choose your batch, or for a very personalised plan choose 1-on-1 at your convenience, weekend mornings or weekdays.

Are you connected with Math Mammoth?

No. We are an independent online school. Math Mammoth is a trademark of its owner, and we describe it only from its own published pages.
