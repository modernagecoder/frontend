A school, a venue and a business district with different capacity questions

All named institutions are sources of local context. None is presented as a partner or endorser of Modern Age Coders.

ABA's Al Irfan campus opened in March 2022

ABA Oman International School says its Madinat Al Irfan campus opened in March 2022. It describes a Learning Center, Athletics Center, Performing Arts Center and interactive outdoor learning spaces for students aged 3 to 18.

The school offers the IB Primary Years, Middle Years and Diploma Programmes. ABA's wider history page says it was founded in 1987 and serves a community representing more than 70 nationalities. These facts inform placement questions but do not make Modern Age Coders an IB provider.

Class-size guides change by stage

ABA's admissions guidance publishes class-assignment guides of 16 for FC1 and FC2, 20 for Kindergarten, 22 for Grades 1 to 10 and 18 per subject class in Grades 11 and 12.

A model that sees the Grade 1 to 10 value and extends it to every grade would be wrong before it begins. The institution itself supplies a piecewise rule. Good software keeps the stage boundaries instead of forcing one continuous pattern across them.

OMRAN describes a multi-asset district

OMRAN Group calls Madinat Al Irfan its flagship urban destination. It places Oman Convention and Exhibition Centre at the heart of the district and names Crowne Plaza Muscat OCEC and Muscat's first JW Marriott as connected hospitality assets.

The same page describes the country's first fully LEED and WELL certified Business Park and names Omantel, OQ and OQEP headquarters there. It says the International Financial Centre of Oman is set to join the district. Existing assets and future elements require different status fields.

OCEC publishes configuration-specific capacities

OCEC's official event-space guide gives Exhibition Halls 1 to 5 a combined area of 22,396 square metres and a plan for 1,408 exhibition booths. Individual halls are not identical.

Hall 1 is listed at 4,880 square metres, with 3,744 theatre seats or 195 exhibition booths. Hall 2 is 4,212 square metres, with 3,648 theatre seats or 165 booths. Layout changes the capacity, so a forecast needs a room and configuration rather than one universal venue number.

A date is part of a fact

ABA's 2022 opening, a current business-park description and a venue configuration table are not timeless constants. Each data row needs a source, effective date and status before it can support a decision.

Madinat Al Irfan forecasting project

A straight first week does not authorise a straight twentieth day

The registration series is invented. It is not connected to ABA, OCEC, OMRAN, a hotel or any actual event.

The tempting line

A synthetic campaign records 120, 150, 180, 210 and 240 registrations on days 1 through 5. A line fits perfectly, adding 30 per day. Extending the formula to day 20 returns 690.

days = [1, 2, 3, 4, 5]
registrations = [120, 150, 180, 210, 240]

def fitted_line(day):
    return 90 + 30 * day

fitted_line(5)   # 240, observed boundary
fitted_line(20)  # 690, unsupported extrapolation

The model fits every observed point. That proves only that the five values are linear. Day 20 is fifteen days beyond the observed boundary and three times farther from day 5 than the entire measured span from day 1 to day 5.

What the line cannot know

Promotion can end, interest can saturate, duplicate records can be removed, capacity can bind and registration rules can change. None appears in the five-point series. A narrow residual of zero inside the training range does not measure uncertainty outside it.

The learner must draw a visible boundary at day 5, label later output as extrapolation and avoid publishing a point estimate without assumptions or an uncertainty treatment. Better work adds holdout days, operational constraints and alternative curves before deciding whether any forecast is useful.

Required response

The fitted line predicts 690 under an unchanged linear process, but the dataset contains no evidence after day 5. Treat day 20 as a scenario, not an observed or validated forecast.

Madinat Al Irfan coding class answers

Can ABA Oman students join these coding classes?

Yes. ABA says its Madinat Al Irfan campus opened in March 2022 and offers the IB Primary Years, Middle Years and Diploma Programmes. Modern Age Coders is independent of ABA and the IB. The free class places a learner from observed coding or mathematics work, not from the programme name, grade or predicted school result.

What is the Madinat Al Irfan extrapolation project?

A synthetic five-day series rises by 30 registrations each day, so a fitted line predicts 690 on day 20. The observed range ends on day 5. Learners must mark the forecast as extrapolation, withhold unsupported certainty, test alternative shapes and ask what capacity, promotion or saturation could break the early linear pattern.

Does the project predict attendance at the Oman Convention and Exhibition Centre?

No. The practice values are invented and are not bookings, registrations, delegates or visitors from OCEC, ABA or another local organisation. Official venue dimensions are cited separately as district facts. The project teaches why a model fitted to a short observed range cannot become a real Madinat Al Irfan forecast without authorised data and validation.

Is there a Modern Age Coders classroom in Madinat Al Irfan?

No. Modern Age Coders teaches from India through live online classes. It has no Madinat Al Irfan branch, Muscat classroom, street address or Omani telephone number. The displayed +91 number is the provider's real Indian WhatsApp and telephone contact. The first class is free, so the family can test delivery before payment.

Can an IB learner use coding for an independent project?

Yes, when the scope is appropriate and school rules are followed. A learner can build a tool, simulation, analysis or website while keeping a development log and citing outside help. Modern Age Coders will teach and review skills, but it will not write assessed work for the student or claim endorsement from ABA or the IB.

Why does venue capacity matter to a forecasting lesson?

Capacity is an example of a real-world constraint that a free-running line can ignore. OCEC publishes different areas and seating layouts for different halls, so one generic maximum is not enough. A sound model names the event space, configuration, sales limit and time window before turning an early registration trend into an operational forecast.

Can adults working in the business district join?

Yes. The taught range is ages 6 to 67. Adults can study Python, web development, databases, automation, data analysis, data science or mathematics after a free diagnostic class. A useful starting goal is a small report or application with known users and constraints. The course does not grant Omani institutional or professional credit.
