What the IGCSE written paper rewards

Paper 2 ends with a 15-mark scenario question on a problem the pupil has never seen. Answers may be written in Cambridge pseudocode or in Python, Visual Basic or Java, and the syllabus warns that solutions in any other language earn no marks.

Among the standard methods the syllabus lists are totalling, counting, and "finding maximum, minimum and average". The Stuttgart project below is built on exactly those three.

What the AP dates mean in practice

For AP Computer Science Principles, the Create task closes before the exam does: a pupil in Stuttgart must have it submitted by the small hours of Saturday 1 May 2027, two weeks before the written paper.

AP Computer Science A falls on a Wednesday afternoon in Stuttgart. A pupil revising with us in April gets the last full practice paper in the week of 3 May.

The German route, for comparison

How is computer science examined at a German Gymnasium in Baden-Württemberg?

Families sometimes move between systems, so it helps to know the other side. The facts are from the Informatik-Monitor 2026/27 of the GI.

Three ways to take it

In the Qualifikationsphase a Gymnasium can offer Informatik as a two-hour elective, a three-hour Basisfach or a five-hour Leistungsfach, the advanced course. Not every school offers all three.

A central written exam

In the Leistungsfach the written Abitur is set centrally for the whole Land. Programming tasks may be answered in the language used in the course, but no programming environment is allowed, and there is no work at a computer.

A new subject lower down

Below the Oberstufe, the Land has made Informatik und Medienbildung compulsory since 2025/26, beginning in grades 5 and 6. Pupils switching in from an international school may meet classmates who have just started it.

We teach alongside school in both systems and award no grades or qualifications of any kind; the school course and its exam are what count. What carries across is the skill: an IB pupil who can write a clean loop on paper is ready for a German written exam with no computer, and an Abitur pupil who can test code is ready for an IB internal assessment.

A Stuttgart data project

Are hot days in Stuttgart becoming more common? Two answers from the same file

Stuttgart's statistics office publishes weather readings back to 1980 as open data. Older pupils use one column, the yearly count of hot days, to see how easily a single pair of years can be made to say anything, and how a fair comparison is built instead.

The file

The dataset holds monthly and yearly values for temperature, rain, sunshine and air pressure. Its notes say the readings come from the Stuttgart weather office station until 1983 and from the Deutscher Wetterdienst automatic station on the Schnarrenberg from 1984.

We extract one row per year, the count the file labels heiße Tage, hot days, for 1980 to 2024, and save it as stuttgart_hot_days.csv with two columns: year and hot_days. That gives 45 years and no gaps.

The trap

Pick 2003 and 2021, and hot days fell sharply. Pick 1993 and 2022, and they rose tenfold. Both statements are true about the numbers, and both are useless, because each depends on which two years you chose.

The fair version compares whole decades of equal length, measured at the same station, and reports how many years went into each average, exactly what an examiner wants to see when a question says "justify".

The first program can be made to support either headline. 2003 had 28 hot days and 2021 only 4, so it reports a fall of 24; 1993 had 3 and 2022 had 30, so it reports a rise of 27. A pupil who quotes one of these in an essay is cherry-picking, even without meaning to.

Data: Landeshauptstadt Stuttgart, Klima- und Wetterdaten, published through Open Data BW, licence CC BY 4.0, downloaded 2 November 2026; readings from the Deutscher Wetterdienst station on the Schnarrenberg since 1984. Averages, lowest and highest years are our own calculations from the yearly hot-day counts.

The windows tell a more careful story than either headline. The most recent decade averages 19.5 hot days a year, well above the other three. But the averages do not rise every decade: 1995 to 2004 sits slightly above 2005 to 2014, because it contains 2003. And the decade with the highest average also holds one of the coolest summers in the file, 2021 with 4. We start in 1985 so every window uses the Schnarrenberg station only; mixing in the old station would change what is being measured.

  1. IGCSE practice. Write the decade loop in Cambridge pseudocode with a FOR loop, a running total and a count, then trace it by hand for 1985 to 1994.
  2. Extend it. Add the lowest and highest year to each window without using min() or max(), the way Paper 2 expects.
  3. IB style. Replace the fixed decades with a ten-year moving average and print one value per year from 1994. Explain in two sentences what smoothing hides as well as what it shows.
  4. AP CSP style. Turn the program into a procedure with a parameter for the window length, and describe in plain English how the procedure uses iteration and selection.

Computer science for international school pupils in Stuttgart: questions

Are the classes in German?

No. We teach exclusively in English, live, which is also the language an international school classroom runs in. Mentors adjust their speed and phrasing to each pupil, which helps pupils whose strongest language is neither English nor German.

Which international schools are there in Stuttgart?

The AGIS directory lists the International School of Stuttgart, with 814 pupils and the IB Primary Years, Middle Years and Diploma programmes. The KMK list of 26 March 2026 also names the SIS Swiss International School Stuttgart-Fellbach. We name them as background only and have no link with either.

Can you help with IB Diploma Computer Science?

Yes. Our IB course covers the concepts, programming in Python or Java and support with the internal assessment, where we explain and question but the work stays the pupil's own. The IB coordinator at school confirms the exam dates.

What is in the IGCSE Computer Science 0478 exam?

Two written papers of 1 hour 45 minutes and 75 marks each: Computer Systems, and Algorithms, Programming and Logic. Paper 2 ends with a 15-mark scenario question answered in pseudocode, Python, Visual Basic or Java, and calculators are not allowed.

When are the AP Computer Science exams in 2027, in German time?

AP Computer Science A is on Wednesday 12 May 2027 in Session 2, which is 13:00 in Stuttgart. AP Computer Science Principles is on Friday 14 May 2027 in Session 1, 09:00 in Stuttgart, and its Create task must be submitted by 05:59 on 1 May 2027 German time.

When are lessons in Stuttgart time?

Weekdays or weekends, flexible: choose your batch, or for a very personalised plan choose 1-on-1 at your convenience, weekend mornings or weekdays. Many international school pupils start at about 16:15 on a weekday or around midday on Saturday.

Does an IB Diploma count as an Abitur?

AGIS says the IB Diploma, with the prescribed subject combinations, is equivalent to the German Abitur. The exact rules are in the KMK recognition agreement, and the school's counsellor is the right person to check a pupil's own subject choice. We do not give admissions advice.

My child may move to a German Gymnasium. Can you help with that too?

We can teach the subject alongside either school, in English. In Baden-Württemberg the written Informatik Abitur in the Leistungsfach is set centrally and allows no programming environment, so we practise code on paper as well as on screen.

How are fees charged?

Monthly, for eight lessons. The page shows euro at the European Central Bank reference rate and the card is charged in US dollars, converted by your bank, so the statement can differ a little. The first lesson is free and needs no card.

Where are the mentors based?

In India. They teach live on video, so a 16:15 lesson in Stuttgart starts at 20:45 for the mentor in winter. The pupil keeps the same mentor through the course.
