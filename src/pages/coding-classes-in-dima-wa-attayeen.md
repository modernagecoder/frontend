FAQ Dima Wa Attayeen coding class questions Local facts, teaching method and terms of service, kept apart.
Is the 51 village figure real? It is published as some 51 villages, and the word some belongs to the source rather than to us. That hedge is kept everywhere the number appears here. The water source counts in the project are teaching values, because no per-village survey is published, and the page labels them wherever they occur.
Why is reading a blank as zero so tempting? Because it lets the arithmetic finish. A missing value stops a mean from computing and a zero does not, so the substitution turns an error into a result. The result is wrong and looks entirely normal, which is the worst combination available.
Is there a Modern Age Coders centre in Dima Wa Attayeen? No. No premises are claimed in Dima Wa Attayeen or anywhere in Ash Sharqiyah North. Classes are live online and need a laptop or desktop, working audio, a current browser and a stable connection. The page describes where the service reaches, not a building.
What is the correct answer when data is missing? Say what you know and say what you do not, in the same sentence. Six villages have no active source and five were not surveyed is a complete, useful and honest answer. Eleven villages have no water is a shorter sentence and it is not true.
Do you teach working adults here, or only school students? Both, to an upper limit of 67. The adult track covers Python, databases and reporting. Anyone who has watched a dashboard drop sharply and then found that a feed had stopped rather than a number falling has already lived this exact confusion. Where the figures are yours to hand over, they make a far better exercise than ours.
What does a learner have at the end of this project? A file where a gap is stored as a gap, a summary that prints its own coverage, and a headline figure written as known and unknown rather than as a single confident number. That habit survives every change of tool.
