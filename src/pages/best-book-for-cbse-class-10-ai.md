## What is the best book for class 10 AI in 2026-27?

There is no single best book: the free CBSE handbook is the base for every student, and a bought book is worth it only for the gap it fills. The four kinds of book do different jobs, so compare the type before the title.

- Type of AI 417 book | What it gives you | What it usually lacks | Suits a student who

- CBSE AI Facilitator Handbook, Class X (free PDF) | The official content of Part B Units 1 to 6, activities, no-code walkthroughs, end-of-unit exercises | Part A, Unit 7, answers beyond the MCQs, board-format papers | everyone, as the base text

- Publisher textbook written to the 417 units | Fuller explanations, more examples, exercises; often Part A in the same book | Solved board papers and exam-style timed practice | finds the handbook hard to learn from alone

- All-in-one guide | Condensed theory, question practice by type, sample papers and revision aids in one volume | The depth of a textbook; activity work | knows the content and wants board-style practice

- Question bank of solved papers | Past board questions sorted by chapter and topic, with worked answers | Teaching; older papers may follow an earlier chapter list | wants volume of practice from January onwards

### The rule of one

Buy one book, not three. Several guides bought at once tend to be started and left. One book worked through to the end, with every wrong answer redone, does more for the marks than a shelf of partly read ones.

### Where the handbook stops

Its exercises have printed answers only for the MCQs. How to answer the rest, unit by unit, is set out on [class 10 AI textbook solutions](/cbse-class-10-ai-textbook-solutions).

## How to check any AI 417 book against the 2026-27 syllabus

Open the contents page and compare it with the 2026-27 unit list below. If the units, the practical-only labels and the session year all match, the book is current; if the chapter titles are the older ones, it was written for an earlier syllabus.

### The 2026-27 units a book must follow

- Unit | Theory marks

- Part A: five employability skills units, 2 marks each | 10

- Unit 1: Revisiting AI Project Cycle and Ethical Frameworks for AI | 7

- Unit 2: Advanced Concepts of Modeling in AI | 11

- Unit 3: Evaluating Models | 10

- Unit 4: Statistical Data | practical only

- Unit 5: Computer Vision | 4

- Unit 6: Natural Language Processing | 8

- Unit 7: Advance Python | practical only

### Five checks before you buy

-
**Session on the cover** Look for 2026-27 or "2027 exam". A 2026-exam edition was made for the previous session, so compare its units with the table before relying on it.

-
**Unit names** Chapters called Data Science, Evaluation or AI Project Cycle on their own belong to the older list.

-
**Units 4 and 7 marked practical** A book that sets long theory answers on Statistical Data is not tracking the current paper.

-
**Part A included** Ten marks sit there. If the book leaves Part A out, plan another source.

-
**Assertion-reason and case items** Both appeared in the 2025 and 2026 board papers, so a current practice book should carry them.

What each unit contains, topic by topic, is on [the 417 syllabus explained](/cbse-class-10-ai-syllabus-explained).

## Is the CBSE AI handbook pdf enough for Class 10?

For the Part B concepts, yes: the board paper is set on the same units, and the handbook is the official statement of them. It is not enough on its own for four things, and each can be covered without buying anything.

- Gap in the handbook | Why it matters | A free way to cover it

- Part A employability skills | 10 theory marks | CBSE's Part A material for the skill subjects, plus our Part A page

- Unit 7: Advance Python | Part of the practical exam and the file of at least 15 programs | The eight suggested programs in the CBSE curriculum, run in Jupyter; worked on Python for CBSE Class 10 AI

- Answers beyond the MCQs | Reflection and case questions are the 2 and 4 mark shapes | Learn the answer method, then check it against model answers

- Board-format practice | Timing, choice and the marking style | The CBSE sample paper and its marking scheme on cbseacademic.nic.in

### How to read the handbook well

- One session at a time. Each unit is split into short sessions; finish one before the next.

- Do the activities. The no-code walkthroughs in Orange and Teachable Machine turn into practical and viva questions.

- Write the Reflection Time answers. Thinking them through is not the same as writing them in the length the marks deserve.

### Where the PDF lives

On cbseacademic.nic.in, under the Skill Education material for AI, the Class X file is named AI_Facilitators_Handbook_X.pdf. An older Class 10 handbook from 2021 is still on the site; it follows the earlier chapter list, so make sure you have the current one.

## Which AI 417 book suits which student?

Match the book to where the student is now, not to what classmates bought. Four common situations, and what tends to work in each.

### Starting late, in November or after
Handbook for the units, with a short revision source for speed. A thick textbook started in December rarely gets finished. The fast route is mapped on [Class 10 AI one shot revision](/cbse-class-10-ai-one-shot-revision).

### Understands the topics but loses marks
The problem is answer writing, not content. One guide or question bank, worked under time and marked strictly, helps more than a new textbook.

### Finds the handbook hard to follow
A publisher textbook written to the 417 units, read alongside the handbook, gives the extra examples and slower explanations that help.

### Aiming for full marks
Handbook plus every exercise type practised, then several timed full papers with the marking scheme beside them. The paper's patterns matter more than a second book.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which book is best for class 10 AI 417?** Start with the free CBSE AI Facilitator Handbook for Class X, which is the official content for Part B Units 1 to 6. Add one bought book only for a clear gap: a 417 textbook for fuller explanations, an all-in-one guide for board-style practice, or a question bank for solved past papers.

**Is Arihant All in One good for AI class 10?** Arihant lists an All in One Artificial Intelligence Code 417 Class 10 for the 2026-27 exam, with brief theory, practice by question type, case based and assertion-reason items, sample papers and mind maps. It is a guide, so it suits a student who already knows the units and wants board-style practice. Use it with the handbook, not in place of it.

**Is there a Kips AI class 10 book for 2026-27?** Kips publishes an Artificial Intelligence skill course book for Class 10 under code 417. The retail listings we checked in November 2026 were labelled 2026 edition or Revised Edition 2026, and none we found was marked 2026-27, so check the session printed on the copy, and its unit names, before buying.

**Where can I download the CBSE AI handbook pdf for class 10?** From cbseacademic.nic.in, in the Skill Education material for AI. The current Class X file is named AI_Facilitators_Handbook_X.pdf. An older 2021 Class 10 handbook is also hosted there, but it follows the earlier chapter list, so use the current file. The download is free and needs no login.

**Is the AI 417 book an NCERT book?** No. CBSE AI 417 is a skill subject, and its official book is the AI Facilitator Handbook prepared by CBSE with Intel. Private publishers such as Sultan Chand and Orange Education sell textbooks written to the same units, and some guides describe their theory as NCERT-based.

**Do I need a guide book for CBSE Class 10 AI?** Not necessarily. The handbook covers the Part B concepts, and the CBSE sample paper and marking scheme are free. A guide helps a student who wants a lot of board-style practice in one place. It does not replace learning the units first.

**Can I use an old edition of an AI class 10 book?** Only with care. Older editions follow earlier chapter titles and may give theory weight to Statistical Data, which is practical only in 2026-27. Check every chapter against the current units and skip what no longer applies, or use the free handbook instead.

**Which book covers Part A and Advance Python?** The CBSE handbook covers neither. Publisher textbooks and guides for 417 usually include Part A, so check the contents page. For Unit 7, the eight suggested programs in the CBSE curriculum, run in Jupyter, matter more than any printed chapter, because it is tested in the practical.
