## Why Further Maths tuition costs more

### A smaller pool of tutors

Plenty of tutors teach A level Maths. Far fewer are comfortable with complex numbers, matrices, induction and the further calculus in Core Pure, so the ones who are can charge more.

### Options vary by school

Schools choose different applied options, such as further mechanics, further statistics or decision maths. A tutor who knows your school's options is worth more than one who only knows Core Pure.

### Two A levels at once

Most students take Further Maths alongside Maths. Paying two separate tutors means paying twice for the shared foundations; one tutor covering both is usually cheaper overall.

### Exam board papers

AQA, Edexcel and OCR each structure their Further Maths papers differently. Practice on the right board's papers saves lessons.

### Degree-level tutors

Most Further Maths tutors hold or are studying for a maths degree. University-level maths tutoring averages about $63 to $70 an hour in the guide we read, which sets the top of the range.

### Online widens the choice

A Further Maths specialist is unlikely to live round the corner. Online lessons let you choose by subject knowledge rather than postcode, without paying for travel.

## Questions to ask a Further Maths tutor before paying

- Have they taught Core Pure to the end of Year 13, not just Year 12?
- Which applied options can they teach, and are they the ones your school chose?
- Do they work from your board's Further Maths papers?
- Can the same tutor cover A level Maths, so foundations are not paid for twice?

- Will they set and mark practice between lessons?
- Is there a first lesson you can watch first?
- Are there fees on top of the hourly rate?
- Will they decline to promise a grade? An honest tutor will.

How much does a Further Maths tutor cost per hour in the UK?

Further Maths tutors are priced at the top of the A level range. The rates table lists what we could find, with a source for each figure and the exchange rate used.

Is a Further Maths tutor more expensive than an A level Maths tutor?

Usually, because fewer tutors can teach Core Pure and the applied options well. Using one tutor for both maths A levels avoids paying twice for the shared foundations.

What does A level Further Maths cover?

A Core Pure part taken by everyone, including complex numbers, matrices, proof by induction, further calculus and differential equations, plus applied options such as further mechanics, further statistics or decision maths, depending on the board and school.

Can my child take Further Maths if their school does not offer it?

Some students do, studying the course outside school and entering the exams through an exam centre. Our [Further Maths course](/courses/a-level-further-maths-course) covers the full specification for AQA, Edexcel and OCR.

Do I need Further Maths for a maths degree?
