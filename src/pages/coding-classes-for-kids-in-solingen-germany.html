Screen time

Is a coding class just more screen time for my child?

It uses a screen, but it is the opposite of scrolling. The difference is who is in charge of what happens next.

Making, not watching

In a game or a video, someone else has decided everything. In a coding class, your child decides what the character does, writes the rules and fixes them when they do not work.

Thinking away from the screen

Mentors often ask a child to plan on paper first: draw the maze, list the steps, guess the result. A good part of each lesson is talking and thinking, not clicking.

Short and finished

A lesson lasts an hour and ends with something your child made and can show you. There is no endless feed and nothing that pulls them back in.

  1. Explain. The mentor shows one small idea, such as making a sprite repeat a dance.
  2. Predict. Before running anything, your child says what they think will happen.
  3. Build. Your child makes their own version, with their own character and rules.
  4. Break and fix. Something goes wrong on purpose, and your child finds out why.
  5. Show. At the end, your child explains what they built in their own words.

NRW schools

When does Informatik start at Solingen schools?

At grade 5. Before that, coding is something a child does at home or in a club. Checked against the GI monitor and the KMK on 2 November 2026.

Primary years

Solingen children go to Grundschule for four years, from grade 1 to grade 4, as in almost every Land. NRW has no compulsory Informatik subject in those years.

So the ages six to nine, when children pick up new ways of thinking most easily, are left to families.

Grades 5 and 6

The Informatik-Monitor 2026/27 of the Gesellschaft für Informatik reports that Informatik is a compulsory subject in grades 5 and 6 at all general school types in North Rhine-Westphalia.

A child who arrives in grade 5 already able to plan steps and use a loop has a head start. Our classes run alongside school and give no marks; the school subject is the one that counts.

In English

Will my child understand a coding class in English?

Every class is live and taught in English only, and young children usually settle in within a few lessons.

English, plainly said

We do not teach in German. Mentors are based in India and teach over live video, in English, and we say so here rather than after a trial.

Fitted to your child

Mentors use short sentences, repeat key words, point at the screen and wait for an answer. They get used to your child's accent, and a German word in an answer is fine.

A small vocabulary

Move, turn, repeat, if, score, sprite. Children need only a handful of words to start building, and they learn them by using them.

Your child sees one mentor throughout the course, so the mentor learns what makes them laugh, what they find hard, and when they need an easy win before a harder step.

A real Solingen project

Which was Solingen's snowiest winter? Ranking 30 winters fairly

The Deutscher Wetterdienst measures how deep the snow lies each morning at Solingen-Hohenscheid. Our older children use 30 winters of it to learn something every ranking needs: a rule for ties.

The data

Station 4741, Solingen-Hohenscheid, has recorded daily rain and snow depth for decades. The DWD Climate Data Center publishes it under the Creative Commons BY 4.0 licence; we downloaded the file on 3 November 2026.

We keep December, January and February of each winter from 1990/91 to 2024/25, but only winters where at least 90 of those days have a snow reading. That leaves 30 winters, saved as hohenscheid_winters.csv.

The question

On how many mornings each winter was there at least 1 centimetre of snow on the ground? That rule is ours, decided before counting. Then: put the winters in order, from snowiest to least snowy.

Sorting is easy. Giving each winter its place turns out to be the interesting part.

Look at places 7 and 8: both winters had exactly 18 snow days. Which one is seventh? The program chose one, but only because of the order the winters happened to arrive in. A ten year old spots that this is not fair.

The fair rule is the one sports use: your place is one more than the number of winters with more snow than yours. Both 18-day winters are seventh, and the next one is ninth. At the bottom, two winters had just one snow day each and share 29th place, so there is no 30th. Every one of the 30 winters had at least one snowy morning.

  1. Ages 6 to 8, no screen. Line up five toys by height. Two are exactly the same height. Who stands where?
  2. Ages 8 to 10, Scratch. Make a list of five scores with two the same, and a script that says each score's place.
  3. Ages 10 to 12, Python. Change the snow rule to at least 5 centimetres. Does 2009/2010 stay first?
  4. Going further. Print the whole ranking and find every pair of winters that share a place.

Solingen parents ask

Coding classes for kids in Solingen: questions from parents

Are the classes in German?

No. All lessons are live and taught in English only. The mentors are based in India; they use short sentences, show everything on screen and adapt to your child's pace and accent, so young children with little English can take part.

Is coding just more screen time?

It uses a screen, but your child is making things, not watching them. Lessons follow plan, predict, build, fix and show, with plenty of talking and thinking, and each one ends after an hour with something finished.

What age can my child start?

From six, with Scratch, where blocks snap together and nothing has to be typed. From about nine, most children are ready for typed Python.

When does Informatik start at Solingen schools?

In grade 5. The Gesellschaft für Informatik reports that Informatik is compulsory in grades 5 and 6 at all general school types in North Rhine-Westphalia. Grundschule has no compulsory Informatik.

When are classes in Solingen time?

Weekdays or weekends, flexible: choose your batch, or for a very personalised plan choose 1-on-1 at your convenience, weekend mornings or weekdays. Solingen children usually come after school or late on a weekend morning.

Which maths contests can my child enter?

The Pangea-Mathematikwettbewerb, with a first round from 23 February to 3 March 2027, and the Känguru der Mathematik on 8 April 2027. Both go through schools, so ask the class teacher.

Is the snow project real Solingen data?

Yes. It uses snow depth from Deutscher Wetterdienst station 4741, Solingen-Hohenscheid, published under the CC BY 4.0 licence and downloaded on 3 November 2026. The snow-day rule and the ranking are our own.

Who will teach my child?

One mentor based in India, live by video, for the whole course. Because India is ahead of Germany, a Solingen afternoon is the mentor's evening.

How many children are in a class?

Fifteen to twenty children of a similar age, or just your child on the one-to-one plan, with two lessons a week either way.

In what currency are fees paid?

In US dollars. We show the monthly fee in euro using the European Central Bank reference rate, and your bank converts the dollar charge, so the euro amount on your statement can vary slightly. The first class is free.
