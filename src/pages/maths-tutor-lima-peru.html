How Lima students are assessed nationally

MINEDU's national assessment, the ENLA, was applied from 11 to 13 November 2025 to more than 200,000 students in 8,700 public and private schools, testing maths in 4° de primaria, 2° de secundaria and 5° de secundaria.

The national results, published in May 2026, were similar to 2023 and 2024 overall, with one bright spot: maths in 2° de secundaria improved on 2023, the first positive change in that grade since schools closed. MINEDU's regional tables were not reachable when we checked, so we print no Lima-only figure.

Where tutoring helps most

Four or six hours a week is a lot of maths, but it is shared by a whole class. Tutoring adds what a full classroom rarely can: one learner explaining their own method, with someone who listens for exactly where it goes wrong.

The common sticking points we see in Lima students mirror the competencies: fractions and proportion in late primaria, algebra in 1° and 2° de secundaria, functions and statistics in the final years.

Maths in English

Why choose a maths tutor who teaches in English in Lima?

For two kinds of family. Some have children whose school already teaches maths in English. Others want maths and English practice in the same hour. Every class is live and in English only; none are in Spanish.

Bilingual, Cambridge and IB schools

Many Lima private schools teach maths in English, or follow Cambridge or IB programmes in which exams are written in English. MINEDU reports that 2,502 students in Peru obtained the IB Diploma in 2025, 47% of them at the public COAR schools and the rest at other schools.

For these students a tutor who explains in Spanish leaves a gap: the method is understood, but the words on the exam paper are not. We teach in the language of the exam, with the notation the school uses.

Spanish-medium schools

For a student at a Spanish-medium colegio, maths in English is a second gain. Maths has a small, exact vocabulary, so it is one of the easiest subjects to follow in a second language, and each class adds listening and explaining in English.

EF's English Proficiency Index 2025 scores Lima city 555, in its high band, well above Peru's 519. Many Lima students are ready for it; the free first class checks.

A Lima maths lesson

Is Metro Line 1 longer than a straight line from Villa El Salvador to Bayóvar?

Obviously, a student says. Then we ask: by how much, and can you prove it from data? This lesson, ours, uses the official list of Line 1 stations and two ideas from secundaria: Pythagoras and the triangle inequality.

The data

The AATE, the Ministry of Transport authority for the Lima and Callao metro, publishes "ESTACIONES DE METRO DE LIMA - LINEA 1" on Peru's Plataforma Nacional de Datos Abiertos under the Open Data Commons Attribution Licence (ODC-By). The file, estaciones2018.xlsx, lists 26 stations from Villa El Salvador in the south to Bayóvar in San Juan de Lurigancho, each with a district, an address and its latitude and longitude written as text.

We copied the station, district and coordinate columns into a small file of our own, estaciones-linea1.csv, keeping the stations in the order the file gives them.

The maths

Latitude and longitude are angles, not distances. Near Lima, one degree of latitude is about 111.19 km (our figure: the Earth's mean radius of 6,371 km times pi over 180). A degree of longitude is shorter, by the cosine of the latitude, which near 12 degrees south is 0.9779.

Over a few kilometres the ground is nearly flat, so we can turn each pair of stations into a right-angled triangle, north-south and east-west, and use Pythagoras for the straight distance between them.

Version 1 treats a degree east the same as a degree north, which is slightly wrong. Version 2 fixes that with the cosine, then does the more interesting thing: it adds up the 25 straight hops from each station to the next.

Straight-line distances between some neighbouring stations (our calculation)
From and toStraight distance
Villa El Salvador to Parque Industrial1.36 km

La Cultura to Arriola1.34 km

Data: MTC, AATE, Estaciones de Metro de Lima, Línea 1 (estaciones2018.xlsx), Plataforma Nacional de Datos Abiertos, ODC-By. Distances are ours, straight lines between the published coordinates; they are not the length of the track, which curves between stations, and we print no official route length.

Three results, three lessons. First, the cosine changed the end-to-end distance by only 0.03 km; ask the student why (the line runs mostly north to south, so the east-west leg is small). Second, the hops add up to 32.39 km against 28.12 km in a straight line, a ratio of 1.152. That is the triangle inequality at work: going from A to C through B is never shorter than going straight from A to C, so every bend adds distance. Third, the real track is longer still, because it curves between stations, so 32.39 km is a lower bound for the track, not its length.

  1. Primaria, 9 to 11. On squared paper, walk from one corner of a 3 by 4 rectangle to the opposite corner along the edges, then diagonally. Measure both. Which is shorter, and is it always?
  2. 1° and 2° de secundaria. Prove that a triangle with sides 3 cm, 4 cm and 8 cm cannot exist. What rule did you use?
  3. 3° and 4° de secundaria. Use Pythagoras by hand on the Cabitos to Angamos hop: the stations are 0.0166 degrees apart north to south and 0.0004 east to west. Check you get about 1.85 km.
  4. 5° de secundaria and IB. Why does a degree of longitude shrink with the cosine of the latitude? Sketch the Earth and a circle of latitude to explain.
  5. Going further. If the metro added a station halfway between two existing ones, but 200 metres to the side, how much would the sum of hops grow? Use the triangle inequality to estimate first, then calculate.

Lima and online classes

Why take maths classes online in Lima?

Because Lima is very large. INEI counted 10,141,775 people in Lima Metropolitana in 2025, across 43 districts, and a weekly trip to a tutor's home or an academy costs real time.

One city, very different distances

San Juan de Lurigancho alone had 1,176,422 people in 2025, more than most Peruvian departments; Villa El Salvador had 451,546. A family at either end of Line 1 is the same distance from an online tutor as a family in Miraflores.

Lima's 2017 city figure, which INEI gives for Lima province together with Callao, was 9,562,280. The 2025 total for Lima Metropolitana does not include Callao, so the two are never compared as growth.

What a family needs

A laptop or tablet, headphones and a steady connection. In Lima Metropolitana, INEI found 92.4% of people aged 6 and over used the internet in the second quarter of 2026.

A pen and paper matter as much as the screen. Students write their working on paper and hold it up, or use the shared whiteboard, so the tutor sees every step.

Maths tutor in Lima: questions and answers

Are the classes in Spanish?

No. Every maths class is live, online and in English only. Tutors speak at a steady pace, follow the student's accent and speed, and when a student answers in Spanish they reply with the English term. For many Lima families the English is part of the point.

Do you tutor IB and IGCSE maths in Lima?

Yes. We teach IB Mathematics Analysis and Approaches and Applications and Interpretation at SL and HL, and IGCSE Core and Extended. We are not connected with the IB, Cambridge or any school, and the school awards the grades.

Can you help a student at a Spanish-medium colegio?

Yes. Tutors follow the CNEB topics the student is meeting in class and explain them in simple English. Maths vocabulary is small and exact, so most students follow it quickly.

Do you prepare students for university admission exams?

No. Admission exams are set in Spanish by each university, which alone decides who is admitted. We teach the maths underneath them, such as algebra, geometry and careful checking, and we are not connected with any university.

What ages do you teach?

From early primaria to adults. Six to eight year olds work on number sense, secundaria students on algebra, functions and statistics, and adults on refreshers, university maths or statistics.

What time are the classes?

Any day, any time, flexible: choose a batch, or for a very personalised plan choose 1-on-1 at the hour that suits your family. Lima is on Peru time all year and the tutor in India is 10 hours 30 minutes ahead, so 08:30 in Lima is 19:00 there.

How does a tutor see my child's working online?

On a shared whiteboard, or by the student holding written work up to the camera. Tutors ask for the method out loud as well as on paper.

How much does maths tutoring cost?

Fees are monthly, shown on this page in US dollars and charged by card in dollars; your bank converts them to soles. Visitors in India see rupees. The first class is free.

Is there a certificate?

Courses that include one end with our own certificate of completion. It is not a Peruvian school grade or qualification and is not issued by MINEDU, the IB or Cambridge.
