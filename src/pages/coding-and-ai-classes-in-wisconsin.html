How many Wisconsin high schools offer computer science?

In 2023 to 2024, 318 of 585 Wisconsin high schools (54.4%) offered computer science, and 8,377 of 262,627 high school students (3.19%) were enrolled in it, according to Code.org (2025). Both figures sit below the national 60% and 6.4%. Modern Age Coders teaches Wisconsin students live online in Central Time, from computational thinking and Roblox to Python and machine learning.

Every number on this page is a share of something, and the first skill we teach Wisconsin students is to ask of what. Just over half of the state’s high schools offered computer science in the latest Code.org year, but a little over three in every hundred high school students were enrolled. Same state, same year, two very different percentages, because one counts buildings and the other counts people. Below are both series as published, a Madison open data project that turns the same idea into a chart a student can defend, and the practical details of live classes for families from Superior to Kenosha.

Signature project

Most Madison parks are small. Most Madison park land is not

The City of Madison publishes every park parcel as open data, each with a Type and an Acreage. Students compute two sets of percentages from the same 297 records and discover that they tell opposite stories.

Choose the denominator

Mini parks are a third of all records but only 3.4% of the acreage. Community parks are about a ninth of the records and more than a third of the land. Neither percentage is wrong; each answers a different question.

Pie or bar?

With nine categories, two of them tiny, a pie chart turns into slivers nobody can compare. Students draw both and usually choose a sorted horizontal bar chart, with records and acres side by side.

Check the parts

Our acreage figures are sums of one field across 297 rows, 6,002.45 acres in all. Rounded shares may not add to exactly 100%, which is a rounding effect and a good thing to say out loud in a write-up.

The code is short and the thinking is not. In pandas, df.groupby("Type")["Acreage"].sum() / df["Acreage"].sum() gives the land shares, and df["Type"].value_counts(normalize=True) gives the record shares. The city’s own description adds a twist worth reading: SPECIAL parcels include golf courses, maintenance facilities, a botanical garden and the zoo, so a student must decide whether those belong in a chart about parks at all, and say which choice they made.

Our sums, not the city’s

All counts, acreage sums and percentages above are our calculations from one download. The City of Madison publishes the parcels for informational purposes and states that it does not warrant their completeness or accuracy, so 6,002.45 acres is a property of this file, not an official figure for Madison parkland.

Class times

Wisconsin is on Central Time

The IANA time zone database lists Wisconsin among the Central Time states, kept under the America/Chicago zone.

After school in Madison

A 5:00 p.m. class in Madison begins at 3:30 a.m. the next day in India during daylight saving time, and at 4:30 a.m. after the November change.

The size of the gap

India stays at UTC plus 5 hours 30 minutes. Wisconsin is UTC minus 5 in summer and UTC minus 6 in winter, so India is 10 hours 30 minutes or 11 hours 30 minutes ahead.

Dates for 2026 to 2027

Under the US rule in the IANA data, clocks go back on Sunday, November 1, 2026 and forward on Sunday, March 14, 2027, each at 2 a.m. We restate class times in Central Time before both.

A percentage of a day

Time zones make a neat percent problem too: an 11 hour 30 minute gap is almost half a day. Students who write a converter with America/Chicago and Asia/Kolkata, instead of typed offsets, see the gap change by itself in November.

Coding and AI

An AI model is 90% accurate. Ninety percent of what?

The question students learn to ask about park percentages is the same one they need for every AI claim.

A claim that a model is 90% accurate sounds clear until you ask what the 90% is a share of: all cases, the easy ones, or a test set the model has already seen. The Madison exercise trains exactly that reflex, because the same 297 parcels give 34.3% or 3.4% for mini parks depending on the denominator.

In our AI course, students build small models themselves and report results with the denominator stated, then use AI assistants to speed up the code while checking every figure they print. That combination, fluent with the tools and sceptical of the numbers, is what we aim for.

Wisconsin students who can say what a percentage is a share of will read both school statistics and AI marketing more clearly than most adults. The longer argument is in is coding still worth it in 2026.

FAQ

Wisconsin coding and AI questions

Answers based on the published data and on how our classes run.

How many Wisconsin high schools offer computer science?

Code.org (2025) reports 318 of 585 Wisconsin high schools, or 54.4%, offering computer science in 2023 to 2024, against 60% nationally.

What share of Wisconsin students take computer science?

The same data reports 8,377 of 262,627 high school students, or 3.19%, enrolled in 2023 to 2024. The national share that year was 6.4%.

Is the Wisconsin trend going up?

For schools, broadly yes: 42% in 2018 to 2019 and 54.4% in 2023 to 2024. For students, no: 4.9% in 2020 to 2021 and 3.19% in 2023 to 2024.

How many Wisconsin schools give AP exams?

The College Board lists 484 Wisconsin schools with AP exams in 2023 and 483 in 2024, public and non-public together.

Does Wisconsin require computer science to graduate?

We print no Wisconsin rule here because the Department of Public Instruction and state statute websites did not respond when we checked on 17 and 18 October 2026. Please ask your school counselor.

Will your classes count for Wisconsin credit?

We do not claim so. Credit belongs to the school district. Our classes add skills and projects alongside school.

What does the Madison parks project teach?

Percent share and chart choice. Students find that mini parks are 34.3% of 297 park records but 3.4% of the summed acreage, and learn to state which denominator they used.

Which time zone are classes in for Wisconsin?

Central Time, the America/Chicago zone. We restate times before the clock changes on November 1, 2026 and March 14, 2027.

When do classes run?

Weekdays or weekends, flexible: choose your batch, or for a very personalised plan choose 1-on-1 at your convenience, weekend mornings or weekdays.

Is there a Wisconsin center I can visit?

No. Every class is live online from India, and there are no Wisconsin premises, nor any claim of them.
