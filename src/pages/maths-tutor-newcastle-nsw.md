# Maths tutor in Newcastle

Newcastle's ocean baths are among the city's favourite places, and they make a fine maths problem. How much water does Newcastle Ocean Baths hold? A map gives the surface area: about 4,204 square metres. The depth is harder, because maps do not record it. So the honest answer is a range: if the average depth is between one and two metres, the baths hold somewhere between 4,203 and 8,407 cubic metres of seawater. Deciding which measurement deserves more care, the area or the depth, is the real lesson. ## How much water is in Newcastle's ocean baths?

A pool with straight sides and a flat floor is a prism: its volume is the area of its surface multiplied by its depth. The area we can measure from a map. The depth we cannot, so we treat it as an unknown and work with a range.

OpenStreetMap draws the outline of each pool. Adding up the outline with the shoelace method gives the surface area. Because depth is not mapped, and the floors of ocean baths are not flat, we used three assumed average depths, 1.0, 1.5 and 2.0 metres, and calculated a volume for each. These depths are our assumption for the exercise, not measurements.

**Pool areas from OpenStreetMap and volumes for assumed average depths (our calculation)**

| Pool | Area (m&sup2;) | Volume at 1.0 m (m&sup3;) | At 1.5 m | At 2.0 m |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Newcastle Canoe Pool | 6,768.0 | 6,768 | 10,152 | 13,536 |
| Merewether Ocean Baths | 5,001.9 | 5,002 | 7,503 | 10,004 |
| Newcastle Ocean Baths | 4,203.5 | 4,203 | 6,305 | 8,407 |
| Soldiers Baths | 1,685.1 | 1,685 | 2,528 | 3,370 |

### The depth dominates

For Newcastle Ocean Baths, moving the assumed depth from 1.0 to 2.0 metres doubles the volume, from 4,203 to 8,407 cubic metres. No amount of care with the area can fix an uncertainty that large.

### The simple area is enough

Rounding the area to the nearest hundred square metres (4,200) changes the 1.5 metre volume from 6,305 to 6,300 cubic metres, less than one per cent. A rough area is good enough; effort belongs on the depth.

### A sense of scale

A 50 by 25 metre pool, 2 metres deep, holds 2,500 cubic metres. At an average depth of 1.5 metres, Newcastle Ocean Baths would hold about 2.5 times as much.

### What students practise

Area of irregular shapes, prisms and volume, unit conversion (one cubic metre is 1,000 litres), working with an unknown, and reporting a range instead of a false single number.

At an assumed 1.25 metres, the baths would hold about 5.25 million litres, a figure students enjoy checking.

### Honest limits

Pool outlines are as mapped; depths are assumed; real ocean baths have sloping, uneven floors and change with the tide. The figures illustrate the method, not the engineering.

Map data: OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.

**The same problem at different year levels**

| Years | Version of the task |
|---|---|
| 3 to 6 | Count squares on a grid drawing of a pool, then multiply by a depth |
| 7 and 8 | Use length times width times depth for a rectangular model |
| 9 and 10 | Find an irregular area, then the volume, then convert to litres |
| 11 and 12 | Model a sloping floor and find the volume by integration |

## Questions about maths tutoring in Newcastle

### How does a Newcastle maths tutor work with my child?

By finding the gaps, teaching the missing ideas clearly and setting practice until they stick, in live online lessons in Newcastle time, privately or in a small group.

### Do you teach all four HSC maths courses?

We do. Newcastle senior students in any of Standard, Advanced, Extension 1 or Extension 2 can learn with us, following the current NESA syllabuses.

### How do you find the volume of a pool?

For a flat-floored pool, multiply the surface area by the depth. Newcastle Ocean Baths has a mapped area of about 4,204 square metres, so at an assumed 1.5 metres average depth it holds roughly 6,305 cubic metres.

### Why give a range instead of one answer?

Because the depth is not measured. A range between the smallest and largest sensible depths is more honest than a single number.

### Can lessons lock in a particular band?

No tutor can honestly promise that. We teach the content well and practise exam technique, but bands, marks and any ATAR depend on the student and the exam.

### Can university students get help?

Yes, with first-year maths concepts, never with assessed work.

### My child finds volume and area confusing. Where do you start?

With real objects and grids: counting squares for area, stacking cubes for volume, then linking both to multiplication. Once the picture is clear, the formulas follow naturally, and problems like the ocean baths become a pleasure rather than a puzzle.

### Is online tutoring suitable for students in the Hunter outside the city?

Yes. Lessons run anywhere with a reliable internet connection, so learners across the wider region book the same lessons in the same local time.

### Are lessons in Newcastle time?

Always. When the clocks go forward in October or back in April, we move the booking for you.
