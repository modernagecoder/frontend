Ans. The short version

A class 10 AI crash course is a short, time-boxed live revision of CBSE 417 before the board exam. A workable plan is six weeks: twelve sessions that revise every theory unit in order of marks (Unit 2 carries 11, Unit 3 10, Unit 6 8, Unit 1 7, Unit 5 4, Part A 10), bring the practical file and viva up to standard, and run five timed mocks, each reviewed. Modern Age Coders runs this inside its Monday and Wednesday 9:00 PM IST batch during the revision phase, or as personalised one to one at the family's own time.

Q1. Is six weeks enough?

Is a class 10 AI crash course enough to score well in 417?

For the theory paper, usually yes: 50 marks over five units and Part A can be revised in six weeks if the student has met the topics at school. The practical half is where a late start hurts, because a file, a project and two vivas cannot all be built in a fortnight. Here is what fits and what does not.

Why heavy units first

Units 2 and 3 together carry 21 theory marks and need practice, not reading. Starting there leaves time for two rounds of mistakes.

Why Part A in blocks

Ten marks across five units, each quick to learn. Spread through weeks 3 and 2, they never crowd out the numericals.

Why an error list

Every lost mark from the mocks goes on one sheet. The last week revises that sheet, not the whole book.

How each mock is reviewed

  1. Marked against the scheme

    Point by point, the way CBSE marking schemes award marks, so students see which line earned what.

  2. Lost marks sorted

    Into "did not know", "knew but slipped" and "ran out of time". Each needs a different fix.

  3. Re-attempted

    Every lost written answer is rewritten before the next session, and goes on the error list.

Q4. What is a crash session like?

One crash session in full: Unit 6 NLP in a single sitting

Unit 6, Natural Language Processing, carries 8 theory marks and one calculation that comes up again and again: bag of words, then TFIDF. Here is how the Monday of week 5 covers it, in the order the class runs.

  1. Recall check

    Five quick questions: what is a token, a stopword, a corpus; stemming against lemmatisation; one script bot and one smart bot.

  2. Normalise three documents

    Lower case, split into tokens, no stopwords needed here because the sentences are short.

  3. Build the dictionary and vectors

    Six unique words; one row of counts per document.

  4. Document frequency, then IDF

    Count how many documents contain each word; IDF = log(N / document frequency), with N = 3.

  5. Read the result aloud

    Which word best identifies document 1? The student must say why, not just point.

  6. Exam turn

    One four-mark question on the same method with new sentences, timed and marked before the session ends.

The point the examiner rewards: a word found in fewer documents gets a higher IDF, so it says more about the document it is in. More worked sets are on bag of words and TFIDF for Class 10 AI, and the rest of the unit on NLP questions.

Questions about the Class 10 AI crash course

What students ask about a class 10 AI crash course

Is there a crash course for class 10 AI 417?

Yes, as a six-week live revision plan. It runs inside our Monday and Wednesday 9:00 PM IST batch during its revision and mock phase, or as one to one starting the day you join. It revises every theory unit by marks, runs five timed mocks and brings the practical file and viva up to standard.

Can I prepare class 10 AI in one month?

For the theory paper, yes, if you have met the units at school: revise Units 2 and 3 first, then 6, 1 and 5, with Part A in short blocks, and sit at least two full timed mocks. The practical is harder in a month if the file is far from 15 programs, so start that on day one.

What should I study in the last month for AI class 10?

Practise rather than read. Numericals in Units 3 and 6 (confusion matrix, bag of words, TFIDF), four-mark answer shapes, Part A short answers, and two full timed mocks with every lost mark rewritten. Keep the last two days for the error list and your file programs.

How many mock tests should I take before the AI board exam?

Two full timed papers is the useful minimum, with a review after each. This plan adds a Section A diagnostic, a final half paper on weak units and two practical mocks. More papers without reviews add little; the review is where the marks come from.

Is a crash course enough to score 90 in AI 417?

It can be, for a student who already understands most units and needs practice, marking and timing. It is not enough for someone meeting the topics for the first time, or whose file and project are not started. We do not promise a score; the diagnostic in the first class gives an honest view.

When does the AI 417 revision batch start?

The batch moves into revision and full mocks from December. A crash student can join at any point; one to one starts the six-week plan on the day you join, counted back from your exam or practical date.

What if my practical exam is before the crash course ends?

The plan is reordered: file programs, the practical mock and both viva rehearsals come first, and theory revision follows. Your school sets the practical date, so tell the mentor at the free demo.

Is the AI class 10 fast track only for weak students?

No. Many students taking it have done well in school tests but have never sat a full two-hour 417 paper or rehearsed a viva. The mocks and reviews are what they need most.

Do I get the mock papers and marking?

Yes. Every mock is set in the paper's format, marked against the scheme and reviewed in class, and each lost written answer is rewritten. All of it is part of the monthly fee shown on this page.

When is the CBSE Class 10 AI exam in 2027?

The 2027 date sheet has not been published yet. In 2026, the AI 417 paper was held on 27 February. Plan your six weeks back from the date once CBSE releases it, and from your school's practical date if that comes earlier.
