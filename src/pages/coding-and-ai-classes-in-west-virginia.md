# Coding and AI classes in West Virginia

**Will West Virginia students need computer science to graduate?** Yes, starting with the class that enters ninth grade in the 2027 to 2028 school year. House Bill 2411, passed April 11, 2025, requires one unit of credit in a high school computer science course, which may be earned in grades eight through twelve. Modern Age Coders teaches coding and AI live online to West Virginia students, from first blocks to AP.

West Virginia&rsquo;s Legislature did not leave the definition loose. Its 2025 law says what computer science is (computers, programming and algorithms, how they are designed and built, and their impact on society) and, just as usefully, what it is not: keyboarding, word processing, digital literacy and getting online do not count. The first students covered are, by our arithmetic, the eighth graders of the 2026 to 2027 school year, and the State Board of Education was required to publish a list of qualifying courses from August 2026. This page sets out the statute as enrolled, the published access and enrollment figures with an honest note on where they disagree, and a statewide data project on the trail inventory the state transportation department publishes.

## The one-credit rule, line by line from the enrolled bill

The Department of Education site turned our request away on 17 October 2026, so this is the statute itself, from the enrolled Committee Substitute for House Bill 2411 on the Legislature&rsquo;s website.

**Computer science in West Virginia Code 18-2-9, subsection (g), as amended by House Bill 2411 (2025 Regular Session)**

| Provision | What the enrolled bill says |
|---|---|
| Passage | Passed April 11, 2025; in effect 90 days from passage, July 10, 2025. The bill status page records approval by the Governor on April 30, 2025, as Chapter 96 of the 2025 Acts |
| Who is covered | Beginning with the entering ninth grade class in the 2027-2028 school year, each public high school student must earn one unit of credit in a high school computer science course before graduating |
| When it can be earned | In grades eight through twelve |
| Course quality | High quality, meeting or exceeding the State Board of Education&rsquo;s curriculum standards |
| Format | A traditional classroom setting, with blended or online formats only where a classroom is not feasible, without interfering with homeschool or virtual students&rsquo; schedules |
| Substitution | The credit may substitute for one math credit or one personalized education plan credit |
| Career Technical Education | The credit is approved for one CTE credit where it is relevant to the student&rsquo;s program of study |
| Transcript | Where it replaces math, the district notes it as equivalent to a high school math course for admission to a West Virginia higher education institution |
| Course list | Beginning in August of 2026, the State Board must make a list of qualifying course options available to all public schools and keep it updated |

### This year&rsquo;s eighth graders

Students entering ninth grade in fall 2027 are the first cohort. By our arithmetic most of them are in eighth grade during 2026 to 2027, a year in which the law already lets the credit be earned.

### An eighth-grade option

Because the credit counts from grade eight, a middle school student who takes a qualifying course early clears the requirement before high school begins, freeing a high school slot for AP or a second CS course.

### The list decides

Which courses qualify is set by the State Board&rsquo;s list. We have not read it, because the department site was unreachable for us; ask the school counselor for the current version.

## Programming counts. Keyboarding does not.

The statute&rsquo;s definition sorts activities sharply, which helps families judge any class, ours included.

**The statutory definition of computer science, and examples of each side (the examples column is ours)**

| The law says | Wording in the bill | Example activities (ours) |
|---|---|---|
| Included | The study of computers, programming, and algorithms, including their principles, their hardware and software designs, their implementation, and their impact on society | Writing and debugging a program, designing an algorithm, explaining how a processor runs code, judging who an AI model treats unfairly |
| Not included | The study of everyday uses of computers and computer applications, such as keyboarding, word processing, digital literacy, or accessing the internet | Typing drills, formatting a document, building slides, searching the web |

The line is useful well beyond school. A summer &ldquo;tech camp&rdquo; that teaches slide design is a fine thing, but under this definition it is not computer science. A course where students write programs, test them against expected output and explain the algorithm is. When a West Virginia family compares options, asking &ldquo;how much of the time is spent writing and testing code?&rdquo; gets close to the statute&rsquo;s own test.

### Our classes are not the credit

The required credit comes from a high school computer science course that meets the State Board&rsquo;s standards. Modern Age Coders is not on any state list and does not award West Virginia credit. Our classes build the skill underneath, so the school course goes smoothly and students who want more get much more.

## What share of West Virginia&rsquo;s trails welcome a bike? Percentages that refuse to add up

The West Virginia Division of Highways publishes a statewide Trail Inventory as open GIS data. We downloaded all 3,454 trail records on 18 October 2026 and used them to teach percentages properly.

**Trail records marked Yes for each use, as a share of all 3,454 records (our calculation)**

| Use field | Records marked Yes | Our share, one decimal | Our share, whole number |
|---|---|---|---|
| hike | 3,218 | 93.2% | 93% |
| bike | 1,444 | 41.8% | 42% |
| horse | 445 | 12.9% | 13% |
| railtrail | 103 | 3.0% | 3% |
| ada | 83 | 2.4% | 2% |

### Pick the denominator first

Every share above divides by 3,454 records, not by trails or by miles. A long trail split into many segments counts many times, so &ldquo;41.8% of records allow bikes&rdquo; is not the same claim as &ldquo;41.8% of trail miles&rdquo;. Students write the denominator into every sentence.

### Rounding never sums cleanly

The surface field holds 41 different labels. Round each share to a whole percent and the 41 numbers add up to 99, not 100. Nothing is missing; rounding each part separately loses fractions. Students prove it in code, then learn why published tables often say &ldquo;may not sum to 100&rdquo;.

### Invisible characters

Most motorized values read Yes or No, but one reads &ldquo; No&rdquo; with a leading space and another is &ldquo;No&rdquo; followed by an invisible backspace character. A plain equality test misses both, so students strip and normalize text before counting.

Then a question with no tidy answer. The inventory&rsquo;s own description speaks of over 5,000 miles of trails. Our total of the geometry lengths, converted from metres, comes to 6,335.3 miles, and the separate trail length field adds up to 6,642.5. Three numbers for one network. Shared segments, measurement methods and the age of the data (the item says it was last updated on August 28, 2021) could all play a part; we cannot say which, and neither can a student without more evidence. Writing that down plainly is the point.

### Ours, not official

Every percentage and mile figure in this section is our calculation from one download. The Division of Highways says the map is provided for general reference and is not warranted in any way, and none of these numbers is an official trail statistic.

## From the Northern Panhandle to Bluefield, one Eastern clock

West Virginia uses the IANA time zone America/New_York, the zone the tz database labels &ldquo;Eastern (most areas)&rdquo;.

### Same time statewide

Wheeling, Charleston, Morgantown and Huntington keep one clock, so a West Virginia batch never has to reconcile two local times.

### Teachers in India

Our teachers keep India time all year. Because only the US side changes its clocks, the distance between the two shifts by an hour in November and again in March.

### Dates this year

Clocks fall back on November 1, 2026 and spring forward on March 14, 2027, following the US rule in the tz database. We confirm every class time in Eastern Time.

**Make the clock a coding exercise** A student who schedules reminders with Python&rsquo;s zoneinfo and America/New_York sees the November change handled automatically; one who adds a fixed number of hours sees the reminder arrive an hour off.

## The law counts programming, not prompting

West Virginia&rsquo;s definition is a quiet answer to the question of whether AI makes coding obsolete.

The statute names programming, algorithms and the impact of computing on society. An AI assistant can produce a trail-percentage script in seconds, but it will divide by whatever total it finds first, round however it likes, and miss the backspace hiding inside a label. Knowing that those choices exist is exactly the study of programming and algorithms the law describes.

In our AI units students use assistants in the open, then check every line: what is the denominator, what happened in rounding, which rows were silently skipped? A student who can audit generated code is learning computer science; one who only accepts it is closer to the everyday computer use the statute excludes.

The required credit ensures every student meets programming once; the habit of questioning output is what makes that meeting count. The longer argument is in [is learning to code still worth it](/blog/is-coding-worth-learning-2026).

## West Virginia computer science questions

### Is computer science required to graduate in West Virginia?

Yes, for students entering ninth grade in the 2027 to 2028 school year and after. House Bill 2411 requires one unit of credit in a high school computer science course before graduation.

### When did the West Virginia CS law pass?

The enrolled bill says it passed April 11, 2025 and took effect July 10, 2025. The bill status page records approval by the Governor on April 30, 2025.

### Can my child earn the CS credit in middle school?

The law allows the credit to be earned in grades eight through twelve, so an eighth grader can take a qualifying course.

### Does the CS credit replace a math credit?

It may substitute for one math credit or one personalized education plan credit, and where it replaces math the transcript notes it as equivalent to a high school math course for West Virginia college admission.

### Does typing or word processing count as computer science?

No. The law excludes everyday computer use such as keyboarding, word processing, digital literacy and accessing the internet.
