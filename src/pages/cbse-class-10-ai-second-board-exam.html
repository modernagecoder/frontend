Ans. The short version

The CBSE second board exam is an optional Class 10 sitting in May, after the compulsory February main examination. Under CBSE's 2026 rules, a passed student may improve up to three subjects out of Science, Mathematics, Social Science and the languages, so Artificial Intelligence (417) cannot be improved there, and internal assessment can never be improved. A 417 paper was held on 20 May 2026 for other categories such as compartment. CBSE has not said whether AI will be in the May 2027 sitting. Q1. What are the two exams?

What is the CBSE second board exam, and where does AI sit in it?

It is a second Class 10 sitting in May, introduced under CBSE's Two Board Examination Policy notified on 25 June 2025 and first held in 2026. The February examination is compulsory for everyone; the May one is optional and open only to the categories CBSE lists.

Q2. Can the AI mark be raised in May?

Is there an AI improvement exam in class 10?

Not in the improvement category. CBSE's 2026 circulars say passed students may improve their performance in any three subjects out of Science, Mathematics, Social Science and the languages, and Artificial Intelligence is none of those. Yet a 417 paper was on the May 2026 sheet, which is why so many families are unsure.

Who the second examination is for, in CBSE's list

  • Improvement: up to three main subjects out of Science, Mathematics, Social Science and the languages, for students who passed.
  • Compartment: students whose first result was compartment, and earlier compartment candidates on their next chance.
  • Compartment plus improvement together.
  • Passed by replacement: students who passed because the sixth subject replaced a failed main subject, who may improve.
  • Not eligible: anyone absent from, or failing, three or more subjects in the first examination, who is placed in the Essential Repeat category and sits next February.

So why was AI on the May 2026 sheet?

The official date sheet for the 2026 second examination listed Artificial Intelligence (417) on 20 May, in the same slot as Information Technology, and CBSE published a marking scheme for that paper. The paper was held. CBSE's circulars do not say, in plain words, which AI candidates sat it; the categories above that do not depend on the improvement list, such as compartment, are the likely route.

One news report of a CBSE webinar in November 2025 said only subjects with more than 50 external marks would be offered in May, which would exclude AI. The official sheet that followed included 417, so we go by the sheet, not the report.

Register early, decide later

CBSE asked schools to list everyone who might sit in the first phase, before results, and let students confirm by paying the fee in a short window after the result. Getting on the early list costs nothing and keeps the option open.

Expect a farther centre

CBSE warned that second-examination centres are fewer, so a student's centre may not be near home, and it will not be changed once allotted.

Count the cost in weeks

Between the April result and the May papers there are only a few weeks. A student who sits two improvement subjects plus Class 11 bridge work will be stretched.

Q4. Is the May paper different?

Best of two exams in AI 417: what actually changes between the sittings

The rules keep the frame the same and change the questions. CBSE set the May 2026 AI paper on the same syllabus and in the same shape as February's: 21 questions, 15 answered, two hours, 50 marks. What differed was the content of each question, as its marking scheme shows.

Which score counts

News coverage of the July 2026 results described the final marksheet as carrying the higher score in each subject a student sat twice. The circulars we read set out eligibility and post-result steps but do not state that rule in those words, so read your own marksheet rather than relying on a summary.

One rule is written down: when a subject was sat in both examinations, the student may obtain both answer books, but verification and re-evaluation are allowed on only one of them, chosen once.

What this tells an AI student

  • The shapes repeat. A confusion matrix case and Bag of Words came back in May with fresh contexts, so method matters more than memorised answers.
  • The contexts do not. Cyber attacks became car batteries. Practising with fresh stories, not past answers, is the right preparation.
  • The practical half travels unchanged. Whatever the theory score in either sitting, the school's 50 marks stay as uploaded.

Questions about the second exam and AI

What families ask about the CBSE second board exam and AI

Can I improve my AI marks in the CBSE second board exam?

Not in the improvement category. CBSE's 2026 circulars allow a passed student to improve up to three subjects out of Science, Mathematics, Social Science and the languages, and Artificial Intelligence (417) is not one of them. A 417 paper was held in May 2026 for other categories such as compartment. CBSE has not yet said whether AI will be in the May 2027 sitting.

Is the CBSE second board exam compulsory?

No. The February main examination is compulsory for every Class 10 student, and the May examination is optional. It is open only to the categories CBSE lists: improvement in up to three main subjects, compartment, compartment with improvement, and students who passed because the sixth subject replaced a failed one. A student absent from three or more papers in February cannot sit in May.

When is the May board exam in 2027?

CBSE had not announced the 2027 second examination schedule when we checked on 6 November 2026. The 2027 main examinations run from 16 February to 9 March, with the AI paper on 24 February. In 2026 the second examination ran from 15 to 21 May, with AI on 20 May, so mid-May is a reasonable expectation, not a promise.

Are the practical marks in AI considered again in the second exam?

They are carried, not redone. CBSE's circular says there is no improvement of internal assessment because it is a yearlong exercise. In AI that covers all 50 practical marks: the file, the practical exam, the project and both vivas. Only the 50-mark theory paper is ever sat in a board examination.

Which marks count if a student writes a subject in both exams?

News coverage of the July 2026 results reported that the final marksheet carried the higher score in each subject taken twice. The circulars we read do not state the rule in those words. One written rule is clear: verification and re-evaluation can be requested on only one of the two answer books for a subject, chosen once.

Is the second exam paper easier or harder than the first?

CBSE sets both on the same syllabus and the same pattern. In AI, the May 2026 paper kept the frame of the February paper, with 21 questions, 15 answered and two hours, and changed the contexts: a cyber-attack case in February became an electric-vehicle battery case in May. Neither sitting is designed to be easier.

Can a student change from AI to IT for the second exam?

No. CBSE's rules for the second examination do not allow a change of subject from the first examination, apart from what the policy permits in Mathematics. No new names are added to the second list either, and student details cannot be corrected between the sittings.

My child failed Maths but passed AI. Do they have to sit in May?

Under the CBSE scheme the passed skill subject replaces the failed main subject, so the Class 10 result is a pass. The second examination lists improvement for students who passed by replacement, so the student may sit Mathematics in May to pass it in its own right. Whether that is worth it depends on how far short the February mark was.

Does the May exam happen at my own school?

Not necessarily. CBSE told schools in 2026 that second-examination centres would be fewer, so a student's centre might not be near home, and that an allotted centre would not be changed. Plan travel for May once the admit card arrives.
