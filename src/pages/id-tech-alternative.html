People look for an alternative for different reasons, usually because they want something weekly in the school year, or a different price, schedule or teacher. This page lays out iD Tech’s programs exactly as its website described them on 17 October 2026, shows how a year-round online school like ours is organised instead, and suggests how the two can even fit together.

What stands out

Range. The site lists more than 50 topics, from Scratch, Python, Java and C++ to Roblox and Lua, Unity with C#, Godot, 3D design and maths tutoring from homework help to calculus.

What to plan around

Camps and academies are seasonal. The home page said registration for the summer of 2027 opens on November 8, so popular weeks are booked months ahead.

Our arithmetic on the most popular package: 4 lessons x 60 minutes = 240 minutes, or 4 hours of live 1-on-1 time a month. That is a useful unit for comparing any private teacher, ours included: ask how many live minutes a month you are buying, and how many students share them.

Lesson packs: what they suit

A pack lets a family buy exactly the number of lessons it wants and switch topic as interest moves, from Python one month to 3D modeling the next. For a curious child who wants to sample, that breadth is the point.

Because a new instructor can be chosen for each booking, it is worth asking whether your child will keep the same person, and how the next teacher learns what the last one covered.

Monthly classes: what they suit

A monthly class fixes the day, the teacher and, in a group, the classmates. It suits a child who needs routine more than variety, and a family that would rather not rebook each week.

The trade-off is less topic hopping: a weekly course follows a syllabus for months before moving on. Neither model is right for every child, and some families use one for a term and the other the next.

Our arithmetic on a camp day

Two hours together plus two hours alone makes four hours of project time a day, of which two are with an instructor (2 + 2 = 4). Over a camp week that adds up to a solid burst of focused work.

The strength of a camp is exactly that intensity: one project, one week, finished.

What happens in week two

The common problem is not the camp but the eleven months after it. Skills built in a week fade without practice, and a child who loved Roblox in July may not touch it again until next summer.

That gap is where families tend to look for a weekly class, whether a private lesson pack or a group course.

A twelve-month plan

A year that uses a camp and a weekly class together

Our example for a 12-year-old who wants to make games. It treats a summer camp and a weekly class as partners, not rivals. Months follow the US school year.

MonthsActivityGoal

  1. Pick one language for the year. If the camp used Luau, keep Luau; if Python, keep Python.
  2. Bring the camp project to the first class. A teacher can see in minutes what the child understood and what they copied.
  3. Set one big project for the winter. It gives weekly classes a direction.
  4. Book next summer early. iD Tech said 2027 registration opens on November 8.

Questions from families

iD Tech and alternatives: common questions

What ages does iD Tech serve?

Its site says ages 7 to 19: summer camps for 7 to 17, teen academies for 13 to 18, and private lessons for 7 to 19.

How long is an iD Tech private lesson?

Sixty minutes on Zoom, according to its private lessons pages, bought in packs of 1, 4, 8 or 12 a month. Four a month is described as the most popular package.

What is an iD Tech virtual camp like?

Its virtual camp page describes a one-week, small-group camp for ages 7 to 17, with two hours a day alongside peers and instructors and two hours of independent project work.

When does iD Tech summer registration open?

On 17 October 2026 its home page said registration for summer 2027 opens on November 8.

How is Modern Age Coders different from iD Tech?

We are a year-round online school with weekly group or private classes, for ages 6 and up, teaching coding and maths, with teachers in India. iD Tech centres on summer camps and academies plus 1-on-1 lessons with US-based instructors.

Can we do an iD Tech camp and weekly classes with you?

Yes. Many families pair a summer camp with weekly classes in the school year. Bring the camp project to your first lesson.

Does iD Tech teach maths as well as coding?

Its private lesson topics include math homework help, elementary math, mental math, pre-algebra, Algebra I and II, geometry, pre-calculus and calculus. We also teach maths, in weekly courses alongside coding.

Do you run in-person camps?

No. All our classes are live online, which means any US state but no campus experience.

When are your classes?

Weekdays or weekends, flexible: choose your batch, or for a very personalised plan choose 1-on-1 at your convenience, weekend mornings or weekdays.

Are you connected with iD Tech?

No. We are a separate company; this page reports iD Tech's public information and links to it.
