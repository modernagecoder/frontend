Maths tutor in Parramatta

Parramatta Park is 85 hectares, according to the agency that looks after it. Most people nod at a number like that without picturing it. A hectare is a square 100 metres on each side, so 85 hectares is 850,000 square metres, or 0.85 of a square kilometre: about a square 922 metres wide. Put it beside the whole suburb of Parramatta, 523.45 hectares by the ABS boundary, and the park is roughly a sixth of that size. Units of area are a Year 7 topic that keeps coming back, in HSC Mathematics Standard, in science and at work. This page shows how we teach them, and every other part of maths, to Parramatta learners from Kindergarten to Extension 2.

A Parramatta worked example

How big is 85 hectares?

Area units confuse people because each step is a factor of 100 or 10,000, not 10. Parramatta's own park makes the conversions concrete.

Greater Sydney Parklands writes that "Parramatta Park is 85 hectares". A hectare is 100 m by 100 m, so it holds 10,000 square metres, and a square kilometre holds 100 hectares. The ABS boundary for the suburb of Parramatta encloses 5.2345 square kilometres. Students convert each figure into all three units before comparing anything.

The same areas in three units (our calculation)
AreaSquare metresHectaresSquare kilometres
One hectare10,00010.01
Parramatta Park850,000850.85
Parramatta suburb (ABS boundary)5,234,500523.455.2345
Western Sydney Parklands52,800,0005,28052.8

The quick way wins

To go from square metres to hectares, divide by 10,000: move the decimal point four places. From hectares to square kilometres, divide by 100. Learners who remember that a hectare is a 100 m square never need a formula sheet for it.

A picture of 85 ha

A square with an area of 850,000 square metres has sides of about 922 m, since 922 times 922 is close to 850,000. That is a walk of just under a kilometre along each edge.

A size comparison only

Parramatta Park is about 16.2% of the suburb's area, but the park does not sit wholly inside the suburb boundary, so this is a comparison of sizes, not a share of land.

The common mistake

Students who know 1 m is 100 cm often assume 1 square metre is 100 square centimetres. It is 10,000, because both sides scale. The hectare table makes that visible.

Western Sydney Parklands, "Covering 5,280 hectares and stretching over 27 km", is about ten times the suburb of Parramatta.

Where it appears later

Area and volume conversions return in Years 9 and 10 measurement, in HSC Mathematics Standard, and in science practicals.

Getting the factor of 10,000 right once saves a lot of marks later.

Sources: Greater Sydney Parklands home page; ABS, ASGS 2021 suburbs and localities, read 14 October 2026. Conversions are our own.

From hectares to cubic metres

Once area units make sense, volume follows the same logic with one more dimension, and the factors grow again.

A question students enjoy

If rain fell evenly to a depth of 1 millimetre across all 85 hectares of Parramatta Park, how much water would land? The area is 850,000 square metres and the depth is 0.001 metres, so the volume is 850 cubic metres, or 850,000 litres.

It is a two-line calculation once the units are under control, and a confusing one until they are.

Why we keep returning to units

Unit slips are among the most common reasons a correct method still loses marks, from Year 7 tests to HSC Mathematics Standard measurement questions.

Students learn to write the unit at every step, so a wrong factor shows up before the final line.

Adults meet the same ideas when comparing land sizes, reading plans or estimating materials. Our adult learners often start here, because the maths is immediately useful and the examples are real.

Questions about a maths tutor in Parramatta

Do you offer maths tutoring for Parramatta students?

Yes, live online in Sydney time, privately or in level-matched classes of fifteen to twenty, from Kindergarten to Year 12 and for adults.

Which Stage 6 maths courses do you teach?

Mathematics Standard, Mathematics Advanced, Mathematics Extension 1 and Mathematics Extension 2, plus one-to-one support for Life Skills and Numeracy.

How many square metres are in a hectare?

10,000. A hectare is a square 100 metres on each side, so Parramatta Park's 85 hectares is 850,000 square metres.

How many hectares is the suburb of Parramatta?

By the ABS boundary, 5.2345 square kilometres, which is 523.45 hectares.

Can you promise an HSC band?

No. We teach thoroughly and report honestly, but bands and ATARs depend on the student and the exams.

Do you help with the OC and selective tests?

Yes, through separate pages and courses for those tests; this page covers everyday maths at every stage.

Are lessons affected by daylight saving?

No. Lessons stay at the same Sydney time all year; we adjust the teacher's side.

Do adults learn with you?

Yes, in adult classes or privately, for work, study or confidence.

How much water is 1 mm of rain over Parramatta Park?

About 850 cubic metres, or 850,000 litres: 850,000 square metres of park times a depth of 0.001 metres. It is a favourite units question in our Years 7 to 10 lessons.

Which Stage 6 maths course should my child take?

It depends on what they enjoy and plan to study next. Standard suits applied maths; Advanced suits calculus-based study; Extension 1 and 2 add depth. One diagnostic lesson usually makes the choice clear.

What do lessons cost?
