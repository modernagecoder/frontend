Coding classes in Saarland

Germany's smallest Flächenland is ahead on Informatik. At Gemeinschaftsschulen and the nine-year Gymnasium, every pupil has it as a compulsory subject from grade 7 to grade 10, six weekly hours in total, which only three Länder reach. Some Gymnasien go further with an Informatik or MINT branch. This page explains those routes, the Oberstufe and its strict Abitur rules, the holidays, and a project that measures distances on the Land's own school map.

Informatik in the Saarland

How much Informatik do Saarland schools teach?

Six compulsory weekly hours across grades 7 to 10, and more on special branches. We take these details from the GI's Informatik-Monitor (2026/27 edition), a survey built from each Land's regulations; Modern Age Coders has no tie to the GI or to the Saarland ministry.

The compulsory subject

After four years of Grundschule, Saarland pupils go to a Gemeinschaftsschule or a Gymnasium. At both, on the nine-year route, Informatik is compulsory: two hours a week in grades 7 and 8, and one hour a week in grades 9 and 10.

The GI counts the Saarland among three Länder, with Mecklenburg-Vorpommern and Thüringen, that already meet the recommendation of six compulsory Informatik hours.

Branches with more

Some Gymnasien run an Informatik branch, a pilot, with four hours a week of Informatik in each of grades 8, 9 and 10. A MINT branch, also a pilot, adds two hours in grade 10.

On the eight-year Gymnasium, which is being phased out, grade 10 can offer a two-hour Informatik elective.

"Not stated" means the monitor gives no separate figure for that branch and grade. A cross-subject Basiscurriculum "Medienbildung und informatische Bildung" covers grades 1 to 10.

Six hours across four years is a solid base, and it brings a different question for parents: one or two lessons a week are enough to meet the ideas, not always enough to become fluent. A pupil who wants to write real programs, or who aims at the Leistungskurs, gains most from regular practice between school lessons. That is the role of our Companion course.

The monitor's Saarland page also reports that the Gymnasium currently runs eight years, with grade 10 as the Einführungsphase, and is switching back to nine years by 2026/27; at the Gemeinschaftsschule the Oberstufe already runs from grade 11 to 13. It expects the Oberstufe structure and the Informatik curricula for grades 11 to 13 to be revised within about two years.

Oberstufe and Abitur

How is the Informatik Abitur set in the Saarland?

Centrally, by commission, and strictly: no computer work and nothing on the desk but a calculator.

Courses

In the Einführungsphase, Informatik can be a two-hour elective at the Gemeinschaftsschule and the nine-year Gymnasium, except on the music branch. In the Qualifikationsphase it can be a two-hour Grundkurs or a five-hour Leistungskurs.

The Leistungskurs has a condition: it can only be taken if the pupil took Informatik in the Einführungsphase. A pupil who drops it in that year closes the door.

The exam

A Leistungskurs in Informatik is examined in writing; a Grundkurs can be written or oral. The written tasks are set centrally: one Abitur selection commission for each level chooses and revises them, and the school authority approves them.

Practical parts, such as programming tasks or applications on a computer, are not allowed. The only aid is a calculator.

Its figures: about 15 percent of Sekundarstufe II pupils took Informatik in 2024/25, about 6 percent of Abitur candidates had it as a written exam subject in 2025/26, and the Land had 211 qualified Informatik teachers, about 4 percent of all teachers. Programming tasks are banned, but programs are not: candidates still read, write and reason about code, entirely on paper. We prepare exactly that, beside the school course: tracing an algorithm by hand, writing a method that is right the first time, explaining a data structure in words. The commission sets the paper and the school awards the Abitur; we give no German grades or qualifications.

A Land-wide project

How far apart are the Saarland's Gymnasien? A map project

The Land's geodata infrastructure, GDI-SL, publishes the locations of the Saarland's schools as open data. Our pupils use it to answer a simple question and to learn why a map in degrees is not a map in kilometres.

The data

The feature type Schulen_SL in the GDI-SL web service Staatliche_Dienste lists 374 schools and colleges, recorded on 20 May 2025, under the licence Namensnennung 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0), credited as © GDI-SL. We saved it on 3 November 2026 as schulen_sl.geojson. We never print a school's name or contact details, only counts and distances.

Each school has a point in longitude and latitude, in degrees, and also two columns, Ost and Nord, giving the same position in metres on a projected grid.

The question

Which two Gymnasien are farthest apart, and how far? A quick idea: one degree is about 111 kilometres, so take the straight-line distance in degrees and multiply by 111.

That works for latitude. It does not work for longitude, because lines of longitude come together towards the poles. At the Saarland's latitude a degree east to west is much shorter than a degree north to south.

Eighty kilometres across the Saarland sounds odd for a Land that is not much wider than that. Version 2 measures the same pair again, this time with the Ost and Nord columns, which are already in metres, so Pythagoras gives a true distance.

The pair is right, a Gymnasium in Merzig and one in Homburg, but Version 1 overstated the distance by about half. The error is not random: almost all of it comes from the east to west part, which Version 1 treated as if a degree of longitude were 111 kilometres long. Version 3 corrects only that, by squeezing the longitude difference by the cosine of the latitude.

  1. Grades 5 and 6, with a globe or an orange. Draw lines from the top to the bottom. Measure the gap between two lines at the middle and near the top. What happens to the gap?
  2. Python in grades 7 and 8. Try both versions. By how many percent was Version 1 too long? Explain why in one sentence.
  3. Grades 9 and 10. Find, for one Gemeinschaftsschule of your choice, the nearest Gymnasium using Version 2's distance. Then try Version 1's. Does the answer change?
  4. Oberstufe. Version 3 is still a flat approximation. Write the haversine formula for distance on a sphere and compare all three methods for the farthest pair.

A small Land

Destatis counts 1,012,141 people in the Saarland on 31 December 2024; Saarbrücken, the capital and the only city above 100,000, has 182,971.

The school file used in the project reflects that size: its farthest pair of Gymnasien are less than 54 kilometres apart.

Planning the year

The Saarland has a winter week, 8 to 12 February 2027, and its Easter holidays start on 30 March 2027, eight days later than in neighbouring Rheinland-Pfalz.

For Oberstufe pupils we plan revision around the school's own exam calendar, which the school confirms.

Coding classes in Saarland: common questions

Are the classes in German?

No, never: every live lesson is in English. The mentor keeps sentences short, waits for answers, gets to know how the pupil speaks, and copes happily with a German word such as Gemeinschaftsschule dropped into an English question.

Is Informatik compulsory in the Saarland?

Yes, from grade 7 to 10 at Gemeinschaftsschulen and the nine-year Gymnasium: two hours a week in grades 7 and 8 and one hour in grades 9 and 10, according to the Gesellschaft für Informatik's Informatik-Monitor 2026/27.

What is the Informatik branch at a Saarland Gymnasium?

A pilot route, according to the Informatik-Monitor, with four hours of Informatik a week in each of grades 8, 9 and 10. A MINT branch, also a pilot, has two hours in grade 10.

Can my child take an Informatik Leistungskurs?

Only if they took Informatik in the Einführungsphase, according to the Informatik-Monitor. The Leistungskurs runs for five hours a week and ends in a written exam.

Is a computer used in the Saarland Informatik Abitur?

No. The Informatik-Monitor states that practical parts such as programming tasks on a computer are not allowed and that a calculator is the only permitted aid. The tasks are set centrally by a commission for each level.

When is the Abitur in the Saarland in 2027?

The Ministerium für Bildung und Kultur publishes the dates, but its website refused our request when we checked on 3 November 2026, so we do not give them here. Your school confirms the exact days.

What time are classes?

Weekdays or weekends, flexible: choose your batch, or for a very personalised plan choose 1-on-1 at your convenience, weekend mornings or weekdays. A lesson at 17:40 in Saarbrücken is 22:10 for a mentor in India in winter.

Will lessons with you show up on a school report?

No. Reports, grades and the Abitur belong to the school and the Land. Our part is the understanding and practice behind them.

Who will teach my child?

A mentor working from India, on live video, who stays the same from the first month on.

How are fees paid?

Monthly, by card. Euro prices are calculated at the European Central Bank's reference rate; the bank turns the US dollar charge into euro on your statement. Nothing is due for the trial class.
