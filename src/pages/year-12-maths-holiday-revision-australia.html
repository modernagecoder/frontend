Year 12 maths holiday revision

Year 12 school holidays are short, and they disappear quickly. Students who use them well tend to do two things: they plan around real dates rather than a vague sense of "the holidays", and they revise in a way that makes topics stick rather than cramming them once. This page gives the dates for NSW and Victoria from the governments' own calendars, and a review method that takes ten minutes a day.

A method that sticks

Spaced review with Leitner boxes

Cramming a topic once feels productive and fades fast. Spaced review, coming back to a skill at growing intervals, is a simple alternative, and the Leitner box system turns it into a routine anyone can follow with index cards.

1. Make the cards

Write one skill per card, such as "differentiate a quotient" or "find a conditional probability", with a fresh practice question on the back.

2. Three boxes, three rhythms

Box 1 is reviewed every day, Box 2 every second day, and Box 3 every fourth day. Every card starts in Box 1.

3. Move the cards

Answer a card correctly and it moves up one box. Get it wrong and it goes back to Box 1, wherever it was.

A worked example, invented by us, over the ten weekdays of the NSW winter break, 5 to 16 July 2027. Box 1 is reviewed on days 1 to 10, Box 2 on days 1, 3, 5, 7 and 9, and Box 3 on days 1, 5 and 9. Follow one card, "differentiate a quotient".

One card through a 10-day holiday with three Leitner boxes
DayBox reviewedResultCard moves to
1Box 1CorrectBox 2
3Box 2CorrectBox 3
5Box 3WrongBox 1
6Box 1CorrectBox 2
7Box 2CorrectBox 3
9Box 3CorrectStays in Box 3

The card was reviewed 6 times in 10 days instead of 10, and the reviews clustered where they were needed: after the mistake on day 5 it came back the very next day, then spread out again once it was secure. Multiply that across fifty cards and the saving in time is large.

The system also produces an honest map of revision. Cards that keep falling back to Box 1 are the topics to bring to a tutor or teacher; cards that sit in Box 3 are secure and need only occasional checks.

The practice question on the back of each card should change every few reviews, so the student is solving a problem rather than remembering an answer. That is where a bank of varied questions, from past papers or a tutor, earns its keep.

This is also a tiny algorithm, and students who code enjoy writing it: a list of cards, each with a box number, and a rule for which boxes to review on a given day. The Python courses on our course atlas start from programs about this size.

A holiday plan

A two-week revision shape that works

Our suggested structure, not an official one. Adjust it to the school's trial dates and the student's weakest topics.

A two-week Year 12 maths holiday plan
DaysFocusDaily routine
1 to 2DiagnoseOne mixed practice set; turn every error into a Leitner card
3 to 6RepairTwo weak topics a day, one hour each, then the day's Leitner boxes
7RestNo maths; the spacing works better with a gap
8 to 11PractiseTimed exam sections, marked honestly, new cards for new errors
12 to 14RehearseFull papers under exam conditions, then light Leitner review only

Two hours a day of focused work, plus ten to fifteen minutes of card review, is enough for most students and leaves room for rest. Longer days usually trade quality for quantity, and tired revision tends not to stick.

Holiday lessons with us follow the same idea: each session starts by reviewing the student's Box 1 cards, then tackles one weak topic properly. We teach with our own questions, never a school's assessment task, and we never promise a band or a study score.

Questions about holiday revision

What Year 12 families ask us

When are the NSW school holidays in 2027?

The NSW Department of Education lists autumn holidays from 12 to 23 April, winter holidays from 5 to 16 July, and spring holidays from 27 September to 8 October 2027, for government schools.

When do Victorian terms run in 2027?

The Victorian Government lists Term 1 from 27 January to 25 March, Term 2 from 12 April to 25 June, Term 3 from 12 July to 17 September, and Term 4 from 4 October to 17 December 2027.

When are the HSC exams?

For 2026, NESA says the HSC written exams start on 13 October and finish no later than 5 November. The 2027 timetable had not been published when this page was written.

When are the VCE maths exams?

In 2026, VCAA scheduled General Mathematics on 30 October and 2 November, Mathematical Methods on 5 and 6 November, Specialist Mathematics on 9 and 11 November, and Foundation Mathematics on 17 November.

What is the Leitner box method?

A spaced-repetition routine. Skills go on cards in numbered boxes reviewed at growing intervals, such as daily, every second day and every fourth day. A correct answer moves a card up a box; a wrong answer sends it back to the first box.

How many hours should a Year 12 student revise in the holidays?

There is no official number. Our suggestion is about two hours of focused work a day plus ten to fifteen minutes of card review, with at least one full rest day each week.

Should the holidays be used for new topics?

Usually not. Holidays suit repairing weak topics and practising exam conditions; new content is better learned during term with the class.

Do other states have similar dates?

Each state sets its own calendar. This page covers NSW and Victoria from their governments' pages; students elsewhere should check their own education department.

Can you guarantee a better result?

No. Revision helps, but results belong to the student, and we never promise a band, study score or rank.

Do you run holiday lessons online?

Yes. Holiday sessions are live and online, usually in the Australian morning, and can be one-to-one or in a small group.
