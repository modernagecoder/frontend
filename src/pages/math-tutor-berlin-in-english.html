The Berlin maths year

Berlin publishes its exam days months ahead. Put them next to the holidays and a plan almost writes itself.

Read on 3 November 2026. The Prüfungsplan states that written MSA exams start at 10:00 and written Abitur exams at 09:00.

What the table says

The Easter holidays end on Friday 2 April 2027 and the MSA maths paper follows on Thursday 15 April 2027. In between sit eight school days and the German and first-language exams on 8 and 13 April. A pupil who means to revise over Easter therefore has the holiday itself and very little after it.

The Abitur maths paper on Wednesday 5 May 2027 is the last of the big written Abitur days in Berlin, after German on 27 April and English on 30 April 2027.

What it does not say

Klassenarbeiten, the class tests teachers set during the year, have no city-wide dates. They are where most marks are made, and every school sets its own. Ask the school for its plan; the table above only shows the fixed points.

VERA-8 is not marked. The IQB says plainly that VERA results are not graded and no school rankings are made, so it is a check on the class, not on your child.

Mittlerer Schulabschluss

How does maths count toward the MSA in Berlin?

It depends on the school type. The Senate describes two routes, and families are often surprised that a Gymnasium pupil does not need the written exam to gain the MSA.

At a Gymnasium

Berlin's page on qualifications at the Gymnasium says the MSA is reached through the year marks of Klasse 10. Three subjects carry special weight: German, maths and the first foreign language. The Senate calls them Kernfächer im MSA, the core subjects of the MSA.

So for a Gymnasium pupil, a whole year of Klassenarbeiten in maths decides the core-subject mark. A tutor's job here is steady work across the year, not a last sprint. The same page adds that at least one mediengestützte Projektarbeit, a media-supported project, is compulsory in Klasse 9 or 10.

At a Sekundarschule or Gemeinschaftsschule

The Prüfungsplan sets the written MSA exams for 2027: German on Thursday 8 April, the first foreign language on Tuesday 13 April and maths on Thursday 15 April 2027, each starting at 10:00. A catch-up date for maths is Monday 24 May 2027.

Here one morning in April matters a great deal, and a calm, practised routine for a written paper is worth as much as any single topic. We teach toward that morning, and the school and the Senate set and mark the exam.

Whatever the route, the content is national. Since 2022 one KMK document sets the maths standards for both the Erster Schulabschluss and the MSA, in the version of 23 June 2022. It sorts school maths into five Leitideen: Zahl und Operation, Größen und Messen, Strukturen und funktionaler Zusammenhang, Raum und Form, and Daten und Zufall. Our Klasse 7 to 10 course uses the same five headings, so a parent can match a weak test to a strand in a minute.

  1. November to January. Find the weak strand. One short task per Leitidee shows where marks are lost, usually fractions, percentages or rearranging an equation.
  2. Winterferien, 1 to 6 February 2027. Rest, then one light week: two classes on the weakest strand only.
  3. February and March. Mixed practice, a full past-style paper written by us every second week, timed in Berlin exam conditions.
  4. Osterferien, 22 March to 2 April 2027. The main revision block: four short sessions a week, each ending with a ten-minute paper without a calculator.
  5. 6 to 14 April 2027. Light touch only. Formulas, units, and sleep. The paper is on 15 April at 10:00.

The five steps are our own plan for a pupil sitting the written MSA in 2027, not a Senate recommendation. Sources for the rules: berlin.de, Abschlüsse am Gymnasium nach Klasse 9 und 10; Prüfungsplan 2026/27; KMK Bildungsstandards Mathematik for the ESA and MSA, version of 23 June 2022.

Taught in English

Can my child learn German school maths in English?

Yes, and many Berlin families choose it on purpose. The maths is the same; only the words differ, and we teach both sets.

Every lesson is live and in English. We do not teach in German, and we say so before the first class. Mentors slow down, rephrase and check understanding, and they adapt to a child whose English is still growing.

A Berlin data project

What can Berlin's tree-watering data teach about totals and rates?

Gieß den Kiez is a CityLAB Berlin web app where residents adopt and water street trees. Its usage numbers are open data, free to reuse, and they hide a lesson every Klasse 8 pupil needs.

The data

The file giess_kpis.csv is the usage summary from Gieß den Kiez - Nutzungsdaten, published through Berlin Open Data under CC0. We downloaded it on 3 November 2026. It has 54 rows from 9 July 2021 to 1 November 2026, and one column, totalwateramount, holds the litres logged in the app since it began.

That makes it a running total: each row adds everything before it. By the row for 1 November 2026 the column has reached 2,410,343 litres. The dataset notes say the figures are updated at the start of each month during the growing season, 1 March to 30 September, and that the app went online at the end of May 2020.

The question

When did Berliners water most? A running total cannot answer that by itself; the answer lives in the differences between rows. Subtract one row from the next and you have the litres added in between.

But the rows are not evenly spaced. Most are a month apart, a few are only 10 to 23 days apart, and one gap runs for more than a year. A difference over 405 days is not a month, so we also divide by the number of days. That one division is the idea of a rate, the same idea as the gradient of a line.

The biggest jump is not the busiest time; it is simply the longest gap. Divided by its 405 days, it shrinks to about 902 litres a day. The real record is August 2022: 253,673 litres in 31 days, 8,183 litres a day. And a running total should never fall, yet this one falls three times, by 17,236 litres between 1 March and 1 April 2025, by 24 litres in January 2026 and by 9,057 litres in May 2026. The file does not say why. In class we treat that honestly: we report the falls and do not invent a reason.

  1. Klasse 5 and 6. A marble jar holds 12, 19, 19 and 31 marbles at the end of four weeks. How many went in each week? Which week added nothing?
  2. Klasse 7. From the table, by what percentage did the summer total change from 2024 to 2025? Round to one decimal place, then check with a rough Überschlag first.
  3. Klasse 8. Plot the running total for 2026 against the day of the year. Where is the graph steepest, and what does the gradient mean in litres per day?
  4. Klasse 9 and 10. Run Version 1, then change it so that it lists every step where the total fell, with its dates. Write two sentences on why a falling running total should make you check the data before any chart.

Abitur 2027

What does the Berlin Abitur maths exam look like in 2027?

One fixed morning, built partly from tasks shared by the Länder. The facts below come from the Prüfungsplan and the IQB.

Date and format

The written Abitur in Mathematik is on Wednesday 5 May 2027 from 09:00, for both the Leistungsfach and the third exam subject, and the Prüfungsplan marks it as a pool subject. The pool is the bank of tasks the IQB coordinates for the Länder, and each Land's own commission chooses which pool tasks it uses.

Pool exams have a Part A without aids and a Part B with aids. At raised level the paper is worth 100 marks over 300 minutes; at basic level, 80 marks over 255 minutes, both including time to choose tasks.

Which year is the Abitur year?

At a Berlin Gymnasium the Oberstufe runs from Klasse 10, the Einführungsphase, to Klasse 12; this is the eight-year route, G8. At an Integrierte Sekundarschule or a Gemeinschaftsschule the Einführungsphase is Klasse 11 and the Abitur comes after Klasse 13.

Our Abitur maths in English guide covers the paper in detail. On this page it matters for one reason: Part A means mental and written algebra without a calculator, and that is built in Klasse 7 to 10, not in the last spring.

Maths tutoring in Berlin: common questions

Are the classes in German?

No. Every lesson is live and taught in English only. Mentors match your child's pace and accent, and each topic ends with the German words a Berlin test will use, so your child can read the task in German and think it through in English.

When is the MSA maths exam in Berlin in 2027?

The Senate's Prüfungsplan for 2026/27 sets the written MSA maths exam on Thursday 15 April 2027, starting at 10:00, with a catch-up date on Monday 24 May 2027. German is on 8 April and the first foreign language on 13 April 2027.

Does a Gymnasium pupil in Berlin sit the MSA maths exam?

berlin.de explains that at a Gymnasium the MSA is reached through the year marks of Klasse 10, with German, maths and the first foreign language as core subjects. Check your child's own situation with the school, which is the authority on it.

When is the Abitur maths exam in Berlin in 2027?

Wednesday 5 May 2027, starting at 09:00, for both the Leistungsfach and the third exam subject. The Prüfungsplan lists it as a pool subject, meaning tasks can come from the bank the IQB coordinates for the Länder.

Which Klassen do you teach?

Klasse 1 to 13. Grundschule maths runs to Klasse 6 in Berlin, so our Grundschule course covers all six years; from Klasse 7 the secondary course takes over, and the Abitur course covers the Einführungsphase and the Qualifikationsphase.

Can you help with Klassenarbeiten during the year?

Yes, that is most of what we do. Send the topic and date of the next test, and the mentor plans the coming lessons around it. We teach the maths and practise with our own questions; the school sets and marks the test.

When are lessons, in Berlin time?

Weekdays or weekends, flexible: choose your batch, or for a very personalised plan choose 1-on-1 at your convenience, weekend mornings or weekdays. A lesson at 16:55 in Berlin is 21:25 for the mentor in India during winter.

Where are the tutors?

Our maths mentors are based in India and teach live online over video with a shared whiteboard. Your child keeps the same mentor every week, which matters more in maths than in almost any other subject.

What does it cost, and in which currency?

A monthly fee covers eight live lessons. We show it in euro at the European Central Bank reference rate; the card is charged in US dollars and your bank converts it, so the statement can differ slightly. The first lesson is free.

Is tutoring in English a problem for German school maths?

Usually not. The methods are the same and the symbols are international, so the real gap is vocabulary, which is why every lesson ends with the German terms. Hearing a method explained a second way, in a second language, often makes it clearer.
