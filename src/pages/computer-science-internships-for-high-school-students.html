Computer science internships for high school students

Searching for a computer science internship in high school turns up the same frustration for most families: the famous programs want college students, adults, or both. Some do take teenagers, and the ones run by federal agencies publish exact rules. This guide reads those rules from the agencies’ own pages, shows where the age lines fall, explains how open source contributions give a 15 or 16 year old real, checkable work, and lays out the plan our teachers use to get a student ready for an application season.

In short

Computer science internships for high school students exist, but they are few and strict. NASA’s OSTEM internships need applicants to be 18 by the start of a session, and NIST’s seven-week SHIP takes juniors and seniors living within 50 miles of its campuses. Most teens first build evidence through projects and open source. Modern Age Coders runs that preparation as live online classes, group or private.

Age lines

Before writing a single application, check the age and grade rules. These are the published lines for the programs on this page, read on 17 October 2026.

What the table tells families

Two of the best known names on the list are closed to a 15 year old, and one is closed until after graduation. That is not a reason to give up. It is a reason to aim at the programs built for teenagers and to start building evidence now.

The word internship also covers very different things. A NIST SHIP place is an unpaid educational research program; a NASA Pathways place is a paid route into civil service. Read each one as its own thing.

Citizenship and location

Both NASA programs list US citizenship, and NIST SHIP asks for it too. NIST also requires a permanent home within a 50-mile radius of the Boulder or Gaithersburg campus, with families providing housing and transport.

A student who is not a citizen, or who lives far from a campus, should put more weight on open source, competitions and school research programs.

Sources: NASA internship programs, NIST SHIP, Google Summer of Code FAQ, LinkedIn User Agreement, GitHub Terms of Service, all read 17 October 2026. None of these organisations has any connection with Modern Age Coders.

2027 deadlines and the application

NASA lists the Summer 2027 deadline as March 1, 2027 (11:59 p.m. ET) and the Fall 2027 deadline as May 24, 2027. A senior graduating in June 2027 who wants that summer should be ready by late February.

One detail is easy to miss: "Letters of recommendation are not part of the NASA Office of STEM Engagement Internships application." The written profile and the student’s own record carry the weight.

Scale and pay, as NASA states them

The page says the Office of STEM Engagement gives more than 2,000 students a year a hands-on internship, and that "The majority of interns receive a paid stipend award, which is based on academic level and session duration."

Our arithmetic on Pathways: 480 hours at a 40-hour week is 12 weeks (480 / 40), which matches the program’s own minimum length. At 20 hours a week it would be 24 weeks.

NIST SHIP

NIST’s Summer High School Intern Program for 2027

The National Institute of Standards and Technology runs one of the few federal programs designed for high schoolers. NIST describes it plainly: "SHIP is a seven-week, unpaid educational internship."

NIST SHIP, from NIST’s program page (read 17 October 2026)
PointWhat NIST publishes
WhenRuns each year from mid-June to early August; actual dates vary
WhereNIST Boulder, Colorado, or NIST Gaithersburg, Maryland
WhoUS citizens who are high school juniors or seniors at the time of application
GradesA minimum unweighted GPA of 3.0 on a 4.0 scale
CommitmentThe full seven weeks; families provide housing and transport
DistanceA permanent home within a 50-mile radius of the host campus
2027 cycleVacancy announcement expected on USAJOBS in mid-October 2026, applications due at the end of January 2027

Why computer science students look here

NIST’s 2026 poster session listed several laboratories presenting together, including ITL, the Information Technology Laboratory. Research at a standards lab often needs data handling, measurement code and careful documentation.

NIST calls SHIP a competitive volunteer program. The page asks applicants to show a strong interest in scientific research, so a student’s own small research style project helps more than a list of courses.

Two dates to reconcile

The page gives two timings: the 2027 announcement is expected in mid-October 2026, while the general How to Apply text says applications usually open on November 1 and close in the last week of January.

Our advice is to watch USAJOBS from mid-October and treat the end of January as the hard edge. Check the NIST page itself before relying on any date.

Open source

Open source: real work a 14 year old is allowed to do

Open source is the closest thing to an internship that has no age gate beyond the platform’s own. GitHub’s documentation sets out how newcomers find a first task.

  1. Start with software you already use. GitHub’s guide calls a daily-use project a great place to begin.
  2. Search by topic. Pages at github.com/topics/<topic> list projects and good first issues for a subject such as machine learning.
  3. Filter for newcomers. Many projects use the good first issue or help wanted labels to mark tasks meant for new contributors.
  4. Check the project is alive. The guide recommends confirming a repository is actively maintained before you start.
  5. Pick a contribution type. Fix or reproduce a bug from the issue tracker, test someone’s pull request, report problems you hit while using it, or improve the docs.
  6. Ask before big changes. Open an issue describing your approach before investing significant time, so you match what maintainers expect.

Why this impresses a reviewer

A merged pull request is public, dated and reviewed by someone outside the family. It shows the student can read an unfamiliar codebase, follow a project’s rules and take feedback, which is most of what an internship mentor hopes for.

Three small merged contributions to one project say more than one large unreviewed side project.

Staying safe and honest

Use an account the student is old enough to hold, with a parent aware of it, and never put a home address or school name in a public profile.

Be open about any AI help in a pull request description. Maintainers increasingly ask, and a student who explains their own change line by line has nothing to hide.

Source: GitHub Docs, Finding ways to contribute to open source on GitHub, read 17 October 2026. The command sequence is ours.

CS internships for high school students: common questions

Can high school students get computer science internships?

Some can, but options are limited. Many well known programs require applicants to be 18 or in college. Federal programs such as NIST SHIP are built for high school juniors and seniors.

Can a high school student intern at NASA?

NASA's page says OSTEM interns must be 18 or older by the session start date, and that high school seniors may apply for the summer session immediately following graduation. Pathways interns must be at least 16 and meet enrollment rules.

When is the NASA Summer 2027 internship deadline?

NASA lists March 1, 2027 at 11:59 p.m. ET for Summer 2027 and May 24, 2027 for Fall 2027. Later cycles publish their own dates.

What is NIST SHIP?

The NIST Summer High School Intern Program is a seven-week, unpaid educational internship at NIST Boulder or NIST Gaithersburg for US citizens who are juniors or seniors, with an unweighted GPA of at least 3.0.

Do I have to live near NIST to apply for SHIP?

Yes. NIST asks for a permanent residence within a 50-mile radius of the host campus, and families provide their own housing and transport.

Can high schoolers join Google Summer of Code?

Not usually. The Google Summer of Code FAQ says contributors must be at least 18 years old. Younger students can still contribute to open source projects directly.

How old do you need to be for LinkedIn?

LinkedIn's user agreement sets the Minimum Age at 16 years old. A high school student's projects and GitHub history matter far more than the profile.

What counts as experience if I cannot get an internship yet?

Finished projects in a public repository, merged open source pull requests, competition entries and the ability to explain your code. All of these can be checked by a reviewer.

When are your classes?

Weekdays or weekends, flexible: choose your batch, or for a very personalised plan choose 1-on-1 at your convenience, weekend mornings or weekdays.

Can Modern Age Coders get my child an internship?

No. We do not place students and make no promise about any application. We teach the skills and help build the projects, history and explanations that applications ask for.
