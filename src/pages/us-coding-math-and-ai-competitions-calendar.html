Using last season as a guide

Where an organiser has only last season’s dates online, the pattern is still useful for planning. USACO’s winter contests, for example, ran on three January and February weekends in 2026, so a student preparing this fall should be ready by early January.

But never register, book travel or skip a school event on the strength of last year’s dates. Wait for the organiser to publish.

Why we do not fill the gaps

Dates copied from secondary sites are a common source of missed deadlines. Every dated row on this page comes from the organiser, and the rest stay blank until the organiser posts them.

When we refresh this page we re-read every source and change the read date at the top.

What the count suggests

November, December, February and March tie as the heaviest months, and the first two are mostly deadlines rather than contests. A family that sorts registrations in October removes most of the fall stress.

April is almost empty, which makes it the natural month to review the season and decide what to enter next year.

How we counted

Each row in the three dated tables counts once, in the month it starts, including the two October rows that had already passed. Rows dated to a whole month or a range of months count in their first month.

The count is a planning aid, not a measure of how hard any month is for a particular student.

Planning

Turning this calendar into a season plan

Our five steps for a family choosing what to enter. The idea is to work backwards from each deadline, so preparation starts early enough.

  1. Pick two or three competitions, not ten. One that suits the student’s strongest skill, and one that stretches them.
  2. Find the earliest registration date for each in the tables above, and the adult who must act on it.
  3. Count back eight to twelve weeks from the first contest date. That is when focused preparation should begin.
  4. Put every date in a shared family calendar with the source link, and check the link again a month before.
  5. After each contest, write down three things to fix before the next one on the list.

Questions from families

The 2026-27 competition calendar: questions families ask

What is the next big competition deadline for US students?

On the read date of 17 October 2026, the next was the Congressional App Challenge, due at noon EDT on Monday, October 26, 2026. The MATHCOUNTS Early Bird rate follows on November 3, 2026.

When are the Presidential SI Challenge deadlines?

Registration closes December 18, 2026 for teams, and projects must be in by December 23, 2026, in both cases at 5:00 p.m. Eastern. The state results come out between February 11 and 15, 2027.

When is MATHCOUNTS in 2027?

Chapter rounds fill February 2027 (1 to 28), state rounds fill March 2027 (1 to 31), and the National Competition meets in Washington, DC on May 9 and 10, 2027.

What are the USA-NA-AIO dates for 2027?

Round 1 takes place on February 19, 2027 (a Friday), 12:00 to 3:00 p.m. Eastern. Sign-ups end on January 31, 2027.

When are the 2026-27 USACO contests?

As of 17 October 2026, USACO had not yet posted a 2026-27 schedule. In the 2025-26 season its online contests ran in January and February with a proctored US Open in late March.

When is Math Kangaroo 2027?

Math Kangaroo USA says the contest is held on the 3rd Thursday in March, and the 2027 competition is in March 2027. Our own count of the calendar gives March 18, 2027; check with your center.

Why are the AMC contests missing?

Our request to the MAA site was refused with an access-denied response, and this calendar only prints dates read on the organiser's own site. Please check the MAA directly for AMC dates.

How often is this calendar updated?

Each refresh re-reads every source and changes the read date at the top. Until then, the source link in each row is the authority if a date has moved.

Do you organise any of these competitions?

We do not. Our part is teaching the skills these contests test; we play no role in running, judging or registering for any of them.

Can classes fit around these contest dates?

Weekdays or weekends, flexible: choose your batch, or for a very personalised plan choose 1-on-1 at your convenience, weekend mornings or weekdays.
