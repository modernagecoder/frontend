## The Al Mouj ratio-of-ratios project

The official Al Mouj fact panel is a small real dataset. Students copy the current values, exact labels and retrieval date into a table, then assign a unit and scope to every field. Residents and properties are stocks. Annual footfall is a flow of visits. Marina berths are capacity. Pedestrian paths are length. Retail experiences are venue counts.

A learner might calculate annual visits per retail venue and residents per residential property. Each ratio could have an interpretation if the source definitions hold. The tempting error divides the first ratio by the second and calls the result an engagement or efficiency index.

The arithmetic produces a number, but the compound unit is visits multiplied by properties, divided by venues and residents. No stated decision uses that unit. The populations and time scopes differ. Dividing one normalised measure by another does not make them comparable.

| Step | What the learner does | What it teaches |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Records value, unit, date and exact source label | Scope cannot be guessed from a short heading |
| 2 | Classifies stock, flow, capacity, venue count and length | Different systems need different denominators |
| 3 | Writes the unit algebra before calculating | Meaningless compound units appear early |
| 4 | Names the decision each ratio should support | Metrics need a purpose |
| 5 | Rejects the ratio of ratios or defines a valid rate | Correct arithmetic can still be bad analysis |
| 6 | Retrieves the current panel before presenting | Development facts can change |

Primary dataset: [Al Mouj Muscat current fact panel](https://www.almouj.com/en/). Students pull the live values themselves and retain the wording.
