March Break coding online: live classes for the break week

March Break is the one full week between the winter holidays and the end of the school year when a child has time to build something properly. In Ontario the dates are not chosen school by school; they come from a provincial regulation, which for 2027 points to Monday 15 March to Friday 19 March. This page explains that rule, shows what PA days are (Ontario's professional activity days, when students stay home and teachers train), compares the kinds of break camps on offer, and sets out how Modern Age Coders students spend a break week or a single day off coding live online, one to one at a time that suits the family or in a regular batch.

The 2027 dates

When is March Break 2027 in Ontario?

Ontario sets its school holidays in a regulation under the Education Act: Regulation 304, School Year Calendar, Professional Activity Days. We read the current version through Ontario's e-Laws service on 22 October 2026. It does not print the dates for each year; it gives a rule, and the dates follow from the calendar.

The school year itself"the school year shall commence on or after the 1st day of September and end on or before the 30th day of June."Ends on or before Wednesday 30 June 2027

Source: R.R.O. 1990, Reg. 304, School Year Calendar, Professional Activity Days, current consolidation (August 13, 2025), read 22 October 2026. The dates in the third column are our arithmetic from the regulation's wording, not dates printed by the province. Modern Age Coders is not connected with the Ontario government or any school board.

Why your board's calendar has the last word

Section 5 of the regulation allows that "a board may designate a school year and school holidays that are different from those prescribed in section 2", once the Minister approves the calendar. Because the regulation allows exceptions, a school or program can run on a different calendar.

Boards also have to adopt and submit their calendars "on or before the first day of March in respect of the next school year", so the 2027 to 2028 dates should be settled by early March 2027. If your child's school runs a different calendar, trust the school.

Outside Ontario

Other provinces leave the break to local calendars. In British Columbia, the School Calendar Regulation requires every school calendar to state "the vacation periods and the dates of statutory holidays", and each board must publish a proposed calendar "at least one month before the date the school calendar must be submitted to the minister".

So a family in Vancouver, Calgary, Winnipeg or Halifax should read the break dates from their own district or board. We do not print them here, because we have not read every board's calendar and will not guess.

One more date for families who book live classes: Eastern clocks go forward on Sunday 14 March 2027, the day before Ontario's break begins. A class time agreed in February moves by an hour for families in British Columbia, Alberta, Saskatchewan and Manitoba, which do not change their clocks, so we confirm every break-week time again that weekend.

PA days explained

What is a PA day? Ontario's professional activity days, simply

Parents new to Ontario often meet the term on a school newsletter. A PA day is a school day with no classes for students, set aside for teachers' professional work. The rules are in the same Regulation 304.

PA days in Ontario, from Regulation 304 and the 2026-27 ministry memorandum
QuestionWhat the official source says
What is a PA day?A "professional activity day" is defined as a school day that "is designated as a day for professional activities on a school calendar". It is counted apart from instructional days, the days on which an instructional program is provided for each pupil. The regulation's French version calls it a "journée pédagogique".
What do teachers do?Professional activity includes evaluation of pupils' progress, consultation with parents, counselling, curriculum and program work, professional development and conferences, "but does not include preparation by teachers for classes or instruction".
How many are there?"Three days shall be designated by the board as professional activity days." In addition, "up to four days may be designated by the board as professional activity days."
How long is the school year?"every school year shall include a minimum of 194 school days", counting PA days as school days.
Who picks the dates?Each board, on its own calendar. There is no single provincial PA day.
How much notice?Boards prepare a detailed summary of each PA day and make it public "at least 14 days before the professional activity day".
What are the 2026-27 topics?The ministry's memorandum of May 15, 2026 sets the mandatory topics, including "Cyber Safety and Artificial Intelligence".

Sources: Ontario Regulation 304 and Mandatory Professional Activity Days for the 2026-27 School Year, both read 22 October 2026.

PA days and coding, in practice

A PA day is a single weekday at home, often with a few days' notice from the newsletter. It suits one focused live class far better than a camp: an hour or so with a teacher, then an afternoon to finish what was started.

Our one-to-one students often book a class on a PA day. The free first class can also be booked on one, which gives a child something to look forward to on a day that otherwise drifts.

Elsewhere in Canada

Other provinces have school days without classes too. What they are called, and when they fall, comes from the local board or district calendar.

Whatever the name, the planning question is the same: how many days off, how much notice, and whether a parent is home. Our timing is flexible enough to fit any of them.

The rule in code

Turning the March Break rule into a Python program

A short program we wrote, and a good first project for a curious Grade 7 or 8 student: take a sentence from a law and turn it into steps a computer can follow. It works out Ontario's March Break for any year from the regulation's own words.

The one case the program refuses

When 14 March is itself a Friday, as it is in 2031, the phrase "the Friday preceding the 14th day of March" can be read two ways: that same Friday, or the one a week earlier. The program does not guess. It returns None and tells you to check your board.

That is a real programming lesson. When a specification is ambiguous, a good program says so rather than quietly picking an answer.

What a student learns from it

Reading a rule word by word: "next following" and "preceding" each become a step. Using a library, here datetime, instead of counting days by hand. Testing against a case you can check on a calendar.

A student can extend it in the break week: add Family Day (the third Monday in February), print a whole school-year holiday list, or turn it into a small web page.

Break-week plans

How our students spend a March Break week coding

We do not run a separate March Break camp; our camp programmes run in the winter break and in the summer. Over March Break we teach short live classes instead: one-to-one students book classes on the days of the week that suit the family, and batch students keep their usual class. These plans are our suggestions for turning those classes into a finished project by Friday.

  1. Before the break: tell us your child's board and break dates, and which days a parent is home. We set class times in your province's clock after the 14 March clock change.
  2. Monday: the first class sets the project and a definition of done that fits in five days.
  3. Tuesday to Thursday: short daily practice at home, with one class in the middle of the week for the hardest step.
  4. Friday: the student presents what they built, explains how one part works, and makes one change live. That last step is the real test of the week.
  5. After the break: the project becomes the first unit of a regular course, so the momentum is not lost when school starts again.

Why the week should end with a change

Anyone can follow along for five days and end with something that runs. The question is whether the child understands it well enough to change it.

That is our promise in a sentence: we teach students to think their way through a problem, not to copy an answer or an AI tool's output. A Friday change shows whether the week did that.

For students with contests ahead

The break falls between CEMC's February contests and its April ones. CEMC lists the 2027 Euclid for Tuesday, April 6, 2027 and the Fryer, Galois and Hypatia contests for Wednesday, April 7, 2027, so the break is the last calm week before them.

CEMC is clear that "all students must participate through their school." We prepare them with practice problems our teachers write, and our CEMC contests page explains more.

A full-day camp or a few live classes?

If the goal is childcare plus fun for the week, a full-day camp is the right tool, and no online class pretends otherwise.

If the goal is that your child can code better on 22 March than on 12 March, a few focused classes with a teacher who will still be there in April usually do more.

For parents

One week will not make a programmer. It can make a child want to become one, which matters more.

Ask your child on Friday to show you one part and change it while you watch. That short demonstration tells you what the week was worth.

For students

Pick a project small enough to finish. A small finished game teaches more than a big half-built one.

Keep a list of every error message you met this week and how you fixed it. It will save you hours later.

March Break and PA day coding: questions and answers

When is March Break 2027 in Ontario?

Ontario Regulation 304 sets March Break as five consecutive days starting on the Monday after the Friday before 14 March. By our arithmetic that is Monday 15 March to Friday 19 March 2027. A board can have an approved different calendar, so check yours.

When is March Break in BC, Alberta or other provinces?

Outside Ontario, break dates come from each school district or board calendar. BC's School Calendar Regulation, for example, requires every calendar to state its vacation periods. We do not print those dates because they vary by district.

What is a PA day?

In Ontario, a professional activity day is a school day set aside for teachers' professional work, with no classes for students. Each board sets three required PA days and may add up to four more, and publishes a summary at least 14 days before each one.

Do you run a March Break coding camp?

Not as a separate camp. Our camp programmes run in the winter break and in the summer. Over March Break we teach short live classes on the days your family chooses, one to one or in your child's usual batch, with a project to show on Friday.

Can my child do coding on a PA day?

Yes. A PA day suits one live one-to-one class, booked a few days ahead, with time afterwards to finish what was started. The free first class can also be booked on a PA day.

What age is March Break coding for?

We teach from age 6. For the break week we suggest Scratch projects for ages 6 to 8, a first Python game for 9 to 12, a real tool or web page for 13 to 15, and contest or course work for 16 to 18.

Is an online class better than an in-person March Break camp?

They do different jobs. A full-day camp gives a day out with friends. A few live online classes give focused teaching, at times you choose, with a teacher who carries on after the break.

What times are classes during March Break?

Weekdays or weekends, flexible: choose your batch, or for a very personalised plan choose 1-on-1 at your convenience, weekend mornings or weekdays. Eastern clocks go forward on 14 March 2027, so we confirm times that weekend.

Can a student prepare for a contest over March Break?

Yes. CEMC lists the 2027 Euclid for Tuesday 6 April and the Fryer, Galois and Hypatia contests for Wednesday 7 April, so the break is a useful week for practice. Students enter through their schools.

Is there a separate camp fee?

No. Break-week classes are part of our normal monthly fee, shown on this page in your own currency. You pay one month at a time.
