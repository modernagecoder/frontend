## Will a Year of the Goat ever be a leap year?

Two repeating cycles, a 12-year cycle of animal years and a 4-year cycle of leap years, line up in a fixed pattern. The lowest common multiple tells us the pattern.

Chatswood's festival page says "Thank you for a fantastic Year of the Horse celebration. See you in February 2027 for Year of the Goat!"; it also shows pictures from "Chatswood's Year of the Dragon". The Goat years are 2027, 2039, 2051 and so on, 12 apart. Leap years are, almost always, the years divisible by 4. Since LCM(12, 4) = 12, adding 12 never changes a year's remainder on division by 4, so the pattern locks in.

**Animal years and leap years (our calculation with the Gregorian calendar rules)**

| Animal years | Example years | Remainder when divided by 4 | Leap years? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dragon | 2024, 2036, 2048 | 0 | Always, until the century rule |
| Horse | 2026, 2038, 2050 | 2 | Never |
| Goat | 2027, 2039, 2051 | 3 | Never |

**The weekday of 13 February in each Goat year (our calculation)**

| Year | Weekday of 13 February | Leap days since the previous Goat year |
|---|---|---|
| 2027 | Saturday | n/a |
| 2039 | Sunday | 3 |
| 2051 | Monday | 3 |
| 2063 | Tuesday | 3 |
| 2075 | Wednesday | 3 |
| 2087 | Thursday | 3 |
| 2099 | Friday | 3 |
| 2111 | Friday | 2 (2100 is not a leap year) |

### One weekday per cycle

Twelve years with three leap days are 4,383 days, which is 626 weeks and 1 day. So 13 February slides one weekday later every Goat year, from Saturday in 2027 to Friday in 2099.

### The simple rule wins, for a while

Counting "one day per year plus one per leap day" predicts every row correctly until 2099. No calendar software is needed for most of this century.

### Then the century rule bites

The year 2100 is divisible by 4 but not by 400, so it is not a leap year. That cycle has only 2 leap days and 4,382 days, exactly 626 weeks, so 2111 is a Friday again.

## The same idea in everyday questions

Once students see that repeating cycles meet at the lowest common multiple, they find the pattern everywhere.

**Cycles that meet, and when (our worked answers)**

| Two cycles | Lengths | They line up again after |
|---|---|---|
| A weekly class and a fortnightly test | 7 days and 14 days | 14 days |
| Buses every 4 minutes and every 6 minutes | 4 and 6 | 12 minutes |
| A 12-year animal cycle and the 7-day week, ignoring leap days | 12 and 7 | 84 (LCM of 12 and 7) |
| Lights flashing every 3, 4 and 5 seconds | 3, 4 and 5 | 60 seconds |

### LCM and HCF together

For two numbers, the lowest common multiple times the highest common factor equals the product. For 4 and 6 that is 12 times 2, which equals 24. Students check the rule on several pairs before they trust it.

The calendar puzzle uses the LCM; simplifying fractions uses the HCF. They are two sides of the same factor list.

### When the simple model breaks

Real calendars carry exceptions, like the year 2100. Good maths students learn to state the model, use it, and then ask where it stops being true.

That habit matters far beyond calendars, from timetables to physics.

## Questions about a maths tutor in Chatswood

### Can Chatswood students have maths lessons with you?

Yes. Lessons are online and scheduled on Sydney time, for Kindergarten to Year 12 and adults, as private lessons or level-matched classes.

### Which of the HSC maths courses can you help with?

All four: Standard for applied maths, Advanced for calculus, and Extension 1 and Extension 2 for students who want more depth.

### Is a Year of the Goat ever a leap year?

No. Goat years are 12 apart and 12 is a multiple of 4, so every Goat year leaves remainder 3 on division by 4. 2027, 2039 and 2051 are all ordinary years.

### What is a lowest common multiple?

The smallest number that is a multiple of two or more numbers. LCM(12, 4) is 12, which is why the animal-year and leap-year cycles line up the same way every time.

### What weekday is 13 February 2027?

A Saturday. In each later Goat year it moves one weekday on, reaching Friday in 2099; because 2100 is not a leap year, it is Friday again in 2111.

### Can you guarantee an HSC band?

No tutor can. We aim for real understanding and tell you frankly how it is going; the band and the ATAR are earned in the exams.

### Do lessons move when the clocks change?

No. Lessons stay at the same Sydney time; we adjust the teacher's schedule.

### Do you teach adults?

Yes, in adult classes or one to one.

### Why is 2100 not a leap year?

Under the Gregorian rules, a year divisible by 100 is a leap year only if it is also divisible by 400. 2000 was a leap year; 2100 will not be.
