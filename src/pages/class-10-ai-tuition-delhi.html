Q3. What does a class actually teach?

A worked Unit 1 case question, the way it is solved in the Monday class

Unit 1, the AI Project Cycle and Ethical Frameworks, carries 7 theory marks and is where long case answers often come from. This question is our own, set on a problem every Delhi school knows in winter, and it is solved the way we solve it live: plan, write, then mark against value points.

Case question (4 marks)

A Delhi school wants an AI system that tells the PE teacher by 7:00 AM whether the morning's outdoor games period should move indoors because the air is too poor. (a) Write the problem statement using the 4Ws problem canvas. (b) Name two data features the team should collect and a source for each. (c) State one ethical concern and how the team should handle it.

(a) The 4Ws, then one sentence

Who: students and the PE teacher. What: outdoor exercise on bad-air mornings can harm health, and today the call is a guess. Where: the school ground, early morning. Why: an early, consistent alert lets PE shift indoors in time. Statement: "Our students and PE teacher have a problem of deciding whether outdoor games are safe when the air is poor in the early morning. An ideal solution would warn the teacher by 7:00 AM so the period can move indoors."

(b) Two features, two sources

Hourly PM2.5 or AQI readings, from the nearest public air-quality monitoring station or a sensor on the school roof. Wind speed and temperature for the morning, from a weather service forecast. Naming a source for each feature is what earns the mark; "data from the internet" does not.

(c) Ethics, with a fix

Students with asthma have more at stake than anyone else, so a rights-based view says the system should lean towards caution: a false alarm only moves a games period, while a missed bad day can cause harm. The team should tune it to miss as few bad days as possible and tell parents how alerts are decided.

In class we mark this as 2 marks for a problem statement that covers all four Ws, 1 for two features with named sources and 1 for an ethical concern paired with an action. That split is our own teaching rubric; the CBSE marking scheme sets the value points for each year's paper. Notice that part (c) quietly sets up Unit 3: leaning towards caution means caring about recall more than precision, which students meet again in the confusion matrix numericals. More questions of this shape are on the case based questions page.

Q4. How does it fit around school and coaching?

A Delhi Class 10 week with AI tuition in it

The class is late on purpose. By 9:00 PM most Class 10 students in Delhi are home from school, sport and maths or science coaching, have eaten, and still have an hour of focus left. Here is how the week runs.

  1. Monday, 9:00 PM: one theory unit, then its questions

    One part of a Part B unit is taught with examples, then attempted at once in the paper's shapes: one-mark objective items, a two-mark answer and a longer case. The teacher marks two or three answers on screen so everyone sees where marks fall away.

  2. Tuesday: a worksheet that takes about twenty minutes

    A short worksheet on Monday's topic, built so it fits after school without eating the evening. It comes back marked in the next class.

  3. Wednesday, 9:00 PM: the practical half

    A program for the file is written in Jupyter, run, broken on purpose and fixed, then explained aloud as if to an examiner. Some weeks it is an Orange workflow instead, or the next step of the project.

  4. Doubts in the class itself

    Anyone can unmute and ask, or share their screen when code will not run. Doubts left over from the worksheet open the next class rather than waiting for a doubt day.

  5. From January: timed papers

    Full mock papers are written at home under time, then gone through live, with every lost mark rewritten. The series is described on the sample paper and mock tests page.

Is online AI tuition as good as a home tutor for Class 10?
