Adam today

A small town with a very large footnote

Adam is cited constantly in Omani history and described in a mixture of firm dates and careful hedges. Both kinds of number end up in the same column.

The birthplace of a ruling house

Ahmad bin Said, who founded the Al Bu Said line, was born here. A town whose main historical record is one person is a town whose data is a handful of rows, and small tables are where imprecision does the most damage.

A quarter still standing

Harat al Busaid is described as one of the oldest lanes in Adam, with around 120 old houses and 6 defensive towers. One of those numbers was counted and one was estimated, and the sentence does not say which is which any more clearly than that.

Dates that are firm and dates that are not

A birth year of 1694 sits alongside a dynasty beginning about 1749. Every history is written like this. The trouble starts only when a spreadsheet treats both as integers, because integers have no way to remember that one of them was hedged.

Arithmetic has no place to put the word about

Subtraction takes two numbers and returns one. It has nowhere to record that one input was approximate, so it does not. The result is as precise-looking as the most precise input, which is exactly backwards: it should be as vague as the vaguest.

Verified local facts

Adam facts with a trail behind them

Each claim traced, and each one marked exact or approximate.

Born here in 1694

Ahmad bin Said, the founder of the Al Busaidi dynasty, was born in Adam in 1694. This is given as a specific year and is treated on this page as exact.

Ruling since about 1749

The Al Bu Said dynasty is described as having ruled Oman since about 1749. The word about belongs to the source and is kept everywhere the figure appears here, including inside the project table.

From governor to imam

Ahmad ibn Said was governor of Suhar, displaced the Yarubids and became imam of Oman, with authority extending to Zanzibar, Pemba and Kilwa. A career spanning several posts and several places is several records, not one.

Around 120 houses and 6 towers

Harat al Busaid is recorded with around 120 old houses and protected by 6 defensive towers. One figure is hedged and the other is not, in the same sentence, which is the ordinary way heritage counts are published.

One of the oldest lanes in Adam

The quarter is ranked among the oldest lanes in Adam and is named as the birthplace. Rankings are ordinal, counts are cardinal, and mixing the two in one table is its own small trap.

A dynasty still in place

The line founded here continues to rule, which means any duration calculated from these dates is open-ended at one end. An interval with one moving endpoint should never be stored as a single number.

None of this is a claim about Modern Age Coders and no connection is implied. Adam is on this page because its two best-known dates are one firm and one hedged, which is the cleanest possible setup for the project.

Signature project

Fifty five years old, give or take twenty

Nobody rounds anything, mistypes anything or misreads a source. The precision disappears inside the minus sign.

1. Write the two dates down as published

Born 1694. Dynasty ruling since about 1749. One of those has a hedge attached and one does not, and both are about to be typed into the same kind of cell.

2. Subtract, because the question is obvious

How old was he when the dynasty began? 1749 minus 1694 is 55. The answer is correct arithmetic on the numbers as entered, and it is printed with no hedge on it at all, because the hedge was never in the cell to begin with.

3. Put the hedge back and look again

If about 1749 means within two years, the answer is 53 to 57. Within five years, 50 to 60. Within ten, 45 to 65. The honest answer is a window between four and twenty years wide, and 55 is only the middle of it.

The exact number contributes nothing to the precision of the total

The six towers are counted exactly. Added to around 120 houses they buy no accuracy whatsoever: the total is uncertain by precisely as much as the word around was already worth. This is the part people find genuinely surprising, and it is why mixing a firm figure with a hedged one and reporting the sum flatly is worse than reporting nothing. For younger learners

Two paper strips, one cut to an exact length and one torn roughly, laid end to end and measured. The child is asked how sure they are of the total. The torn edge answers the question better than any explanation, and there is no screen involved.

For teenagers

Python computes the accession age under several readings of about 1749 and prints every window beside the bare 55. The learner then writes a function that refuses to return a single number when any input carried a hedge.

For adults

Uncertainty travels as data rather than as a comment, sums of measured and estimated figures are kept apart, and reported precision is capped by the weakest input rather than by the display format.

The 1694 birth year, the about 1749 dynasty date, the governorship of Suhar, the around 120 houses and the 6 towers are all published. The plus or minus two, five and ten year readings of about are illustrative, chosen to show how the window widens, and the page says so in the table and here. No source states how wide about is, which is precisely why the word matters.

FAQ

Adam coding class questions

Local history, teaching method and service terms, kept separate.

Are the Adam dates in the project real?

Yes. Ahmad bin Said was born in Adam in 1694 and the Al Bu Said dynasty is described as ruling since about 1749, with the word about coming from the source. The plus or minus two, five and ten year readings are illustrative, because no source states how wide about is, and the page says so in the table itself.

Why is losing the word about such a serious problem?

Because the loss is invisible and permanent. Once 55 is written into a cell, nothing downstream can recover the fact that one of its inputs was hedged. Every later calculation inherits a confidence the underlying evidence never had, and each step makes the claim harder to walk back.

Is there a Modern Age Coders centre in Adam?

No. No premises are claimed in Adam or anywhere in Ad Dakhiliyah. Classes are live online and need a laptop or desktop, working audio, a current browser and a stable connection. The page describes where the service reaches, not a building.

Does adding an exact number to an estimate improve it?

No, and this is the part people find genuinely surprising. Around 120 houses plus 6 exactly counted towers is uncertain by exactly as much as the 120 was. The precise figure contributes no precision to the total. It only makes the total look more carefully assembled than it is.

Can an adult in Adam study rather than a school student?

Yes, up to 67. Adults take Python, analytics and reporting, and anyone who has produced a forecast from a mixture of firm actuals and rough estimates has lived this. Their own model is the sharpest exercise wherever the data is theirs to use.

What does a learner have at the end of this project?

A dataset where the hedge lives in its own column, a function that will not return a bare number when an input was approximate, and the habit of printing a window rather than a midpoint. None of that depends on the language they learned it in.
