Haima today

A town, a wilayat and one name between them

Administrative seats almost always share their name with the area they administer, which quietly doubles every statistic about them.

The seat of the governorate

Haima is the capital of the Al Wusta Governorate, positioned centrally on the Muscat Salalah main road. Being the seat is why the name appears on records that are about the town and on records that are about a far larger area.

Settled around water

Permanent settlement began after oil explorers sank a water well here, and that well together with another at al-Ajaiz became the first permanent water sources in Jiddat il-Harasiis. A place with a documented founding is a place whose records start at a known point.

Desert on all sides

Haima has a hot desert climate with brief mild winters and long hot summers. The area the name has to cover is enormous and almost entirely empty, which is exactly why the town figure and the wilayat figure diverge.

Two correct answers to one question that was never finished

Neither number is an error and neither source is careless. The question how many people live in Haima simply has two readings, and a figure sitting in a cell carries no memory of which one it answered.

Verified local facts

Haima facts with a trail behind them

Each claim traced, with the boundary it applies to named.

Capital of Al Wusta

Haima is the capital of the Al Wusta Governorate, which itself consists of the four wilayats of Haima, Mahout, Duqm and Al Jazer. The governorate, the wilayat and the town are three nested things and two of them share a name.

Two figures from one census

The 2020 census gives the town 7,814 people. A population database gives the wilayat 10,093 for the same census. Both are published and this page uses both, each labelled with what it counts.

Founded on a well

Permanent settlement began after oil explorers sank a water well at Haima. A settlement with a cause has a start date, and start dates are the boundary conditions of any series about it.

The first water in the Jiddat

That well and another at al-Ajaiz became the first permanent water sources in Jiddat il-Harasiis. Two wells named separately is a reminder that the surrounding area has its own identity distinct from the town.

On the road between the ends of the country

Haima sits centrally on the Muscat Salalah main road. Position on a route is why a place this size has a governorate seat at all.

Hot desert, by classification

The climate is hot desert with brief, pleasant, dry winters and long hot summers. A classification describes a regime rather than a reading, and the two should not share a column.

Modern Age Coders has no association with the wells, the road or the governorate administration and claims none. Haima is used here because a seat that shares its name with its wilayat is the most ordinary way a single figure comes to mean two things.

Signature project

The same one hundred and forty cases, at 13.9 or 17.9 per thousand

Nothing is wrong with either population figure. The error is made in the moment somebody puts one of them under a line.

1. Write down both published populations

Seven thousand eight hundred and fourteen for the town. Ten thousand and ninety three for the wilayat. Both from the 2020 census, both correct, differing by 2,279 people or 29 per cent of the smaller.

2. Build a rate the ordinary way

Take a count of something across the wilayat and divide by the population of Haima. Both halves are sourced, both are recent, and the phrase population of Haima is exactly as specific as anybody ever writes it. The case count used here is illustrative and the page says so.

3. Do it the other way and compare

The same 140 cases come out at 13.9 per thousand on the wilayat population and 17.9 on the town. That is a 29 per cent difference produced entirely by which of two correct numbers went underneath, and both versions cite a real census.

Both halves are sourced, which is what makes it so hard to challenge

A reviewer asking where these numbers came from gets a good answer for the numerator and a good answer for the denominator. Each is a real figure from a real census. The fault is in the join between them, and a citation on each half is precisely what stops anybody looking at the join. Every figure above was computed before this was written.

FAQ

Haima coding class questions

The place, the teaching and the terms, answered one at a time.

Are both Haima populations real?

Yes. The 2020 census gives the town 7,814 and a population database gives the wilayat 10,093 for the same census. Both are published and both appear here, each labelled with what it counts. The 140 cases used to build the example rate are illustrative and the page says so.

Why is a sourced figure not enough?

Because a citation covers where a number came from, not what it counts. Both halves of a broken rate can carry perfect references. A reviewer checking provenance finds two good answers and no reason to look at the join between them, which is where the error lives.

Is there a Modern Age Coders centre in Haima?

No. No premises are claimed in Haima or anywhere in Al Wusta. Classes are live online and need a laptop or desktop, working audio, a current browser and a stable connection. The page describes where the service reaches, not a building.

Which population should somebody use?

Whichever matches the numerator. If the count covers the wilayat, use the wilayat figure; if it covers the town, use the town. The rule is not which number is better, it is that both halves of a fraction have to describe the same area over the same period.

Is this open to working adults, or school students only?

Both, with 67 as the upper limit. Adults work in Python, analytics and reporting. Anyone whose organisation reports a per-head figure where the head count comes from a different system than the numerator is looking at this exact fault. Bring the real numbers if they are yours to share.

What comes out of this project?

Column names that carry their unit, an assertion that numerator and denominator share a boundary and a period, and the habit of publishing both official figures when two exist. All three apply to any indicator anybody builds.
