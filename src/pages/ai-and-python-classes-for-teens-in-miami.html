For a coder, the most useful line in that table is the first one. A county that is wider than some states is a county where “near” means very different things in different places. In Brickell a library may be a short walk away; in the far south it can be a drive. That difference is exactly what a distance calculation can measure, and it is why the signature project below starts with geography rather than with a chatbot.

Florida and computer science

What Florida law says about computer science in school

From Florida Statutes section 1007.2616, Computer science and technology instruction, read on the Legislature’s Online Sunshine site on 18 October 2026.

Section 1007.2616 tells public schools to give students in grades K-12 opportunities to learn computer science, and says those opportunities must include computer science courses in middle school and high school. It also closes a gap for students whose school does not run a course: if a district does not offer an identified course, it must provide access through the Florida Virtual School or other means. And it lets eligible computer science courses and technology certifications count toward the mathematics or science requirements for a high school diploma. On access, Code.org (2025), 2025 State of AI + CS Education, records 463 of 1,192 Florida high schools offering a foundational computer science course in 2023 to 2024, from state education agency data. In short, the law guarantees a route; whether a Miami teen takes it, and how far they go, is still a choice.

What we could not read

The Florida Department of Education website refused our request on 18 October 2026, so this page quotes the statute itself rather than department guidance, and no course list is reproduced.

Signature project

How far is the next library? Haversine distances across Miami-Dade

Miami-Dade County publishes its public library branches as map points. Teens download all 50, write the haversine formula in Python, and find each branch’s nearest neighbour.

ResultLibrary recordsOur distance
Most isolated branchNaranja Branch to South Dade Regional6.03 miles
Island branchKey Biscayne Branch to Coconut Grove Branch5.28 miles
Third most isolatedSouth Dade Regional to Palmetto Bay Branch4.20 miles
Closest separate sitesCoconut Grove Branch and Virrick Park Branch0.70 miles
Same coordinateWestchester Regional Library and a second Westchester record0.00 miles
Median nearest-branch distanceAll 50 records1.78 miles
North to south spanNortheast Dade-Aventura Branch to Naranja Branch36.1 miles

1. Read the points

The county’s feature service returns 50 library records with LAT and LON fields. Students load them into a list of dictionaries and check that every latitude sits near 25.5 to 26 degrees, a quick sanity test that catches swapped columns.

2. Write haversine

Flat-map distance is wrong on a sphere, so students code the haversine formula with math.radians, math.sin, math.cos and math.asin, using an Earth radius of 3,958.8 miles, and test it on two points whose distance they can check on a map.

3. Search for the nearest

A double loop compares each branch with every other branch, 50 times 49 comparisons, and keeps the smallest. Then the discussion: why does Key Biscayne look isolated when it is close to the mainland as a bird flies?

The zero in the table is the most instructive number on the page. Two records, Westchester Regional Library and a Westchester health and wellness information point, share exactly the same coordinate, so the naive nearest-neighbour code reports a distance of 0.00 miles. A student has to decide what that means: are they one building with two services, or a data entry shortcut? Either way, the program needs a rule, such as skipping identical coordinates or merging them, and the student has to write that rule down. That is a real software engineering decision, made on real public data.

Ours, not the county’s

Every distance here is our calculation from one download on 18 October 2026. Straight-line distance is not travel distance, and Miami-Dade County says the data is provided as is and is not guaranteed to be accurate or complete. The county has not reviewed these results.

A stretch task turns the project toward AI. Once a teen has nearest distances for every branch, they can cluster the 50 points with k-means and compare the clusters with the CITY field. Does the algorithm rediscover Miami Beach as a group? It is a gentle first look at unsupervised learning, built on numbers the student already understands, instead of a black box on someone else’s data.

Coding and AI

Why a Miami teen should learn Python before leaning on AI

An assistant can write a distance function. A student needs to know when its answer is quietly wrong.

Ask an AI tool to find the nearest library to each branch and it will happily produce code. It may use flat-earth distance, which drifts at this latitude, or it may report a zero without comment, as the Westchester records show. A teen who has written haversine by hand spots both problems in seconds; one who has not will copy the result into a school project.

That is the habit our classes build. Students use AI assistants for speed, then verify: they test a function on a pair of points they can check, they read every line before running it, and they explain in their own words why the output makes sense.

Miami teens who can question a confident answer are the ones ready for serious AI work, whatever they go on to study. The longer argument is in is coding still worth learning in 2026.
