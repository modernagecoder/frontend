Eligibility

Who can take the Hunter College High School entrance exam in 2027?

A candidate must meet three conditions: be in 6th grade at a NYC or neighboring school or be homeschooled, live full time in one of the five boroughs as of September 1, and hold a minimum qualifying score from 5th grade.

Qualifying scores for the January 27, 2027 Hunter entrance exam (Hunter College Campus Schools, Eligibility for Fall 2027 Entry, read 31 October 2026)
Child’s 5th grade testingWhat qualifies
Took the NYS exams in Spring 2026ELA scale score of 478 and Math scale score of 497, both met
Took an approved non-NYS exam in 5th grade (Fall 2025 or Spring 2026)90th national percentile in both Reading and Math, first sitting only

Why this comes first

Every bit of preparation is wasted if the child cannot sit the exam, so check the 5th grade scores before anything else. Hunter asks for an official score report or an NYC Schools Account screenshot showing three-digit scale scores; it will not accept performance levels or percentage ranges instead.

If the child took both the NYS exams and another approved test, Hunter says only the NYS scores count. A child who missed the cutoff may not take another exam in 6th grade to try again.

Residency and grade

Residency means living full time in the five boroughs as the primary home on September 1 of the application year. Hunter adds that property ownership alone does not count and checks the address on the family’s NYS IT-201 tax form.

The overview page is blunt: "The only entry year for HCHS is 7th grade." Students are not admitted into grades 8 to 12, and "There are NO exceptions to grade entry."

Applying

How do you apply for the Hunter entrance exam, and by when?

Applications go through Ravenna, an online system. The fall 2027 application opened at noon on Wednesday, September 23, 2026 and closes at 2:00 PM on Friday, December 4, 2026. Hunter charges no application fee.

  1. Check eligibility. Confirm the 5th grade score and residency rules above before starting. Hunter states that an invitation letter is not needed to apply.
  2. Create a Ravenna account. Go to ravenna-hub.com, create an account, and add a student profile for the child. Each child applying needs their own profile.
  3. Find Hunter College Campus Schools in the Ravenna school directory, choose the High School application type and answer every question.
  4. Upload the score evidence: an NYS score screenshot or an official report from another approved exam. Hunter verifies every score with the child’s school.
  5. Submit by 2:00 PM on Friday, December 4, 2026. Do not send recommendation letters, report cards, awards, photos or essays; Hunter asks families not to.
  6. Sit the exam on Wednesday, January 27, 2027. Hunter says plainly that there are no make-up dates for students who miss it.

The exam

What is on the Hunter College High School entrance exam?

Three sections in three hours: 50 multiple-choice reading comprehension questions, 30 multiple-choice math questions and a writing assignment. Students may do the sections in any order, and calculators are not permitted.

The math section in Hunter’s words

Hunter says the Mathematics section "assesses students’ understanding of mathematical concepts and their ability to apply mathematical reasoning and problem-solving skills." The questions "may require students to perform calculations, recognize patterns and relationships, interpret information, and solve problems in a variety of mathematical contexts."

Unlike a state test, there is no published standards list. The safest reading is the full grade 6 curriculum, done flexibly: fractions, decimals and percent, ratios, negative numbers, area and volume, simple data, and the patterns that tie them together.

Use Hunter’s free samples

Hunter publishes two sample entrance exams with math solutions and study guides on its Entrance Exam 2027 page. It notes that they "were not administered in any previous year" and that actual content will vary.

Hunter also states: "HCHS does not publish any portion of the actual exam and does not endorse any test preparation service or agency." We send every student to those samples and never copy their questions into our lessons.

Scoring

How is the Hunter entrance exam scored?

The two multiple-choice sections are computer-scored first. Hunter then sets threshold scores to decide who advances to the essay-reading round, and the essays are read blind before students are selected.

From exam day to decision (Hunter College Campus Schools, Entrance Exam 2027, scoring and student selection, read 31 October 2026)
StageWhat Hunter describes
1. Machine scoringReading and math multiple-choice answers are computer-scored and checked for accuracy
2. ThresholdsThreshold scores decide who advances to essay reading; they may vary from year to year
3. Essay roundA panel reads essays blind, identifying students only by HCHS identification number
4. SelectionStudents are selected for admission after the essay review
5. WaitlistA pool of waitlisted students; the waitlist is not ranked
AfterNo rescoring, rereading or reconsideration; families do not receive scores

What this means for math

Math counts most at stage 2. The multiple-choice score decides whether a student’s essay is read at all, so strong math and strong reading together get the writing in front of the panel.

Because Hunter tells families neither the scores nor whether a child reached the essay round, practice tests at home are the only feedback a student will get. That makes honest, timed practice with the official samples more valuable.

What we will not say

No tutor can tell a family where the thresholds will fall, because Hunter sets them each year and does not publish them. We make no prediction of admission.

We teach the math, the pacing and the habits of checking that multiple-choice rewards, and we tell parents plainly what we see in practice.

Pacing

How long should each Hunter math question take?

Hunter sets one three-hour block for all three sections, so the student chooses how much goes to math. Our table shows the time per question for three sensible splits; the arithmetic is ours.

Seconds per math question under three time budgets (our calculation from the published 30 questions)
Minutes given to mathSeconds per questionWhat remains for reading and writing
60120 (3,600 / 30)120 minutes
70140 (4,200 / 30)110 minutes
80160 (4,800 / 30)100 minutes
  1. Settle the order at home. With the two Hunter samples, try math first once and reading first once. Keep whichever order leaves the student calmer for the writing piece.
  2. Two passes through the math. First pass: answer every question that yields within about two minutes and mark the rest. Second pass: return to the marked ones. A paper exam allows this, so use it.
  3. Eliminate before guessing. With five choices, a blind guess is right one time in five, or 20 percent. Crossing out two impossible answers leaves three, which makes a guess right one time in three. The arithmetic is ours.
  4. Estimate first. Without a calculator, a rough answer before the exact one catches slips. If the estimate is about 40 and the work gives 400, something went wrong.
  5. Bubble carefully. Hunter publishes a guide to filling out the Scantron. A shifted row of bubbles can cost more than any hard question.

Why we teach elimination on purpose

Five choices is more than many school tests use, and a careful look at the options often rules out two quickly: a negative answer for a length, a fraction bigger than 1 for part of a whole, a number with the wrong last digit.

We practice spotting impossible choices as a skill of its own, with our own five-choice problems, so it becomes a habit rather than a last resort.

Time for the writing piece

Hunter’s writing assignment can be up to two pages with time to plan. If math takes 80 minutes, reading and writing share 100. A student who races through math to protect the essay often loses more than they gain.

We help students find a split that holds up under a full timed sample, then stick to it.

Worked examples

Three worked Hunter-style math examples

Problems we invented at a strong grade 6 level. They are not Hunter questions and are not taken from either Hunter sample exam.

Example 1: parts of a whole, in two steps. A jar holds red, blue and green marbles. One third are red, one quarter are blue, and the remaining 20 are green. How many marbles are in the jar? Reasoning: red and blue together are 1/3 + 1/4 = 4/12 + 3/12 = 7/12. So green is 5/12 of the jar, and 5/12 of the total is 20, which makes 1/12 equal to 4 and the whole jar 48. Check: 16 red, 12 blue, 20 green, total 48. A five-choice list would likely include 60, the answer for someone who treats green as one third.

Example 2: a fraction of a fraction. Lena cuts a ribbon in half and gives one half away. She cuts the rest into four equal pieces and uses three of them. What fraction of the original ribbon did she use? She kept 1/2, and used 3/4 of that: 3/4 x 1/2 = 3/8. The common slip is answering 3/4, which is the fraction of the kept half, not of the whole ribbon. Reading the last words of the question again, "of the original ribbon", saves the point.

Example 3: a repeating pattern. The digits 1, 4, 2, 8, 5, 7 repeat forever: 142857142857... What is the 1,000th digit? The block has 6 digits, so divide: 1,000 = 6 x 166 + 4. After 166 full blocks we need the 4th digit of the block, which is 8. Hunter’s own description mentions recognizing "patterns and relationships"; finding the cycle length and using the remainder is the whole idea.

What the examples share

Each needs only grade 6 content, but each is two or three steps, and each has one tempting wrong answer that a careless reader would choose from five options.

None needs a calculator, but all of them need clean written working, because a jumbled page makes it easy to lose a twelfth or a remainder.

How we teach from them

Before writing, the student says the plan aloud: "add the known parts, find what is left, scale up." Next, one number is swapped for a new one and the student works it through once more, which tests the method rather than the memory.

Last, we write the five choices ourselves and ask the student which wrong answers a rushed reader would pick. Knowing the traps is half of avoiding them.

Practice set

Ten original practice problems for Hunter math

Written by our teachers for 6th graders preparing for January 27, 2027. No calculator. About 140 seconds each matches the middle row of the pacing table.

  1. What is 2/3 of 3/4 of 48?
  2. A shirt is on sale for 30 percent off and costs 28 dollars. What was the original price in dollars?
  3. The ratio of cats to dogs at a shelter is 3 to 5. There are 16 more dogs than cats. How many animals are there?
  4. What is the smallest whole number that leaves a remainder of 1 when divided by 2, by 3 and by 4, and is greater than 1?
  5. A rectangle has perimeter 30 cm and its length is twice its width. What is its area?
  6. Evaluate negative 7 + 12 minus (negative 5).
  7. The mean of four test scores is 85. What must the fifth score be for the mean of all five to be 87?
  8. How many whole numbers from 1 to 100 are multiples of 3 or of 5?
  9. A cube has a surface area of 54 square inches. What is its volume?
  10. If 5 pencils cost as much as 2 pens, and a pen costs 1.25 dollars, how much do 8 pencils cost?

Where 6th graders slip

Problem 2 tempts students to add 30 percent to 28, giving 36.40, which is not the original price. Problem 8 tempts them to add 33 and 20 without removing the numbers counted twice.

Problem 9 needs the surface area split into six faces before the edge length appears. Students who divide by 4 or by 8 have mixed up their cube facts.

For parents

Steps 3 and 4 rarely appear in a school math class. A timed Hunter sample at home shows them clearly.

Because Hunter does not share scores, keep your own record of each practice: questions right, time used, and the ideas missed.

For students

Before choosing an answer, ask whether it even makes sense. A share of something cannot be more than the whole.

Keep a page of every mistake and why it happened. Read it the week before the exam.

Questions from NYC families

Hunter entrance exam math: what families ask

When is the Hunter College High School entrance exam in 2027?

Hunter College Campus Schools lists the entrance exam on Wednesday, January 27, 2027, for 7th grade entry in fall 2027. There are no make-up dates for students who miss it.

What score do you need to take the Hunter entrance exam?

For students who took the Spring 2026 5th grade NYS exams, Hunter lists minimum scale scores of 478 in ELA and 497 in Math, both required. Students with other approved exams need the 90th national percentile in Reading and Math.

How many math questions are on the Hunter entrance exam?

Hunter says the Mathematics section has 30 multiple-choice questions, each with five answer choices from A to E. Reading Comprehension has 50 questions, and there is also a writing assignment.

How long is the Hunter entrance exam?

Students have three hours to complete the whole exam and may do the three sections in any order. The split of time between reading, math and writing is the student's own choice.

Can you use a calculator on the Hunter exam?

No. Hunter's Entrance Exam 2027 page states that calculators are not permitted, so all math is done by hand.

When is the deadline to apply to Hunter College High School?

Hunter lists the deadline as 2:00 PM on Friday, December 4, 2026, through the Ravenna online system. The application opened at noon on Wednesday, September 23, 2026, and there is no fee to apply.

Does Hunter accept the ISEE, SSAT or MAP for qualifying?

Not this year. Hunter lists ERB Milestones, NWEA MAP and iReady as not approved, and the ISEE and SSAT as not accepted this year. Approved exams include ERB CTP 5, ITBS, TerraNova 3 and Stanford 10.

Do families get their child's Hunter exam score?

No. Hunter says students and parents do not receive scores for any part of the exam and are not told whether a student advanced to the essay round. It also does not rescore exams or reconsider decisions.

Do you use real Hunter questions in class?

No. Hunter states it does not publish any portion of the actual exam. We write our own problems and send students to Hunter's two free sample exams, which we never copy.

What days and times are Hunter math classes?

Weekdays or weekends, flexible: choose your batch, or for a very personalised plan choose 1-on-1 at your convenience, weekend mornings or weekdays.

Are you connected with Hunter College High School?

No. Modern Age Coders is an independent online school, not connected with Hunter College, Hunter College Campus Schools or CUNY. Hunter says it does not endorse any test preparation service, and that includes us.
