# Refund Policy

**Last Updated:** October 2026

## 1. General Refund Policy

At Modern Age Coders, we strive to ensure absolute satisfaction with our courses. We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee for all our online courses, provided that you have not completed more than 20% of the course content.

## 2. Plans Paid for 6 Months at Once

If you paid for 6 months at once and the student stops early, we refund every session that has not been taken yet. The refund is the number of sessions left multiplied by the per-session price of your 6-month plan, which is shown on the plan card when you enrol.

**Example:** a 1-on-1 plan of 24 sessions is paid for 6 months at once. After 10 sessions the student stops. The 14 sessions left are refunded at the plan's per-session price.

For 6-month plans this replaces the 7-day and 20% limits in sections 1, 3 and 4: you can ask for it at any point during the 6 months. Sessions already taken are not refunded.

## 3. Eligibility

To be eligible for a refund, you must submit your request within 7 days of the original purchase date. Refunds are only applicable to your initial purchase and are not valid for renewals or repeated subscriptions.

## 4. Non-Refundable Items

Certain items are strictly non-refundable, including:

- Courses where more than 20% of the content has been viewed or downloaded.
- One-on-one mentorship sessions that have already been conducted.
- Customized coding bootcamps or special arrangements.

## 5. Processing Time

Once your refund request is received and approved, we will initiate a refund to your original method of payment. You will receive the credit within 7 to 10 business days, depending on your card issuer's policies.

## 6. How to Request a Refund

If you wish to request a refund, please contact our support team at [connect@modernagecoders.com](mailto:connect@modernagecoders.com) with your order details and reason for the refund.
