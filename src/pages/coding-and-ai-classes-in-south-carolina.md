## One unit of computer science, written into Regulation 43-234

South Carolina&rsquo;s graduation requirements sit in the State Board of Education regulations, which the South Carolina Legislature publishes in its Code of Regulations, Chapter 43. We read the regulation there on 18 October 2026, because the Department of Education website did not respond.

**What Regulation 43-234, Defined Program, Grades 9 to 12 and Graduation Requirements, says about computer science and credit**

| Topic | The regulation text |
|---|---|
| Total units | The student must earn a total of twenty-four units of credit, set out in a table of subjects |
| Computer science | The unit table lists computer science 1.0 |
| Keyboarding | A student who earned the one-half credit in Keyboarding by the 2017 to 2018 school year is awarded one-half unit of credit for Computer Science |
| What a unit means | One unit for an academic standards-based course of at least 120 hours of instruction; one-half unit for at least 60 hours |
| Advanced Placement | Schools whose structure includes grades eleven and twelve must offer Advanced Placement courses |
| Virtual courses | Where a school&rsquo;s courses do not meet a student&rsquo;s needs, Virtual SC courses, if available, must be offered by the district |

### Since 1997 to 1998

State law, Section 59-39-100 of the South Carolina Code, records that the diploma rose to twenty-four units for students entering ninth grade in the 1997 to 1998 school year.

### The Profile of the Graduate

The same section says that for students entering ninth grade from the 2018 to 2019 school year, the twenty-four units are prescribed in that section and in State Board regulation, to support the Profile of the Graduate.

### What 120 hours buys

A full unit is a substantial course, but it is still one course. It can cover the basics well; it cannot also cover a second language, AP preparation and a portfolio of projects.

We quote the regulation and statute as published and add no interpretation of how individual districts schedule the unit. Families with a question about a particular course should ask their school counselor, who works from the district&rsquo;s course coding manual.

## How many Charleston parks are there? A lesson in duplicates

The City of Charleston Open Data hub publishes a Parks layer with one row per park or recreation facility. Counting rows gives one answer. Counting parks properly gives another, and the gap teaches more than any tutorial.

**What the Charleston Parks layer contains (our counts from the download of 18 October 2026)**

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Rows in the layer | 132 |
| Distinct FACILITYSITEID values | 131 |
| Site ID that appears twice | FAC-848 |
| Rows marked PARKFAC and RECFAC | 115 and 17 |
| Amenity cells that are a space, empty or null | 1,640 of 1,848 |

### Pick the key

Park names look like a key, and here they nearly are, but the robust choice is the facility site ID. Grouping by it shows 131 sites in 132 rows, so exactly one site is listed twice.

### Near, not exact

The two FAC-848 rows are not identical copies. One link begins https and the other http, and they disagree on RESTROOM, DOGPARK and ULTIMATEGOLF. Deleting either row silently changes the amenity counts.

### Three ways to say nothing

An amenity can be YES, NO, a single space, an empty string or null. PICNICAREA alone has 27 YES, 50 empty strings and 55 nulls. Students map all three blanks to one value before counting.

**Amenities marked YES across all 132 rows, before deduplication (our counts)**

| Amenity field | Rows marked YES |
|---|---|
| PLAYGROUND | 43 |
| PICNICAREA | 27 |
| BASKETBALL | 21 |
| TENNIS | 15 |
| DOGPARK | 11 |

The project ends with a decision rather than a formula. Students write a rule, for example keep the row with the https link, document it in a comment, and rerun the counts: the dog park total then becomes 10 instead of 11. In pandas the check is df.duplicated(subset=["FACILITYSITEID"], keep=False), which returns both FAC-848 rows so they can be compared side by side. The regions in the data, from the Peninsula with 70 rows to Cainhoy with 1, make a second exercise in grouping once the duplicate is resolved.

### Ours, and provisional

Every count here is our own, from one download. The City of Charleston states that it makes no warranty about the data and disclaims any guarantee of accuracy or completeness, and the city publishes no park total in this form. A student who can explain exactly why their total is 131 has learned the lesson.

## Eastern Time for the whole state

In the IANA time zone database, South Carolina appears in the list of states on US Eastern Time, kept under America/New_York.

### A worked example

A 6:00 p.m. lesson in Columbia begins at 3:30 a.m. in India while daylight saving lasts, and at 4:30 a.m. once standard time returns. The South Carolina time never moves; only the teacher&rsquo;s does.

### Two changes a year

The IANA rule for the United States moves clocks on the first Sunday in November and the second Sunday in March. This school year that means November 1, 2026 and March 14, 2027.

### Confirmed in writing

Every class time we send is written in Eastern Time, and we resend it in the week of each clock change so that nobody misses a lesson.

**Clocks inside code** Students who build a simple class reminder learn to store times with a named zone such as America/New_York, not a fixed offset, so that the reminder survives the November change without a fix.

## Ask an AI how many parks Charleston has, and it will count rows

The tool is quick. The judgement about what counts as one park is still the student&rsquo;s.

Hand a chatbot the Charleston file and ask for the number of parks, and the likely reply is 132. Ask it to remove duplicates and it may delete one FAC-848 row without mentioning that the two rows disagree about a restroom and a dog park. The answer looks tidy and hides a choice nobody made on purpose.

Students who have done the deduplication by hand know to ask which key was used and which copy was kept. That is how we teach AI in every course: use it to save typing, never to skip the decision, and always read what it changed.

South Carolina already gives every student a unit of computer science; the habit of checking a result is what turns that unit into a skill. The longer argument is in [why learning to code still matters in 2026](/blog/is-coding-worth-learning-2026).

## South Carolina coding and AI questions

### Is computer science a graduation requirement in South Carolina?

Yes. State Board of Education Regulation 43-234 lists computer science 1.0 among the twenty-four units of credit required for a South Carolina high school diploma.

### Does keyboarding count toward the computer science unit?

Partly. The regulation says a student who earned the one-half credit in Keyboarding by the 2017 to 2018 school year is awarded one-half unit of credit for Computer Science.

### How many hours is one unit of credit?

Regulation 43-234 allows one unit for an academic standards-based course of at least 120 hours of instruction, and one-half unit for at least 60 hours.

### How many South Carolina high schools offer computer science?

Code.org (2025) reports 276 of 300 South Carolina high schools, or 92%, offering computer science in 2024 to 2025.

### How many students take computer science in South Carolina?

The same data reports 61,733 of 240,463 high school students, or 25.67%, enrolled in 2023 to 2024, against 6.4% nationally.

### Do South Carolina high schools have to offer AP?

Regulation 43-234 says schools whose structure includes grades eleven and twelve must offer Advanced Placement courses. The College Board lists 380 schools with AP exams in 2024.

### Can a class with you count as the computer science unit?

We make no such claim. Units are awarded by schools and districts under the regulation. Our classes add depth and projects alongside the school course.

### What does the Charleston parks project teach?

Deduplication. Students find that 132 rows describe 131 park sites, compare the two rows for one site, choose a rule for which to keep and recount, labelling every result as their own.

Eastern Time. We confirm every time in Eastern and resend it around the changes on November 1, 2026 and March 14, 2027.
