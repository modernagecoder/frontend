Census 2025

How many people live in the Ica region?

INEI's 2025 census put the department of Ica at 1,033,084 people. They live in five provinces, and the regional capital is the city of Ica, in the province of the same name.

Cities and towns, as last counted

City-level figures from the 2025 census are not out yet, so the most recent ones are from 2017. In that census INEI counted 282,407 people in the city of Ica, treated as a single urban area that spreads over several districts.

For other towns INEI's 2017 directory lists one populated centre at a time: Chincha Alta 66,349, Pueblo Nuevo 62,604, Pisco 61,518 and Nasca 22,859. Chincha Alta and Pueblo Nuevo are next to each other, but they are separate entries, and we keep them that way rather than inventing a combined figure.

Children and teenagers in the count

Of the 1,016,682 people the 2025 census actually counted in Ica, 105,823 were aged 6 to 11 and 107,343 were aged 12 to 17. Those are roughly the primaria and secundaria years.

Whether a child lives in Ica city or in a village in Palpa makes no difference to us. Every learner opens the same live class over video, at the hour the family has chosen.

School in Ica

Do colegios in the Ica region teach programming?

No colegio in Peru has to. The national curriculum, the CNEB, asks every area to build digital skills, but there is no programming or computing subject on the regular secundaria timetable, and Ica follows the same curriculum as the rest of the country.

What the national curriculum says

Law 28044 gives Peruvian children six primaria years followed by five secundaria years, and MINEDU places a child in a grade by the age they have on 31 March. In the CNEB, the only technology competency is number 28, which asks pupils to get along in ICT-built virtual spaces. Being transversal, it belongs to every area at once and to none in particular.

Under the regular school day, secundaria pupils have 35 hours a week: Matemática takes four of them and work education two, while programming takes none. A teenager in Pisco who wants to write real code has to find it outside the timetable.

What the DRE Ica has added in 2026

On 20 April 2026 the DRE Ica announced it had created a programme for pupils with high abilities by Resolución Directoral N.° 2056-2026. Its own note says 40 pupils were picked for the first stage and that Ica became the fourth region in the country to run this kind of teaching.

The DRE Ica also reported in March 2026 that 23 schools in the region were among the winners of the national school innovation contest run by FONDEP, with Ica, Pisco and Chincha provinces taking part most. Those are projects led by teachers inside schools, not programming lessons for every pupil.

MINEDU did launch a Python scheme, Fábrica de Programadores, in July 2025, but its first intake was 1,000 pupils of 4° and 5° de secundaria from Lima Norte, with the PCEP exam open to graduates. Ica had no share in it. Nationally, the ministry reported 6,225,000 pupils in 53,631 schools on the first day of the 2026 year, 16 March; the DRE Ica said in June 2026 that the UGEL of Ica province alone looks after more than 128 thousand pupils. Families who want programming for any of them are looking at classes outside school, such as ours.

What the numbers say

In 2025 about 69 Ica homes in every hundred had an internet service, against 57 in Peru as a whole, and about 89 in every hundred people aged six and over used the internet somewhere. Home internet in Ica rose by more than five points between 2022 and 2025.

Computers are the one measure that did not grow: 36.9 per cent of Ica homes had one in 2025, slightly fewer than in 2022, although still above the national 33.9. So the connection is usually there, and the question in an Ica home is more often "which computer?" than "which network?".

What we ask families to check

Any laptop or desktop bought in the last several years runs our classes. Learners from about nine upwards need a keyboard, because typing, running and fixing code is the lesson. Younger children doing block coding can manage on a tablet.

In the free first class the mentor looks at the picture, the sound and whether the learner can share a screen. If something is missing, we say so before any payment, and suggest what to borrow or adjust.

The language

Are the classes in Spanish or in English?

English, in every class. Our mentors are based in India, teach live over video, slow down for learners who are still building their English, and never switch the lesson into Spanish.

English in Ica, measured

EF's English Proficiency Index for 2025 ranks Peru 52nd of 123 countries with 519 points, in its moderate band. EF also lists the Ica region (spelled Ika on its page) at 468 and the city of Ica at 467, both in the low band and below Peru's overall score.

For us that means many Ica children will be learning English while they learn to code, and the mentor plans each lesson with that in mind.

How it works in practice

The mentor names each idea with the same English word every time, shows it in code straight away, and checks understanding with a question rather than "OK?". A child who answers in Spanish gets the English phrase back, kindly, and the class moves on.

Code itself helps. Words like print, input, for, while and return are English, and children pick them up as tools before they think of them as vocabulary.

Families who want more support at the start can choose one-to-one classes, where the mentor can repeat as often as needed. Families who already use English at home or at a bilingual colegio usually notice no difference at all. An Ica data project

Where do the animals exported from Ica go?

SENASA, Peru's agricultural health service, publishes a register of animal export certificates handled in the Ica region as open data. It looks like a simple counting job. It is really a lesson in checking that every row of a file has the shape its header promises.

The file

The dataset is "Movimiento de Exportación de Animales en la Región de Ica - Resumen 2023-2026", published on datosabiertos.gob.pe under SENASA with an Open Data Commons Attribution licence (ODC-By), which allows reuse, including by a company, as long as the source is credited. It was created on 3 July 2026 and last changed on 2 November 2026.

We saved the CSV on 9 November 2026 as exportacion_animal_ica.csv. It holds 234 rows from January 2023 to July 2026. Most rows are dogs (127) and reptiles (66), followed by cats, wild felines and a few lots of frozen turkey meat. Each row has 26 named columns, including the product, the inspection date, the exit point and the destination country.

The question

Which countries receive the most animal exports certified in Ica? SENASA's own data dictionary describes the column Pais destino as "País en donde desembarca el producto", the country where the product is landed. So the first program simply counts that column.

We count rows, not animals, because one row can be one dog in a kennel or a box of many reptiles. That choice is ours and we say so before printing any result.

None of the top three is a country. The first is a SENASA air control post in Callao, the second is Ica itself and the third is a border crossing in Tacna. The program did exactly what it was told; the file did not keep its promise. Opening a few rows by hand shows why: in most rows every value after the packaging columns sits two places to the right of its label, so the column called Pais destino actually holds the exit point, and the real country has drifted into the column called Estado.

Version 2 tests each row's shape before trusting it. The Estado column should only ever hold ACTIVO or INACTIVO, which makes it a good landmark. If the landmark is where the header says, the row is read normally. If it turns up two places further right, the row is read with that shift. If it is in neither place, the row is counted as unexplained and left out of the answer.

Only 33 of the 234 rows match their header; 200 are shifted by two places, and one fits neither shape. Read correctly, El Salvador comes first with 66 rows, and a quick check shows that every one of those 66 rows is a reptile shipment. Colombia, Chile and Argentina follow. A learner who stopped at Version 1 would have reported a Callao office as the favourite destination of Ica's animals, with total confidence.

  1. Under 8, with cards. Write five animals and five countries on cards, then shuffle one row of country cards two places along. Ask: which dog is now going to which country? Why is that wrong?
  2. Block coding, around 8 to 10. Build two lists in Scratch, animals and countries, and a loop that pairs them. Then move one list by two and watch the pairs go wrong.
  3. First Python, around 10 to 12. Run Version 1, then print one full row with its header next to it and point to the exact column where the shift begins.
  4. Ages 13 and over. Find the single unexplained row and decide, in writing, whether it should be repaired, left out or reported to SENASA. Then merge FELINO SILVESTRE and FELINOS SILVESTRES and count again.
  5. Going further. The file number column, N° de expediente, holds only nine different values, such as 2.60E+11. Explain how a twelve-digit number becomes that, why you cannot count shipments with it, and why identifiers should be read as text.

Able pupils

What can a highly able pupil in Ica do beyond school?

Ica's new talent programme reaches 40 pupils in its first stage, so most able children in the region will still be looking for stretch elsewhere. Contests and serious coding are two of the best-known routes, and both start with the colegio.

Maths and computing olympiads

The ONEM, Peru's national school maths olympiad, held its first stage in every colegio on Wednesday 8 July 2026, at three levels: 1° and 2° de secundaria, 3° and 4°, and 5°. El Peruano reported that the 2026 edition drew more than 1.7 million pupils. Entry is always through the school.

Modern Age Coders has no connection with MINEDU, the ONEM, the DRE Ica or the talent programme. We never reuse contest problems. We train the habits contests reward with problems we write ourselves.

A practice problem of ours

A table has 26 columns. In some rows, every value after column 14 has moved two places to the right, and the last two columns overflow. If a row is shifted, which column holds the value that belongs in column 22, and how many values in that row are now under the wrong heading? (Ours. Answer: column 24, and twelve values, from column 15 to column 26.)

Problems like this sit between maths and programming. They reward counting carefully, checking the edge cases and writing the reason down.

What time would classes be in Ica?

At the time your family chooses. Ica keeps Lima's clock, Peru has no summer time, and the mentor in India arranges the day around your slot.

Clock facts: in the tz database, America/Lima is five hours behind UTC and has not used summer time since 1994; INACAL is responsible for Peru's official time. India is five and a half hours ahead of UTC all year, so India is always ten and a half hours ahead of Ica.

Since neither country moves its clocks, a class time agreed in March still works in September. When you book, you give the time on your own clock and the system shows the mentor the India equivalent. Plenty of Ica families start in the summer break, between January and March, and simply keep the same slot when colegio begins again.

Questions from Ica

Coding and AI classes in the Ica region: questions and answers

Are the classes in Spanish?

No. Every class is live, online and taught in English only. Mentors speak slowly and clearly, match the learner's pace and accent, and when a child answers in Spanish they give the English words back and carry on.

Can my child join from Chincha, Pisco, Nasca or Palpa?

Yes. Classes are held over live video, so any province of Ica works the same way. A child in Nasca and a child in Ica city join the same kind of class, with the same mentor quality.

At what age can a child start coding?

From six, with block coding and puzzles that need no reading of code. Most children are ready to type Python at around nine or ten, and the free first class helps us judge that.

When are the classes?

Any day, any time, flexible: choose a batch, or for a very personalised plan choose 1-on-1 at the hour that suits your family. India is ten and a half hours ahead of Ica, so a class at 09:30 in Ica is at 20:00 for the mentor.

Do schools in Ica teach programming?

Not as a subject. Peru's national curriculum spreads digital skills across all areas and gives programming no hours on the regular secundaria timetable. The DRE Ica's 2026 talent programme serves 40 pupils in its first stage.

Is our home internet good enough?

In most Ica homes it is: INEI found that 69.1 per cent of Ica homes had an internet service in 2025. A laptop or desktop matters more than speed for learners over nine, and the free class checks both.

Do you prepare students for the ONEM?

We build the reasoning olympiads reward, using problems we write, never official papers. Students enter the ONEM through their colegio. Modern Age Coders is not connected with MINEDU, the ONEM or the DRE Ica.

Can adults in Ica take classes?

Yes, in adult groups or one to one. People working in agriculture, exports, tourism or offices often begin with Python and move on to automation or data analysis.

How are fees paid?

Monthly, by card, in US dollars, and your bank converts the charge into soles. Visitors browsing from India see rupees instead. The first class is free and asks for no card.

Will my child receive a certificate?

Where the course includes one, Modern Age Coders issues its own certificate of completion. It is not a Peruvian school qualification and is not issued or endorsed by MINEDU, the DRE Ica or any colegio.
