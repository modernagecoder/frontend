Q3. What does a numerical look like in class?

A Unit 3 evaluation numerical, worked in full

Unit 3, Evaluating Models, carries 10 theory marks, and confusion matrix numericals are its most predictable questions. This one is ours, set on a Pune monsoon, with every figure checked in Python before it went on the page.

Question (4 marks)

A model predicts each morning whether it will rain in Pune that afternoon. Over 120 days it gave: rain predicted and it rained, 30; rain predicted but it stayed dry, 10; no rain predicted but it rained, 6; no rain predicted and it stayed dry, 74. (a) Draw the confusion matrix. (b) Find accuracy, precision, recall and F1 score. (c) A school uses the forecast to decide whether to hold sports day outside. Which matters more, precision or recall? Give a reason.

Actually rainedActually dry
Predicted rainTP = 30FP = 10
Predicted no rainFN = 6TN = 74

  1. Accuracy

    (TP + TN) / total = (30 + 74) / 120 = 104 / 120 = 0.87 (0.8667). The model is wrong on 16 days.

  2. Precision

    TP / (TP + FP) = 30 / 40 = 0.75. When it says rain, it is right three times in four.

  3. Recall

    TP / (TP + FN) = 30 / 36 = 0.83 (0.8333). Of the 36 rainy days, it caught 30.

  4. F1 score

    2 x P x R / (P + R) = 2 x 0.75 x 0.8333 / 1.5833 = 0.79 (0.7895). The plain average of P and R, 0.7917, happens to round to the same 0.79 here, but on other numbers it does not, so always use the formula.

  5. (c) Precision or recall?

    Recall. A missed rainy day (FN) means children and equipment caught in a downpour; a false alarm (FP) only moves the event indoors. A good answer names the costly error and links it to the metric.

One more line we always add in class: a lazy model that says "no rain" every day would be right on the 84 dry days, an accuracy of 0.70, with a recall of zero. That is why accuracy alone is never the whole answer. More numericals of this kind are on the confusion matrix numericals page, and the ideas behind them on the evaluating models page.

Q4. What actually happens in one live class?

Inside one 9 PM class, step by step

Every class follows the same shape, so a Pune student knows what is coming and nothing is wasted: doubts first, one idea taught, tried, marked and checked before the class ends.

  1. Open with doubts

    Questions left over from the worksheet or the school lesson come first, answered on the shared screen so the whole group benefits.

  2. Teach one thing properly

    On a Monday it might be precision and recall; on a Wednesday, a NumPy program or an Orange workflow. One idea, with examples, rather than a whole chapter rushed.

  3. Everyone attempts it

    Students write the answer or the code themselves, in their own notebook or Jupyter, under a short time limit. Nobody copies from the teacher's screen.

  4. Mark it where everyone can see

    Two or three attempts are shared and marked against value points. Students see exactly which line earned a mark and which did not, then fix their own.

  5. Close with one exit question

    A single quick question that shows whether the idea landed. If many miss it, the next class starts there. The worksheet for the week follows; there are more on the worksheets page.

Q5. How are the project and viva handled?

The 417 project and its viva: 15 marks your school awards

The project carries 10 marks and the project viva 5, and the school assesses both, usually close to the practical exam. We help the student choose a project that is small enough to finish and their own enough to explain.

Wet or dry waste, from photos

The student photographs household waste, labels each picture wet or dry and trains an image classifier in Orange. Clear data, a real local use, and an easy viva story about why some photos fool the model.

Will it rain this afternoon?

The student keeps a simple log of morning conditions and whether it rained, then reasons about the model using the confusion matrix above. Good for a student who enjoys the Unit 3 numericals.

How did people feel about it?

The student writes or collects short reviews of a school event and runs sentiment analysis, linking the project to Unit 6. Ideal for a student who prefers text to images.

  1. Pick early, in class

    The idea is fixed in the first weeks so it can grow alongside the units, one project-cycle stage at a time.

  2. Write it up as you go

    Each stage, from problem scoping to evaluation, is recorded the week it is done, so there is no write-up panic at the end.

  3. Explain it out loud, twice

    Each student presents their project to the class and answers follow-up questions, then does it again a fortnight later. More ideas are on the project ideas page, and the oral questions on the viva questions page.

Is there class 10 AI tuition in Pune?

Yes, online. Modern Age Coders has no premises in Pune; its CBSE Class 10 Artificial Intelligence (417) classes are taught live by video. How do I choose a 417 tutor in Pune?

Ask which units are in the theory paper, how the practical file is taught, whether Orange is used as well as Python, how written answers are marked, how the viva is prepared and whether you can watch a class first. Clear answers to all six show the tutor knows this particular paper.

Which CBSE regional office covers Pune schools?

CBSE's Pune regional office, in Kharadi, covers Maharashtra, Goa, Daman and Diu, and Dadra and Nagar Haveli, according to the regional offices page on cbse.gov.in. Schools handle it for registration, admit cards and exam centres, so families seldom need to contact it.

My child is on the Maharashtra state board. Can they take AI 417?

No. Subject 417 is offered only at CBSE-affiliated schools, so SSC students do not sit it. These classes are for CBSE students. If your child is moving to a CBSE school before Class 9, 417 may become available there, since the choice of skill subject is usually made at the start of Class 9.

Are Unit 3 numericals covered?

Yes. Evaluating Models carries 10 theory marks, and accuracy, precision, recall and F1 from a confusion matrix are practised under time and marked on screen, with the reasoning about which metric matters in each situation. A student who can only plug numbers into formulas usually loses the last mark of a case question.

Does the tuition help with the school project?

Yes. The project is worth 10 marks and its viva 5. The idea is chosen early, built one project-cycle stage at a time in Wednesday classes, written up as it goes, and presented aloud at least twice before the school assesses it.

For many students it is the first free hour after school, travel and other tuition. If your child is too tired by then, or has commitments on those nights, one to one can be scheduled earlier or at the weekend.
