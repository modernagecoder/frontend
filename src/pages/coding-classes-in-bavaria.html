Coding classes in Bavaria

In Bavaria the school a child attends decides how much computing they meet: a Mittelschule pupil has Informatik every year from grade 5, a Realschule pupil has a whole subject called Informationstechnologie, and at a Gymnasium it turns on the branch until grade 11. This page lays that out, gives the 2027 Abitur dates from the ministry's own notice, lists the holidays (Bavaria's autumn break is this very week), explains our live classes in English, and ends with a Munich data project that starts by finding the wrong answer.

Informatik at Bavarian schools

How much Informatik does each school type teach in Bavaria?

Three school types, three different answers. The Gesellschaft für Informatik summarises the Bavarian school ordinances in its Informatik-Monitor 2026/27; this table follows it.

Computing in Bavarian secondary schools, by school type
School typeGradesComputing on the timetable
Mittelschule5 to 10Informatik, compulsory, one lesson a week in every grade
Mittelschule7 to 10Optional: Informatik und digitales Gestalten, two lessons a week
Realschule5 to 10Informationstechnologie, compulsory; 7 to 11 weekly hours in total, by subject group
Gymnasium, all branches6 and 7One lesson a week of Informatik inside Natur und Technik
Gymnasium, NTG branch9 and 10Informatik, compulsory, two lessons a week
Gymnasium, all branches11Two compulsory lessons a week of an informatics subject

Source: Gesellschaft für Informatik, Informatik-Monitor 2026/27, Bayern page, read 2 November 2026, compiled from the Mittelschulordnung, Realschulordnung and Gymnasialschulordnung. At the Realschule the total depends on the Wahlpflichtfächergruppe: eleven hours in group I, eight in group II, seven in IIIa and nine in IIIb, plus four optional profile hours. At the economics branch (WWG) the subject in grades 9 and 10 is Wirtschaftsinformatik.

The Gymnasium branch matters

A Bavarian Gymnasium is organised in branches, called Ausbildungsrichtungen: humanistic, languages, science and technology (NTG), music, economics and social sciences. Only the NTG branch has compulsory Informatik in grades 9 and 10, so two pupils at the same school can leave grade 10 with very different amounts of programming behind them.

Grade 11 evens things out a little. The monitor records that in grade 11 every branch has two compulsory hours of an informatics subject: Informatik at the NTG, Wirtschaftsinformatik at the economics branch and spät beginnende Informatik, late-start Informatik, everywhere else.

Back on nine years

Bavaria has returned to the nine-year Gymnasium. The ministry's Gymnasium page says the reintroduced G9 covers grades 5 to 13 in 2025/26, and the monitor describes grade 11 as the Einführungsphase and grades 12 and 13 as the Qualifikationsphase.

In the Qualifikationsphase an NTG pupil can continue Informatik with three hours a week at basic level or five hours at raised level; the late-start course continues at three hours at basic level. The monitor gives 11 % as the share of Bavarian upper secondary pupils taking Informatik in 2024/25, from KMK figures.

Two more numbers set the scene. Bavaria is home to 13,248,928 people, 1,505,005 of them in Munich, the Land capital (Destatis, 31 December 2024). For 2025/26 the monitor reports 1,248 qualified Informatik teachers at Bavarian Realschulen and Gymnasien, 3 % of all their teachers, with 75 newly hired in the year. KMK's school overview also explains a name that confuses newcomers: Mittelschule is simply Bavaria's word for the Hauptschule.

The Abitur in 2027

When is the Informatik Abitur in Bavaria in 2027, and what is allowed?

The Staatsministerium announced the 2027 dates in the Bayerisches Ministerialblatt in September 2025. The exam format comes from the Informatik-Monitor.

Abitur 2027 at Bavarian Gymnasien: written exams and the colloquium
DateWhat is written
21 April 2027All raised-level subjects except German, maths, English, French, biology, chemistry and physics
27 April 2027German
30 April 2027English
3 May 2027French
5 May 2027Mathematics
7 May 2027All basic-level subjects except English, French, biology, chemistry and physics
11 to 13 May 2027Biology, physics and chemistry
31 May to 4 June and 7 to 11 June 2027Colloquium exams (two exam weeks)

Source: Bayerisches Staatsministerium für Unterricht und Kultus, Abiturprüfung 2027, notice of 18 August 2025, BayMBl. 2025 Nr. 354, read 3 November 2026. Informatik is in neither exception list, so by the notice's own rule a written Informatik exam falls on 21 April 2027 at raised level and on 7 May 2027 at basic level; that reading is ours, and your school confirms the timetable. Additional oral exams must be finished by 18 June 2027.

Five subjects, three written

The ministry's Gymnasium page sums up the format in one line: the Abitur consists of three written and two oral exams. In the colloquium a pupil is examined in conversation, which is where explaining an algorithm aloud, in clear German, matters.

The monitor adds that Informatik at raised level is examined in writing or orally, that it cannot replace the required foreign language or science among the exam subjects, and that Wirtschaftsinformatik and late-start Informatik can be taken as oral exams.

Programming on paper

The written Informatik exam is set centrally and, in the monitor's words, large parts of it are programming tasks on paper with no practical work at a computer. Permitted aids are an approved formula collection, the ministry's document of mathematical formulas, an approved scientific calculator and a current spelling dictionary.

For a teenager used to an editor that underlines mistakes, that is a different skill. We practise it directly: a task, a timer, a sheet of paper, then a line-by-line review. The school course and teachers remain what counts for the Abitur; we do not set or mark school work.

Holidays

When are the Bavarian school holidays in 2026/27?

Bavaria starts its summer last of all the Länder in 2027, and its autumn holiday falls in the first week of November. Lessons with us carry on through the holidays unless a family pauses them.

Two dates are worth circling. The Easter holiday ends on 2 April 2027, less than three weeks before the first written Abitur day on 21 April 2027, so Oberstufe pupils often want extra paper practice in March. And the summer break that runs until 13 September 2027 is long enough for a beginner to finish a whole short course, such as Scratch, before grade 5 starts.

A Munich data project

Which transit stop is furthest north? A project that begins with a wrong answer

The Landeshauptstadt München publishes the full stop list of the regional transport association MVV as open data. With it our learners practise the check that saves every mapping project: make sure you know which column is which before you calculate anything.

The data

The file MVV_Haltestellen_Report_s26.csv lists 11,061 stops with a number, a name, a place (Ort), a global ID and two coordinate columns labelled WGS84 X and WGS84 Y. It is published under CC BY 4.0, which permits reuse with credit to the Münchner Verkehrs- und Tarifverbund GmbH (MVV). We downloaded it on 3 November 2026.

In maths class, x runs left to right and y runs bottom to top. On a map that would make X the east-west position (longitude) and Y the north-south position (latitude). That is the assumption most beginners make, and it is the one Version 1 makes.

The question

Which stop in the file is furthest north? It sounds like one line of code: take the largest y. A pupil who writes that line will get an answer, print it proudly, and be wrong.

The point of the project is not the stop. It is the habit of checking a result against something you already know before you believe it.

Passau sits in the far east of Bavaria, on the Austrian border, so "northernmost" is already suspicious. The number gives it away: 13.45 is a perfectly good longitude for Passau and an impossible latitude for anywhere in Bavaria, which lies between roughly 47 and 50.5 degrees north. Version 2 checks one stop everyone knows, Marienplatz in the centre of Munich, at about 48.14 degrees north and 11.58 degrees east.

In this file, X holds the latitude and Y the longitude: the labels are the other way round from school maths. With that fixed, the northernmost stop is Nürnberg Hbf and the southernmost is Seefeld in Tirol, across the border in Austria, both reached by regional trains in the association's list. Version 1 also counted 888 stops with no coordinates at all, which a careful report mentions instead of hiding.

Version 3 asks a second question with a second trap: which five stops are closest to Marienplatz? Measuring straight in degrees treats a step east as long as a step north. At Munich's latitude it is not: a degree of longitude covers only about two thirds of the distance of a degree of latitude, because the lines of longitude meet at the poles. Multiplying by the cosine of the latitude fixes it.

Data: Haltestellenliste MVV, published by the Landeshauptstadt München, licence CC BY 4.0, credit Münchner Verkehrs- und Tarifverbund GmbH (MVV), downloaded 3 November 2026. All counts and rankings are our own calculations. The 111.2 km per degree is the usual round figure for one degree of latitude; good enough for ranking stops in a city, not for surveying.

  1. Ages 8 to 10, no screen. Draw a grid on squared paper, mark your home and school, and write each as (across, up). Then swap the numbers by mistake and see where you land.
  2. Ages 10 to 12, Scratch. Move a sprite to x and y values you type in, then swap them and watch it jump. Which way is north on the stage?
  3. Ages 13 to 16, Python. Run Version 1, explain in one sentence why its answer cannot be right, then write Version 2 before you read ours.
  4. Going further. Version 3 changes only the fifth place. Find the stop where the two methods disagree most, and explain the disagreement with a sketch.

Contests

Which computing contests can a pupil in Bavaria enter this year?

The national ones, mostly through school.

The Informatik-Biber, run by Bundesweite Informatikwettbewerbe for grades 3 to 13, takes place from 9 to 20 November 2026; Bavarian pupils return from their autumn break just in time, and the school registers them. The 45th Bundeswettbewerb Informatik has its first round open from 1 September to 23 November 2026 and is open to young people up to 21. We describe what these contests test and train with original problems we write ourselves; we never reproduce an organiser's tasks or solutions and are not connected to BWINF.

Coding classes in Bavaria: questions and answers

Are the classes in German?

No. We only teach in English, live, with mentors based in India who adapt to each learner's pace and accent. When a school term in German comes up, the mentor gives the English word next to it.

Is Informatik compulsory in Bavaria?

It depends on the school type. The Informatik-Monitor 2026/27 records compulsory Informatik one lesson a week in grades 5 to 10 at the Mittelschule, the compulsory subject Informationstechnologie at the Realschule, and at the Gymnasium an Informatik focus in grades 6 and 7 plus two compulsory hours in grade 11.

My child is at a Gymnasium but not in the NTG branch. Will they learn programming?

Some. They get one weekly lesson with an Informatik focus in grades 6 and 7 and a two-hour late-start Informatik course in grade 11, according to the Informatik-Monitor. Compulsory Informatik in grades 9 and 10 is only at the NTG branch.

When is the written Informatik Abitur in 2027?

The ministry's notice in the Bayerisches Ministerialblatt sets 21 April 2027 for raised-level subjects not listed separately and 7 May 2027 for basic-level ones. Informatik is not listed separately, so by that rule its written exam falls on those days. Your school confirms the timetable.

Is the Informatik Abitur done on a computer?

No. The Informatik-Monitor says the written tasks are set centrally and large parts are programming on paper, with no practical work at a computer.

Can your lessons replace the Abitur course at school?

No, and they are not meant to. Our mentors add programming practice and handwritten tasks in the exam style next to the school course. Only the school's own course and exams count towards the Abitur, and we never give school grades.

When are lessons in Bavarian time?

Weekdays or weekends, flexible: choose your batch, or for a very personalised plan choose 1-on-1 at your convenience, weekend mornings or weekdays. A lot of Bavarian pupils pick a slot between mid-afternoon and early evening.

Do lessons stop during the Bavarian holidays?

Not unless you want them to. Many families keep going through the autumn break of 2 to 6 November 2026 and pause for part of the summer, which runs from 2 August to 13 September 2027.

How is the fee charged?

The page shows a monthly fee in euro, based on the European Central Bank reference rate. Your card is charged in US dollars and your bank converts it, so small differences on the statement are normal. The first lesson is free.

Are you part of a Bavarian school or the ministry?

No. We are an independent online school and have no connection with the Bavarian Staatsministerium, the KMK, BWINF, the MVV or any school.
