Why the date is less useful than it looks

When the date sheet arrives, there are usually only two or three months left before the AI paper. By then a student should have finished every theory unit, closed the practical file and the project, and started full papers. The date fixes the day, but your preparation has to be planned well before it. That is why our batch works to a calendar set by the shape of the session rather than waiting for the circular.

The morning slot, and what it asks of students

Last year the AI paper started at 10:30 AM IST and ran for two hours. Our Monday and Wednesday classes are at 9 PM, so in January the timed mock papers move to weekend mornings, written at home at 10:30 AM and reviewed live in the next class. A student who has only practised at night writes a slower first paper in the morning, and the mocks remove that surprise early.

Reading the choices like a strategist

Add up what is printed: 30 objective marks for 24 counted, 10 Part A written marks for 6, 12 short-answer marks for 8 and 20 long-answer marks for 12. That is 72 marks of questions for a 50-mark paper. The choice is generous, but it only helps a student who knows in advance which questions they will leave out. Our mock reviews end with a short decision list for each student, for example "Computer Vision has no long answer, so my four-mark choices come from Units 1, 2, 3 and 6".

Unit 2, Advanced Concepts of Modeling, is the only unit with two long answers and two short ones, which is why it gets the most Monday classes. The blueprint also notes that perceptron questions are asked only in the objective format, so the weighted-sum calculation is drilled as a quick one-mark problem rather than as an essay.

The instructions on the cover

  • 21 questions, answer 15. Five objective questions in Section A, sixteen written ones in Section B, of which ten are answered.
  • Attempt each section in order. The paper asks for questions of a section to be attempted in the correct order, so scattered answers cost time and invite mistakes.
  • No negative marking in the objective section, so no question there should be left blank.
  • Word limits: 20 to 30 words for two-mark answers and 50 to 80 for four-mark answers. Longer answers earn nothing extra and eat the clock.
  • Two hours. About 50 minutes for Section A leaves 70 for the written answers, the split our mocks train.

Q5. Which exam rules catch families out?

Four rules worth knowing before February

417 cannot sit beside 165

CBSE's scheme of studies does not allow Artificial Intelligence (417) to be offered together with Computer Applications (165). Students who moved schools mid-way, or whose school offers both, sometimes find the wrong one on their record. Settle it in the LOC window, not in February.

The second exam is for improvement

The May sitting lets students improve their performance in up to three of Science, Mathematics, Social Science and the languages. Artificial Intelligence did appear on the May 2026 sheet, but CBSE's notice does not spell out the rules for skill subjects, so ask your school before counting on a second attempt at 417. The safer plan is to treat February as the only chance.

The practical is half the subject

Fifty of the hundred marks are awarded in school, by your child's teacher and an examiner, on the practical file, a practical exam, a project and two vivas. A student who hands in a thin file in January has already lost marks the theory paper cannot recover. Our Wednesday classes keep the file growing a program at a time from the start of the session.

The theory paper rewards the official vocabulary

The marking scheme awards value points: named stages of the AI project cycle, the terms precision and recall, the names of the bioethics principles. Answers in everyday language that miss those words lose marks even when the idea is right. Every mock is marked against the scheme so students learn the vocabulary examiners look for.

Q6. How does the live batch prepare for that date?

Two live classes a week, with doubts cleared in every class

A calendar only helps if someone keeps to it. In our Class 10 AI batch the teacher does, every week, live on video, with the same students and the same teacher from October to the paper.

  1. Monday at 9 PM: theory in the paper's own shapes

    A Part B unit is taught for understanding, then practised as the blueprint asks it: objective questions first, then a two-mark answer inside thirty words, then a four-mark case study inside eighty. From November, ten minutes of every Monday go to Part A, so those ten marks are never left until the end.

  2. Wednesday at 9 PM: the practical half

    One program for the practical file is written in Jupyter Notebook, run, explained and documented. The project moves forward in fixed steps across the term, with a short viva rehearsal on each finished piece, so neither the file nor the project is left to a December rush.

  3. Live doubts, raised before the class moves on

    Any student can stop the class, share a screen and ask. Doubts from homework open the next class. With two classes a week a doubt never waits longer than a few days, and the teacher sees the same confusion surface in several students and fixes it for the room.

  4. From December: the sample paper, then full mocks

    Once CBSE posts the 417 sample paper for the session it is solved live, and then three full fifty-mark mocks follow in January and February, each written under time and reviewed in class. How the series runs is described on the sample paper and mock tests page.

  5. Report to parents at each milestone

    After the diagnostic, after the practical file closes and after each mock, parents get a short progress report: marks by unit, what is improving and what needs home practice. In the board year, nobody should be guessing how their child is doing.

What is the paper pattern for CBSE Class 10 AI 417?

The subject is 100 marks: a 50-mark theory paper of two hours and 50 marks of school practical. In the latest official sample paper, students answer 15 of 21 questions: Section A has 24 marks of objective questions, and Section B has 26 marks of short answers of 20 to 30 words and long answers of 50 to 80 words. Part A employability skills carries 10 of the theory marks and five AI units carry 40.

Which AI units carry the most marks?

Advanced Concepts of Modeling carries 11 theory marks and Evaluating Models 10, followed by Natural Language Processing 8, Revisiting the AI Project Cycle and Ethical Frameworks 7 and Computer Vision 4. Statistical Data and Advance Python carry no theory marks and are assessed in the practical.

Has the 417 sample paper for 2026-27 been released?

Not when we checked on 13 October 2026. CBSE posted the main-subject sample papers in late September 2026, but the 417 link still opened the 2025-26 paper. The curriculum for 2026-27 is unchanged, so the 2025-26 design is the right one to prepare for, and the new paper is solved live in our classes as soon as it is posted.

Can a student take both AI 417 and Computer Applications 165?

No. CBSE does not allow Artificial Intelligence (417) to be offered with Computer Applications (165). If your child studies AI, check with the class teacher that code 417 is on their LOC, the list of candidates the school submits, before the correction window closes on 4 November 2026.
