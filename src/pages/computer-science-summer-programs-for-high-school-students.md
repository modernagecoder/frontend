### What CS Scholars actually teaches

Carnegie Mellon describes CS Scholars as a "four-week deep dive into computer science" and a "fully funded, merit-based program": tuition, housing, meal plans and some field trips are covered. Because it is fully funded, documentation of financial need is part of every application.

The programming course is in Python and covers variables, functions, conditionals, loops, lists and dictionaries, testing and debugging. The page says plainly that "no coding experience is necessary", and that students who complete it may be invited back to the AI Scholars program the following summer.

### What COSMOS publishes about selection

COSMOS is a four-week residential program run across six University of California campuses: Davis, Irvine, Los Angeles, Merced, San Diego and Santa Cruz. Students apply to one campus.

Its home page is unusually direct: each campus can take "about 150-250 participants", "The selection is competitive." and "A typical COSMOS student has a GPA of 3.5 or above." It is open only to California high school students.

Sources: [CMU CS Scholars](https://www.cmu.edu/pre-college/academic-programs/computer-science-scholars.html), [CMU Pre-College Summer Session](https://www.cmu.edu/pre-college/academic-programs/summer-session.html), [MIT BWSI FAQ](https://bwsi.mit.edu/faq/) and [application page](https://bwsi.mit.edu/apply/), [COSMOS](https://cosmos-ucop.ucdavis.edu/), all read 17 October 2026. ## What a week of summer computer science really costs in hours

College-credit summer courses move at twice the normal pace. Carnegie Mellon is one of the few programs that publishes the hours, and the numbers are worth reading before a student commits.

**Weekly hours in CMU Pre-College Summer Session, from the program&rsquo;s FAQ, with our totals**

| Course load | Published hours per week | Our total for six weeks |
|---|---|---|
| One 9-unit course | 21 | 126 |
| One 12-unit course | 28 | 168 |
| A 9-unit and a 12-unit course (our sum) | 49 | 294 |
| Two 12-unit courses (our sum) | 56 | 336 |

### Reading the table

The FAQ says a student in a 9-unit course "should expect to spend 21 hours on average on that class per week", and 28 hours for a 12-unit course, both including class meetings. Residential students must take two courses for all six weeks.

Our arithmetic: two 12-unit courses come to 56 hours a week, more than a full-time job. That is why the program warns against summer jobs or test prep alongside it.

### What the grades mean

Summer Session courses are the same undergraduate courses CMU students take, graded A, B, C, D or R. For Pre-College students in good standing, only A and B grades stay on the permanent Carnegie Mellon transcript.

The page is also clear that admission to Summer Session "does not guarantee admittance to Carnegie Mellon University" as an undergraduate, and that "We do not offer any courses online."

For CS Scholars, the published day runs Monday to Friday from morning into the early evening, with homework and office hours after. The page also rules out any concurrent summer program, virtual or in person, so students choose one summer commitment.

## MIT Beaver Works: a summer program you qualify for by studying online first

Beaver Works Summer Institute works differently from the others. Admission depends heavily on a free online course taken in the spring, so a student&rsquo;s preparation is visible to the selectors.

### Rules families miss

The FAQ is blunt that "Our courses reset each year." A student who finished an online course last year must take it again to apply for this summer.

There is no residence hall. The FAQ says "We will not offer a residential program", and under MIT rules for minors an adult must stay with an in-person student for the month.

### Who can apply, and the cost rule

Applicants must be in a grade no higher than 11th and not below 10th, and "Students must be living in the United States to be accepted into the summer program." Anyone may take the online courses.

BWSI says the summer program is free for qualifying families and charges a fee to families above an income-and-assets threshold it states on its apply page and FAQ. Housing is never included. Read the current wording there.

Beaver Works lists 14 summer courses, among them CogWorks, Cyber Operations, Medlytics and Quantum Software. Many require the Python Core course alongside the course a student wants, which is why Python comes first in our plan below.

## Count down the 2027 deadlines in ten lines of Python

A small program that uses exactly the ideas the CS Scholars syllabus lists: variables, a dictionary, a function and a loop. It is ours, and it runs in any Python 3 install.

### Extend it as practice

Add the BWSI online course start as a second kind of date, a start rather than a deadline, and print the two kinds differently.

Then make the program warn when any deadline is under 30 days away. Our arithmetic says COSMOS is open for exactly 30 days, January 6 to February 5, so the warning would fire for the whole window.

### Why a planning script is a good first project

It solves a real problem the student has right now, so they care whether it is correct. Every bug shows up as a wrong number of days.

Written up honestly, a tool like this is also something to describe in an application essay about why the student enjoys programming.

## Our twelve-month plan toward a 2027 summer program

This is our plan for a 10th grader starting in October 2026 with little or no code. It assumes about four hours a week: two live lessons plus practice.

**October 2026 to September 2027 (our plan; the dates in bold type are the programs&rsquo; own)**

| Month | What the student does | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| October 2026 | Start Python: variables, loops, functions | Writes the deadline counter above unaided |
| November 2026 | Lists, dictionaries, debugging | Fixes three of their own bugs and explains each |
| December 2026 | Choose programs; ask two teachers for recommendations | A shortlist and two yes answers |
| January 2027 | Essays drafted; COSMOS opens January 6 | Drafts read by a teacher |
| February 2027 | CS Scholars closes February 1; BWSI online course opens February 1; COSMOS closes February 5 | Applications in, BWSI course started |
| March 2027 | CMU Summer Session basic round closes March 1; BWSI summer application | Every application submitted |
| April to June 2027 | Keep building one real project | A finished project on a public page |
| July to September 2027 | The program, or our live summer classes | One project written up for next year |

Our arithmetic: four hours a week from mid-October to February 1 is about 15 weeks, roughly 60 hours of Python before the first deadline. That is enough to write and explain small programs like the one above, which is what a "no coding experience is necessary" program still rewards in an essay.

| Stage | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| 1. Interested | Likes computers, has not written a program |
| 2. Beginner | Writes short Python programs with help |
| 3. Independent | Plans, writes and debugs a small program alone |

### For parents

A 10th grader at stage 1 in October can reach stage 3 by February with steady weekly work.
Recommendation letters come from teachers who have seen effort. Ask early in the fall.

### For students

Pick programs by the rules first, then by interest. A program you are not eligible for is not a choice.
Keep notes on what you built and what broke. Essays are easier when the stories are written down.

## Computer science summer programs for high schoolers: common questions

### When do computer science summer programs for high school students close for 2027?

Earlier than most families expect. CMU CS Scholars closes February 1, 2027, COSMOS closes February 5, 2027, and the CMU Summer Session basic round closes March 1, 2027.

### Is CMU CS Scholars free?

Carnegie Mellon describes it as fully funded: tuition, housing, meal plans and some field trips are covered. The page says documentation of financial need is required with the application.

### Do you need coding experience for CS Scholars?

No. The 2027 page says no coding experience is necessary. Applicants must be 16 by June 26, 2027, in 10th grade when applying, and a US citizen or permanent resident.

### How does MIT Beaver Works Summer Institute admission work?

Students register for a free online prerequisite course that opens on February 1, then complete a summer application in March. The FAQ says online course grades are an important part of the decision.

### Can a senior apply to these programs?

Mostly not, as published for 2027. BWSI takes students no higher than 11th grade, CS Scholars wants 10th graders, and CMU Summer Session wants sophomores or juniors. COSMOS lists students completing grades 8 to 12, so a California senior should ask COSMOS directly.

### Who can apply to COSMOS?

Only California high school students. The home page lists applications opening January 6, 2027 and closing February 5, 2027, and says a typical student has a GPA of 3.5 or above.

### How many hours a week is CMU Summer Session?

The FAQ gives about 21 hours a week for a 9-unit course and 28 for a 12-unit course, including class. Residential students take two courses, so 49 to 56 hours by our count.

### Are any of these programs online?

BWSI runs some courses virtually over Zoom. CMU Summer Session says it offers no courses online, and CS Scholars and COSMOS are residential.

### When are your classes?

Weekdays or weekends, flexible: choose your batch, or for a very personalised plan choose 1-on-1 at your convenience, weekend mornings or weekdays.
