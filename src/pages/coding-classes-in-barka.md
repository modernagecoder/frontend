Signature project

## The timeline that puts 2021 after 2025

The source dates are correct. The bug appears only after the program compares their display labels as ordinary text.

### 1. Collect the original labels

The learner records the visible publication label, source URL, event type and claim. The four core rows come from Asyad, the heritage ministry and a current Silal inspection record.

### 2. Demonstrate the wrong sort

Alphabetical ascending order places labels beginning with 11, 16 and 18 before the label beginning with 3. The interface therefore pushes 3 November 2021 below events from 2025 and 2026.

### 3. Parse and validate

The program uses a declared day-month-year format, writes an ISO value, rejects impossible dates and sorts by the parsed field. The original label remains visible for readers.

| Display label | Parsed ISO date | Source event | Text-sort risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 3 November 2021 | 2021-11-03 | Khazaen Dry Port operations announcement | The leading 3 can sort after every label starting with 1 |
| 11 August 2025 | 2025-08-11 | Secure customs corridor announcement | Month name is ignored by early character comparison |
| 16 September 2025 | 2025-09-16 | Barka Fort management agreement | A correct-looking local order can hide a wrong year order |
| 18 March 2026 | 2026-03-18 | Consumer-protection inspection at Silal | The year is compared too late in the string |

### Dates need an event type too

Sorting fixes chronology but not meaning. A publication, operating start, inspection and contract signature are different events. The schema must not flatten them into a field called project date.

## Preserve the label, store the date, expose failures

One field serves readers and another serves chronological operations.

| Stage | Rule | Error caught | Output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Capture | Keep source label and URL | Lost provenance | Auditable raw record |
| Parse | Use declared format and locale | Day-month ambiguity | Typed date or explicit failure |
| Validate | Reject impossible or incomplete values | Silent rollover and defaults | Quarantine queue |
| Sort | Order by ISO date or timestamp | Lexicographic chronology | Correct timeline |
| Display | Render for the audience | Machine format replacing readable text | Readable label backed by typed data |

The answers distinguish local sources, teaching data and service details.

Yes. Adult learners can request Python, databases, web development, data analysis, AI automation or another suitable route, subject to placement and teacher availability. A work goal can shape the project, but learners must use public or authorised data and must never upload confidential employer, customer or shipment records.

### What is the Barka date-sorting project?

The learner collects publication dates from public Barka source pages and first sorts their human-readable labels as text. The resulting timeline is wrong because day numbers and month names are not chronologically ordered strings. The repair parses each date into a real date value, preserves the original label and source, and sorts by the parsed field.

### Does the project use real Barka event dates?

Yes, the source-document dates are real and cited, including public pages about Khazaen Dry Port, Barka Fort and Silal. The learner does not invent the published dates. Any extra rows used to test invalid formats are synthetic and labelled. The timeline describes publications and announcements, not guaranteed completion dates for every project.

### Does Modern Age Coders have a Barka branch?

No Barka office or classroom is claimed. Classes are live online. Learners use a laptop or desktop, reliable internet, audio and a current browser. The teacher works in real time through explanation, coding, screen sharing and feedback. This page describes service availability for Barka, not a physical location.

### Why is alphabetical date sorting wrong?

Text sorting compares characters from left to right. In labels such as 3 November and 11 August, the first character can dominate even though the year and month decide chronology. A safe pipeline parses the input with a declared locale or format, stores an ISO date or timestamp and reports rows that cannot be parsed.

Standard private tuition is USD 150 per month for one learner with one teacher on the same usual frequency. Course, format, recurring time and availability are confirmed before payment.

Friday and Saturday options may be discussed subject to availability. School, work and transport schedules, examinations and Ramadan routines should be mentioned before confirmation.
