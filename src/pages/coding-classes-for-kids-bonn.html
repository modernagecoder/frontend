Data, maths and Informatik

Do Bonn children learn about data before they learn to code?

Yes. Data handling is part of primary maths in every Land, and NRW adds compulsory Informatik in grades 5 and 6. The pieces fit together better than most parents expect.

In primary maths

The KMK maths standards for the primary stage, adopted in 2004 and revised in 2022, organise content around five big ideas. One of them is "Daten und Zufall", data and chance: reading and making tables and charts, and thinking about likelihood.

In grade 3, all Länder run VERA-3, compulsory written comparison tests in German and maths. The maths tests cover the same five ideas, data and chance among them. VERA results are not graded, and no rankings of schools or Länder are made. The 2027 test window is 12 April to 26 May 2027.

In grades 5 and 6

Bonn children attend Grundschule for grades 1 to 4. In grades 5 and 6, NRW teaches Informatik as a compulsory subject at every general school type, with its own Kernlehrplan for grades 5 and 6, as recorded by the GI Informatik-Monitor 2026/27.

After grade 6 Informatik becomes an elective, and at the end of the road the NRW Informatik Abitur is written centrally and on paper, without a computer.

A real Bonn project

Commuter route or Sunday ride? Comparing Bonn's bike counters with a ratio

The City of Bonn counts bicycles every hour at its permanent counting points and publishes the results as open data. Children use a year of these counts to sort Bonn's routes into school-and-work routes and weekend routes, and discover why a difference can mislead where a ratio does not.

The data

The file "Fahrradmessstellen Ergebnisse Radzählungen 2023" comes from the Bundesstadt Bonn through Open.NRW. It is published under CC0, so anyone may reuse it without conditions. Our copy is from 2 November 2026.

It has 8,760 rows, one per hour of 2023, and a column for each counter: the three Rhine bridges (Kennedybrücke, Nordbrücke, Südbrücke) and paths such as the Wilhelm-Spiritus-Ufer, the Von-Sandt-Ufer and the Mc Cloy Weg.

The question

Is a route busier on weekdays or at weekends? A route used for school and work should be busier from Monday to Friday; a leisure route by the river should fill up on Saturday and Sunday.

The first program works out the average number of bikes per weekday and per weekend day for five counters, then subtracts one from the other.

The Kennedybrücke shows a huge difference, 2,835 more bikes on an average weekday. The Mc Cloy Weg shows only 292 more on a weekend day. Does that mean the Kennedybrücke is a much stronger commuter route than the Mc Cloy Weg is a leisure route? Not necessarily. The Kennedybrücke is simply far busier overall, so any difference there comes out big. Comparing raw differences between a giant and a small path is like comparing pocket money and a salary by how much each went up.

Now every counter sits on the same scale, whatever its size. Above 1, a route is busier on weekdays; below 1, at weekends. The Kennedybrücke carries 1.64 weekday bikes for every weekend bike. The Mc Cloy Weg carries 0.78: for every 100 weekend riders there, only 78 come on an ordinary weekday, so weekend use is about 28 percent higher. That makes it as clearly a weekend route as the bridge is a weekday one, which the differences in Version 1 completely hid.

  1. Ages 6 to 8, with coins. Put 6 coins in one pile and 3 in another, then 60 and 57. Which pair is "more different"? Talk about it before you count.
  2. Ages 8 to 10, in Scratch. Store weekday and weekend counts for three made-up paths and let a sprite say "weekday route" or "weekend route" by checking whether the ratio is above 1.
  3. Ages 10 to 12, in Python. Try both versions, then add the Nordbrücke and the Estermannufer to the list and check the table above.
  4. Going further. Split the year into months and work out the ratio for each one. Does the Mc Cloy Weg become even more of a weekend route in summer?

Free contest

Is there a free informatics contest a Bonn child can try?

The Informatik-Biber. It is a short online puzzle contest, costs nothing, needs no prior knowledge and takes pupils from grade 3 to grade 13. This year it runs from 9 to 20 November 2026.

The idea

The Biber is organised by Bundesweite Informatikwettbewerbe (BWINF). Its puzzles test informatics thinking, such as following rules, spotting patterns and finding efficient routes, without asking for any code.

BWINF says it fits well into a school lesson, at 30 to 40 minutes. Children can work alone or in pairs.

Practical steps

Schools register and run it during lessons, so ask the class teacher whether the school is joining between 9 and 20 November 2026. The Schnupper-Biber practice round has been online since 14 September 2026 for anyone who wants to try at home.

BWINF plans to publish results in December 2026 and to send certificates in January 2027.

We are independent of BWINF and do not copy Biber tasks. The puzzles we write for class share the same spirit as the coin task above: a small question where the first instinct is wrong and a moment of careful thought gets it right.

Bonn parents ask

Coding classes for kids in Bonn: frequently asked

Are the classes in German?

No. Classes are live and taught only in English. The mentor adapts speed, words and accent to your child and checks understanding often, so children who are still building their English can follow comfortably.

When do Bonn schools start teaching Informatik?

In grade 5. North Rhine-Westphalia teaches Informatik as a compulsory subject in grades 5 and 6 at all general school types, to a Kernlehrplan, according to the GI Informatik-Monitor 2026/27. From grade 7 it is an elective.

What does data have to do with primary maths?

Data and chance is one of the five big ideas in the KMK primary maths standards, and it is part of the VERA-3 maths tests that every Land runs in grade 3. Reading a table with code is a natural next step.

When is VERA-3 in 2027?

The IQB gives the VERA-3 window for 2027 as 12 April to 26 May 2027. The results are not graded and are not used to rank schools or Länder.

When are classes in Bonn time?

Weekdays or weekends, flexible: choose your batch, or for a very personalised plan choose 1-on-1 at your convenience, weekend mornings or weekdays. In Bonn, after-school slots and Sunday mornings are popular.

Where are the mentors based?

In India. They teach live over video, and a class at 16:50 in Bonn is at 21:20 for the mentor in winter and 20:20 in summer.

Can my child do the Informatik-Biber?

If the school takes part, yes. The Biber is free, open from grade 3 and needs no coding. The 2026 contest weeks are 9 to 20 November. We have no link with BWINF.

What is the bridge project?

An original Python project on the City of Bonn bike counters for 2023. Children compare weekday and weekend cycling, see that raw differences favour the busiest counter, and use a ratio to compare all routes fairly.

How large are groups?

Fifteen to twenty children of similar age, or one-to-one lessons if you prefer.

How do fees and payment work?

Fees are monthly, shown in euro at the European Central Bank reference rate and charged in US dollars, which your bank converts. The trial class is free.
