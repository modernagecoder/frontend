### Individual pace inside a group

Its group classes are described as a consistent weekly class where every child follows an individual learning path with the energy of a small peer group. Children advance as they show skills, and graduation exams mark the move up a level.

That combination, one group but personal pace, is a distinctive design worth asking about in a trial.

### What families should ask

How many children are in the group your child would join, and at how many different levels? How long do students typically stay on one level?

Coding with Kids&rsquo; FAQ covers these questions; the answers depend on the child, which is why its welcome step comes before the first class.

## When children start using AI: a published rule

Few schools publish this. Coding with Kids states that AI supports the work while students own the thinking, and introduces it in stages.

**AI on the Coder&rsquo;s Ladder (Coding with Kids llms.txt, last updated August 12, 2026) and our practice**

| Stage | Coding with Kids | Modern Age Coders (our practice) |
|---|---|---|
| Blocks | Levels 1 to 2: AI tools are not introduced yet | Younger children learn what AI is, without accounts on AI tools |
| First typed code | Level 3: AI can explain errors and help debug | A teacher explains errors first; AI only where the tool allows the age |
| Applications | Levels 4 to 6: AI supports debugging, testing and documentation | Teens use age-appropriate AI with disclosure in their notes |
| Advanced | Level 7 and above: disciplined software-engineering workflows | Review, testing and planning habits in project courses |

The two approaches point the same way: fundamentals first, AI later and openly. For any school, ask the same three questions: at what age do students first use an AI tool, which tools, and how is AI use recorded in their work? A school that can answer clearly has thought about it.

### Why delay AI for beginners

A child who has never fixed a bug alone cannot judge whether an AI suggestion is right. Learning to read an error message, find the line and test a fix is the skill that later makes AI help safe and useful rather than a shortcut.

Both schools treat the block stage as the place to build that habit without assistance.

### Age rules on the tools themselves

Separately from any school policy, AI products set their own minimum ages in their terms, and some are for adults only. A good class plan respects both: the school&rsquo;s stage and the tool&rsquo;s age rule.

We never place a student under 18 on a product whose terms are for adults.

## Live teaching time over four weeks

Our arithmetic from the published class lengths. Billing periods at Coding with Kids run four to eight weeks depending on the month, so four weeks is used here only as a common unit.

**Live minutes over four weeks at Coding with Kids (our calculation from its published class lengths)**

| Format | Per week | Over four weeks |
|---|---|---|
| Group class | About 75 minutes | 4 x 75 = 300 minutes, or 5 hours |
| Private class | 50 minutes | 4 x 50 = 200 minutes, about 3.3 hours |
| Office Hours for an eligible absence | 30 minutes | As needed |

### Why the private number is smaller

A private class is shorter but every minute is aimed at one child, while a 75-minute group class shares the teacher. Minutes alone do not measure attention.

That is why both columns are worth having before you compare any school, including us: ask for live minutes a month and the number of children sharing them.

### Missed classes

For group classes, Coding with Kids supports eligible absences with a 30-minute instructor-led Office Hours session. For private classes, one eligible missed class per billing period may be credited when reported at least 24 hours ahead.

Ask every school for its rule in writing; it matters more over a year than any single feature.

## Coding with Kids and alternatives: common questions

### What ages does Coding with Kids teach?

Ages 5 to 18, according to its own site read on 17 October 2026, starting with Little Coders at 5 to 7.

### Are Coding with Kids classes live?

Yes. It describes its delivery as live instruction only, not self-paced video courses, online and in person.

### How long are its classes?

Its programs page lists weekly group classes of approximately 1 hour 15 minutes and weekly private classes of 50 minutes. Camps typically run five days.

### What is the Coder's Ladder?

Its ten-level curriculum, likened to a martial arts belt system. The company says achieving Level 10 means mastering all fundamentals of software development.

### When does Coding with Kids introduce AI?

Its published guidance says AI tools are not introduced at Levels 1 to 2, can help explain errors from Level 3, support debugging, testing and documentation at Levels 4 to 6, and sit within software-engineering workflows from Level 7.

### Does Coding with Kids teach in person?

Yes, in several US regions including Denver and Boulder, Washington DC, Maryland, Portland, Austin, Virginia and parts of Washington State, according to its site menu.

### How is Modern Age Coders different?

We are online only and teach coding and maths from age 6 into adulthood, in batches, mini batches of up to six or privately, with teachers in India. Coding with Kids teaches ages 5 to 18 online and in person on its own ladder.

### How do new students join a Coding with Kids group?

Its group-class page describes a Welcome Class, then the first weekly class, a short Parent Orientation call and an individual scorecard. New students can join throughout the year, typically every four weeks.

### When are your classes?

Weekdays or weekends, flexible: choose your batch, or for a very personalised plan choose 1-on-1 at your convenience, weekend mornings or weekdays.

### Are you part of Coding with Kids?

No. The two schools are unrelated; we summarise its public pages and link to them.
