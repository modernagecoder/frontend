## What does the word Muscat cover?

Official boundaries matter because search pages often collapse the old city, the wider urban corridor and the governorate into one vague label.

The [Muscat Governorate](https://gom.gov.om/en/) places the capital on the Sea of Oman, with the Eastern Hajar Mountains and North and South Sharqiyah to the east, South Al Batinah to the west and Ad Dakhiliyah to the south. Muscat Municipality describes the governorate as about 4,000 square kilometres. The official structure contains six wilayats. Muscat is also romanised as Masqat in some English references, but this page uses Muscat consistently.

## Where did the leftover students go?

The Ministry of Education publishes school-level student counts by school, grade and sex. That makes a Muscat cohort a clean way to expose a small programming operation that can silently erase real people.

Source: Oman Ministry of Education open dataLevel: Grade 6 and upConcept: Integer division and remainder

The Ministry's [open-data catalogue](https://home.moe.gov.om/region/opendata/days-day20) lists a 2024/2025 dataset of student counts by government school, grade and sex, plus a matching school dataset by governorate. A learner filters the current files to Muscat and selects one grade cohort.

The ordinary task is to estimate how many coding groups are needed if each group holds eight students. A beginner writes `groups = students / 8`, stores the result as an integer and reports only the full groups. The output looks tidy.

The missing value is the remainder. If the cohort is not exactly divisible by eight, some students have vanished from the plan. Rounding down loses them. Rounding up produces the required group count but does not explain that the last group is smaller.

The trap

Integer division answers how many complete groups fit. It does not allocate every student. The quotient and remainder are both part of the result. A defensible report uses ceiling division for capacity, retains the remainder for the final group and verifies that allocated places are at least the cohort size.

The same bug appears in buses, hotel rooms, shipping cartons, database pagination and work rosters. It is especially dangerous when a dashboard reports only full units, because the people in the remainder disappear without an error message.

| --- | --- | --- |
