## One standard of class, from metro to small town

Good coding teachers in India have always been concentrated in a few metros. A student in Bhagalpur, Udupi or Shimla usually had two options: a crowded local institute or a recorded course nobody finishes. A live online class removes that gap. The mentor, the batch and the projects are the same wherever the student logs in from.

We also teach to the board the student actually sits. **ICSE** Classes 9 and 10 write Java in BlueJ. **CBSE** Computer Science and Informatics Practices in Classes 11 and 12 are taught in Python with SQL. State boards and IGCSE or IB schools follow their own syllabus. Tell us the board and class on the demo, and the mentor plans around it instead of a generic course.

Beyond school, the work is the same everywhere: write real code, build something that runs, and keep going. Kids start with Scratch from age 6, most move to Python around 12, and college students and working adults come for DSA, full-stack development and AI.

## Coding classes in India: FAQs

Are these coding classes in India online or offline?Live online. Every class is taught live by a mentor, and the student joins from home on a laptop or desktop. That is what lets a student in a small town get the same teacher, batch and projects as a student in Bengaluru or Delhi, with no commute and no centre timetable to fit around.Which cities and states do you teach in?All of India. We have dedicated pages for more than 130 Indian cities, 21 states and 47 neighbourhoods in Kolkata and Howrah, but the classes themselves are online, so a student anywhere in the country can join. If your city is not on the list, book a demo and we will place you in the right batch.What age can my child start coding?From age 6, on block-based tools like Scratch where the logic is visual and nothing depends on typing speed. Most children are ready for typed Python or Java around 12. Our kids track runs 6 to 12, the teens track 13 to 18, and college students and adults have their own courses, up to age 67.Do you teach the CBSE, ICSE and state board syllabus?Yes. ICSE Computer Applications in Classes 9 and 10 is taught in Java with BlueJ. CBSE Computer Science and Informatics Practices in Classes 11 and 12 are taught in Python with SQL, and ISC Computer Science in Java. We also run CBSE Class 10 maths board preparation. For state boards, IGCSE and IB, tell the mentor the syllabus on the demo and the plan follows it.How big are the batches?There are three formats. A group class has 15 to 20 students. A mini batch has up to 6 students, so each one gets much more speaking time. Personalized 1-on-1 is four private classes with your own mentor, scheduled whenever it suits you.What timings do the classes run at?Group classes and mini batches run at set times in IST, including after-school, evening and weekend slots. Before you enrol, talk to our team on call or WhatsApp so they can check whether a batch is open at the time you want, or whether the current batch has already started. If no batch time fits, 1-on-1 classes are fully flexible.How do the fees work?Every plan is billed monthly and you can cancel any time. The current fee for each format is shown in the pricing section of this page and on our pricing page, in your own currency. Start with a free demo, and pay only once you are sure it is the right class.What is the difference between a free demo and a Priority Demo?A free demo is scheduled as mentor slots open up, so it can take some time. A Priority Demo is a full live class of about 45 to 60 minutes with a mentor reserved for you, today or tomorrow, followed by a written skill report and a personal learning roadmap on WhatsApp. If you enrol, the demo fee is adjusted against your first month's fee.Will my child get a certificate?Yes, every student who completes a course receives a completion certificate. More useful than the certificate are the projects: games, websites, apps and models the student built themselves and can show in a school portfolio, a college application or an interview.
