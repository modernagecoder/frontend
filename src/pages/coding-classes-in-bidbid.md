Is the 40 towers figure real? Yes. Bidbid is described as having 40 towers scattered in different villages, and the page uses that number exactly as published. The six village counts in the project table are labelled assumptions, because no source consulted for this page gives a village count for the wilayat.
Why is an invented denominator so hard to spot? Because the finished rate carries no trace of it. Two towers per village and forty towers are printed in the same typeface and read with the same confidence. One of them can be traced to somebody who went and counted, and the other cannot, and nothing on the page tells them apart unless the writer chooses to say so.
Is there a Modern Age Coders centre in Bidbid? No. No premises are claimed in Bidbid or anywhere in Ad Dakhiliyah. Classes are live online and need a laptop or desktop, working audio, a current browser and a stable connection. The page describes where the service reaches, not a building.
What should a learner do when the denominator is missing? Report the count and say the rate is unavailable. If a rate is genuinely needed, give the range across plausible denominators and name the assumption in the sentence rather than burying it. Both options are more useful than one tidy number that cannot be checked.
Can an adult in Bidbid study rather than a school student? Yes, up to 67. Adults work in Python, analytics and reporting, and anybody who has been asked for a per-head or per-branch figure that nobody had the head count for has met this already. Their own report makes the best exercise where the data is theirs to use.
What does a learner have at the end of this project? A table showing how far one rate moves under reasonable assumptions, a function that declines to divide without a sourced denominator, and the habit of writing the assumption into the sentence. Those survive every change of tool.

Standard private tuition is USD 150 per month for one learner with one teacher on the same usual frequency. Course, format, recurring time and availability are confirmed before payment.

Private tuition is one learner with one teacher. If no suitable group is ready, a learner can discuss private tuition or wait for an appropriate cohort.

Friday and Saturday options may be discussed subject to availability. School, work and travel schedules, examinations and Ramadan routines should be mentioned before confirmation.
What happens in the free Bidbid class? The teacher finds out what the learner is trying to become able to do and sets one task at that level.
