Coding classes in County Westmeath

What are the best coding classes in County Westmeath?

Westmeath holds 96,221 people, up 8% since 2016, spread between two towns of almost identical size and a great deal of lake and farmland. Home working is low here at 26% against a national 32%, so most families need an evening class rather than an afternoon one, and few of them live near a centre that runs one. That is precisely the gap a live online class fills: the same teacher, the same hour every week, whether a learner is in Mullingar, Moate or out towards Lough Ree. We teach ages 6 to 67, in ability groups of five to ten or one to one. The first lesson costs nothing; then a group place is USD 100 a month and a private one USD 150.

Westmeath County Council publishes a list of its 49 recycling bring banks, and it is a tidy file: no duplicate rows, sensible coordinates, a column for each material. Four of the five material columns say YES on every single site. The fifth, for textiles, is more interesting. Forty-two sites say YES. Six say NO, meaning the council has confirmed that site does not take textiles. And one site, at Horseleap, says nothing at all: the cell is empty. Those last two are not the same thing, and no ordinary piece of code will tell them apart unless somebody thought about it first. Ask for the sites that take textiles and you get 42. Ask for the sites that do not and you get six, or seven, depending entirely on how your code treats an empty cell. One bring bank in the county falls into the gap between confirmed absent and never recorded, and finding it is the best half hour a young data learner here can spend.

Two towns of the same size, one of them in two counties

Mullingar, cleanly counted

Mullingar holds 22,667 people entirely within Westmeath, with an average age of 36.8 and 22.5% of its people aged under 15.

Athlone, across the Shannon

Athlone is published as "Athlone, Counties Westmeath and Roscommon" with 22,869 people, because the town spans the river and the county line. It is not a Westmeath-only figure.

The younger towns

Kinnegad, at 3,064 people, has an average age of 34.1, the youngest of any Westmeath town of at least 500 people, with Moate close behind at 3,013 people.

Where people come from is worth a line of its own. Just over half of Westmeath residents, 52% or 50,115 people, were born in the county; 29% were born elsewhere in Ireland; and 18%, some 17,565 people, were born outside the State, up from 16% in 2016. The largest non-Irish groups are Polish at 1,832, UK at 1,357, Indian at 1,034 and Lithuanian at 1,005. One small honesty note: the statistics office puts the non-Irish share at 11% in one release and 12% in another, and we quote both rather than choose.

Signature project

Where one bring bank disappears

A short, complete dataset, published by the county itself with an admirably honest disclaimer, and exactly one cell that changes the answer.

What we measured in the published file, read 16 September 2026
Value in the textiles columnSitesWhat it means

NO6The council records that this site does not take textiles
An empty cell1, at HorseleapNothing was recorded either way

Why the blank is dangerous

Most quick fixes treat it as a no, or drop the row entirely. Either choice silently converts an unknown into a fact, and the site at Horseleap either loses a service it may have or gains one it may not.

The right handling

Three categories, not two: yes, no and not recorded. Report all three, and say in the write-up how many rows fell into the third.

The disclaimer matters too

The council states plainly that it provides the information without guaranteeing that it is accurate, correct or complete. That sentence is permission to check, not a reason to dismiss the file.

What we checked and did not find

It is worth saying what this file gets right, because most of our other county pages are about things going wrong. The coordinates are consistent: the eastings and northings are in Irish Transverse Mercator metres, and they agree with the separate latitude and longitude columns, so there is no coordinate trap here at all. There are no duplicate rows. Athlone and Mullingar each appear several times, which is correct, because each town genuinely has several bring banks. One settlement name looks misspelled, rendered as Ballnacarrigy where the usual spellings run Ballinacarrigy or Ballynacargy, and we flag that rather than correct it, since the file contains no second spelling to prove a pattern against.

The output is a count of sites offering textile recycling, a count of those confirmed not to, a count of those unrecorded, and a sentence explaining why the third number exists. A learner who produces that has understood something most adults handling spreadsheets never do.

Living in the county

Two routing keys, two lakes and a long drive to anywhere

Westmeath sits in the middle of Ireland, which is convenient for crossing and awkward for getting to a class.

How people travel

Of 37,866 people travelling to work, 24,378 drove, 2,897 walked, 893 took a bus, 518 cycled and 339 went by train, with an average journey of 30.4 minutes, up from 28.8 in 2016.

Students on the road

Of 3,390 third-level students commuting, driving was the most popular option at 975 people, which tells you plenty about public transport in the midlands.

Who has moved here

3,028 people moved into the county in the year before the census, 1,748 from elsewhere in Ireland and 1,280 from outside the State, while dual Irish citizenship rose from 1,826 to 3,180. The county also recorded 974 Irish Travellers, down 3% on 2016.

Why 26% matters here

Only 26% of the Westmeath workforce works from home at least one day a week, against 32% nationally, so most households have both parents out of the house until the evening. The classes that fill first here are the later ones, and they are the ones we open first.

Coding and AI

An assistant will answer this in one line. Why should a Mullingar teenager count three ways?

Because the question has two defensible answers and a tool will give you one of them.

Ask how many bring banks in Westmeath take textiles and the answer is 42, which is correct. Ask how many do not and the honest answer is six confirmed, plus one unrecorded, and almost no automated approach will separate those. A tool asked to tidy the column will usually fill the blank with something plausible, and the resulting file will look complete while quietly containing a decision nobody made.

What a learner brings is the willingness to keep a third category and to write down what it means. That is not a technical skill so much as an honest one, and it is the difference between a result that holds up and one that merely looks finished.

So a Westmeath child learning to code in 2026 is learning to notice what is absent, which is a harder and more useful habit than noticing what is there. The longer argument is in why a child should still learn to code in 2026.

What families in the midlands ask before booking.

How many people live in County Westmeath?

Census 2022 counted 96,221, up 8% or 7,451 since 2016, with an average age of 38.6, just below the national 38.8.

Is Athlone or Mullingar the bigger town?

Athlone is published at 22,869 and Mullingar at 22,667, but Athlone's figure spans Westmeath and Roscommon because the town crosses the Shannon. Mullingar is the largest town wholly inside the county.

Which Westmeath schools first offered Leaving Certificate Computer Science?

Coláiste Mhuire in Mullingar and Moate Community School were both on the January 2018 first-phase list, which is better than several larger counties managed.

What is the Westmeath data project?

Students take the council's list of 49 recycling bring banks and work out how many offer textile recycling. The answer depends on how their code treats one empty cell, at Horseleap, which is neither a yes nor a no.

Is the dataset unreliable?

No, it is a good file: no duplicate rows and consistent coordinates. The council also states openly that it does not guarantee the information is complete, which is exactly the kind of honesty that invites checking.

Is there third level in the county?

The Technological University of the Shannon has its Athlone campus at University Road, N37 HD68. We are not connected with the university.

What hours do classes run?

Late afternoons, evenings and weekend mornings, with evening slots most popular here. Our teachers are four and a half hours ahead of Irish time in summer and five and a half in winter.

Is there a Modern Age Coders classroom in Westmeath?

What do County Westmeath coding classes cost?

The first lesson is free. Continuing costs USD 100 a month for a shared place, which brings two live lessons a week and about eight a month with five to ten learners, or USD 150 a month one to one on the same schedule. Everything is agreed before payment.

How many learners are in a group?
