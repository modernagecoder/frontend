Signature project

How big is a Houston park? A histogram from city data

The City of Houston’s GIS office (COHGIS) publishes a map layer of city parks. We downloaded its attribute table on 18 October 2026 and sorted the park sizes into bins, the way a child would in a first data project.

1. Count first

The layer held 380 park records on download day: 378 in Harris County and 2 in Fort Bend. A child checks that number before drawing anything, because a chart of the wrong rows is worse than no chart.

2. Try equal bins

With bins of 1,000 acres, almost every Houston park lands in the first bar and the chart says nothing. The largest single record, listed at 10,488.7 acres, stretches the axis so far that the small parks vanish.

3. Choose better bins

Bins that widen as they go (1, 5, 10, 25, 50, 100, 500) show the real shape: most parks are small, a few are enormous. Our median is about 4.75 acres, far below the average a big park drags upward.

The answer is ours, not the city’s

These bins and the median are our own calculation from one download. Most acreage values carry the source label GIS Value (367 records), with a handful from a survey, a parcel or a deed, and the city states that COHGIS data is for general reference and comes with no warranty. A child who learns to write that sentence under a chart has learned something most adults skip.

A second question uses the Park_Type column: 167 records are labelled Neighborhood Park, 22 Pocket Park, and 30 have no type at all. Counting the blanks before charting the rest is the habit we want. The first version of this project takes one or two classes in Python for a ten- or eleven-year-old; younger children do the same counting with sticky notes and a Scratch bar chart.

Kids and AI

Should a Houston child learn to code if AI can write programs?

Yes, and the park histogram shows why in one picture.

Ask an AI tool for a chart of Houston park sizes and it will happily draw the first, useless version with equal bins, one tall bar and a long empty tail. It will not notice that 30 parks have no type, or that the biggest record is a lease. A child who has built the chart by hand spots both in seconds.

That is the skill coding teaches young children: checking. They learn that a computer does exactly what it is told, so every result needs a reason. With that habit in place, AI tools become something a child can question later rather than something they simply believe.

We teach the checking first, with blocks and then Python, and bring in AI tools only when a child is old enough to use them under the provider’s own age rules. The longer argument is in is coding still worth learning in 2026.

What age can a Houston child start coding with you?

From age 6. The youngest start with drag-and-drop blocks and lots of talking; typed Python usually begins around age 10, depending on reading and typing.

How do I judge whether a coding class is any good?

Ask who reads your child's code, how the level is set, what your child will finish, how large the group is, and what happens when they get stuck. A strong class has a clear answer to each.

How many children are in a batch?

Fifteen to twenty children at the same level, or one to one if you prefer a teacher for your child alone.

What is the Houston parks project?

Children download the COHGIS Houston Parks layer, count its 380 records and turn the park sizes into a histogram, then explain why equal-width bins hide most of the parks.

Are the park numbers on this page official?

No. The bins and the median are our own calculation from a download on 18 October 2026. The official figures, such as over 380 developed parks, come from the Houston Parks and Recreation Department.

What do Texas schools teach about technology?

TEA says revised technology applications standards for kindergarten to grade 8 were approved in 2022 and implemented from the 2024 to 2025 school year. Our Texas page covers the high school rules.

When are classes for Houston families?

Weekdays or weekends, flexible: choose your batch, or for a very personalised plan choose 1-on-1 at your convenience, weekend mornings or weekdays. Times are confirmed in Central Time.

Will daylight saving change my child's class time?

The Houston time stays as agreed. The US switch dates are November 1, 2026 and March 14, 2027, and we confirm the Central Time slot around each change.

Do you have a center in Houston?

No. Every class is live and online, taught from India, so you only need a computer, sound and a steady connection at home.
