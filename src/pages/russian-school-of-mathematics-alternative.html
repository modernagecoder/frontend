Russian School of Mathematics alternative: compare the facts, then choose

The Russian School of Mathematics, usually called RSM or simply "Russian math", is one of the most widely recognized after-school math programs in North America. Families look for an alternative for ordinary reasons: there is no branch nearby, the schedule clashes with something else, the child is outside the grades RSM-Online serves, or they want one-to-one teaching. This page sets out what RSM says about itself, side by side with how Modern Age Coders works, and leaves the choice with you. We do not claim either is better; they are built differently.

How RSM grows

RSM says its first class was held outside Boston, Massachusetts, and that "We do not franchise", opening a new location only when an experienced teacher is available to lead it. It reports serving over 80,000 students across North America.

It also states: "A majority of RSM branches are accredited by the Western Association of Schools and Colleges."

RSM’s view of tutoring

RSM is clear that it is a classroom program. Its programs page says: "We offer tutoring only as a short-term solution to prepare our students to join their peers in the classroom."

It explains that classrooms let students verbalize and debate ideas. That is a considered position, and worth knowing if you are weighing a group against a private tutor.

Two ways

What does solving one problem two ways look like?

Most serious math programs, RSM included, talk about deep reasoning rather than drills. Here is what that means in practice, with two problems we wrote, each solved by reasoning first and by algebra second.

The age puzzle, by reasoning

A parent is 4 times as old as their child. In 20 years the parent will be twice as old as the child. How old is the child now? Our worked answer: call the child’s age one unit, so the parent is four units and the gap between them is three units.

The gap between two ages never changes. In 20 years the parent is twice the child’s age, so the gap then equals the child’s age then: one unit plus 20 equals three units. So two units are 20, one unit is 10. The child is 10 and the parent is 40.

The same puzzle, by equation

Let the child be c, so the parent is 4c. In 20 years: 4c + 20 = 2(c + 20). Expanding gives 4c + 20 = 2c + 40, so 2c = 20 and c = 10.

Check: 40 + 20 = 60 and 10 + 20 = 30, and 60 is twice 30. The reasoning route explains why the answer works; the equation route scales to harder versions.

Choosing

How should a family decide between RSM and an alternative?

A short process that works for any comparison. It takes two or three weeks and gives you real evidence rather than reputation.

  1. Write down what you want from math outside school. Catching up, going ahead, competitions, or confidence. Each program serves these differently.
  2. Check the practical limits first. Is there an RSM branch within reach, or is your child within RSM-Online’s grades 2 to 10? Do the class times fit your week?
  3. Take the free start each program offers. RSM offers a free evaluation; we offer a free first class. Watch how your child talks about each afterwards.
  4. Ask the same questions of both. Use the checklist above, and write the answers down.
  5. Decide for one term, not forever. Review after about ten weeks: is your child keen, and is the homework manageable?

Families sometimes combine programs, for example a classroom program during the school year and live online competition classes in the summer. That is a perfectly reasonable plan if the child enjoys both and the hours stay sensible.

RSM alternatives: common questions

What is a good alternative to the Russian School of Mathematics?

It depends on why you are looking. Families often want online classes, 1-on-1 teaching, different hours or grades outside RSM-Online. Modern Age Coders teaches live online in a batch of 15 to 20 or one to one; compare the criteria and try a free class.

What grades does RSM-Online teach?

RSM says RSM-Online is open to students in grades 2 to 10. Its in-person branches describe a K to 12 program.

How big are RSM classes?

RSM states that its average class size is 12, with three levels per grade. Our group batches are 15 to 20, and we also offer 1-on-1.

How many hours a week is RSM?

RSM says its online classes meet anywhere from 1.5 to 4 hours per week, depending on the grade level.

Why is it called the Russian School of Mathematics?

RSM says the name comes from its approach, which is based on elite math schools in the former Soviet Union, adapted to the US environment.

Does RSM offer private tutoring?

RSM says it offers tutoring only as a short-term solution to prepare students to join their peers in the classroom. We offer 1-on-1 as a full, ongoing option.

Is Modern Age Coders better than RSM?

We do not claim that. The two are built differently: RSM runs classroom programs with branches and an online school, while we teach live online only, with group and 1-on-1 options and coding alongside math.

How much does RSM cost?

We do not quote other providers' fees. RSM says it emails a PDF of tuition by grade on request. Our own fees are shown on this page.

When are your classes?

Weekdays or weekends, flexible: choose your batch, or for a very personalised plan choose 1-on-1 at your convenience, weekend mornings or weekdays.

Are you connected with RSM?

No. Modern Age Coders is independent and not affiliated with the Russian School of Mathematics. RSM's names are its trademarks.
