# Coding Classes in Qurum

> Qurum Natural Reserve protects about 80 hectares of mangrove wetland in central Muscat, with more than 200 recorded bird species. Modern Age Coders turns evidence like this into programming and mathematics work for learners from 6 to 67. Teaching is live online, in small groups or privately, after a free placement class.

## Qurum has unusually rich local evidence

Oman's Environment Authority describes Qurum Natural Reserve as about 80 hectares of mangrove forest dominated by *Avicennia marina*. It records more than 200 bird species, 40 crustacean species and 50 mollusc species, along with small fish and a nursery role for commercially important fish. NCSI separately publishes an annual visitor-count series for the reserve beginning in 2014.

Muscat Municipality names Qurum Natural Park and the Qurum beach walkway among public places used by families. Shatti Al Qurum also contains Royal Opera House Muscat, which its official account dates to 2011. The House of Musical Arts maintains a music research library with recordings, scores and electronic resources. Nature, public recreation, arts and schools occupy the same locality without being interchangeable datasets.

## Two schools, several curriculum languages

Qurum International Private School says it opened in 1983 as a co-educational school with about 90 students and 12 teachers. By 1998 it recorded 698 students, 61 teachers and 34 classrooms. It now describes more than 1,000 students from kindergarten through Grade 12 and names Omani national bilingual and monolingual curricula, Cambridge and Pearson Edexcel routes.

Al Sahwa says it began in Shatti Al Qurum in 1993 with one class at each level from Kindergarten 1 to Grade 11 and about 300 students. It now reports more than 1,100 students, 200 staff and 31 nationalities. The school records PYP authorisation in 2013, MYP and DP authorisation in 2020-2021, and Thanawiya Amma alongside the Diploma Programme.

These facts do not support automatic placement by school. A Cambridge learner may know Python syntax but not testing. An IB learner may frame an inquiry well but lack file-handling experience. A bilingual national-curriculum learner may reason correctly while using different English vocabulary. The free class checks the work itself.

## The Qurum paired-series project

NCSI publishes annual visitor counts for Qurum Natural Reserve. A student downloads the current series and pairs it with another official Muscat tourism indicator for matching years, such as hotel guests from the NCSI Data Portal. The source unit, geography, update date and missing years remain attached to each field.

The first output indexes both series to a common starting value, plots them and calculates a correlation. If both fall during a disruption and later rise, the two lines can look persuasive. The confident wrong claim says reserve visits caused hotel demand, or that hotel demand caused reserve visits.

Correlation does not establish either direction. Travel restrictions, tourism recovery, reserve access rules, reporting practice and broader economic changes can affect both. The student must consider reverse causality and common causes. An observational time-series chart can show association, but it cannot settle the mechanism by itself.

| Stage | Action | Audit question |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Record unit, geography and time definition for each source | Are the rows comparable? |
| 2 | Join only matching years and preserve blanks | Did missing become zero? |
| 3 | Plot raw values and indexed values | Did scale manufacture similarity? |
| 4 | Recalculate with disruption years separated | Does one period control the result? |
| 5 | List plausible common causes and reverse directions | What else moves both series? |
| 6 | Report association unless causal evidence exists | Does the conclusion match the design? |

Primary starting points: [NCSI Al Qurum Natural Reserve visitor series](https://data.gov.om/mcjdlrd/reseves?tsId=1000260) and the [NCSI Data Portal](https://data.gov.om/). Students retrieve current data themselves and keep the publication notes.
