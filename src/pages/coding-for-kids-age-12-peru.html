Coding for kids age 12 in Peru

Twelve sits on a border. In Peru it usually means the first year of secundaria, a new timetable, new teachers and, for some, a first maths olympiad. In coding it means the end of children's tools and the start of real ones. Here we write for mothers and fathers hoping to see a twelve year old to cross that border with a teacher beside them, live and in English.

Who this page is for

Who are these coding classes for twelve year olds for?

Families in Peru whose child is twelve, usually in their first year of secundaria, and who can follow a class in English or want to grow into it.

Young people who have outgrown blocks

They built Scratch games at nine or ten and now find them too simple. They want to type, to build things that look like real software, and to understand how the apps on their phone work.

Beginners starting late

Twelve is not late. Beginners of this age learn Python faster than younger children, because they read quickly, follow longer explanations and can plan ahead.

Puzzle solvers

Some twelve year olds love the maths more than the screen. Code lets them test conjectures, count cases and see why an algorithm works, which is good training for contests.

In Spanish, programación para niños de 12 años compares Python, Roblox and Godot at this age. For the years after twelve, coding classes for teens in Peru covers building, competing and certification.

What twelve year olds learn

What programming can a twelve year old learn?

The real thing, at a gentler pace. A twelve year old can learn the same ideas an adult beginner learns, as long as each idea arrives with a project that makes it worth learning.

Ideas that click at twelve

Functions. Writing a piece of code once and calling it with different inputs. Twelve year olds like the feeling of building their own tools.

Lists. Keeping many values together and working through them with a loop: scores, names, the numbers in today's lesson.

Algorithms. A precise method that always works, such as a sieve for primes or a search through a sorted list. This is where coding and mathematics meet.

Projects that hold attention

A game with levels and a saved high score. A small website about a hobby. A program that answers a maths question nobody in the class could do by hand.

Twelve year olds lose interest in exercises that lead nowhere. Every topic in our classes ends in something they can show a friend, and every project is explained aloud, in English, at the end.

Twelve is also when many young coders choose a direction: making games, building for the web, or solving contest problems. They do not have to choose for ever. A term of each is a good way to find out, and the free class is a good way to start.

Starting secundaria

What changes when a child in Peru starts secundaria?

A five-year stretch begins, with a new weekly plan and, for those who want it, a new set of contests. The table lines up each grade with MINEDU's normative age and with the ONEM level it competes in.

Ages from MINEDU, Edades normativas (normative age counted on 31 March each year). Levels from the bases of the XXII Olimpiada Nacional Escolar de Matemática 2026 as sent to schools by UGEL 06 in an oficio múltiple. Both read on 9 November 2026.

The secundaria plan

Ley 28044 says secundaria "dura cinco años" and gives students "una formación científica, humanista y técnica". Under the regular school day the CNEB gives every grade 4 weekly periods of Matemática, 4 of Comunicación, 3 of Inglés and 2 of Educación para el Trabajo.

With 45-minute periods, that is 180 minutes of mathematics and 135 of English a week, by our sum. There is still no computing area: digital skills remain a competency shared across subjects, so coding at school depends on the school.

What that means for a twelve year old

Secundaria is busier, and there are more teachers to adjust to. Coding classes that run at an hour the family chooses fit round that better than an after-school club fixed to one afternoon.

English at school goes up to three periods a week. A twelve year old who also codes in English gets a lot of practice in precise, technical language, the kind that is hardest to pick up in a general class.

Contests at twelve

Which olympiads can a twelve year old in Peru take part in?

In maths, the ONEM from first grade of secundaria. In computing, the OPI is open to secondary students and gets harder later. We describe them from the organisers' own documents, and we are not connected with any of them.

ONEM, the school maths olympiad

The ONEM 2026 bases put 1° and 2° de secundaria in Nivel 1, with categories Alfa for public schools and Beta for private ones. The school stage ran nationwide on Wednesday 8 July 2026; its paper had ten closed constructed-response items and lasted two hours.

According to El Peruano on 11 October 2026, the 2026 edition drew more than 1.7 million secondary students from its first stage. Entry is through your child's school, which runs the first round; the winners go on to the UGEL, regional and national stages.

OPI, the computing olympiad

The Olimpiadas Peruanas de Computación, organised by UTEC, are "abiertas gratuitamente a todos los estudiantes de secundaria del país". The OPI 2025 rules allowed only C++ in the final, in-person phase and banned generative AI for solving problems.

A twelve year old is early for the OPI final, but not too early to prepare. Python teaches the thinking; C++ comes later for the contest itself. Our page on ONEM preparation explains, in Spanish, how we train with problems we write ourselves.

One more door opens soon after twelve. MINEDU's COAR schools offered 2,650 places in their 2026 admission for public-school students who had finished 2° de secundaria. We mention it only so families know the timeline; we do not prepare for COAR admission and make no promise about any contest or admission result. We never reproduce an organiser's problems; every practice problem we use is our own.

An original Python lesson

How do we teach Python to a twelve year old? A sieve for primes

This is our lesson for twelve year olds who know loops and lists. It is more than two thousand years old, it fits in fifteen lines, and it shows a habit that contest coders need: count the work, not just the answer.

The idea, on paper first

Write the numbers from 2 to 100. Circle 2, then cross out every multiple of 2 after it. Circle the next number not crossed out, 3, and cross out its multiples. Keep going. Whatever is circled at the end is prime. The method is called the sieve of Eratosthenes.

The mentor asks one question before any code: when you reach 7, which is the first multiple you actually need to cross out? Most twelve year olds say 14. The answer is 49, because 14, 21, 28, 35 and 42 were already crossed out by 2, 3 or 5.

In Python

We keep a list of True and False values, one per number: True means "still might be prime". Crossing out means setting a value to False.

Because of the question about 7, each prime starts crossing out at its own square, p * p, and the outer loop can stop once p * p is bigger than the limit. We also count how many numbers get crossed out, as a check.

Then the question that turns a twelve year old into a programmer who thinks about cost: how much work did starting at p * p save? We write a version that can start at either 2 * p or p * p and counts every crossing-out step, even when a number is crossed out twice.

Two answers can be equally right and not equally good. The faster version was not found by typing; it came from the paper question about 7. That is the order we teach in: think, then code, then measure. It is also why AI tools that produce an answer instantly are no use to a twelve year old learning this: the answer was never the point.

  1. Our practice, check by hand. Which numbers below 30 are crossed out more than once in Version 2 when you start at 2p? List them.
  2. Our practice, twins. Use the list of primes to print every pair that differs by 2, like 11 and 13. How many pairs are there below 100?
  3. Our practice, the stopping rule. Explain, in English, why the outer loop may stop once p times p passes the limit.
  4. Our practice, estimate. Without running anything, write down a guess for the number of primes below 100,000. Then run it and compare.

AI at twelve

Can a twelve year old use ChatGPT or other AI for coding?

Not yet. Twelve is one birthday short of the age the main chatbot makers set, and for learning to code that year is a gift rather than a loss.

The rules

OpenAI asks ChatGPT users to be 13 or older, and Google sets 13 for Gemini on personal and school accounts. Anthropic sets 18 for Claude. None of them is for a twelve year old, so we do not use them in class.

What happens at thirteen

In our teen courses, students who have learnt to code first meet AI tools where the maker allows it, with a parent's permission where required, and use them to check and explain, never to replace thinking.

Contest rules agree

The OPI 2025 rules banned generative AI for solving problems. A young coder who can solve problems unaided is ready for that rule and for the real work behind it.

Every major AI tool and the youngest age its maker allows is listed, with quotes from the makers' own terms, in our AI tools age guide.

Progress at twelve

How does a twelve year old progress in coding over a year?

These five stages are ours. They decide which group a twelve year old joins and when they are ready for our teen courses at thirteen.

Five stages for twelve year olds (ours)
StageTypical timingCan buildCan explain
1. Fluent basicsMonths 1 to 2Programs with loops, decisions and inputWhat each line does
2. Own toolsMonths 2 to 4Functions reused across a projectInputs, outputs and why
3. DataMonths 4 to 6Programs that keep and search listsHow the program finds an item
4. AlgorithmsMonths 6 to 9A sieve, a sort, a search, written from an ideaWhy it is correct and how much work it does
5. ProjectsMonths 9 to 12A planned project, finished and testedWhat they would change next time

For parents

Stage 4 is where coding and school maths meet. A twelve year old who can explain why a method is correct is learning proof, not only programming.

Ask to see the finished project at stage 5, and ask what your child would change. A thoughtful answer is the clearest sign of progress.

For you, at twelve

Count the work, not only the answer. Two programs can both be right; one can be far better.

When you are stuck, write the idea in plain English first. The code is easier after that.

Parents in Peru ask

Coding at age 12: questions from families in Peru

Are the classes in Spanish?

No. Classes are live and online, and they are taught only in English. Mentors speak clearly, follow your child's speed and accent, and write the important words on the shared screen. At twelve, most students follow well, and those who find it hard at first usually settle within a few weeks.

Is twelve a good age to start coding?

Yes. Twelve year olds learn typed code quickly because they read fast and can plan. A complete beginner of twelve often catches up with children who started much younger.

Should my twelve year old learn Python or C++?

Python first for almost everyone: it lets them think about the problem instead of the punctuation. C++ makes sense later for contests such as the OPI, whose 2025 final allowed only C++.

Does 1° de secundaria in Peru teach programming?

Not as its own area. The national plan gives secundaria Matemática, Comunicación, Inglés, Educación para el Trabajo and other areas, with digital skills as a shared competency. Some schools add computing in their own hours.

Can my child enter the ONEM at twelve?

Yes, through the school. The ONEM 2026 bases place 1° and 2° de secundaria in Nivel 1. Schools run the first stage, so ask yours. We are not connected with the ONEM and we never use its problems; our practice problems are our own.

When could classes be in Peru time?

Any day, any time, flexible: choose a batch, or for a very personalised plan choose 1-on-1 at the hour that suits your family. India stays ten hours thirty minutes ahead, so 16:30 for your child is 03:00 the next day for the mentor.

Can a twelve year old use ChatGPT in your classes?

No. ChatGPT and Gemini set 13 as their minimum age and Claude sets 18. Twelve year olds learn to solve problems without them; students meet AI tools in our teen courses where the maker allows.

What comes after twelve?

At thirteen, students can move into our teen courses: full Python, Java, web development, AI and contest programming. The mentor suggests the next step from what your child has built.

Do you prepare students for COAR admission?

No. We mention COAR only because its admission is for public-school students who have finished 2° de secundaria, which is close to this age. Our classes are coding and maths, and we promise no admission or contest result.

Is Modern Age Coders part of MINEDU or connected to schools in Peru?

No. Modern Age Coders runs independently, teaches from India, and has no tie with MINEDU, UTEC, a UGEL or any Peruvian school. Nothing we issue is a Peruvian grade; students who finish a course receive a completion certificate that we issue ourselves.
