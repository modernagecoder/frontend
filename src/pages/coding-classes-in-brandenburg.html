Informatik in Brandenburg

Is Informatik a compulsory subject in Brandenburg?

No. The Gesellschaft für Informatik lists Brandenburg among the six Länder without Informatik that every pupil must take, and records no binding plans to introduce it. What exists is a set of options.

Where Informatik can appear in a Brandenburg school career
School and gradesWhat the rules allow
Grundschule, grades 1 to 6No Informatik subject; media education across subjects
Oberschule and Gesamtschule, 7 to 10Informatik as a Wahlpflicht elective
Oberschule and Gesamtschule, 9 and 10Up to five hours of Schwerpunkt teaching in Informatik, if the school chooses it
Gymnasium, 9 and 10Up to seven hours of Schwerpunkt teaching in Informatik, if the school chooses it
Gymnasium, grade 10Two-hour Informatik elective in the Einführungsphase

Source: Gesellschaft für Informatik, Informatik-Monitor 2026/27, Brandenburg and national pages, read 2 November 2026, citing the Sekundarstufe I-Verordnung and the Gymnasiale-Oberstufe-Verordnung. At the Gesamtschule the Einführungsphase is grade 11, with a three-hour Informatik elective in the first half-year and a Grundkurs or Leistungskurs in the second. One framework curriculum covers the Informatik elective in grades 7 to 10 at every school type.

The school decides first

The word that matters in the rules is kann: a school can offer Informatik within its Schwerpunkt or elective hours. Whether a particular Oberschule or Gymnasium in Cottbus, Frankfurt (Oder) or the Uckermark actually runs the course depends on its timetable and its teachers.

And teachers are scarce. For 2025/26 the monitor reports 305 Informatik teachers in Brandenburg, 3 % of all teachers, with 1.8 % qualified to teach the subject in the upper secondary years, and 11 newly hired in the year.

Who takes it in the end

Even so, a fair number of pupils choose it. The monitor gives 15 % as the share of Brandenburg upper secondary pupils taking Informatik in the 2024/25 school year (a KMK figure), about the national average of 15 % it quotes, and 5 % as the share of Abitur candidates with Informatik as an exam subject, from the Ministry for Education, Youth and Sport (MBJS).

Informatik can also replace a science among a pupil's Abitur exam subjects in Brandenburg, the monitor notes, which is not the case in every Land.

Brandenburg is home to 2,556,747 people, 184,754 of them in Potsdam, the Land capital (Destatis, 31 December 2024). Its primary school runs for six years, to the end of grade 6, as in Berlin; after that a pupil moves to an Oberschule, a Gesamtschule or a Gymnasium. At the Gymnasium the Abitur comes after grade 12; at a Gesamtschule with an Oberstufe it comes after grade 13.

The Abitur

How is the Informatik Abitur written in Brandenburg?

By the pupil's own school, and with a computer allowed. Maths and other core subjects, by contrast, are central, on dates the Ministry fixes for the whole Land.

Informatik: decentral

The Informatik-Monitor states that written Abitur tasks in Informatik are developed decentrally in Brandenburg. For each course the teachers submit two task proposals, one of which is chosen, and work at a computer is allowed in every task of each proposal, limited to where the subject needs it. The teacher also lists the aids the pupils will receive.

The MBJS circular for 2026/27 fits that picture: Informatik is not among the subjects with a central Abitur, and it says that the dates for written exams in decentral subjects are planned within each school.

What to practise

An exam written by the course teachers rewards steady work in that course more than generic exam tricks. And an exam where a computer is allowed still needs fast, accurate coding: time spent hunting a syntax error is time not spent on the problem.

So with Oberstufe pupils we do both: timed tasks at the keyboard and short written explanations of how a solution works. The school course and its teachers are what count for the Abitur; we teach alongside and never set or mark school work.

A Brandenburg data project

How many schools in Brandenburg lead to the Abitur? Count the meaning, not the label

The Ministry for Education, Youth and Sport publishes the locations and types of all school sites in the Land as an open web service. Our pupils use it to practise the step every analyst learns the hard way: a category label can combine several things, and an exact match quietly misses them.

The data

We requested the full layer of school sites from the MBJS web map service as JSON and saved it as schulen_brandenburg.json on 3 November 2026. The service states its licence as Datenlizenz Deutschland Namensnennung 2.0, crediting the Ministerium für Bildung, Jugend und Sport, so it may be reused with that credit.

Each of its 964 records has a school number, a name, an address, a district (Kreis), a school type (Schulform) and more. Some school types are single, such as Grundschule. Others are combined, such as Oberschule mit Grundschule.

The question

Two questions a parent might ask: how many sites teach the Grundschule grades, and at how many can a pupil reach the Abitur? A beginner filters for the label Grundschule and the label Gymnasium, and stops.

Version 1 does exactly that. Version 2 counts what the labels mean. Version 3 checks whether the two groups overlap.

The exact match missed 54 sites with primary grades and 66 routes to the Abitur. GOST stands for gymnasiale Oberstufe: a Gesamtschule mit GOST leads to the Abitur just as a Gymnasium does, and a vocational school centre (OSZ) with a berufliches Gymnasium leads to it too. The label Gymnasium alone does not cover the meaning a parent has in mind.

QuestionExact labelLabel contains the idea

Data: Ministerium für Bildung, Jugend und Sport des Landes Brandenburg, Schulen im Land Brandenburg (WFS-MBJS-SCHOOL), Datenlizenz Deutschland Namensnennung 2.0, retrieved 3 November 2026. The counts are our own and count school sites as listed in the service, which includes vocational and special schools; they are not official school statistics.

Four sites, each a Gesamtschule mit Grundschule und GOST, are in both groups. So 537 and 177 add up to 714, but only 710 different sites stand behind them: when categories combine, parts can add up to more than the whole. Spotting that before someone else does is what separates a careful analyst from a quick one.

  1. Ages 9 to 11, no screen. In a class, 12 children have a cat, 9 have a dog and 3 have both. How many children have a pet? Draw two overlapping circles to check.
  2. Ages 11 to 13, Scratch. Make a list of school names and count how many contain the word Grundschule, not just the ones that are exactly Grundschule.
  3. Ages 13 to 16, Python. Run Version 1, write down what you think Version 2 will print, then run it and explain the difference.
  4. Going further. Count Abitur sites per Kreis. Which district has the most, and does dividing by the number of all sites in that district change the order?

IOI 2027 in Potsdam

What is happening in Potsdam in September 2027?

The 39th International Olympiad in Informatics, hosted by Germany for only the second time.

Bundesweite Informatikwettbewerbe (BWINF) announces the 39th IOI for 12 to 19 September 2027 at the Hasso-Plattner-Institut in Potsdam, the first in Germany since 1992. The German team is chosen through the Bundeswettbewerb Informatik, whose first round for 2026/27 is open until 23 November 2026, followed by training camps. For a teenager in Brandenburg this is a rare chance to see the top of the subject close to home. Our competitive programming lessons use original problems in the olympiad style and never reproduce BWINF or IOI tasks; we are not connected to BWINF or the IOI. For younger pupils, the Informatik-Biber runs from 9 to 20 November 2026 through schools.

Coding classes in Brandenburg: frequent questions

Are the classes in German?

No. All lessons are live and taught in English only, by mentors in India who adjust to the learner's pace and accent. German school terms are named next to the English ones.

Is Informatik compulsory in Brandenburg?

No. The Informatik-Monitor 2026/27 names Brandenburg among six Länder without Informatik for all and knows of no binding plans to introduce it. Schools can offer it as an elective from grade 7 and as a Schwerpunkt in grades 9 and 10.

Who writes the Informatik Abitur in Brandenburg?

The school. According to the Informatik-Monitor, teachers submit two task proposals per course and one is picked; pupils may work on a computer wherever a task needs one.

When is the maths Abitur in Brandenburg in 2027?

The MBJS circular 06/25 sets the central written maths exam for 5 May 2027 at 9:00, with the resit on 24 May 2027.

Will lessons with you count towards the Abitur?

No. Our practice at the keyboard and on paper runs beside the school course, and only that course and its exam count. We hand out no grades and no certificates of any school kind.

Can my child aim for the IOI in Potsdam?

The IOI 2027 team is selected through the Bundeswettbewerb Informatik and BWINF training camps, so the route starts with that contest. We train the problem-solving skills with our own problems and have no part in selection.

When are classes in German time?

Weekdays or weekends, flexible: choose your batch, or for a very personalised plan choose 1-on-1 at your convenience, weekend mornings or weekdays. Afternoons from about 15:45 suit many pupils in the Land.

How many learners are in a group?

Fifteen to twenty at a similar level. A one-to-one plan gives a learner a mentor of their own and their own pace.

In which currency do we pay?

Fees are shown in euro at the European Central Bank reference rate and charged in US dollars, so your bank makes the conversion and the statement may differ a little. The first class costs nothing.

Are you connected to the Ministry or to BWINF?

No. Modern Age Coders runs its own online classes and has no tie to the MBJS, the KMK, BWINF, the IOI or any Brandenburg school.
