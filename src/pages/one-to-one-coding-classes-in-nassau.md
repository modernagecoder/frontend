# One to one costs more and is usually the wrong choice. Here is when it is not.

> Private one to one Python and AI tuition for learners in Nassau and across The Bahamas costs USD 150 a month against USD 100 for a group batch of five to eight. Our honest default recommendation is the group, because much of the learning in this subject comes from watching peers attempt the same problem. Private teaching is the right answer in five specific situations, including the only one that is structural here: the weekday early-morning slot, seven to eight in Nassau, which no group can fill because of the time difference to our teachers in India. The first class is free either way.

## Four situations where private teaching is worth the difference

A group batch of five to eight is the right purchase for most students and it is cheaper. These are the cases where it is not.

**A specific deadline.** An application, a competition entry, a submission with a date on it. A group moves at the group's pace, which is correct for the group and wrong for somebody with three months.

**A learner well ahead or well behind the batch.** Both ends are badly served by a middle. A student who finishes everything in twenty minutes learns to coast, and a student who is lost learns to hide it.

**A schedule that will not fit a fixed slot.** Weekday 07:00 Nassau time exists as a one to one option specifically because it works for students who would rather do this before school, and it is not a viable group slot.

**An adult with one objective.** A working professional who wants automation for a real task at work does not need a syllabus. They need somebody to shape the sessions around the task, and they usually finish in a few months rather than a year.

## What the first month looks like privately

| Session | What happens | What we learn |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | One problem, no teaching. The student attempts it | Where they actually are, rather than where a form said |
| 2 | The same problem, harder, with help withheld deliberately | Whether they can sit with something not working |
| 3 | A project chosen by them, negotiated down in size | What they will still care about in six weeks |
| 4 | The first thing finished and written up | Whether the write-up is the hard part for them |

Session two is the diagnostic one. Almost everything about how a learner will do over twelve months is visible in how they behave in the ten minutes after something stops working, and it is not visible at all in what they already know.

## What one to one does not fix

**Motivation.** A private teacher can shape a syllabus around what a student wants. If the student does not want anything in particular, the format makes no difference and we would rather say so than take the fee.

**The absence of peers.** Group students get something real from watching somebody else solve a problem differently, and from having to explain their own reasoning to somebody their own age. One to one loses that, and for a sociable twelve year old the loss can outweigh the gain.

**Time.** Machine learning takes months privately as well. Private teaching removes waiting; it does not remove the subject.

## Timing and price

USD 150 a month, billed in US dollars, charged monthly with no minimum term. The weekday 07:00 Nassau slot is 16:30 in India and is the most flexible option we have; Saturday mornings work for either format.

Weekday afternoons and evenings Bahamian time fall in the small hours in India and we decline them, privately as well as in groups. A tired teacher at half past two is not a private tutor, it is a booking.
