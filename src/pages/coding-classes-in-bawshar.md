## What makes Bawshar different inside Muscat?

Bawshar, also romanised Bousher, is one of the six wilayats in Muscat Governorate. This page uses Bawshar consistently except inside proper names such as Indian School Bousher.

The NCSI Statistical Year Book table for 2024 recorded 94,540 Omani residents and 346,359 expatriate residents in Bawshar, a total of 440,899. The official Muscat Governorate site identifies Bayt al-Sidra Thuraiya and the Al-Fath forts among the wilayat's historical landmarks. These details matter because Bawshar is not simply a modern suburb beside one road.

Muscat Municipality documented the duplication of about two kilometres of Bawshar Road from the Muscat Expressway area towards the roundabout opposite Muscat Private Hospital. The project also included pedestrian paths and a footbridge opposite Imam Muhanna bin Sultan School. The honest online-class argument is not that road work has failed. It is that two online lessons remove two optional return journeys from a family schedule.

## The Bawshar student data project

The Oman Ministry of Education publishes 2024/2025 student counts by government school, grade and sex, with a matching school file by governorate. Students download the current files, identify Bawshar schools with a documented matching method, and calculate one total per school.

The ordinary question is whether Bawshar has one typical school size or several distinct groups. A wide-bin histogram can show one broad mound. A narrow-bin histogram can split exactly the same observations into several apparent peaks.

The trap is bin width. It is a modelling choice, not a cosmetic setting. Bins that are too wide hide structure. Bins that are too narrow turn random gaps into a story. A responsible analysis plots several defensible widths, states the rule used and checks whether a claimed cluster survives the choice.

| Step | What the student does | What it teaches |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Downloads current student and school data and documents Bawshar matches | Local filtering must be auditable |
| 2 | Builds one checked enrolment total per school | The unit of analysis comes first |
| 3 | Plots equal-width histograms with several bin widths | A chart default is an analytical decision |
| 4 | Compares the result with a dot plot and summary table | Another view can expose invented peaks |
| 5 | Reports only patterns that survive reasonable width changes | Robust claims should not depend on one setting |

Primary source: [Oman Ministry of Education open-data catalogue](https://home.moe.gov.om/region/opendata/days-day20). Students pull the current files themselves.
