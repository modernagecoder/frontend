## How far is each Pasco attraction, according to its own description?

Pasco's provincial council publishes a list of tourist attractions in which the distances are buried in ordinary Spanish sentences. Getting them out with a program is a classic first task in text processing, and Pasco's list contains two traps that catch almost everyone.

### The open dataset

The Honorable Municipalidad Provincial de Pasco publishes "Lista de Atractivos Turísticos de la Provincia de Pasco" on datosabiertos.gob.pe under the Open Data Commons Attribution License (ODC-By), first released on 10 November 2022 and last modified on 29 March 2023.

Our copy, atractivos-pasco.csv, has 26 rows: 18 marked as Pasco province and 8 as Oxapampa province. The description column is free text, such as "a 40 km al sur de la ciudad de Cerro de Pasco (1 hora en auto aproximadamente)".

### Two things to notice first

The CODIGO_UBIGEO column holds 190101 on all 26 rows, even for attractions in Oxapampa province. That is a location code that has been copied down the column, so this project ignores it and reads place names from the text.

Some district names are spelt as the council typed them, such as HUALLAY and PALLANCHARA. We keep them as published.

**Version 1: find a number before "km"** (Python)
```python
import csv
import re

with open("atractivos-pasco.csv", newline="", encoding="latin-1") as f:
    rows = list(csv.DictReader(f))

found = 0
for row in rows:
    text = row["DESCRIPCION_DEL_ATRACTIVO"]
    match = re.search(r"(\d+) km", text)
    if match:
        found += 1
        print(row["NOMBRE_DEL_ATRACTIVO_TURISTICO"].strip()[:28], match.group(1))
print("Attractions with a distance:", found, "of", len(rows))
```
It prints eleven lines, from Iglesia Inmaculada Concepcio 18 through Replica de la Casa de Daniel 8 and Catarata el Leon 5 to Poblado de Pozuzo (820 msnm) 87, then Attractions with a distance: 11 of 26.

Version 1 looks as if it works. Eleven attractions report a distance, and the numbers seem sensible. But check one by hand. The replica of Daniel Alcides Carrión's house is described as "a 1.8 km de la ciudad de Cerro de Pasco", and the program printed 8. The pattern \d+ means "one or more digits", so it skipped the 1 and the full stop and matched only the 8 right before " km". The second trap is quieter: the numbers do not all start from the same place. The two waterfalls are measured from Villa Rica, Pozuzo and Villa Rica from Oxapampa, everything else from Cerro de Pasco. A list of bare numbers mixes them up.

**Version 2: decimals allowed, starting point captured** (Python)
```python
import csv
import re

PATTERN = r"(\d+(?:\.\d+)?) km[^(]*?(?:ciudad|poblado) de ([^(]+?) \("

with open("atractivos-pasco.csv", newline="", encoding="latin-1") as f:
    rows = list(csv.DictReader(f))

by_start = {}
for row in rows:
    match = re.search(PATTERN, row["DESCRIPCION_DEL_ATRACTIVO"])
    if match:
        km = float(match.group(1))
        start = match.group(2)
        by_start.setdefault(start, []).append(km)

for start, distances in sorted(by_start.items()):
    print(start, sorted(distances))
print("Distances read:", sum(len(d) for d in by_start.values()))
```
It prints: Cerro de Pasco [1.8, 18.0, 37.0, 40.0, 43.0, 66.0, 86.0], Oxapampa [71.0, 87.0], Villa Rica [5.0, 12.0] and Distances read: 11.

The new pattern has two groups. (\d+(?:\.\d+)?) takes digits with an optional decimal part, so 1.8 survives whole. (?:ciudad|poblado) de ([^(]+?) \( captures the place named after "ciudad de" or "poblado de", up to the bracket that opens the travel time. Grouping by that place gives three separate lists. The Huaguruncho snow peak, the furthest from Cerro de Pasco at 86 km, and Pozuzo, 87 km from Oxapampa, are now plainly measured from different towns.

**Distances read from the descriptions, grouped by starting town (our extraction)**

| Measured from | Attractions | Distances (km) |
|---|---|---|
| Cerro de Pasco | 7 | 1.8, 18, 37, 40, 43, 66, 86 |
| Oxapampa | 2 | 71, 87 |
| Villa Rica | 2 | 5, 12 |

Data: Municipalidad Provincial de Pasco, Lista de Atractivos Turísticos de la Provincia de Pasco, ODC-By. The extraction and grouping are ours. Fifteen of the 26 descriptions contain no distance in this form and are left out. One description gives Villa Rica's altitude as 1480 msnm, while INEI's 2017 list puts the populated centre at 1,551 metres: two sources, two points, both printed as published.

1. **Under 8, with a map.** Draw three towns and a few places around each. Say how far each place is "from" which town, and why you must say which.

## Does school in Pasco include programming?

Not as a subject. Pasco, like every region, follows the national curriculum, which spreads digital skills across all areas.

### The national framework

Primaria lasts six years and secundaria five, under the General Education Law, and MINEDU assigns grades by age on 31 March: a six year old starts primaria, a twelve year old starts secundaria.

The curriculum names a technology competency, number 28, about working in virtual environments, and makes it transversal. In the standard 35-hour secundaria week there are four hours of maths and none called programming or computing.

### What the region is planning

On 30 March 2026 the Gobierno Regional de Pasco reported work on its regional education project, the Proyecto Educativo Regional (PER) Pasco, with objectives and strategic actions linked to the PER to 2036.

In June 2026 its energy and mines directorate held an Ecoferia Estudiantil in which secundaria pupils presented environmental projects. In March 2026 it opened a new building for the I.E.E. María Parado de Bellido at the start of the school year.

Pasco pupils can enter national contests on equal terms. The ONEM maths olympiad begins with a round in each school, held on 8 July 2026 for its 2026 edition, which attracted more than 1.7 million secundaria pupils. UTEC's OPI and its sister computing olympiads charge nothing to secundaria pupils. We have no link with MINEDU, UTEC, the regional government, the provincial council or any contest; our practice material is our own.

## At what time can someone in Pasco take a lesson?

At the time that suits them. Pasco keeps Peru time all year, and the mentor in India arranges the lesson around it.

**Pasco time against India time (examples, not a schedule)**

| Pasco | India | Mentor's time of day |
|---|---|---|
| 05:00 | 15:30 | Afternoon |
| 07:30 | 18:00 | Early evening |
| 09:30 | 20:00 | Evening |
| 14:00 | 00:30 | Just after midnight |
| 17:30 | 04:00 | Next day, before dawn |
| 20:30 | 07:00 | Next day, morning |

Peru is on UTC minus 5 hours in every month, without daylight saving (tz database, America/Lima); India is on UTC plus 5 hours 30 minutes. India is therefore 10 hours 30 minutes ahead of Pasco all year round.

Since neither country moves its clocks, the hour you agree at the start never shifts. You book in Pasco time.
