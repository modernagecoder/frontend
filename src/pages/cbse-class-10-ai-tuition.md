## Which is better for class 10 AI tuition: a batch, one to one, a local tutor or recorded videos?

For most students a live batch that teaches 417 specifically gives the most for the money; one to one suits a student with a clash, a late start or one stubborn unit. A local tutor is only as good as their knowledge of the 2026-27 curriculum, and recorded videos work as a supplement, not as tuition.

### Where a local tutor wins
Sitting beside the student, a good tutor sees handwriting, posture and attention in a way a screen never fully does. If you find one who has taught 417 since the curriculum change and can run Orange on a laptop, that is real value.

### Where recorded videos win
They are free or cheap and can be paused. For a quick recap of bag of words the night before a test, a good video is fine. They cannot mark an answer, notice a habit or ask a viva question back.

### Where live online wins
A teacher who does only this subject, a shared screen that shows the student's own notebook, and no travel in a Class 10 year already full of school tests and other tuition.

## What does a week of AI 417 tuition look like across the year?

Two live classes, a short piece of set work after each, and a test at the end of every unit. What fills those slots changes with the term, so here are four real-shaped weeks from four points in the 2026-27 session.

- Week | Monday class | Wednesday class | Set work before the next class

- Early term, Unit 2 | Rule-based against learning-based models; supervised, unsupervised and reinforcement learning sorted with everyday examples | Orange: load a small dataset, train a decision tree, read its branches aloud | Classify eight scenarios as classification, regression or clustering; one two-mark answer

- Mid term, Unit 3 | The confusion matrix from a word problem; precision against recall and when each matters | Python: count TP, FP, FN, TN from two lists and print all four metrics | Two numericals with new figures; a reason for which metric a hospital should watch

- Late term, Unit 6 | Text normalisation, bag of words, TFIDF worked by hand on three short documents | Orange: sentiment analysis on a set of product reviews; one file program on word counts | A full bag of words table for a new corpus; one four-mark NLP answer

- Revision, February | Timed Section B: written answers only, marked on screen against the scheme | Practical mock: an unseen program, then three viva questions on the student's own file | Re-attempt every lost mark from the mock; read back the file programs

## A sample AI 417 tuition lesson: classification, regression and clustering

This is a cut-down version of a real Unit 2 Monday. Unit 2, Advanced Concepts of Modeling in AI, carries 11 theory marks, the most of any unit, and most of its lost marks come from mixing up three words.

### Step 1: one sentence for each

- Classification predicts a label from a fixed set, using labelled examples: pass or fail, spam or not spam.

- Regression predicts a number on a continuous scale, using labelled examples: tomorrow's rainfall in millimetres.

- Clustering groups similar items when no labels exist at all: shoppers with similar baskets.

### Step 2: sort these before reading on

- A bank predicts whether a loan will be repaid.Answer Classification. Two labels, repaid or not.

- An app estimates a flat's rent from its size and area.Answer Regression. Rent is a number.

- A music service groups listeners by what they play, with no categories given.Answer Clustering. No labels, so unsupervised.

- A model predicts a student's board percentage from mock scores.Answer Regression. The output is a percentage, a number.

- A camera app decides whether a leaf is healthy, rusted or blighted.Answer Classification. Three labels is still classification.

- A school groups 600 students by reading habits to plan library clubs.Answer Clustering. The groups are discovered, not given.

Question 5 is the one most students miss: they think classification means only two classes.

### Step 3: a four-mark answer, marked

Question. Differentiate between supervised and unsupervised learning with one example of each. (4)

```
Supervised learning is when a
teacher supervises the model.
Unsupervised learning has no
teacher. Example: spam filter.
```

```
Supervised: the model learns from
labelled data, where each example
already has the correct answer.  [1]
e.g. a spam filter trained on mails
marked spam / not spam.          [1]
Unsupervised: the data has no
labels; the model finds patterns
or groups on its own.            [1]
e.g. grouping customers by their
buying habits (clustering).      [1]
```

## How online AI tuition for CBSE marks work, runs mocks and handles doubts

Every written answer is marked against the way CBSE's marking schemes award points, every mock is reviewed in class, and doubts are taken live rather than sent to a queue. The steps below are the same in the batch and in one to one; only the pace differs.

-
**Answers are written, then marked on screen** Students write their answer first. The teacher then shows the point-by-point marking, as in the Unit 2 example above, and the student rewrites any answer that lost a mark.

-
**Unit tests feed a weak-unit list** Marks are logged by unit. The unit with the most lost marks is the one that comes back in the next mixed practice, with new numbers and new scenarios.

-
**Full mocks in the paper's shape** From December, mocks follow the 2025-26 sample paper layout: 24 marks of objective items and 26 of written answers, two hours, with the choices inside each section. The 2026-27 sample paper for 417 is not out yet.

-
**The practical is rehearsed, not assumed** Practical mocks give an unseen program on Units 4 to 7, then a short viva on the student's own file and project.

-
**Doubts first, then new work** A doubt raised in class is worked on the shared screen at once. Doubts from set work open the next class before anything new is taught.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is tuition needed for class 10 AI?** Not always. A student whose school teaches 417 well, including Python in Jupyter and the Orange tool, may only need practice papers. Tuition helps when the school covers theory but rushes the practical, when written answers keep losing marks, or when the student has no one to rehearse the viva with. Half of the 100 marks are practical, so that gap matters.

**How do I find a good AI 417 tutor?** Ask three things: which units are practical only in 2026-27 (Units 4 and 7), whether they teach Orange Data Mining and Python in Jupyter, and whether they can show a marked four-mark answer. A tutor who knows the current unit list and marks against the scheme is worth trying. Book a trial class before paying for a term.

**Is there AI tuition near me for CBSE Class 10?** In most cities, AI 417 tutors are fewer than maths or science tutors, and many teach general computers rather than this subject. Online tuition removes the distance problem: the same live class reaches a student in any city or country. If you do find a local tutor, check they know the 2026-27 unit list.

**Is online AI tuition as good as offline for CBSE?** For 417 it often works better, because the practical is done on a computer anyway: the teacher sees the student's own notebook or Orange workflow on the shared screen and fixes it live. Offline tuition is stronger for students who need someone beside them to stay focused. The free demo is the honest test.

**How much does class 10 AI tuition cost?** The batch and one to one fees are shown on this page in your own currency, billed monthly with no admission fee. To compare with a local tutor, divide the monthly fee by the classes in that month: eight to ten for the Monday and Wednesday batch, four for one to one in India, eight outside India.

**How many classes a week is the AI tuition?** The batch meets twice a week, Monday and Wednesday at 9:00 PM IST. One to one is one private class a week in India and two outside India, on days you choose. Each class leaves a short piece of set work, and every unit ends with a test.

**Does the tuition cover the practical file and project?** Yes. The file of at least 15 programs is built from the Wednesday lab work, the project is guided in stages, and both the practical viva and the project viva are rehearsed. These are 50 of the 100 marks, so they are taught, not left to the last month.

**When should a Class 10 student start AI tuition?** At the start of the session gives the most even pace, with time for every unit and the file. Starting by October still leaves room for the full syllabus before December mocks. After that, a compressed plan or one to one is the realistic route.

**Can my child join AI tuition in the middle of the year?** Yes. A student joining mid-year enters the batch at the unit it is on, and missed units are covered through set work and doubt time, or through a few one to one sessions if the gap is large. The mentor suggests which after the free demo.

**Is the first tuition class free?** Yes. The first class is a free live demo with a teacher. Afterwards the mentor tells you which units need the most work and whether the batch or one to one suits your child. There is also a paid priority demo with a written skill report.
