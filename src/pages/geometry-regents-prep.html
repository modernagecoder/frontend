The exam

What is the format of the Geometry Regents?

Thirty-five questions in four parts, for 80 credits. It looks like the Algebra I design at first glance, but Part II is longer and Part III shorter.

Geometry examination design (NYSED Geometry Educator Guide, read 31 October 2026)
PartQuestionsCredits eachCredits in the part
Part I (multiple choice)24248
Part II (constructed response)7214
Part III (constructed response)3412
Part IV (constructed response)166
Total (as published)3580

What each part asks

Two-credit questions may involve several steps and ask students to show work or justify. Four-credit questions ask students to “explain, justify, and/or prove geometric relationships” and to reason quantitatively in real-world modelling.

The single Part IV question is the proof. NYSED describes it as developing “multi-step, extended logical arguments and proofs involving geometric relationships.”

Constructions count

NYSED says constructed-response questions require understanding “as well as demonstrating geometric concepts through constructions”. The standards include a cluster called Make geometric constructions (G-CO.D).

A construction earns credit only when the arcs are visible. Practising with a real compass, not only with software, is part of preparation.

Source: Geometry Educator Guide, published by NYSED’s assessment office and read on 31 October 2026. Our school is independent of NYSED and of every New York district.

Domains

What topics are on the Geometry Regents?

Six domains. Similarity, right triangles and trigonometry is the largest; congruence, where most proofs live, is close behind.

Geometry blueprint: percent of test by credit (NYSED Geometry Educator Guide, read 31 October 2026)
DomainCodePercent of credits
Similarity, Right Triangles, and TrigonometryG-SRT29 to 37%
CongruenceG-CO27 to 34%
Expressing Geometric Properties with EquationsG-GPE12 to 18%
Modeling with GeometryG-MG8 to 15%
CirclesG-C2 to 8%
Geometric Measurement and DimensionG-GMD2 to 8%

The two big domains

G-SRT and G-CO together carry well over half the credits. Similar triangles, the trigonometric ratios, rigid motions and triangle congruence are where a student’s year should be spent.

Congruence also holds the cluster Prove geometric theorems (G-CO.C): theorems about lines and angles, triangles and parallelograms.

Coordinates and modelling

G-GPE is algebra in geometry’s clothes: slopes, distances, midpoints, and proving that a shape is what it claims to be on a grid. Students who are good at Algebra I often find these their easiest credits.

Circles and solid measurement are small on the blueprint, so they deserve attention in proportion: secure, but not at the cost of proof.

Tools and pi

What tools and formulas can you use on the Geometry Regents?

A compass, a straightedge and a graphing calculator. The reference sheet is short, and NYSED is strict about how pi is used.

Geometry Regents tools and rules (NYSED Geometry Educator Guide and Reference Sheet for Geometry, read 31 October 2026)
ItemWhat NYSED says
TimeThree hours
CalculatorExclusive use of a graphing calculator for the full exam; no symbol manipulation
Compass and straightedgeMust be available to every student; schools supply the tools
Value of piUse the pi symbol or pi key; 3.1416, 3.14 or 22/7 are unacceptable unless the question says otherwise
Reference sheetDetachable, at the end of the booklet: volume of a cylinder, general prism, sphere, cone and pyramid

Why the pi rule matters

Our example: the volume of a sphere of radius 10 is (4/3)π(10)³. With the pi key that is 4188.79..., which rounds to 4189. With 3.14 it is 4186.67, which rounds to 4187. The second answer is wrong by NYSED’s rule, even though the method is right.

Students used to 3.14 from middle school need to unlearn it early in the year, not on exam day.

What the sheet leaves out

The geometry reference sheet holds the volume formulas and nothing else. Area of a circle, circumference, the distance formula, the trigonometric ratios and the special right triangles are not on it.

Those must be known. We drill them as short daily recall, a few minutes at the start of each lesson.

NYSED publishes the Next Generation Geometry Reference Sheet and the Geometry resources page, read 31 October 2026.

A proof

How to write a coordinate geometry proof for the Regents

Our own problem, in the G-GPE style. The method matters more than the answer: claim, evidence, and a sentence that ties them together.

  1. The problem (ours). Quadrilateral ABCD has vertices A(-2, 1), B(2, 4), C(5, 0) and D(1, -3). Prove that ABCD is a square.
  2. Plan before calculating. A square is a rhombus with a right angle. So we will show all four sides are equal, then show one pair of adjacent sides is perpendicular.
  3. Distances. AB = √((2 - (-2))² + (4 - 1)²) = √(16 + 9) = 5. BC = √(3² + (-4)²) = 5. CD = √((-4)² + (-3)²) = 5. DA = √((-3)² + 4²) = 5. All four sides are 5, so ABCD is a rhombus.
  4. Slopes. Slope of AB = (4 - 1)/(2 - (-2)) = 3/4. Slope of BC = (0 - 4)/(5 - 2) = -4/3. Their product is -1, so AB is perpendicular to BC and angle B is a right angle.
  5. Concluding statement. “ABCD is a rhombus because all four sides are congruent, and a rhombus with a right angle is a square. Therefore ABCD is a square.”

Where proof credit is lost

Calculating every slope and length correctly and then stopping. The question said prove, and a list of numbers is evidence without a conclusion.

Using the wrong definition: four equal sides alone gives a rhombus, not a square. Name the property you are proving at each step.

A second example, trigonometry (ours)

A wheelchair ramp rises 1.2 meters over a horizontal run of 9 meters. Find the angle it makes with the ground, to the nearest tenth of a degree.

tanθ = 1.2/9, so θ = tan-1(0.1333...), about 7.6 degrees. Write the ratio first, then the inverse: the grader looks for both.

Scoring and levels

Results come in five levels. The geometry guide’s Level 3 description says those students “meet the content area requirements for a Regents diploma”, with a warning that the next course may need extra support.

Each sitting has its own conversion chart on nysedregents.org, turning raw credits out of 80 into a scale score and level.

Where Geometry fits in a diploma

Under current NYSED rules a Regents diploma needs one math Regents, and the advanced designation needs three. For most students Geometry is the second of those three.

Through its NY Inspires plan NYSED wants passing Regents exams to stop being a diploma condition from fall 2027. That needs a Board of Regents vote; today’s rules hold for every student in the meantime.

Sources: June 2027 Regents schedule, August 2027 schedule, final dates memo and NY Inspires timeline, read 31 October 2026.

A plan

How to prepare for the Geometry Regents, week by week

Our twelve-week plan counted back from Tuesday, June 22, 2027.

  1. Weeks 1 and 2: recall drill and a diagnostic. Formulas not on the sheet, then one full recent exam marked with the rating guide.
  2. Weeks 3 to 5: congruence and proof. Rigid motions, triangle congruence criteria, and a two-column or paragraph proof every lesson.
  3. Weeks 6 to 8: similarity and trigonometry. Scale factors, the side-splitter idea, trigonometric ratios and their inverses, with a wording-to-diagram habit for word problems.
  4. Week 9: coordinate geometry. Slope, distance and midpoint proofs, finishing each with a concluding statement.
  5. Week 10: constructions and circles. Compass work by hand, arc length, sector area and the equation of a circle.
  6. Weeks 11 and 12: full papers. Two three-hour rehearsals with the student’s own compass and calculator, and the pi key every time.

Proof readiness

Four stages of writing a geometry proof

How students usually grow into the 6-credit question, in our experience of teaching it.

Stages of proof writing (ours)
StageWhat it looks likeNext step
1. CalculatorFinds numbers, never states whySay the reason out loud for each step
2. ListingWrites true statements in no clear orderStart from the given, end at the claim
3. ChainingEach step follows from the last, with a reasonName the theorem or definition used
4. ConcludingEnds with a sentence that answers the questionWrite proofs under time

For parents

Ask your child to explain one homework proof to you. If they can tell the story, they can usually write it.

Geometry marks often drop from Algebra I marks in the first term. That is common and fixable, once proof writing clicks.

For students

Before writing a proof, say in one sentence what you are going to show and how.

Use the pi key. Every time.

Geometry Regents questions

Geometry Regents: questions students ask

When is the Geometry Regents in June 2027?

Tuesday, June 22, 2027, at 1:15 p.m., on NYSED's June 2027 examination schedule. It is also given in the January period and on Wednesday, August 18, 2027, at 12:30 p.m.

How many questions are on the Geometry Regents?

Thirty-five, in four parts: 24 multiple choice, 7 two-credit questions, 3 four-credit questions and one six-credit question. NYSED's guide gives a total of 80 credits.

Is the Geometry Regents hard?

It is different rather than simply harder: it asks students to prove and construct, not only calculate. Students who practise written proofs and constructions all year usually find the exam far more manageable.

Do you need a compass for the Geometry Regents?

Yes. Every student must have access to a compass and a straightedge, which the school provides, and a graphing calculator for all three hours.

Can you use 3.14 for pi on the Geometry Regents?

No, unless a question says so. NYSED's guide says students should use the pi symbol or the pi key, and that 3.1416, 3.14 or 22/7 are unacceptable.

What formulas are given on the Geometry Regents?

Only volume formulas: cylinder, general prism, sphere, cone and pyramid, on a detachable reference sheet. Area, circumference, distance and the trigonometric ratios must be known.

What is the biggest topic on the Geometry Regents?

Similarity, right triangles and trigonometry, at 29 to 37 percent of the credits, followed by congruence at 27 to 34 percent, according to NYSED's blueprint.

When did the Geometry Regents change?

NYSED's implementation memo set the first new Next Generation Geometry exam for June 2025, with the previous exam given for the last time in January 2025.

When are your Geometry classes?

Weekdays or weekends, flexible: choose your batch, or for a very personalised plan choose 1-on-1 at your convenience, weekend mornings or weekdays. Times are set in Eastern Time.

Are you affiliated with NYSED?

No. Modern Age Coders is an independent school that teaches geometry live online. We quote NYSED's public documents and send students to NYSED's own past exams.
