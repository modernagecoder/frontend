11 plus maths tuition in North Yorkshire

North Yorkshire has three grammar schools and two different entrance tests. The council runs the Ripon test, written and marked by GL Assessment, and from September 2026 it contains a maths section for the first time: roughly 25 questions in 25 minutes, with no practice questions first. The two Skipton schools set their own test instead, in English and maths. Modern Age Coders teaches the Year 5 maths behind both, live online, in a class or one to one, and this page sets out what each test asks, the dates for 2027 entry, and how a habit of estimating quickly earns its keep at about a minute a question.

Is there maths in the North Yorkshire 11 plus?

Yes, at all three grammar schools, though not in the same test. North Yorkshire Council says that from September 2026 the Ripon Grammar School test is a verbal skills paper and a non-verbal reasoning with mathematics paper, supplied and marked by GL Assessment, and that "the mathematics section has approximately 25 questions (25 minutes)" with content up to the end of Year 5. The 2026 sitting for September 2027 entry was on Saturday 12 September. Ermysted's Grammar School in Skipton uses an FSCE test of two English and maths papers, sat on Saturday 26 September 2026, and Skipton Girls' High School runs its own selection tests. Modern Age Coders teaches the Year 5 and 6 maths behind these tests live online, in a group or 1-on-1, through our 11 plus maths preparation course.

Three schools, two tests

Which grammar schools are there in North Yorkshire?

Three, and the council names them on its own selective schools page: Ripon Grammar School, Ermysted's Grammar School and Skipton Girls' High School. Only the Ripon test is the council's to run.

What the council does and does not do

The council's page is direct about its role: "We are only responsible for organising entrance tests for Ripon Grammar School." For the two Skipton schools it says they "arrange and run their own selection tests", with their own application processes, timescales and deadlines.

Whatever the school, the council makes one point that catches families out every year: "Registering for and sitting an entrance test is not an application for a place at the school." The common application to the council is a separate step, due by 31 October of Year 6.

Why the split matters for maths

A child sitting only the Ripon test meets maths as one timed multiple-choice section after two blocks of non-verbal reasoning. A boy sitting at Ermysted's meets maths mixed with English across two papers, half of it answered by writing short answers rather than choosing.

Some families near the middle of the county consider both towns. If so, the two tests need slightly different practice habits, which we come to below, and two separate registrations with two separate deadlines.

What changed in 2026

A block of about 25 multiple-choice maths questions in 25 minutes, drawn from the national curriculum up to the end of Year 5. The council adds that "There are no practice questions for this section."

Before and after

Until the September 2026 sitting, the council says, there was no maths at all: a 50-minute verbal reasoning paper of 80 questions and a non-verbal paper of four ten-minute sections of 20 questions each. A Ripon family who prepared an older sibling for that format is now preparing for a different test.

The council gives its reason for the change. The reasoning elements keep testing potential "irrespective of schooling and background", while "By adding a new Key Stage 2 element, the tests will also respond to the teaching and learning which is conducted in your child's school."

What "no practice questions" means in practice

In the non-verbal sections the instructions come with worked practice items before the clock starts. The maths section has none, so a child must already know how a multiple-choice maths question looks and how to mark the answer sheet before the day.

GL Assessment's free familiarisation booklets, which the council links, are the right place to see the format. The council notes they "are not definitive", and that including a question type does not guarantee it will appear.

The clock is part of it

The council puts it bluntly: "It is essential that your child attempts to complete all the questions within the time allowed". It adds, "The time element is part of the test."

Year 5 content only

Both the English and the maths sections stop at the end of the Year 5 national curriculum, because the test falls in the second week of Year 6.

Marked by GL

The council says the papers are "produced, marked and standardised by GL Assessment", and trained invigilators run the day at Ripon Grammar School.

The Ripon calendar

When is the Ripon Grammar School 11 plus for 2027 entry?

For children starting Year 7 in September 2027 the test was on Saturday 12 September 2026, at Ripon Grammar School. Registration closed on 30 June 2026.

If registration was missed

The council says a late application means the child cannot sit the test before National Offer Day, and late testing then happens in April before the September start. The exceptions are a serious illness backed by medical evidence and a family moving into the Ripon catchment area.

A child can sit the Ripon test only once in a school year. A Year 7 pupil who has already sat it waits for the Year 8 entry test.

Illness on the day

Families who think a child is unwell must tell the admissions team before the day, or ring the emergency number on the letter on the day and confirm in writing by 5pm on the following Monday. A child absent without notice is withdrawn from testing.

The council also says that bringing a child to the test confirms they are fit and well, so a family in doubt should ask rather than sit and hope.

The cut-off

What is the Ripon Grammar School 11 plus pass mark?

There is no fixed pass mark. The council finds the highest scoring 28 per cent, or as close as possible, of Year 6 children living in the Ripon area, and that number sets the cut-off for the year.

How the total is built

The council says the marks are first standardised to allow for each child's age, and then "your child's verbal skills and non-verbal reasoning and mathematics scores are added together to give a total score which is used to determine whether they have reached the cut-off mark."

So from 2026 the maths section sits inside one of the two scores that are added. It is not a separate hurdle with its own pass line.

Reading the three numbers honestly

All three published cut-offs come from the older test, which had no maths section. The September 2026 sitting is the first on the new papers, so nobody can say yet where its line will fall, and we would not guess.

Reaching the cut-off makes a child suitable for entry; it does not secure a place. The council says plainly that if the school is oversubscribed "the normal oversubscription criteria will apply". Ripon's published admission number is 117, which includes 14 boarding places.

What the written half changes

With options on the page, a careful estimate can rule out wrong answers. With a blank box, there is nothing to rule out: the child must reach the number. Our lessons for a Skipton boy therefore pair the estimation habit below with a written calculation that the estimate then checks.

Because English and maths share the same papers, a question may also hinge on reading the wording closely. The school asks for no special subject outside the primary curriculum.

Skipton Girls' High School

The school says that, as an academy, it is responsible for its own admissions, and that girls who wish to join in Year 7 "sit a set of selection tests". Its admissions page links FSCE familiarisation guides for parents and for children, and a free online preparation offer for disadvantage-funded pupils through Atom Learning.

Our lesson family

How can a child answer a maths question in about a minute?

Twenty-five questions in 25 minutes is roughly a minute each, with no warm-up. The single most useful habit at that pace is a quick estimate made before any working. The examples and practice set below are ours, written for this page; they are not GL or FSCE questions.

Round first, then look

Before reading the options, round each number to one or two significant figures and get a rough answer in your head. The rough answer tells you which options are even possible.

Cross out the impossible

With five options, an estimate often removes two or three at once. What is left needs only a last digit, or a check of whether the answer is a little above or below the estimate.

Know which way you rounded

If both numbers were rounded up, the true product is smaller than the estimate. Saying that aloud is what turns rounding from a guess into reasoning.

Why estimation, of all topics

Year 5 of the national curriculum asks pupils to round any number up to a million to the nearest 10, 100, 1,000, 10,000 and 100,000, and to use rounding to check answers. It is on the syllabus that both North Yorkshire tests cap at, and it speeds up nearly every other topic on it.

It also protects a child from the most common timed-test error: a slip that produces an answer ten times too big, which a ten-second estimate would have caught.

What we do not do

We do not reproduce GL or FSCE questions, and we do not sell the idea that a shop-bought test predicts a score. The council warns that the standardised marks for "off the shelf" tests are not comparable with the marks achieved in the tests designed for North Yorkshire.

On coaching, the council writes: "Coaching for the tests is not required. However, we cannot prevent this." We teach Year 5 and 6 maths that stands on its own, test or no test.

North Yorkshire 11 plus questions

What North Yorkshire parents ask

Is there maths in the Ripon Grammar School 11 plus?

From the September 2026 sitting, yes. North Yorkshire Council says the non-verbal reasoning with mathematics paper ends with a maths section of approximately 25 questions in 25 minutes, with content up to the end of Year 5 and no practice questions.

When was the Ripon 11 plus for September 2027 entry?

On Saturday 12 September 2026, at Ripon Grammar School. Registration ran from 30 April to 30 June 2026, and the council says results are issued no later than 16 October.

What is the Ripon Grammar School pass mark?

There is no fixed mark. The council sets the cut-off at the score that picks out the highest scoring 28 per cent, or as close as possible, of Year 6 children living in the Ripon area. Published cut-offs were 203 for 2026 entry, 196 for 2025 and 195 for 2024, all on the older format.

Who writes the North Yorkshire 11 plus papers?

For Ripon, GL Assessment produces, marks and standardises the papers, and the council organises the test. Ermysted's Grammar School uses Future Stories Community Enterprise Ltd (FSCE), and Skipton Girls' High School runs its own selection tests.

What is in the Ermysted's Grammar School entrance test?

Two papers of English and maths questions from FSCE. One uses multiple choice answer options and the other asks for short written answers. The scores are standardised and age-weighted.

Does passing the test guarantee a grammar school place?

No. The council says reaching the cut-off does not guarantee a place, and the oversubscription criteria apply if the school has more qualified applicants than places. You must still apply to the council by 31 October.

Can my child sit the Ripon test twice?

Not in the same school year. The council says a child can sit the Ripon test only once per school year, and a Year 7 pupil who has sat it would next try for Year 8 entry.

Should I pay for 11 plus coaching in North Yorkshire?

The council says coaching for the tests is not required, though it cannot prevent it, and that shop-bought practice test scores are not comparable with its own. We teach Year 5 and 6 maths, never test items, and make no promise about any score.

Are the Ripon practice papers official?

The council links GL Assessment's free familiarisation booklets and online material. It says they show the range of skills and how answers are marked, but are not definitive.

When are your maths lessons?

Weekdays or weekends, flexible: choose your batch, or for a very personalised plan choose 1-on-1 at your convenience, weekend mornings or weekdays. We book every lesson in UK time.
