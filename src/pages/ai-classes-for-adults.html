The short answer

Adults from their mid-twenties to their sixties learn AI here without a career switch attached: analytics without programming for owners and managers, AI tools mastery for output-bottlenecked professionals, Python automation for chore-killers, and generative AI for the ones who get hooked. The elephant, addressed

What every adult thinks on the first evening, and what actually happens

We have watched hundreds of adults join a first session. The internal monologue is so consistent we can print it, and so wrong we can print what replaced it.

There is also an unfair advantage worth naming, because adults never credit themselves with it. The scarce skill of the AI era is knowing what you want, stating it precisely, and judging whether what came back is right. Twenty-five years of writing emails, managing budgets, raising children and running rooms is decades of training in exactly that. Teens out-click you; you out-specify them; and in this field, the specifier wins. Our teachers watch this play out in every mixed-age context we run, and it is why the adult batches move faster than adults expect.

What adults genuinely find harder is being publicly mid-learning: the clumsy fortnight where mistakes happen in front of witnesses. The honest fixes are structural, not motivational, and we use both: adult-only rooms where every witness is equally clumsy, and a one to one option for those who would rather do the clumsy fortnight in private and join a batch after.

The first month, concretely

What you will have made by week four

Adults deserve outcomes, not curricula. Per path, this is what exists after four weekly sessions and the practice between them.

On the analytics path

Your own sales, expenses or bookings file, cleaned and turned into a small dashboard you built: monthly trends, the three numbers that matter, and one honest surprise you had not seen. Owners usually find the surprise pays for the course before week six, and they are only half joking.

On the tools path

A personal working kit: the drafting, summarising, research and image tasks of your actual week, each mapped to a tool you now drive with judgement, plus the checking habits that keep machine output from embarrassing you in front of a client. The output bottleneck loosens visibly.

On the automation path

One chore, dead. The weekly report that assembled itself, the files that renamed themselves, the reminder emails that sent themselves. Small, real, and running on your machine, with just enough Python understood to grow it. The second chore dies faster than the first.

A word on scheduling, because it decides more adult enrolments than curriculum does. Batches are pinned to time-zone bands rather than to India's clock: an evening slot in London is an evening slot in London, and the teacher is the one keeping odd hours, not you. If your work pattern is genuinely irregular, shifts, travel, on-call weeks, say so on the first call and start one to one, where the calendar bends the other way. The design goal is simple: the class fits the life, because an adult course that demands the life reshape itself around it does not get month two.

Questions adults ask

Usually at 11 pm, usually after months of thinking about it

Am I genuinely not too old for this at 50 or 60?

Genuinely. Our current oldest students are in their sixties, adults are our fastest-growing group, and the brand teaches 6 to 67 by design rather than as a slogan. What the decades change is style, not capacity: adults want the why before the what, prefer real use cases to toy exercises, and dislike being rushed, all of which our adult batches are built around. The one real obstacle is the fear of looking clumsy, and it dissolves in a room where everyone is a grown-up beginner together.

Do I need a technical background or fast typing?

Neither. The analytics course was built specifically for non-programmers and involves more clear thinking than typing, and the AI tools track is about judgement and workflow rather than code. Where code does appear, in automation and generative AI, it is introduced from zero at adult pace. Slow typing is irrelevant in the AI era for a slightly funny reason: the machine does most of the typing, and precision of thought is the input that matters.

How much time does this take alongside a job and a family?

One live session a week, scheduled in evening and weekend slots across time zones, plus two or three hours of practice you place wherever your week allows. The design assumption is a full life, not a gap year: sessions are recorded nowhere, but teachers brief you back in after a missed week, and exam seasons, work crunches and school holidays are planned around rather than penalised.

Will I be in a class with teenagers?

No. Adult batches are adult-only, five to eight people, and it matters more than it sounds: the questions are different, the examples are different, the humour is different, and nobody wants to ask their beginner question in front of a fifteen year old who codes like breathing. One to one is also available and is the format many adults choose for their first two months.

I run a small business. Which course actually helps me?

Start with the data and AI analytics course: it turns the spreadsheets you already have, sales, stock, bookings, into answers, without programming. Add the AI tools course if your bottleneck is producing content, quotes and correspondence. The automation course comes third, when you know exactly which weekly chore you want to never do again. That ordering follows money saved per hour invested, which is the correct metric for an owner.

Will this help my current job rather than a new one?

That is the design centre of this page. Most of our adult students are not switching careers; they are accountants, teachers, managers, doctors and shop owners adding capability to the job they already have. The projects in class are your projects: your reports, your rosters, your client letters, rebuilt with the new tools. Career switching is a different journey with its own page and its own course, and we will point you there honestly if that is what you actually want.

Group or one to one for an adult beginner?

Both work, and the pattern we see is age-flavoured: adults who hated school tend to love one to one, at least at first, because the clumsy phase happens in private. Adults who miss learning with peers thrive in the batch, where the shared beginner-hood becomes half the pleasure. The free class lets you feel the group format before deciding, and moving between formats later is a conversation, not a contract change.

Is there a certificate I can put on LinkedIn?

Yes, every course carries a completion certificate, and we will give you the adult version of our standard honesty: the thing that impresses in a profile is the line underneath the certificate, where you describe the tool you built or the process you automated, concretely. Our courses are structured so that line exists. Post both; lead with the concrete one.

What do adult AI classes cost?

USD 100 a month for the adult group batch and USD 150 a month for one to one, identical in every country we serve outside India, billed monthly with no joining fee and no minimum term. Nothing about the adult tracks is priced differently from the rest of the site, and the first class is free without a card, at an hour that suits your time zone.

How do I start without committing to anything?

Send the form with a time that suits you, and the free class does the rest: a real session on your actual use case, your spreadsheet, your weekly chore, your curiosity, with an adult batch teacher. You will know within the hour whether this is your thing, and if the honest answer is that a different course or no course fits better, that is the answer you will get.
