## Dhofar is ten wilayats, not one survey location

Administrative evidence defines the region before a learner makes a regional claim.

### Ten named wilayats

Oman's [Governorates System](https://bur.gov.om/assets/pdf/en/governorate-system.pdf) lists Salalah, Taqah, Mirbat, Rakhyut, Thumrait, Dhalkut, Al Mazyunah, Muqshin, Shalim and the Hallaniyat Islands, and Sadah.

### Salalah is the centre

The same official system identifies Salalah as the governorate centre. A capital can be an important sampling stratum, but it is not automatically a substitute for the other nine wilayats.

### Separate wali offices

The current [Dhofar government portal](https://gov.om/en/dhofar-governorate) lists wali leadership for Taqah, Mirbat, Rakhyut, Thumrait and the other wilayats separately.

### Sunday to Thursday administration

The government portal publishes a Sunday-through-Thursday business week for the governorate office. That is an official-office fact and not the schedule of Modern Age Coders.

### Far southern geography

Oman's [Foreign Ministry regional guide](https://www.fm.gov.om/en/about-oman/state/oman-by-region/) places Dhofar in the far south, bordering Al Wusta, the Arabian Sea, Yemen and Saudi Arabia.

### Approximate regional scale

The Foreign Ministry gives an approximate Dhofar area of 99,300 square kilometres and describes mountains, plains, coastal settlements and the Empty Quarter. An approximate regional area is not a city measurement.

The target population comes before the questionnaire

If a claim says Dhofar, the sampling frame needs defensible coverage of the people and places the claim intends to describe. More responses from one city do not repair a missing region.

## Eastern coastal wilayats contribute distinct evidence

Official tourism sources show why Dhofar should not be treated as one uniform setting.

### Wadi Darbat near Taqah

The official [Experience Oman Dhofar guide](https://experienceoman.om/en/destinations/dhofar) places Wadi Darbat in mountains overlooking Khor Rori, just after Taqah on the coast.

### Khareef has a defined tourism season

Experience Oman describes the khareef period as June to September, when mist and light showers cover plains and mountains. A seasonal visitor survey needs a date field.

### Many landscape types

The same guide names sandy beaches, deserts, fertile plains, green mountains and archaeological monuments. A single central-city sample cannot stand for every setting.

### Mirbat Castle overlooks the old port

The official [Mirbat Castle page](https://experienceoman.om/destinations/dhofar/mirbat-castle) places it on Mirbat's eastern coast overlooking the old seaport and traditional market.

### Rectangular plan and two towers

The tourism source describes a rectangular castle with two main entrances, two defensive towers and two floors. These are heritage facts, not household-survey variables.

### Past functions need time labels

The page describes military, administrative, residential, storage and reception spaces. A dataset that records use should retain the historical period rather than flattening it into one category.

## The Salalah sample that was labelled as all Dhofar

A synthetic survey gives a plausible answer to the wrong geographic question.

### 1. Read the headline

A dashboard says 80 percent of Dhofar learners report reliable study connectivity. The number is calculated correctly from 72 positive responses among 90 observations.

### 2. Inspect the sampling frame

Every selected record has the same wilayat value: Salalah. The questionnaire may be clear and every selected person may respond, yet the regional label is unsupported.

### 3. Rebuild geographic coverage

The learner defines the target population, adds documented wilayat strata, shows separate synthetic results and limits the conclusion to the frame actually observed.

### A bigger Salalah sample would still be a Salalah sample

Increasing the first row from 90 to 9,000 observations could reduce sampling variability for the selected frame. It would not create coverage of Taqah, Mirbat, Rakhyut, Thumrait or the remaining Dhofar wilayats.

Every response and rate in this table is invented for teaching. It is not a measurement of connectivity, education, households or opinions in any Dhofar wilayat.

## Eight checks before the regional rate is published

The audit records who the study meant to describe and who could actually be selected.

| Check | Required question | Failure if omitted | Safer output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Target population | Exactly who does the claim describe? | Label expands after analysis | Write scope before collection |
| Sampling frame | Who had a chance to be selected? | Excluded places remain invisible | Publish frame coverage |
| Geographic stratum | Which wilayat supplied each record? | Capital sample becomes regional | Retain explicit identifiers |
| Selection method | How were people chosen? | Convenience is called random | Document procedure and seed |
| Eligibility | Who qualified for the question? | Ineligible records enter denominator | Store rules and exclusions |
| Response status | Did a selected person answer? | Sampling and non-response blur | Report separately by stratum |
| Weight evidence | What population totals support weights? | Arbitrary multipliers imply accuracy | Use sourced, dated controls |
| Uncertainty and limits | How precise and transferable is the estimate? | One rate looks universal | Show interval and coverage warning |

### Sampling bias is about who could be asked

The original frame contains only Salalah records. The problem exists before any participant decides whether to answer.

### Non-response is a separate question

If selected people do not reply, that creates a response problem. This project holds response constant so the sampling-frame failure remains clear.

### Stratification makes geography visible

Records carry a wilayat identifier and selection probability. Small strata are not silently merged into the capital.

### Weights need external evidence

A weight is not a guess that makes the table look balanced. It depends on a dated, relevant population control and the actual selection design.

### Rates need numerators and denominators

The interface displays the count behind every percentage. A tiny row is not granted the same apparent certainty as a large one.

### Language follows evidence

If only one city was sampled, the caption says one city. The code does not convert local evidence into a regional claim.

## Questions about coding classes across Dhofar

The answers distinguish live class facts, public regional evidence and synthetic survey data.

### Who can join coding classes across Dhofar?

Modern Age Coders teaches learners aged 6 to 67, including beginners, school students, university learners and working adults in Dhofar. Placement depends on observed ability, pace and objective rather than location or age alone. The free first class may use logic, mathematics, typing, debugging, data interpretation or a short coding task to identify a suitable course and format.

### What is the Dhofar sampling-bias coding project?

The learner audits a synthetic connectivity survey that sampled only Salalah residents but was labelled as a Dhofar-wide result. The code exposes the sampling frame, adds separate synthetic strata for Taqah, Mirbat, Thumrait and Rakhyut, and reports the limits of any conclusion instead of treating one city as the whole governorate.

### Why can a Salalah-only survey not describe all Dhofar?

Dhofar has ten wilayats with coastal, mountain and desert settings. A sample chosen entirely from the governorate centre excludes people in other places by design. A large number of Salalah responses can estimate the selected Salalah population more precisely, but it cannot remove the geographic coverage error or justify a Dhofar-wide claim.

### How does the learner repair the Dhofar survey?

The learner defines the target population, builds a sampling frame that covers the required wilayats, selects observations within documented strata, records response and eligibility, applies defensible weights only when population evidence supports them, and publishes uncertainty and exclusions. The repair addresses who was asked, not only how many answers arrived.

### Are the Dhofar survey results real public statistics?

No. Every response, rate and respondent count in the classroom tables is synthetic. Official sources are cited only for public administrative, geographic, tourism, heritage, university and wilayat facts. The exercise does not measure actual internet access, school provision or household opinion anywhere in Dhofar.

### Does Modern Age Coders have a physical centre in Dhofar?

No Dhofar office or classroom is claimed. Lessons are live online. A learner joins with a laptop or desktop, stable internet, audio and a current browser. The teacher explains, observes work, reviews code and gives feedback synchronously. This page describes regional service availability rather than a local branch.

Yes. Learners in Taqah, Mirbat, Rakhyut, Thumrait and other Dhofar wilayats may request an online class, subject to course and time availability. The service does not require travel to Salalah. The recurring schedule is agreed before payment, and the learner needs a suitable computer, internet connection and working audio.
