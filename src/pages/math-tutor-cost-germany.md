## Why does one maths tutor in Germany cost more than another?

Tutoring prices in Germany are not collected in any official statistic we could find, and this page never prints what other tutors or companies charge. What it can show is which choices push any price up or down, ours included.

**What changes the cost of maths tutoring, and what to check (our analysis)**

| Driver | Why it moves the cost | What to check |
|---|---|---|
| One to one or group | The tutor's hour is shared, or it is all yours | How many pupils, and whether they are at the same level |
| Level | Oberstufe and Leistungskurs maths need a tutor who knows that maths well | Has the tutor taught this Klasse and this exam? |
| Time before an exam | Few weeks means more lessons per week, or less covered | How many teaching weeks are really left (see below) |
| Frequency and length | Two hours a week costs more than one, and achieves more | Live minutes per month, not the number of sessions |
| At home, centre or online | Travel time costs someone, the tutor or the family | Who travels, and is travel time charged? |
| Continuity | A tutor who stays learns where the pupil goes wrong | Will it be the same person every week? |
| Terms | Packages, fixed terms or monthly plans | Notice period, and what happens to unused lessons |

Table: our own analysis of maths tutoring. Without an official source for tutoring prices, the page gives no market figures.

### The comparison that works

Work out what each offer charges for sixty minutes of live time with the tutor, then ask how many pupils share those minutes. A cheap hour shared with nine others and a dear hour alone can be equally good value; they buy different things.

Add travel. An hour at home with a tutor who drives forty minutes each way is rarely priced as one hour of work, and an hour at a centre costs the family a journey too.

### The comparison that misleads

Comparing package prices without counting the hours, or the cost of a first month without the notice period. A package of twenty lessons bought in March is worth less than it looks if the exam is in May and half of April is Osterferien.

Comparing grades promised. No honest tutor can promise a mark, and we do not.

## Which maths tests and exams in Germany are worth paying a tutor for?

Not all of them. Germany has comparison tests that carry no mark at all, and leaving exams that count for a certificate. Knowing which is which saves money.

**Maths tests and exams in the 2026/27 school year, with the dates the official sources give**

| Test or exam | Klasse | Date | Does it count? |
|---|---|---|---|
| VERA-3 | 3 | Test window 12 April to 26 May 2027 | No: not graded, no school or Land rankings |
| VERA-8 | 8 | Test window 8 February to 25 March 2027 | No: not graded, no school or Land rankings |
| ZP10 Mathematik, Nordrhein-Westfalen | 10 | Thursday 20 May 2027; resit date Thursday 3 June 2027 | Yes: part of the Klasse 10 leaving certificate |
| Mittlerer Schulabschluss, Berlin | 10 | Set in the Land's exam plan | Yes: the MSA is reached after Klasse 10 |
| Abitur Mathematik, Nordrhein-Westfalen | Q2 | Wednesday 5 May 2027, Leistungskurs and Grundkurs | Yes: a written Abitur exam |

Sources, read 2 and 3 November 2026: IQB, VERA-3 and VERA-8 pages and the VERA page of its Bildungsstandards section; Ministerium für Schule und Bildung NRW, Standardsicherung, Zentrale Prüfungen 10, Termine 2027 (Hauptschule, Realschule, Gesamtschule, Sekundarschule, Primusschule, Gymnasium with a Klasse 10 and other school types), and Zentralabitur 2027; Senatsverwaltung für Bildung Berlin, Prüfungen und Abschlüsse. Other Länder set their own Klasse 10 and Abitur dates.

### VERA: a check-up, not an exam

VERA-3 and VERA-8 are compulsory comparison tests, run every year in every Land, sometimes under other names such as KERMIT or Lernstandserhebungen. The IQB, which develops them, states that the results are not graded and that no rankings between schools or Länder are made.

They measure, a year early, how far a class has come towards the KMK standards for Klasse 4 and Klasse 9. That makes a VERA result useful information for a family. It is not a reason to pay for weeks of test practice.

### Where tutoring pays

Klasse 10 exams such as the ZP10 in Nordrhein-Westfalen count towards a leaving certificate, and the written Abitur counts towards the final grade. The KMK's maths standards for the Erster and Mittlerer Schulabschluss have been one combined document since 2022, binding in all Länder, so the content is broadly shared even when the papers differ.

The longer the gap that has opened, the earlier help should start. A pupil who has struggled with fractions since Klasse 6 needs months, not a fortnight before the ZP10.

We teach alongside school. We do not set or mark German exams and we award no German qualification; the school course and the Land's exam are the ones that count. Past ZP10 papers are published by the NRW ministry for schools (a login is required), and the IQB publishes past Abitur pool tasks, so pupils can practise on the real thing through their school.

## How many weeks of maths tutoring are left before the exam?

This decides how many months a family pays for, and the first answer most people give is too high. Our students check it in Python for a Klasse 10 pupil in Nordrhein-Westfalen who starts on Monday 9 November 2026.

### The plan

The ZP10 maths paper in Nordrhein-Westfalen is on Thursday 20 May 2027. Tutoring starts on Monday 9 November 2026. How many weeks of help are there?

The quick answer divides the days by seven.

### What the quick answer forgets

School holidays. The KMK table for 2026/27 gives Nordrhein-Westfalen Christmas holidays from 23 December 2026 to 6 January 2027, Easter holidays from 22 March to 3 April 2027, and a free day on 18 May 2027, two days before the exam.

Many families travel or rest in those weeks, and planning as if they were teaching weeks overstates the time left.

**Version 1: divide by seven** (Python)
```python
from datetime import date

start = date(2026, 11, 9)    # first tutoring week
exam = date(2027, 5, 20)     # ZP10 Mathematik, Nordrhein-Westfalen

weeks = (exam - start).days // 7
print("Weeks until the exam:", weeks)
```
It prints Weeks until the exam: 27.

Twenty-seven weeks sounds comfortable. But 192 days divided by 7 is 27.4, and the floor division quietly drops the part-week before the exam, while every holiday week is still counted as a week of work. Version 2 treats the time as a set of days. It builds the set of school weekdays before the exam, subtracts the set of holiday days for each break, and then asks which calendar weeks still contain at least one school day.

**Version 2: subtract the holidays** (Python)
```python
from datetime import date, timedelta

start = date(2026, 11, 9)
exam = date(2027, 5, 20)
ferien = [(date(2026, 12, 23), date(2027, 1, 6)),   # Weihnachtsferien NRW
          (date(2027, 3, 22), date(2027, 4, 3)),    # Osterferien NRW
          (date(2027, 5, 18), date(2027, 5, 18))]   # Pfingstferien NRW

def days(first, last):
    return {first + timedelta(n) for n in range((last - first).days + 1)}

schooldays = {d for d in days(start, exam - timedelta(1)) if d.weekday() < 5}
for first, last in ferien:
    schooldays -= days(first, last)

weeks = {d.isocalendar()[:2] for d in days(start, exam - timedelta(1))}
open_weeks = {d.isocalendar()[:2] for d in schooldays}
print("Calendar weeks before the exam:", len(weeks))
print("Weeks with no school day:", len(weeks - open_weeks))
print("Weeks left for tutoring:", len(open_weeks))
```
It prints Calendar weeks before the exam: 28, Weeks with no school day: 3 and Weeks left for tutoring: 25.

**Going further: from weeks to months** (Python)
```python
classes = 2 * len(open_weeks)
print("Classes at two a week:", classes)
print("Months of eight classes:", classes / 8)
```
It prints Classes at two a week: 50 and Months of eight classes: 6.25.

Three whole weeks have no school day at all: the week after Christmas and both weeks of Osterferien. That leaves 25 weeks, or 50 classes at two a week, which is just over six months of a plan with eight classes a month. Run with the NRW maths Abitur date, Wednesday 5 May 2027, the same program prints 26 calendar weeks, 3 without a school day and 23 left. For a family the useful number is the last one: it tells you how many months of help you are really buying and when they need to start.

1. **Ages 10 to 12, with a calendar.** Count the Mondays from 9 November 2026 to 17 May 2027, then cross out those that fall in your Land's holidays.
2. **Ages 13 to 15, Python.** Run Version 1 and Version 2 and explain the difference between 27 and 28 in one sentence.
3. **Ages 15 and up.** Change the holiday list to your own Land's dates from the KMK table and your own exam date. How many weeks are left for you?

## One to one or a group: which is better value for maths?

It depends on what the pupil needs, not on the price alone. Both formats are live, with the same mentor every week.

### When one to one is worth it

A pupil with a specific gap, for example negative numbers or fractions, who loses confidence in front of others. A pupil in the last months before the ZP10 or the Abitur who needs exam papers marked and talked through. An Oberstufe pupil whose Leistungskurs moves faster than any group could.

In one to one the mentor can stop at the exact step that went wrong and stay there until it is secure.

### When a group works better

A pupil who is broadly on track and wants steady practice, or one who learns by hearing how others solve the same problem. Groups are placed by level, so a pupil is not pulled along faster or held back for long.

In a group the pupil explains a method aloud to others, which is one of the surest ways to find out whether they really understand it.

Families can start in one format and change. A common pattern is a group through most of the year and a few months of one to one before the exam. Every class is taught in English; mentors give the German term beside every idea, Bruch beside fraction, Ableitung beside derivative, because school papers in Germany use German words.

## Is online maths tutoring cheaper than a tutor at home?

Often, because nobody travels. But the real question is whether the pupil learns as well online, and that depends on how the lesson is run.

### Travel

At home, someone pays for the tutor's journey, in the price or in fewer tutors to choose from. Online, the hour is all teaching.

### Writing

Maths needs writing. A good online lesson uses a shared whiteboard or a camera on the pupil's paper, so the mentor sees every line of working.

### Choice

Online, a family is not limited to the tutors who live nearby, which matters for English-speaking families and for Oberstufe maths.

Our classes are online only, live, and in English. We are a good fit for families who want that, and not for a child who needs a German-speaking tutor at the kitchen table. The trial class is the honest way to find out which kind of child yours is.
