Ans. The short version

For a class 10 AI previous year question paper, start with the CBSE 417 main papers of 2025 and 2026. Both had 21 questions, of which a student answers 15, in two hours for 50 marks: 24 objective marks in Section A and 26 written marks in Section B. Every paper we read had a four-mark confusion matrix case, a four-mark Bag of Words table, a stemming question, a chatbot type and assertion-reason items. The 2025 paper was set on the older syllabus. Modern Age Coders rehearses these shapes live on Monday and Wednesday at 9:00 PM IST, or one to one.

Q1. What did each year ask?

What did the CBSE Class 10 AI board papers ask in 2025 and 2026?

Both papers used the same frame: 21 questions, 15 to answer, two hours, 50 marks. What changed between them was the syllabus underneath. Here is each paper described in our words, from one set per year that we read in full.

What the frame looked like in both years

  • Section A, 24 marks. Five questions of six one-mark parts each: any 4 of 6 from employability skills, then any 5 of 6 in each of the four AI questions.
  • Section B, 26 marks. Any 3 of 5 employability two-markers, any 4 of 6 AI two-markers, any 3 of 5 AI four-markers.
  • Word limits printed on the paper: 20 to 30 words for two marks, 50 to 80 for four.
  • No negative marking in Section A in either year.

Assertion-reason is already here

Each of the three papers above carried assertion-reason items inside Section A, alongside two-statement items where you judge Statement 1 and Statement 2 separately. So these formats have appeared in 417 papers, not just in IT 402. CBSE has not said how many a 2027 paper will carry, so treat them as one of several Section A shapes, not the main one. A full practice set is on assertion and reason questions for Class 10 AI.

The two safe four-markers

The matrix case and the Bag of Words table appeared as four-mark questions in every paper we read. Together they are 8 of the 12 long-answer marks a student needs, so they are worth practising until they take under eight minutes each.

The rising unit

Ethics moved from a single essay on bias to a mix: a value-based or sector-based item, an assertion on bioethics and a long case. Unit 1 now rewards knowing the four bioethics principles by name and applying them.

The thin unit

Computer Vision has only four theory marks, and it was asked as one-mark facts and a two-mark explanation. Pixel arithmetic and RGB storage cover nearly all of it.

Q3. Section A, in the board's shapes

CBSE class 10 AI question paper practice with solutions: Section A shapes

Ten original one-mark items, one for each format the recent papers used: plain MCQ, a quick calculation, assertion-reason, two statements and one-word answers. Try each before opening the answer.

Items 1 to 5

  1. A model is tested on 500 samples: TP 150, TN 280, FP 40, FN 30. How many predictions were correct?
    (a) 180
    (b) 430
    (c) 320
    (d) 70
    Answer (b) Correct means TP + TN = 150 + 280. Option (d) is the number of wrong predictions, FP + FN.
  2. Out of 60 emails a filter marked as spam, 45 really were spam. Its precision is
    (a) 0.45
    (b) 0.60
    (c) 0.75
    (d) 1.33
    Answer (c) Precision = TP / all predicted positive = 45 / 60. Recall cannot be found: we are not told how many spam emails were missed.
  3. Assertion (A): A rule-based chatbot can answer only the questions its makers wrote replies for. Reason (R): A rule-based system does not learn from new data on its own.
    (a) Both true, R explains A
    (b) Both true, R does not explain A
    (c) A true, R false
    (d) A false, R true
    Answer (a) It is limited because it cannot learn, so the reason is the cause of the claim.
  4. Statement 1: Lemmatisation always returns a real dictionary word. Statement 2: Stemming is faster because it only cuts off word endings.
    (a) Both correct
    (b) Both incorrect
    (c) Only 1 correct
    (d) Only 2 correct
    Answer (a) Judge each statement alone. They do not have to explain each other in this format.
  5. A screen is 1280 x 720 pixels. How many pixels does it show?
    (a) 2,000
    (b) 92,160
    (c) 921,600
    (d) 2,764,800
    Answer (c) 1280 x 720 = 921,600. Option (d) multiplies by 3 again, which counts RGB values, not pixels.

Items 6 to 10

  1. A model groups news articles into topics with no labels given. Name the learning type and the subcategory.
    Answer Unsupervised learning, clustering. Name both: the question asks for two things.
  2. A bank's model lets a fraudulent payment through as genuine. Which outcome is this?
    Answer False negative: reality yes (fraud), prediction no.
  3. Which value of the four bioethics principles is broken when an AI shares a patient's records without asking them?
    Answer Respect for autonomy: the patient's right to decide about their own data and care.
  4. Assertion (A): Accuracy alone can hide a weak model. Reason (R): When one class is rare, a model that always predicts the common class still scores high accuracy.
    Answer Both true, and R explains A. This is why the papers also ask for precision, recall or F1.
  5. Inputs 1, 0 and 1 reach a perceptron with weights 0.5, 0.2 and 0.4 and a bias of -0.6. What is the weighted sum plus bias?
    Answer 0.5 + 0 + 0.4 - 0.6 = 0.3. Write the three products first, then add the bias, so a slip is easy to find.

Q4. The four-mark shapes, solved

CBSE class 10 AI question paper 2026 with solutions, the way the long answers repeat

Two original four-mark questions in the two shapes that every paper we read included, answered the way the value points split the marks: half a mark for each formula, half for substitution, the rest for the result or the table.

Other four-mark shapes from the recent papers

  1. Compare AI, ML and DL with one example each and draw how they nest.
    Answer AI is any technique that lets a machine act intelligently (a chess program); ML is AI that learns patterns from data (a spam filter); DL is ML using many-layered neural networks (face unlock). Draw DL inside ML inside AI. One mark per term, one for the labelled diagram.
  2. Name the AI domain for each, with a reason: (i) an app that reads a shop sign aloud in another language; (ii) a camera that counts cars at a toll gate.
    Answer (i) Natural Language Processing, because it translates text, though reading the sign first uses vision, so mention both for safety; (ii) Computer Vision, because it detects and counts objects in images. One mark for each domain, one for each reason.

Where students lost these marks in our mocks

  • No table drawn. Writing TP = 120 in a sentence instead of a labelled matrix.
  • Reality and prediction swapped. The 2025 scheme put prediction down the side; the 2025-26 sample paper put reality down the side. Label both axes and you are safe either way.
  • Steps not named in Bag of Words, so half the marks vanish.
  • Over-long answers. 50 to 80 words is the printed limit for four marks.

How each mark is split is set out on the 417 marking scheme, explained.

A note on finding papers

CBSE publishes its sample papers and marking schemes on cbseacademic.nic.in, and has posted main-exam marking schemes on cbse.gov.in. Those are the safest sources. Copies on other sites can carry typing errors in the answers, so check any number you copy by working it yourself.

What we could not verify

We read one set of each paper. Other sets of the same year use different questions in the same blueprint, so a topic we list for 2026 may have been asked differently in your region's set. We have not checked any 2024 or earlier paper ourselves, and the second board exam of 2026 is not covered here.

Questions about Class 10 AI past papers

What students ask about class 10 AI previous year question papers

Where can I get the class 10 AI previous year question paper?

CBSE posts sample papers and their marking schemes on cbseacademic.nic.in, and has published main-exam marking schemes on cbse.gov.in, which reproduce the questions. Those are the safest sources. This page describes what the 2025 and 2026 main papers asked and gives original practice in the same shapes, with worked answers.

What was asked in the CBSE class 10 AI question paper 2026?

In the set we read, Section A mixed MCQs, assertion-reason and two-statement items across Part A and every theory unit. Section B asked, among others, the seven Cs of communication, ICT uses, sustainable development, ethics frameworks, RGB storage, train-test split, stemming, a comparison of AI, ML and DL, an AI domain question, a cyber-security confusion matrix case and a Bag of Words table.

Is the 2025 AI paper still useful for 2027?

Partly. The 2025 paper followed the older syllabus, so its items on SQL, CSV files, data science applications, IoT and multiple intelligences are no longer theory topics. Its confusion matrix case, Bag of Words table, neural network question, chatbot item and ethics essay still match the 2026-27 units closely.

What are the most repeated questions in class 10 AI?

In every paper we read: a four-mark confusion matrix case, a four-mark Bag of Words table, a stemming or lemmatisation item, a chatbot type, a false positive or false negative from a situation, and an overfitting or unseen-test-data item. Neural networks and ethics appeared in every paper, in different mark bands.

Are assertion reason questions asked in the class 10 AI 417 paper?

Yes. The 2025 main paper, the 2025-26 sample paper and the 2026 main paper we read each had assertion-reason items in Section A. CBSE has not said how many a 2027 paper will include, so practise them as one Section A format among several.

How many questions are in the class 10 AI board paper?

Twenty-one, of which you answer 15. Section A has five objective questions worth 24 marks, each with six one-mark parts and a choice. Section B has sixteen written questions worth 26 marks, of which you answer ten. The paper lasts two hours.

Do previous year papers include Python questions?

The 2025 paper, set on the older syllabus, touched data handling topics. From 2025-26, Advance Python and Statistical Data are assessed only in the practical, so the theory paper does not ask you to write code. Practise programs for the practical file and lab exam instead.

How many previous year papers should I solve for class 10 AI?

Solve the 2025-26 sample paper and the 2026 main paper fully under time, then use the 2025 paper for the topics that still count. After that, fresh questions in the same shapes help more than further old papers, because the newest syllabus has only one board paper so far.

Is this page a copy of the CBSE question paper?

No. We describe each paper in our own words and write our own questions and answers. For the exact wording, read the official marking schemes. Our aim is to show the shapes that repeat and the working that earns each mark.
