> A class 10 AI crash course is a short, time-boxed live revision of CBSE 417 before the board exam. A workable plan is six weeks: twelve sessions that revise every theory unit in order of marks (Unit 2 carries 11, Unit 3 10, Unit 6 8, Unit 1 7, Unit 5 4, Part A 10), bring the practical file and viva up to standard, and run five timed mocks, each reviewed. Modern Age Coders runs this inside its Monday and Wednesday 9:00 PM IST batch during the revision phase, or as personalised one to one at the family's own time.

## Is a class 10 AI crash course enough to score well in 417?

For the theory paper, usually yes: 50 marks over five units and Part A can be revised in six weeks if the student has met the topics at school. The practical half is where a late start hurts, because a file, a project and two vivas cannot all be built in a fortnight. Here is what fits and what does not.

- Part of the 100 marks | Marks | Can six weeks fix it? | What the crash plan does

- Section A, objective items | 24 | Yes | Timed sets by unit, then mixed; traps named

- Section B, written answers | 26 | Yes, with marking | Answer shapes for 2 and 4 marks, rewritten after marking

- Practical file, at least 15 programs | 15 | Partly: depends on how many exist now | Three or four programs a week, each understood

- Practical examination | 15 | Yes, if school's date is not too close | Two practical mocks on unseen tasks

- Viva on the practical | 5 | Yes | Questions on the student's own programs

- Project | 10 | Only a small one, started at once | A tight scope chosen in week 1

- Project viva | 5 | Yes, once the project exists | A rehearsal in week 2 before the paper

Dates first. The 2027 CBSE date sheet is not out yet; in 2026 the AI 417 paper was on 27 February. Your school sets the practical examination date, which can come weeks before the theory paper. Count your six weeks back from whichever comes first, and if the practical is very close, the plan swaps its order so the file and viva come first.

## The six-week AI 417 revision batch plan, session by session

Twelve live sessions, two a week, ordered by marks and difficulty: the heavy units first, Part A in short blocks, mocks from week 3. Week 6 is six weeks before the paper; week 1 is the final week.

- Weeks to go | Monday session | Wednesday session | Work between sessions

- 6 | Diagnostic mock (Section A) then Unit 2: model types, perceptron sums | Unit 3: confusion matrix, precision, recall, F1, which metric when | Project topic fixed; count the file programs that exist

- 5 | Unit 6: normalisation, bag of words, TFIDF; script and smart bots | Section B drill on Units 2, 3 and 6, marked on screen | Three file programs: lists and NumPy statistics

- 4 | Unit 1: project cycle, 4Ws canvas, ethical frameworks, case questions | Practical mock 1: unseen program on Units 4 and 7, short viva | Three file programs: charts and a CSV

- 3 | Unit 5: pixels, RGB counts, CV tasks; Part A units 1 to 3 | Full mock 1: 50 marks, two hours, timed | Rewrite every lost answer; project data gathered

- 2 | Review of mock 1 as a doubt session; Part A units 4 and 5 | Full mock 2, then practical mock 2 with project viva rehearsal | File complete and read aloud once

- 1 | Review of mock 2; the error list, unit by unit | Final mock: a half paper on the weakest two units, then exam-room routine | Light revision only; sleep before the paper

### Why heavy units first
Units 2 and 3 together carry 21 theory marks and need practice, not reading. Starting there leaves time for two rounds of mistakes.

### Why Part A in blocks
Ten marks across five units, each quick to learn. Spread through weeks 3 and 2, they never crowd out the numericals.

### Why an error list
Every lost mark from the mocks goes on one sheet. The last week revises that sheet, not the whole book.

## The crash course mock test schedule, with marks and timing

Five timed mocks over six weeks: a diagnostic, two full papers, a final half paper and two practical mocks counted as one series. Full mocks follow the 2025-26 sample paper layout, because the 2026-27 sample paper for 417 has not been released.

- Mock | Weeks to go | What it covers | Marks | Timed

- 1. Diagnostic | 6 | Section A: Part A any 4 of 6, AI units any 20 of 24 | 24 | Yes, objective only

- 2. Practical mocks | 4 and 2 | Unseen program on Units 4 to 7, viva on own file; project viva in week 2 | Practical | Yes

- 3. Full mock 1 | 3 | Whole theory paper: Section A 24, Section B 26 | 50 | Two hours

- 4. Full mock 2 | 2 | Whole theory paper, new questions, same layout | 50 | Two hours

- 5. Final half paper | 1 | The two weakest units from mocks 3 and 4 | Set per student | Yes

### Inside Section B, as the 2025-26 sample paper set it

- Block | Choice | Marks

- Part A short answers, 2 marks | any 3 of 5 | 6

- Part B short answers, 2 marks | any 4 of 6 | 8

- Part B long answers, 4 marks | any 3 of 5 | 12

- Section B total |  | 26

### How each mock is reviewed

-
**Marked against the scheme** Point by point, the way CBSE marking schemes award marks, so students see which line earned what.

-
**Lost marks sorted** Into "did not know", "knew but slipped" and "ran out of time". Each needs a different fix.

-
**Re-attempted** Every lost written answer is rewritten before the next session, and goes on the error list.

The wider set of papers used across the full year is on [the sample paper and mock tests page](/cbse-class-10-ai-sample-paper-and-mock-tests), and the way each answer is marked is explained on [the 417 marking scheme](/cbse-class-10-ai-marking-scheme).

## One crash session in full: Unit 6 NLP in a single sitting

Unit 6, Natural Language Processing, carries 8 theory marks and one calculation that comes up again and again: bag of words, then TFIDF. Here is how the Monday of week 5 covers it, in the order the class runs.

-
**Recall check** Five quick questions: what is a token, a stopword, a corpus; stemming against lemmatisation; one script bot and one smart bot.

-
**Normalise three documents** Lower case, split into tokens, no stopwords needed here because the sentences are short.

-
**Build the dictionary and vectors** Six unique words; one row of counts per document.

-
**Document frequency, then IDF** Count how many documents contain each word; IDF = log(N / document frequency), with N = 3.

-
**Read the result aloud** Which word best identifies document 1? The student must say why, not just point.

-
**Exam turn** One four-mark question on the same method with new sentences, timed and marked before the session ends.

```
D1: AI helps doctors
D2: AI helps farmers
D3: farmers grow food

         ai helps doctors farmers grow food
D1        1   1     1       0      0    0
D2        1   1     0       1      0    0
D3        0   0     0       1      1    1

Doc freq  2   2     1       2      1    1
IDF     0.176 0.176 0.477 0.176 0.477 0.477

TFIDF for D1:
ai 0.176, helps 0.176, doctors 0.477
```

The point the examiner rewards: a word found in fewer documents gets a higher IDF, so it says more about the document it is in. More worked sets are on [bag of words and TFIDF for Class 10 AI](/bag-of-words-tfidf-class-10-ai), and the rest of the unit on [NLP questions](/cbse-class-10-ai-nlp-questions).

## AI class 10 fast track: joining in the last months, batch or one to one

There is no separate crash batch. A student who starts late joins the Monday and Wednesday batch, which by then is in its revision and mock phase, or takes one to one so the six-week plan can start on the day they join. Which suits depends on when you start.

- Starting | What usually works | Why

- November or early December | The batch, plus a few one to one sessions for any unit school has not yet covered | There is time for the batch's own revision and full mock series

- Mid December to early January | The six-week plan above, one to one or batch plus one to one | The batch is already mid-mock; a private plan can start at week 6

- Less than four weeks before the paper | One to one, compressed: heavy units, two full mocks, file and viva | Every session must go on the student's own gaps

- Studying from abroad | One to one at a local hour, two private classes a week | Matches the plan's twelve-session rhythm in a home time zone

In India the one to one plan is one private class a week; for a twelve-session crash, families usually pair it with the batch or ask the mentor at the free demo how to fit the plan to the weeks left. If the gap is mostly one unit, a short private plan from [a Class 10 AI private tutor](/cbse-class-10-ai-private-tutor) is the faster fix. ## Frequently asked questions

**Is there a crash course for class 10 AI 417?** Yes, as a six-week live revision plan. It runs inside our Monday and Wednesday 9:00 PM IST batch during its revision and mock phase, or as one to one starting the day you join. It revises every theory unit by marks, runs five timed mocks and brings the practical file and viva up to standard.

**Can I prepare class 10 AI in one month?** For the theory paper, yes, if you have met the units at school: revise Units 2 and 3 first, then 6, 1 and 5, with Part A in short blocks, and sit at least two full timed mocks. The practical is harder in a month if the file is far from 15 programs, so start that on day one.

**What should I study in the last month for AI class 10?** Practise rather than read. Numericals in Units 3 and 6 (confusion matrix, bag of words, TFIDF), four-mark answer shapes, Part A short answers, and two full timed mocks with every lost mark rewritten. Keep the last two days for the error list and your file programs.

**How many mock tests should I take before the AI board exam?** Two full timed papers is the useful minimum, with a review after each. This plan adds a Section A diagnostic, a final half paper on weak units and two practical mocks. More papers without reviews add little; the review is where the marks come from.

**Is a crash course enough to score 90 in AI 417?** It can be, for a student who already understands most units and needs practice, marking and timing. It is not enough for someone meeting the topics for the first time, or whose file and project are not started. We do not promise a score; the diagnostic in the first class gives an honest view.

**When does the AI 417 revision batch start?** The batch moves into revision and full mocks from December. A crash student can join at any point; one to one starts the six-week plan on the day you join, counted back from your exam or practical date.

**What if my practical exam is before the crash course ends?** The plan is reordered: file programs, the practical mock and both viva rehearsals come first, and theory revision follows. Your school sets the practical date, so tell the mentor at the free demo.

**Is the AI class 10 fast track only for weak students?** No. Many students taking it have done well in school tests but have never sat a full two-hour 417 paper or rehearsed a viva. The mocks and reviews are what they need most.

**Do I get the mock papers and marking?** Yes. Every mock is set in the paper's format, marked against the scheme and reviewed in class, and each lost written answer is rewritten. All of it is part of the monthly fee shown on this page.

**When is the CBSE Class 10 AI exam in 2027?** The 2027 date sheet has not been published yet. In 2026, the AI 417 paper was held on 27 February. Plan your six weeks back from the date once CBSE releases it, and from your school's practical date if that comes earlier.
