What a year in each course actually consists of

Computer Science Principles

A PROJECT COURSE
  • Nine hours of class time building one program of your own choosing
  • Submit the code, a video of it running, and a written reference
  • 70 multiple choice questions in 120 minutes, 70 per cent
  • Two written responses in 60 minutes about your own program, 30 per cent
  • Any language you like. Nothing is prescribed

Computer Science A

A PROGRAMMING COURSE
  • Four units, ending in data collections, which carries the most marks
  • No project, no portfolio, nothing to submit before exam day
  • 42 multiple choice questions, 55 per cent
  • Four free response questions typed as Java, 45 per cent
  • Java, and only Java, with a quick reference sheet provided
