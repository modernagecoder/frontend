Modern Age Codersexam morning
Read this list once on the morning of the paper
01Keywords in capitals. IF, REPEAT, PROCEDURE.
02Four spaces of indentation, but THEN and ELSE take two.
03Assignment is the left arrow, never the equals sign.
04Equals means comparison. Not equal is a pair of angle brackets.
05Strings take double quotes, single characters take single quotes.
06A REAL literal needs a digit each side of the point. 4.0, not 4.
07Declare arrays with both bounds. ARRAY[1:30] OF STRING.
08A CONSTANT can only be a literal. Never an expression.
09DIV and MOD are functions here. DIV(10, 3), not 10 DIV 3.
10Comments start with two slashes and run to the end of the line.
