## Hard rules
1. **Write only `content/ca/<slug>.js`** for your rows, plus raw source copies under `Desktop/SkyCoders/keyword-research-2026-09/ca/src-pilots/<slug>/` (or `src/<slug>/`). Never edit any other repo file. Never run `build.js` without `--dry-run`; never `git add`, commit or push.
2. **Facts at the primary source, fetched raw** with `curl -sL -A "Mozilla/5.0"`. Quote only strings you grepped in the raw file. Facts already verified in `facts-schools-contests.md` / `facts-geo.md` may be used as they stand (they carry their quote, URL and saved file). Never use WebFetch or a search summary as a source. On 403/429/Cloudflare stop for that host; never retry around it. Never sum published parts into a total. Label our own calculations, examples, plans and practice problems as ours.
3. **Kill rule:** a page with fewer than 8 verified facts of its own is not written; report SKIP with the reason.
4. **Currency-neutral copy (Canada-specific):** never write "USD", "US dollars", "CAD", "Canadian dollars", "C$", "CA$" or a typed `$` figure in copy. Fees render from the template as price anchors that show each visitor their own currency. Say "monthly fee", "per month", "eight live classes a month". No prices in FAQ text at all.

FAQ answers must not contain `&rsquo;`, `&ldquo;` or `&gt;` (schema check): use plain `\'` inside JS single quotes. Title at most 65 characters, description 145 to 165 characters, 8 to 12 FAQ items. Canadian spelling where Canadians use it (centre, colour, programme only for official names) but "math", and grades not years.

Any time table uses the 2026 offsets in `facts-geo.md` section 3: BC UTC-7 all year (permanent after 8 March 2026), Alberta and Saskatchewan UTC-6 all year, Manitoba UTC-5 from 1 November 2026, Ontario and Quebec UTC-5 winter / UTC-4 summer (back 1 November 2026, forward 14 March 2027), Atlantic UTC-4 / UTC-3, Newfoundland UTC-3:30 / UTC-2:30. 7. **No credit claims:** every page about a credit course (ICS3U, ICS4U, ICD2O, MHF4U, MCV4U, Math 30-1 ...) says plainly, near the top and in one FAQ, that we teach alongside the student's school course and "do not grant Ontario credits" (or the province's).
