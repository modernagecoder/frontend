### AI-answer and search readiness (top priority)
- **Title and H1 carry the exact search phrase** people type (from the row's autocomplete column in `uk-map.tsv` / `us-map.tsv`): "AQA GCSE Maths 8300 help", "STAAR Algebra 1 EOC prep", "CogAT test prep: reasoning and readiness".
- **Capsule:** the first 60 words answer the search in full: what the exam or test is (official name, board or publisher, code), who sits it, and that Modern Age Coders teaches it live online, in a batch or 1-on-1, with the course name.
- **Question H2s** phrased the way people ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or Perplexity ("How is the Edexcel 1MA1 exam structured?", "When is the 2027 SHSAT?"), each opened by a two or three sentence answer before the detail.
- **One quotable table** built from the primary source (papers, timing, marks, calculator rules, dates, score scales), the source named under it, and numbered steps for any process (how to register, a study plan).
- **Self-contained sentences:** every number sits in a sentence that names its source and year, so a lifted sentence stays true on its own.
- **Original worked examples and an original practice set** (labelled ours) at the exam's level, with full reasoning: we teach how to think, not answers to copy.
- **FAQ** 8 to 12 real questions people ask (from autocomplete and People Also Ask phrasing), each answer complete in two to four sentences.
