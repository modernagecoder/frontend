## Writer brief (every agent reads this whole section)

### Hard rules
1. **Write only `content/us/<slug>.js`** for your assigned rows, plus raw source copies under `Desktop/SkyCoders/keyword-research-2026-09/us/src/<slug>/`. Never edit any other repo file. Never run `build.js` without `--dry-run`, never `git add`, never commit, never push.
2. **Facts at the primary source, fetched raw** with `curl -sL -A "Mozilla/5.0"` (for Roblox docs add `.md`). Quote only strings you grepped in the raw file. Never use WebFetch or search summaries as a source. On 403/429/Cloudflare stop for that host; do not retry around it. Never sum published parts into a total. Label our own calculations, examples and plans as ours.
3. **Kill rule:** a page with fewer than 8 verified facts of its own is not written; report it as SKIP with the reason.
4. **Copy rules:** no em dash, no en dash (write "2026 to 2027"), no "Premium", no emoji, at most one visible "best" (except slugs starting `best-`), no typed `$` figures (prices render from the template), no "weekend batch", never bare "IST" (write "India time"), no prices in FAQ text, fees text says "US dollars" (never "your local currency"). FAQ answers must not contain `&rsquo;`, `&ldquo;` or `&gt;` (schema check): use plain `\'` inside JS single quotes. Title at most 65 characters, description 145 to 165 characters, 8 to 12 FAQ items.
5. **Timing line, exactly:** "Weekdays or weekends, flexible: choose your batch, or for a very personalised plan choose 1-on-1 at your convenience, weekend mornings or weekdays." Use it in `how.lede` (ag) or a delivery cell (cg) and one FAQ.
6. **Claims:** no grade, score, admission, job or salary promises; no track-record claims we cannot show ("most of our students...", "the best entries we see"); under-18 pages never recommend 18+ tools; no crime, health, money, religion, disaster or identity content; no race or gender statistics; never name an individual school or person except an official quoted by their own organisation; address data as counts only. No affiliation claims: say we are not connected with the organisation.
7. **Uniqueness:** overlap limit 3% against every page on the site. Write every sentence fresh, including the boilerplate-like fields (`start.note`, `start.readFirst`, `start.formNote`, `fees.lede`, `reviewsLede`, `projectsLede`, `elsewhere[].p`, catalogue blurbs, `how.cells`, footer `bottomRight`). Do not reuse sentences from the pilots or from each other.
8. **Links:** only to URLs that exist today (`grep -q "modernagecoders.com/<path></loc>" sitemap*.xml`). Do not link to other planned US pages that are not built yet.
9. **Courses:** only slugs that resolve: `node -e "require('./scripts/nl/lib/catalogue').course('<slug>')"`.

### Page families
- **Phase 2 and 3 topic doors (ag):** answer the search in the first 60 words (capsule), then real depth: a table built from the primary source, numbered steps, real code that uses only documented APIs, an original worked example or practice set (labelled ours). AI-answer ready: quotable sentences that name their source and date.
- **Competitions (Phase 3):** official rules and dates from the organiser only; dates stated with the cycle year so they stay true later; how to prepare; our plan labelled ours.
- **AP pages:** College Board (apcentral.collegeboard.org, reports.collegeboard.org) only. Calculators: explain the published scoring structure and give a worked example clearly labelled as an estimate method, never an official conversion unless College Board publishes one.
- **State data pages (Phase 3) and state pages (Phase 4, cg):** state education department page for the CS requirement and standards (find it with the department's site search or sitemap; if blocked, say so in rejectedClaims and use only what remains); Code.org (2025) State of AI + CS Education raw sheet `keyword-research-2026-09/us/src-codeorg/sheet.xlsx` (tabs: Access Percent over the Years, Most Recent Access Data, Participation Percent, Most Recent Participation Data; never the Geography, Gender or Race tabs; cite "Code.org (2025). 2025 State of AI + CS Education"); College Board `src-cb/ap-by-state-2024.xlsx` (schools with AP exams 2023 and 2024); IANA tzdb for the state's zone(s) (`https://data.iana.org/time-zones/tzdb/northamerica`); one signature project on a state or city open dataset fetched with curl (ArcGIS Hub APIs work; check the licence text), our numbers labelled ours. US daylight saving 2026 to 2027: back November 1, 2026, forward March 14, 2027.
- **Metros (Phase 5, cg):** the city's own open data portal for the project, the state CS rules in one paragraph, time zone, and nothing that repeats the state page.
- **Phase T, school coding tools (ag):** help a student who is stuck in CodeHS, Code.org App Lab, BlueJ, Eclipse, IntelliJ, PyCharm, VS Code, Google Colab, Jupyter, GitHub Classroom, MakeCode Arcade, Scratch or Roblox Studio. Use the tool's official documentation and help pages only. Real error messages and real fixes, numbered troubleshooting steps, a small code example that reproduces and fixes the error. **Never answer keys or solutions to graded assignments**; say plainly that we teach the concept so the student can solve it themselves. The offer: live help with a teacher, batch or 1-on-1.
- **Comparisons:** a criteria table with facts from each provider's own pages (format, ages, live or recorded, group size if published), dated; never their prices unless published on their site; no claims of being better; let the reader choose.
