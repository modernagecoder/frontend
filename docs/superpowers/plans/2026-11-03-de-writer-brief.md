- **Länder (`land`):** that Land's Informatik rule, school-year and holiday dates (KMK), Abitur facts, its school types, the Destatis population of the Land and its capital, and one Land-wide open-data project.
- **City pages (`city`, `maths`, `school`, `stadt`):** one local data project (ours) on that city's own open data with a reuse licence that allows commercial use (Datenlizenz Deutschland Zero 2.0 or Namensnennung 2.0, CC BY, CC0, public domain; never CC BY-NC, never "nur nicht-kommerziell"). Use `opendata.tsv` where the city is listed; otherwise search GovData (`scripts/govdata-search.mjs "<city>"`) or the city portal and check the licence on the dataset page.
