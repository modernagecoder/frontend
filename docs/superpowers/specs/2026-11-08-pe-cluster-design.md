## 3. Rules for every page

All rules of the earlier clusters bind (UK/US/CA/DE writer briefs), plus:
1. **English-taught, said plainly.** Spanish pages put "Clases en vivo, en inglés, con profesores desde la India" in the answer capsule and near the H1, and frame it as a benefit (coding or maths plus daily English practice), never hide it. Never claim Spanish-language teaching.
2. **Timing (owner):** flexible, any time, batch or 1-on-1; no timetable, no "weekend batch" promise. Pages may show a Lima to India time table (Peru is UTC-5 all year, no daylight saving; India is 10 hours 30 minutes ahead), and families pick demo times in their own clock (already built).
3. **Facts only from primary sources** (MINEDU, INEI, universities, ONEM, OPI/UTEC, IB, EF), quoted from raw files, every number with its year. School-calendar dates came from OCR of scanned PDFs: check the PDF image before printing a date. No political names (ministers change).
4. **No accreditation claims:** never imply MINEDU, SUNEDU, COAR or university endorsement, or a "certificado oficial". Our own certificate of completion only, if the course already gives one.
5. **Never name a real school as a source of students.** Name schools only as context with their own page as source.
6. **Spanish quality:** native Peruvian Spanish, "usted" to parents, Peruvian school terms (primaria, secundaria, 5to de secundaria, colegio, clases particulares, vacaciones útiles). A native-quality review pass on the first Spanish pilot before any sweep.
7. **Robotics honesty:** robotics pages are "robótica o programación" explainers; never imply robot kits or hardware.
8. **Pre-university honesty:** admission exam pages explain the exams (dates, subjects, from the universities' own pages) and offer maths reasoning practice in English; never promise admission results.
9. Copy rules as always: no em or en dashes, no "Premium", no emoji, at most one "best" per page (superlative in title and meta only, with a "what best means" section), no typed prices (fees render from the template), no competitor prices, no AggregateRating, brand numbers only from `scripts/brand-facts.json`, no fake local address or phone, never noindex.

## 8. Owner decisions and questions for a lawyer

Research only, not legal advice. Quotes and sources in `facts-market/LEGAL.md` (20 questions there).
1. **Prices in USD only.** Peru's consumer code (Ley 29571 Art. 6.1) normally requires a soles price next to any foreign-currency price, with the exchange rate. It exempts sales "desde y hacia el exterior", which may cover a provider in India. If not, it clashes with your one-currency rule. **Question for a Peruvian lawyer.** Until then: USD only, as on every other non-India page.
2. **Libro de Reclamaciones (complaints book).** Ley 32495 (11 Nov 2025) requires e-commerce platforms to show a permanent link to a complaints book and answer within 15 working days; the regulation was still a draft in July 2026. **Decision:** add a simple online complaints-book page for Peru visitors (low cost)?
3. **Children's data (DS 016-2024-JUS):** parents consent for children under 14; 14 to 17 consent themselves; foreign providers targeting Peru need a representative "en o para" Peru and rules apply to data sent abroad. **Decision:** add parental-consent wording to forms shown in Peru?
4. **Tax on digital services (D.Leg. 1623, from 1 Dec 2024):** covers essentially automatic services, but "conferencia remota" is listed; whether live classes fall in is unclear. Question for a tax adviser.
5. **Re-run keywords from a Peruvian IP (VPN) or share GSC data for Peru** before P3 titles are locked. Spanish data is usable now; English data is not.
6. **New courses C1 and C2** (§4): build, or reuse the existing olympiad and PCEP courses (my recommendation: reuse)?
7. **Spanish review:** can someone who speaks Peruvian Spanish read the first Spanish pilot, or should I rely on a careful native-style pass?
8. **What the vacaciones útiles pages sell (deadline item).** Peruvian summer programmes have a start date, a length and an age band. We have a holiday-camp plan (one payment, USD 200 international) but no defined length or start window on the site. **Decision:** sell the holiday camp with a defined format (how many weeks, classes per week, ages, start window January to March 2027), or sell ordinary flexible monthly classes under a summer label?
