## 6. Competitions and exams: rules for every such page

1. Preparation, never affiliation: name the organiser or authority, link it, say we are independent.
2. Every date and format re-verified at the organiser in the build week; the page shows "checked on <date>".
3. Original practice only; past papers are linked, never reproduced.
4. No entry fees printed (they are AUD; one currency rule).
5. No score, band, ATAR, medal or selection promises.
6. Assessment integrity: HSC, VCE, QCE and WACE school-based and major projects are the student's own work under each authority's rules; we teach technique and never write assessed work.

## 8. Uniqueness

One content module and dossier per page (8+ `requiredMentions`, unique site-wide, collision-checked by `register.js`); one lesson family per page, unused anywhere on the site (grep `content/` first); seven-word shingle overlap at or under 3% (aim 2.5%) against every page in the cluster; all prose written fresh, never adapted from a sibling. ## 9. Design and conversion

- Place pages on `cg-`, doors, exams and competitions on `ag-`. Accent per page: HSV saturation .40 to .85, value .30 to .62, no hue in 255 to 335 or 175 to 205, contrast at least 4.8:1 against #EDE7DA (stricter than the build's 4.5:1 gate), with a unique page code.
- Market object for every module (proven by `scripts/nl/test-market-au.js`): `{ name: 'Australia', iso: 'AU', dial: '+61', lang: 'en-AU', locale: 'en_AU', geoRegion: 'AU', brandTag: 'AU', phoneLabel: 'Australian mobile number', phonePlaceholder: '491 570 156', gradeLabel: 'Year level or age', minDigits: 9, stripTrunk: true }`. The placeholder is on ACMA's list of numbers reserved for fiction. The normaliser turns 0491..., +61 491..., 0061 491... and 61491... into the 9 national digits.
- Course cards with real thumbnails first; priority demo CTA (`data-pd-book`); four contact touchpoints plus the sticky bar; WhatsApp prefill names the page's place; `formPage` is the page's own path.
- USD only: group USD 100 a month, one to one USD 150, first class free. **Cost pages:** the `mp-` cost pattern named in the brief (`gcse-maths-tutor-cost-uk`) does not exist on any branch, so cost pages are built on `ag-` with our USD prices and per-hour and per-term arithmetic only. AUD market rates (converted to USD at a dated RBA rate, in `data-price` spans shown only to non-India visitors) wait for that pattern and owner decision 6.
- The +91 number is presented as Indian; no Australian office, address, phone or `LocalBusiness`. No em or en dashes, no emoji, no gradients.
