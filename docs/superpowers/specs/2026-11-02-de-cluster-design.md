## 3. Rules for every page

All rules of the UK/US/CA clusters apply (plan `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-31-uk-us-exams-cluster.md`, writer brief), plus:
1. **English-medium, said plainly.** English pages say teachers are in India and teach in English (Hindi on request). German pages put "Unterricht auf Englisch, mit Mentoren aus Indien" in the capsule and the H1 area, and frame it as a benefit (coding plus English practice), never hide it. Never claim German-language teaching.
2. **No timing promise in CET beyond the truth.** Germany is 4h30 behind India in winter and 3h30 in summer; afternoon and evening German slots are realistic, weekend mornings too. Use the owner's flexible timing line. Families pick demo times in their own clock (already built).
3. **Facts only from primary sources**, quoted from raw files; KMK, Land ministries, IQB, GI Informatik-Monitor, competition organisers, Destatis. Every date with its year and source.
4. **No accreditation claims:** never Bildungsgutschein, BuT/Lernförderung, ZFU approval, AZAV, or "anerkannt" unless the owner obtains it.
5. **Never name a real school as a source of our students.** Name international schools only as context ("Munich has several international schools") with the school's own page as source, never "our students come from".
6. **German-language quality:** German pages are written natively (formal "Sie" to parents, "du" never), with correct school terms (Klasse, Oberstufe, LK/GK, Abitur, MSA, ZP10, Quali). A native-quality review pass before shipping the first German pilot.
7. **No legal, tax or immigration advice.** Explainers describe what official sources say.
8. Copy rules as always: no em/en dashes, no "Premium", no emoji, at most one "best", no typed prices (fees render from the template), no competitor prices.

## 8. Owner decisions and questions for a lawyer (before selling to German consumers at scale)

Research only, not legal advice. Quotes and sources in `facts-market/LEGAL.md`.
1. **Distance-learning law (FernUSG).** Without the required approval from the state body (ZFU), a distance-learning contract is void (§7) and fines reach €10,000. The Federal Court of Justice (12 June 2025, III ZR 109/24) treated live sessions with recordings available afterwards as distance learning and read "learning-success control" broadly. ZFU's own FAQ says purely live two-way video teaching is equivalent to classroom teaching. Our recordings, progress reports and 6-month prepay matter here. A draft bill (27 Aug 2026) would repeal most of the law from 1 July 2027; the current law applies now. **Question:** do our live classes need ZFU approval, or should German plans be offered without recordings as a substitute for attendance?
2. **14-day withdrawal right** for consumer distance contracts, the new "withdraw contract" function (§356a BGB, since 19 June 2026), and a "cancel contracts here" button for subscriptions (§312k). Our refund policy is 7 days. **Decision:** adopt a 14-day withdrawal and the two buttons for German customers?
3. **Price display:** total price including all charges up front (Preisangabenverordnung). The 2.5% gateway charge at checkout and the euro-shown, dollar-charged model need a lawyer's view.
4. **Children's data (GDPR Art. 8):** consent age 16 in Germany; our forms collect a child's age band and a phone number, and the privacy page has no GDPR section. A possible EU representative (Art. 27). **Decision:** add a GDPR privacy section and parental-consent wording to forms shown in Germany?
5. **Impressum (Digitale-Dienste-Gesetz §5):** whether it binds a provider in India targeting Germany is a lawyer question; adding a simple Impressum page is low cost.
6. **VAT:** since 1 January 2025 live-streamed teaching to EU consumers is taxed where the customer lives; the non-Union OSS scheme is the route. Question for a tax adviser.

Recommendation: build P0 to P2 content now (informational value, and leads arrive by WhatsApp anyway), and settle items 1 to 4 with a German lawyer before the Germany checkout goes live in euro. Other owner choices:
- 7. Re-run autocomplete from a German IP (VPN) or share GSC data for Germany, to validate titles before P2.
- 8. German city pages (10): keep (they catch real demand) or drop (German city demand is mostly in-person)?
- 9. Teaching in German: if any mentor can teach in German, German pages could offer it; otherwise English only, as planned.
